## Serm Suk PCL and Thai Drinks (est cola, ThaiBev)
- GotoManager, 29 October 2023 (https://gotomanager.com/cover-story/5234; text re-decoded and saved on 20 October 2026 as `gotomanager_5234_2023-10-29_est_pepsi_history.md`; S): Serm Suk held the Pepsi licence from 1953; PepsiCo ended the contract in 2012 and set up Pepsi-Cola (Thai) Trading Co., Ltd. to produce and distribute Pepsi in Thailand from 1 November 2012; the est launch was on Friday 2 November 2012; Serm Suk later came under the ThaiBev group, and Thai Drinks Co., Ltd. markets est. The page names no current holder of Pepsi. The current Pepsi company is Suntory PepsiCo Beverage (Thailand), per the two Thansettakij articles above. When Pepsi passed from Pepsi-Cola (Thai) Trading to Suntory PepsiCo Beverage (Thailand) is not stated in the pages read. So Serm Suk is not the Pepsi bottler now, and the task description of 20 October is out of date on this point.
- Serm Suk revenue (P, SET filings, re-read at source on 20 October 2026; see `sermsuk_mda_and_form250_2_revenue.md`): sales and services THB 10,905 million (FY2022), 12,371 million (FY2023) and 13,153 million (FY2024), fiscal years to 30 September. The total includes est, Crystal drinking water and distributed brands. Its carbonated soft drinks line (Form 250-2, page 11) is THB 3,972.69 million (FY2022), 4,550.15 million (FY2023) and 3,964.26 million for the nine months to 30 June 2024. Serm Suk makes est under a manufacturing contract for the ThaiBev trademark owner, so this line is Serm Suk's own revenue, not est brand sales at the trademark owner's prices.
- Thai Drinks Co., Ltd. revenue was not found. ThaiBev's FY2025 results page (https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/1OD9LM5B28IVXJ69/868094_YearEnd%202025) is a cover page with attached PDFs that were not opened; its non-alcoholic beverage segment would in any case include water and other non-carbonated drinks.
- est share: 8.6 percent by volume (Thansettakij, 16 March 2024); 10.6 percent (Thansettakij, 13 October 2025, measure not stated, up from 7 percent before the 2023 rebrand).

## AJ Thai Co., Ltd. (Big Cola)
- Sales THB 3,600 million in 2024 and a target of THB 4,500 million for 2025 (Thansettakij, 1 March 2025, company statement: https://www.thansettakij.com/business/marketing/620945). The article prints "ยอดขายรวม" (total sales) and does not say whether the figure covers carbonated drinks only or other AJ Thai drinks too, so 3,600 is a proxy for Big Cola's carbonated sales, not a reported carbonated figure. Thansettakij of 1 March 2025 (`thansettakij_620945_2025-03-01_bigcola.md`) also gives the carbonated market as "over 62,000 million" for the year before, with growth of 3 percent. Thansettakij of 3 April 2025 (`thansettakij_624003_2025-04-03_pepsi_bigcola.md`), in the Big Cola section, gives dark cola as about 85 percent of the carbonated market, worth "about 6 หมื่นล้านบาท" (about 60,000 million, no year, no source). Share target 7 percent in 2025 and 15 percent by 2027 (Marketeer, 12 March 2025).
