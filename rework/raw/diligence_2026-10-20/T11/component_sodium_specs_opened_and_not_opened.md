# Component sodium specifications: what was opened and what could not be (20 October 2026)

## Opened

### Potassium bicarbonate, food grade: Armand Products Company sales specification (supplier sheet)

- URL: https://www.armandproducts.com/products/media/potassium-bicarbonate-food-grade/ (PDF, 1 page, 264.9 KB; title "Armand KBC Food Specs 0522", revision 05/22, PDF dated 26 May 2022). Linked from https://www.armandproducts.com/products/potassium-bicarbonate/ . Date read: 20 October 2026. The PDF text was extracted locally (PyMuPDF).
- Tier: S (a supplier's own specification, a company statement; not a compendium). Status: SOURCED for what the sheet says.
- Specification as printed: total alkalinity (as KHCO3), dry basis, 99.5 to 101.5 wt percent; loss on drying 0.25 wt percent max; normal carbonate (as K2CO3) 2.5 wt percent max; heavy metals (as Pb) 0.001 wt percent max; residual solvents meets requirements; identification positive for potassium and bicarbonate. Notes: meets USP/NF chemical test requirements; meets cGMP and Food Chemicals Codex purity standards.
- **The sheet has no sodium limit.** The FCC text itself was not opened (paywalled), so whether FCC sets one is not known.
- Our reading (DERIVED): "total alkalinity as KHCO3" counts any sodium bicarbonate as alkalinity, so an assay of 99.5 to 101.5 percent does not bound sodium (a mixture with several percent sodium bicarbonate still reads inside the range). The assay cannot be used to calculate a sodium ceiling.
- Technical information sheet https://www.armandproducts.com/products/media/potassium-bicarbonate-tds-0522/ (REV 05/22): molecular weight 100.12; solubility in water 23 percent by weight at 20 C; pH of a 1 percent solution about 8.2; decomposes without melting into K2CO3, H2O and CO2, rapidly at 176 C. No sodium value.

## Not opened (and why); each stays UNVERIFIED

- Food Chemicals Codex monographs (potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, mannitol, isomalt, erythritol, sucralose): paywalled (USP). The US 21 CFR 172.831 (sucralose) refers to the FCC 7th edition without reproducing it (checked through the eCFR API, 20 October 2026).
- JECFA specification monographs: the FAO compendium index and the WHO JECFA database pages open, but the pages hold no specification text and the monograph PDFs could not be located without a working search route (the tool's search budget was spent); the JECFA database entry for sucralose (INS 955) gives ADI 0 to 15 mg/kg bw and states only "Specs Code R (1993)".
- EU Regulation 231/2012 specifications (EUR-Lex, legislation.gov.uk copy): the pages returned no text to the fetch tool.
- Citric acid anhydrous (Jungbunzlauer product page https://www.jungbunzlauer.com/ingredients/citric-acid-anhydrous/): page opened, gives pH (about 1.8 in 5 percent solution) and no sodium or ash value; no datasheet link.
- Mannitol (Roquette Pearlitol page): the guessed URL returned 404; no mannitol datasheet was opened.
- Sucralose, lemon flavour (any carrier), maltodextrin: no supplier sheet was available to open.
- Plastic film: Notification 435 (overall migration 10 mg/dm2 for PET) is recorded in rework/32_pet_compliance.md (read 19 October 2026); no sodium limit is recorded there for PET, but the other tables were not checked, and the notification was not re-read here.
