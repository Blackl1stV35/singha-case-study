# singha-case-study

Team working repository for the Singha Ultimate Challenge 2026 entry, "The Innovative Bev & Bite".

This repository is public on GitHub. Its team records are not written for judges. Only the files in `rework/judge/` are meant for the judges.

## Current state (18 October 2026)

- The primary candidate is the sodium-free lime fizz cube, made with potassium bicarbonate and citric acid. See [rework/45](rework/45_working_candidate_rework.md) and [rework/52](rework/52_pivot_and_retirement.md).
- The leaf-wrapped frozen drink and the midlife soy drink are retired. Their records are kept, not deleted.
- The entry decision is open. Registration closes on 22 October 2026 at 23:59. No submission has been made, and the submission text is pended.
- The SINGHA name and the lion emblem are authorized for the product proposal (user, 18 October 2026). The 330 ml serving reference is not cleared. See [rework/54](rework/54_package_redesign_vs_singha_can.md).

## Where to start

Read [rework/README.md](rework/README.md). It gives the reading order, the folder guide, and the status of every record.

## Folder guide

- `rework/`: the numbered records and the decisions.
- `rework/judge/`: the four judge-facing files, and nothing else.
- `rework/mockup/`: the team build for the cube (scripts, style tokens, label and hero renders, and the 3D page).
- `rework/screen/`: analysis scripts, their results, and verification output.
- `rework/raw/`: raw workflow outputs and register extracts.
- `rework/charts/`: chart images.
- `rework/archive/`: retired assets kept for the record.
