## Reading (team translation; revised 20 October 2026)

- The THB 76,900 million figure appears in a company infographic (Hadthip PCL, March 2026) as the nationwide carbonated soft-drink market. It is not a sum of bottler revenues. The infographic's source line names Hadthip, not KResearch. The article prints no year for the figure, no definition beyond "carbonated soft drinks nationwide", and no retail-or-manufacturer statement. The same panel gives the 2025 NARTD change and the 2025 company results, so the reference year is probably 2025, but that is an inference (not stated). Thansettakij of 8 September 2025 attributes the same figure to Kasikorn Research Center for 2025 (see kresearch_76900_chain.md).
- The deck wording "2026 bottler data" is therefore accurate only as "published in March 2026 in one bottler's infographic (Hadthip, the Coca-Cola bottler for 14 southern provinces)". It is not accurate as a description of what the figure is: a 2025 estimate that a named bank source is reported to have published, not a total of bottler data.
