# Proposal structure

Proposer: Singha Corporation. Holding company: Boon Rawd Brewery in the company's own description; press and company sources describe the group differently. A trademark aggregator lists Boon Rawd Brewery as owner of SINGHA marks (S), and the Thai owner is not confirmed. Product: the Singha Lemon Soda Cube, shown as "Lemon Soda" (one dissolvable cube in one 330 mL serve), set against the existing Singha Lemon Soda can.

Tiers are defined once, in Appendix A (P, S, SOURCED, UNVERIFIED, DERIVED, PROXY, ASSUMPTION, COMPANY CLAIM). Team-only blocks are removed from the submission copy.

Gate owners are roles, not people. The team's twelve settings (deck slide 13, exhibit 6C) each carry a basis, an owner role and a timing. The only ask is the executive sponsor's signature on the Gate 0 record.

## 1. Executive summary

- `submission/executive_summary.md` (copy of the judge summary): problem statement, identity problems, proposal, KPI and expected goal, and the ask, which is a gated proof programme, not a launch (deck slide 2)
