Executive summary

One cube, dissolved in one 330 ml serve, made in the glass.
Problem
Lemon drinks are sold ready to drink in cans. No dissolvable cube was found on sale in Thailand. The gap is a way of drinking, not a flavour.
Identity
Singha already sells a lemon can that its maker lists as sugar-free. Lemon Soda overlaps its flavour. The name needs a register search before use. The lion and SINGHA are for this proposal only.
Proposal
One cube per 330 ml serve. Desk case at 200,000 cubes a year: NPV THB 0.17 million after excise and tax. Across the stated ranges, the chance of a positive NPV is about one in four.
KPI
At least 3 units per store per week; repeat of 15% or more in both cohorts; pilot ceiling THB 84,686; a measured decision at M10 to M11: scale one route, repeat once, or stop.
A proof programme, not a launch. The executive sponsor signs the opening record: one owner per stage and the first budget ceiling.
