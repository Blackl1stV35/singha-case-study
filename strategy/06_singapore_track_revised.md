# Singapore track: revised verdict plan (group perspective)

Perspective: the Boon Rawd group, as in `05_korea_track_revised.md`. Group zero-sugar SKUs in Singapore are excluded from the competitor set, per the user (2026-10-17). They are noted as group presence only.

## Group presence in Singapore (context, not competitors)

- FairPrice carries four group SKUs (soda water 24 by 325 ml, soda water 6 by 325 ml, lager and beer cans). No group zero can is on FairPrice in the capture.
- Lazada carries Singha Lemon Zero Soda No Sugar 330 ml by 24 ("Direct Imported from Thailand", seller not captured) and Singha Yuzu Lemon Zero 325 ml by 24 (search snippet S$29.90, undated, out of stock).
- The RedMart price of S$39.90 per 24 cans (20 percent off) was reported in the product-fit scan but was not reproduced in the open-items scan. It is unverified.
- Cold Storage lists no group SKU.

## Unresolved identities

- **Singapore importer of record for the zero cans: unverifiable.** The FairPrice pages carry no importer line and the Lazada listing names none. The Singapore importer is also the BCRS producer, so this identity must be fixed before the first shipment.
- **Singha Ventures Pte. Ltd. (UEN 201225099C):** described by an aggregator as a corporate venture fund of Singha Corporation. Not confirmed from ACRA (detail page returned 403). It is an investment vehicle on that description, not an importer.
- **IPOS status of SINGHA in classes 32 and 30: unverifiable.** The IPOS applicant search hit a CAPTCHA. The WIPO designation of IR 625021 (SINGHA LAGER BEER, class 32) to Singapore is unchecked.

## Financial read-across (`04_financial_model_results.md`)

- Base case: NPV about -S$1.0m; the year-3 share (0.6 percent) falls short of the break-even share (1.25 percent).
- Bull case (price at the ceiling, faster ramp): NPV about +S$7.7m; K1, K2, K3 and K7 all pass.
- The decisive variable is the realised CIF price, followed by the exworks cost and the fixed cost.

## Open items that decide the verdict

1. Singapore importer of record and BCRS producer, from the can label or an ACRA check on the exact name.
2. IPOS ownership of SINGHA in classes 32 and 30, and availability of the new product name, from a direct IPOS search.
3. Botanical extracts: which named botanicals have a 20-year food-use history, and which need SFA review. This is a gate for K6.
4. Listing or slotting cost at FairPrice, Cold Storage and 7-Eleven. No public figure found.
5. Freight: no current Thai to Singapore container rate. The model uses USD 1,200 per 20 ft as an assumption.
6. Importer margin: no beverage benchmark found. The retailer margin is a grocery proxy (Sheng Siong gross margin 31.3 percent).
7. Operations: no verified Thai can-filler with FSSC 22000 (see `02_product_fit_innovation.md`). The group's own factories are the natural route if they run can lines; this is unverified.
