Task T16. Route: WebSearch (lead), then WebFetch (model-written extracts with quoted sentences). Tier S for each page. The engagement figures are vendor blog tables, not measured Thai data.

## A. Qoruz, "Engagement rate benchmarks to aim for in 2025"

- URL: https://qoruz.com/blog/engagement-rate-benchmarks-to-aim-for-in-2025/
- Author Harshi Jain; published 15 May 2025; no last-updated date. Date read 20 October 2026. Tier S (vendor blog; no data source cited for the table).
- Table, "Average engagement rate benchmarks by platform (2025)", columns Nano (1K to 10K), Micro (10K to 50K), Mid (50K to 500K), Macro (500K to 1M), Celebrity (1M and above):
  - Instagram: 6.0%, 3.5%, 2.5%, 1.5%, 0.8%
  - TikTok: 10.0%, 7.5%, 6.0%, 3.5%, 2.0%
  - YouTube: 5.0%, 4.0%, 3.0%, 2.5%, 1.2%
- Denominator: followers ("Engagement Rate = (Total Engagements / Total Followers) x 100"). The TL;DR also says impressions; the table does not say which.
- Conflict inside the same page: the FAQ gives Instagram nano 4 to 6 percent and micro 2 to 4 percent; the TL;DR gives micro 3 to 6 percent; the FAQ gives TikTok nano 8 to 10 percent, micro 6 to 8 percent and mid 4 to 6 percent. These do not match the table. Kept as a spread.
