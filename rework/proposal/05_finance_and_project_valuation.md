# Finance and project valuation: Lime Cube (19 October 2026)

This is a model output for one product, the Lime Cube, sold in domestic Thailand. It is not investment advice.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing or release, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the page text was read, and the public URL is given. UNVERIFIED: not read, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, with the inputs and their tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## Scope: the project, not the group

- One product in domestic Thailand. No group data is used.
- A dissolvable lime cube, unpriced, with no measured price response.
- Composition per 330 mL: potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 2.10 g (0.52 g reacts with the bicarbonate; 1.58 g is free acid that sets the pH); sucralose 33 mg (about 100 mg/kg). No sodium carbonate is used.
- Dose: 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL at a working pH of 3.5, an anchor only; the dose gate is for R&D to define. The pH rests on pKa values (S, UNVERIFIED: read from rendered images only).
- Exchange rate: 33.55 baht per US dollar, Bank of Thailand midpoint, 15 October 2026 (P, SOURCED; Source 7).
- Solubility is 0.655 volume at 30 C and 0.592 at 35 C (S, SOURCED; Source 10), so the dose is within solubility from 5 to 35 C (DERIVED). That range is a working range; the Bangkok climate figures are UNVERIFIED. This is a pure-water check, not a fizz test. A 0.44 g/L sparkling threshold (S, abstract only; Source 12) is a soft-drink proxy and sets no dose.
- Sweetener: sucralose is permitted at 300 mg/kg in category 14.1.4 (flavoured drinks), condition 127, applied to the drink as ready to drink (P, SOURCED; Source 8). Only the flavoured-drink rows of the annex were read. Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis there. Condition TH3 covers electrolyte drinks only and is not claimed for the cube.
- Method: pre-tax, five years, no terminal value, 8% to 12% discount rate (ASSUMPTION; base 10%).

Against the company's can: its lime sparkling water is a 330 mL ready-to-drink can at 15 THB suggested retail (P, SOURCED; Source 2). The products overlap on flavour and differ in form and pack: the cube dissolves in the glass, one cube per pouch. At a matching shelf price, the 3.84 THB cube cost is 26% of a 15 THB serve (DERIVED). The company's sugar-free, 0% calories wording is its own claim; this model makes no such claim for the cube.

A 15 THB, 330 mL serve (about 45 THB per litre, DERIVED) sits inside the 25.0 to 85.6 THB per litre band of two retailer listings (S, SOURCED; Source 3).

About 128,580 cubes fit in a 20 ft container in 12-cube cartons (DERIVED; S, SOURCED; Source 11; 92% packing is an ASSUMPTION).

Potassium is 317 mg per cube, 9.1% of a Thai 3,500 mg per day reference (DERIVED; S, SOURCED; Source 9). A composition figure only.

## Limits

- The 1.30 THB pouch base is an ASSUMPTION near the midpoint of an UNVERIFIED overseas listing (USD 0.03 to 0.05). No domestic quote has been found or requested. The 0.67 to 2.70 THB range is also UNVERIFIED.
- Fixed costs, tooling, registration, trademark, pouch minimums and marketing are unsourced; the grid covers them.
- Excise: the 14% ad valorem rate applies if the value base applies, and the base was not read; the 1.26 THB figure assumes that base. Rate from the B.E. 2560 notice; later notices not searched. Whether a dissolvable cube falls under the item is open.
- VAT, freight, cold chain, income tax, depreciation, working capital and terminal value are excluded.
- Comtrade values have estimated weights and no stated currency or unit.
- The sucralose price is UNVERIFIED. The 12-cube cost includes a carton share of THB 0.42 per cube (THB 5 per 12-cube carton, from a retail box listing used as a PROXY). Print and processing are placeholders.
- The wording of condition 127 is to be confirmed. No bench test has been run, and taste is untested.
- One cube per pouch is unconfirmed with the organiser. No supplier quote has been obtained and no outside contact has been made. Break-even assumes the 0.55 recipe.
