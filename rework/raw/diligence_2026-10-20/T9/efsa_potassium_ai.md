# EFSA adequate intake for potassium (read 20 October 2026; page re-fetched after verification)

URL: https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/press/news/161025  (EFSA news story "Dietary reference values: advice on potassium", 25 October 2016)
Tier P (the regulator's own page, summarising EFSA Scientific Opinion, EFSA Journal 2016;14(10):4592, https://doi.org/10.2903/j.efsa.2016.4592). The opinion itself was tried twice (https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2016.4592, and the redirect from https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4592) and returned HTTP 403, so the opinion text was not opened.
Read through WebFetch (extraction). Quoted lines:
- "3,500mg for adults including pregnant women."
- "4,000mg for lactating women."
- Children: 750 mg (7-11 months), 800 mg (1-3 y), 1,100 mg (4-6 y), 1,800 mg (7-10 y), 2,700 mg (11-14 y).
A search-result abstract (not the opened page) adds that it is an adequate intake (AI), not an average requirement, based on blood pressure and stroke evidence.

Upper level: the news story says nothing about an upper level (UL) for potassium (checked by a second fetch on 20 October 2026 with that question). EFSA's position on a UL is therefore UNVERIFIED in this repo; it would be settled by the opinion text, which was not reachable. No claim about an EFSA upper level is made.
