## Clause 3 (Thai), the list of foods that must show the nutrition panel and the GDA of energy, sugar, fat and sodium

Thai wording of the opening: "ให้อาหารที่อยู่ในภาชนะพร้อมจำหน่ายต่อผู้บริโภค ดังต่อไปนี้ เป็นอาหารที่ต้องแสดงฉลากโภชนาการ และค่าพลังงาน น้ำตาล ไขมัน และโซเดียม แบบจีดีเอ" (foods in a container ready for sale to consumers, the following must show the nutrition label and the GDA of energy, sugar, fat and sodium).
Item (6) beverages in a sealed container:
- (6.1) beverages that contain or are made from fruit, plants or vegetables, with or without dissolved carbon dioxide or oxygen, except plant drinks dried in the form of an infusion;
- (6.2) beverages that contain or are made from ingredients that are not fruit, plants or vegetables, with or without dissolved carbon dioxide or oxygen, prepared as flavoured ready-to-drink;
- (6.3) beverages under (6.1) or (6.2) in dry form (ชนิดแห้ง).

## Clause 5 (Thai) and the Annex

- Show the nutrition panel in the format of Annex 1 of the nutrition labelling notification (clause 5(1), as replaced by 446 clause 1).
- Show the GDA in the format of the Annex: four rounded cylinders for energy, sugar, fat and sodium; amounts per package (one serving size multiplied by the number of servings per package); units kcal, g, g, mg; percentages based on 2,000 kcal, 65 g sugar, 65 g fat and 2,000 mg sodium (394 Annex, English page 6; the 466 Annex repeats the format with colour changes).
- Clause 10 (Thai): the notification does not apply to (1) foods sold directly to the consumer by the cook; (2) foods for which another notification already sets the nutrient declaration.

## Thai FDA GDA Q&A, group on beverages (Thai, page 4 of the FAQ file)

- Sweet syrups and concentrated drinks (น้ำหวาน หรือเครื่องดื่มเข้มข้น): not subject to GDA, because clause 3(6) covers only (6.1) to (6.3) as quoted above.
- Soda water (น้ำโซดา): not subject to GDA because it is not a beverage under the notification on beverages in sealed containers.
- Dried plant leaves and flowers for infusion: not subject; the same plant parts boiled and flavoured and sold ready to drink: subject.
- No Q&A entry was found on a cube or tablet that dissolves into a flavoured sparkling drink.

## What this means for the cube (our reading, DERIVED)

- A dry-form flavoured beverage in a sealed pouch falls under 3(6.3) on the text. The Thai FDA has not ruled on a dissolving cube; classification is a stated limit. If 3(6.3) applies, the sodium amount (in mg and as a percentage of 2,000 mg) must appear on the pack whether or not any sodium claim is made.
- Sodium under 5 mg is declared as 0 (rounding table of Notification 445 Annex 1), and the declared value must come from laboratory analysis (see thai_fda_qa_445_sugars_analysis.md).
