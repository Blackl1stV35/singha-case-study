# Paid social benchmarks for a cross-check of the Thai cost per click, read 20 October 2026

Task T16. Route: WebSearch (lead), then WebFetch (model-written extracts). All figures are global or cross-industry and are PROXY for a Thai beverage campaign. No Thai beverage benchmark for CPC, CPA or view cost was found.

## A. Influee, "Facebook Ads Benchmarks 2026" (reports Triple Whale data)

- URL: https://influee.co/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks
- Article date: 16 July 2026 (per the extract). Date read 20 October 2026. Tier S (secondary; reports Triple Whale's analysis of about 35,000 ad accounts, January to December 2025).
- Food and beverage (medians, 2025): CTR 1.85% (up 5.48% year on year); CPA $38.15 (down 0.22%); conversion rate 2.02% (down 0.08%). CPC not provided. CPM not provided for food and beverage.
- Platform median CPA: $38.19 (the extract gives this as the platform median; its label is the article's).
- Caveat: e-commerce store data from a US-centred tool. Not Thai, not a KOL campaign.

## B. Vaizle, Facebook ad benchmarks (5,200 Meta ad accounts, 2025)

- URL: https://insights.vaizle.com/facebook-ad-benchmarks/
- Date read 20 October 2026; "collected in 2025". Tier S (vendor snapshot; method self-reported; median, top 25 percent and bottom 25 percent given).
- Average 2025: CTR 2.46%; CPC $0.82; CPM $5.97; lead conversion 3.41%. Across all industries and regions.

## C. Triple Whale, benchmark page

- URL: https://triplewhale.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks. HTTP 403 when read. The figures above are from the Influee article that reports it. Not opened as a primary.

## D. Conversion between sources (DERIVED in the model)

- Vaizle CPC $0.82 is about THB 27.5 at 33.55 baht per dollar (DERIVED). Phoenix Thai CPC THB 11.5 (saved in 14_phoenix_cpm_cpc_recheck.md) is less than half of this. The two sources are not comparable: one is a Thai agency average for Facebook and Instagram, the other a global median or average across industries.
- Implied click-through from Phoenix: CPM THB 120 divided by (CPC THB 11.5 times 1,000) gives about 1.04% (DERIVED). Triple Whale and Vaizle report 1.85% and 2.46% CTR. The Phoenix implied CTR is lower, which is consistent with a lead-generation mix.
- Food and beverage CPA of $38.15 is about THB 1,280 at 33.55 (DERIVED). The model's base cost per first purchase of THB 1,150 (11.5 divided by a 1.0 percent click-to-purchase assumption) is of the same order. This is a cross-check, not a source for the base.
