# Background and trends: Thailand, domestic market

Scope: Thailand, domestic market. Thai-language sources are summarised in English. Overseas analogues are labelled, because no domestic evidence for dissolvable cubes was found.

## Background: the group and its beverage business

Boon Rawd Brewery is the holding company, following a 2000 reorganisation. Singha Corporation was set up in 2001. Singha Corporation is a subsidiary of Boon Rawd Brewery and manages more than 150 group companies (S; current English home page, undated, read 19 October 2026; the count of more than 150 is from the About Us page, Source 1). It reports directly to the Boon Rawd board (Source 1; S; SOURCED).

The current English home page describes Singha Corporation as a subsidiary of Boon Rawd Brewery. An older affiliate wording, on a page with a 2014 footer, is superseded and is not used (S). The beverages category lists six products: Singha Water, Singha Soda, Singha Lemon Soda, Purra, Purra Vitamin Water and FIJI (Source 2). Treating that category as the nearest unit for a non-alcoholic product is an ASSUMPTION.

The group already sells a lime sparkling water, which is a different product from Lemon Soda. Its launch release, dated 13 March 2026, describes Singha Sparkling Water Lime Flavour, a 330 ml can priced at 15 baht, launched in March 2026 (Source 3; P; SOURCED). A news reprint posted 17 March 2026 gives the same flavour, can size and price (Source 4; S; SOURCED). The release calls the can sugar-free with 0% calories; this is the company's claim, not checked here. It gives no ingredient list, and carbonation and canning capacity are not evidenced here.

The Singha Lemon Soda can overlaps Lemon Soda on flavour. The difference lies in form and pack. Lemon Soda is a dissolvable cube that makes the drink in the glass, one cube per pouch. The company's lemon soda is a ready-to-drink can, listed on the company product page (Source 5; S; SOURCED; retail sale not dated), and its price is not verified. Lemon Soda carries no Singha name.

Limit: no consolidated accounts, innovation budget or group R&D spend were available, and no group financial ratio is used.

## Limits

Several pages were read through an automated summary; re-check their figures against the live page before any published use. No domestic study of dissolvable cubes has been found, and no Thai retail shelf search has been run. Global market-size estimates are not used.

The carbon dioxide and pH figures are desk-model results. No bench or sensory test has been run, and taste is untested. The working pH of 3.5 is an ASSUMPTION. The gate for the 0.55-volume dose is for R&D to restate before the run (section 10, Gate 3); none is set here.

Only the flavoured-drink rows of the Thai food-additive annex were read, not the full annex. Unverifiable figures, including two Thai consumer surveys and two trend percentages, are not used. The Singha Lemon Soda can's national retail reach and retail sale date are not verified here.
