## Listing and IPO status

- Singha Corporation Co., Ltd. and The Boon Rawd Brewery Company Limited carry the legal form "Co., Ltd." (บริษัทจำกัด), not a public company ("(มหาชน)", PCL). Only a public company can list on the SET, so neither entity is a listed company as filed with DBD (DERIVED from the legal form in the Creden record).
- The group's SET-listed companies named by efinancethai (12 Mar 2026) https://www.efinancethai.com/efinReview/efinReviewMain.aspx?name=er-c-20852 (S) are Singha Estate (S), S Hotels and Resorts (SHR) and BG Container Glass (BGC). The MGR Online article names Singha Estate and BGC as public companies.
- One extended Thai-language search and one extended English-language search on 20 October 2026 found no report of an IPO plan for Singha Corporation or Boon Rawd Brewery. The absence of news is not proof that none exists.
- Investing.com (search summary, page not opened) put Boon Rawd Brewery at 47.91% of Singha Estate at 16 Mar 2026; not used.
