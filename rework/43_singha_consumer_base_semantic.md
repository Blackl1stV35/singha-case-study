# Singha consumer base: semantic map (18 October 2026)

Status: collection and clustering complete. Verification and critique complete. **No segment is verified as supported.** Treat the map as a list of hypotheses for the consumer test, not as a description of Singha's customers.

Scope: Thailand only. Public information only; no internal or group data. Products: Singha sugar-free and zero-sugar soda and sparkling lines (red, pink, yuzu, ume and classic lemon zero; sugar-free lime sparkling water, March 2026). No contact with any outside party.

Method: 83 statements were collected from four channels (retail reviews, social and forum posts, news and brand statements, public surveys). 35 named a Singha product. 29 of those were placed into 8 semantic segments, grouped by the main reason, occasion or concern each statement expresses. Grouping used meaning, not product name, keyword or sentiment. Raw output: the workflow journal (wa6w915d4), not copied into the repo.

## 1. What the collection could and could not reach

- **Retail review text was not obtained.** Lazada, Shopee, Makro Pro, Tops, 7-Eleven online, Watsons and Boots gave no usable Singha review text, so the 20-to-40 retail target was not met. Price-point evidence rests only on social posts.
- **The lime sparkling water has no consumer statements at all.** Its only statements are launch coverage and brand claims. This is the product closest to the candidate, so the gap matters most here.
- **Shopee returned a traffic verification page** during the same session (see rework/42 and the browser step). It was not bypassed.
- **Sources are self-selected.** Lemon8 supplies 15 of the 29 placed statements (52%), TikTok 6, Pantip 4, X 3 and one blog. Silent buyers are absent. The only negative voice among the placed statements is one packaging-condition thread.

## 3. What this means for the cube candidate

- **The two segments with the best cube fit (mixing and hot-season chilled drinking) are the least checked.** Neither was verified, and the cube-fit ratings were never tested against evidence.
- **No source shows any consumer response to a frozen or dissolvable format.** The cube fit is an inference from the use occasions, not a finding.
- **The consumer-test design should therefore test the two strong-fit segments first,** with a symptom- and occasion-screened panel, and treat the Lemon8 evidence as a source of questions only.
- **Market context is weak.** The only Thai figures found are a brand-stated share, a vendor survey (GlobalData, 2023, n = 508, with the 81 and 62 percent figures attached to alcohol alternatives) and a Kantar headline without a sample size. None is Singha-specific.

## 4. Limits closed as stated limits

- No Thai retail review counts or dates were obtained. This gap is closed as a limit under the no-contact rule.
- No Singha sales, share or panel data exists in the public record. Every count in this document is a source's own figure or a count of statements.
- Dates: most posts fall between 2020 and 2025, and many show an edit date rather than the original. Prices (15 to 17 baht) may have changed.
- Sodium figures differ across sources (about 45, 85 and 105 mg per serving, for different flavours or sources). Check the can label before using any figure.
- The lime sparkling water's aroma is described as lime by most sources and as lemon by one. Unresolved until the label is read.
- X, TikTok and YouTube were read from search snippets, and Facebook and Instagram were not searched.
