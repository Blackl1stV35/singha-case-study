## Page 1, as read (our translation)

- Clause 1: repeals MoPH Notification No. 108 (B.E. 2530) on mineral-salt beverages.
- Clause 2: mineral-salt beverages are foods with a quality or standard prescribed.
- Clause 3: "mineral-salt beverage" means a beverage in which mineral salts are the main ingredient, and includes dry-form mineral-salt beverages.
- Clause 4: a mineral-salt beverage must meet the following quality or standard. (1) One litre of mineral-salt beverage consists of:
  - (1.1) sodium not less than 460 mg and not more than 920 mg;
  - (1.2) glucose or fructose sugar not less than 2 percent by weight, or sucrose not less than 4 percent by weight;
  - (1.3) potassium not more than 195 mg (if present);
  - (1.4) bicarbonate not more than 793 mg (if present);
  - (1.5) citrate not more than 819 mg (if present).
  A mineral-salt beverage may use other mineral salts besides those in (1.3) and (1.4), or other sugars, in amounts approved by the Food and Drug Board's office.
- Clause 4(2) to (5): characteristic flavour and taste; no sediment other than from flavourings; water to meet the quality standard of the MoPH notification on drinking water in sealed containers; microbial limits (coliforms less than 2.2 per 100 mL by MPN; no E. coli) as amended.

## Use in the T9 record

- The cube drink does not meet this composition: sodium 0 by design (the binder's sodium is not checked), no sugar, potassium about 959 mg per litre (316.6 mg in 330 mL; 4.9 times the 195 mg ceiling), citrate about 6.3 to 6.4 g per litre as the anion or as citric acid (7.6 to 7.8 times the 819 mg ceiling). So it cannot be sold as a mineral-salt beverage under this notification.
- Whether a drink made from the cube is treated by the Thai FDA as a flavoured drink (category 14.1.4 of Notification 468) or as a mineral-salt beverage (Clause 3, "mineral salts as the main ingredient") is not settled by this text. Condition TH3 on the sucralose row of Notification 468 is tied to this class: the saved 468 extract reads it as applying to electrolyte drinks that must be used with sugar; the saved 418 page reads it as excluding products under Notification 195. Both readings point to this notification. Update, 20 October 2026 (second pass): the Notification 468 Annex row and the TH3 text were read as page images (`thai_notification_468_rows_reconfirmed_2026-10-20.md`). TH3 reads: for mineral-salt beverage products, the additive must be used together with sugar of the type and amount set in the mineral-salt beverage notification. The 418 reading (an exclusion) is superseded; the two readings differ in the direction of the condition, and both tie it to this notification.
