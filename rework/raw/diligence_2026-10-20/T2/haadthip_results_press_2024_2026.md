## FY2025 (year to 31 December 2025)

- The Thai Press, article of 3 March 2026 reporting a company release of 2 March 2026. URL: https://www.thethaipress.com/2026/158871

## Basis and source notes added 20 October 2026 (verification fix; Thairath and Marketeer pages re-read the same day)

- Marketeer, 24 March 2026 (https://marketeeronline.co/archives/460572), re-read. For the southern NARTD share the article says (Thai, verbatim from the extract) "หาดทิพย์ ครองส่วนแบ่งตลาดเชิงมูลค่าในอุตสาหกรรม NARTD ภาคใต้ถึง 23.2%", so the 23.2% is a share by value (S, company statement relayed by the press). For carbonated soft drinks the article says only "หากนับเฉพาะกลุ่มน้ำอัดลมจะอยู่ที่ 78.2%" and does not say value or volume. The article does not mention Nielsen; The Thai Press (3 March 2026) and Mitihoon name Nielsen for 2025.
- The 25% for 2024 (Mitihoon, 13 March 2025) and the 79.3% (Marketeer, 6 March 2024; undated in the article, re-opened 20 October 2026) name no source and no basis in the extracts. Treat as company statements with no source named.
- Thairath, 27 May 2025, re-read: the revenue row for 2023 and 2024 is labelled "รายได้รวม" (total revenue): 7,863.97 and 8,189.09. Net profit 598.56 and 601.68; net margin 7.60% and 7.35%.
- Marketeer's table is labelled "ยอดขาย" (sales) and "กำไร" (profit), with 2021 to 2025 values 6,544 / 535; 7,092 / 435; 7,863 / 598; 8,189 / 601; 8,368 / 568. Its 2021 to 2024 values equal Thairath's total revenue, so its 8,368 for 2025 is probably on the same total-revenue basis, but that is not confirmed.
- The company release gives revenue from sales of 8,258.4 for 2025, up 1.6%, and the company's own investor relations page prints 8,258 under a row labelled total revenue (รายได้รวม). A rise of 1.6% does not reconcile with 8,189.09 on either 2025 figure (rework/screen/coca_cola_system_derived_results.json: +0.8% on 8,258.4 and +2.2% on 8,368), so the release's growth rate is probably on a sales basis (implied 2024 sales about 8,128, DERIVED). The basis of the Haad Thip FY2025 revenue is therefore a stated limit.
