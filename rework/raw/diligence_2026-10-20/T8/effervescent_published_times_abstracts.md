# Published effervescent tablet disintegration times (one opened full text; others abstract level)

- Date read: 20 October 2026.
- Tier: Atchade et al. 2025 is P (full text opened, see atchade2025_effervescent_tablet_fulltext.md). The others are S at abstract level through Consensus index records (articles not opened), except where noted. All are PROXIES: pharmaceutical tablets of small mass, not drink cubes.

## Atchade et al. 2025, International Journal of Applied Pharmaceutics 17(6), 388-394 (opened; see the separate page)
- Five formulations, 0.45 to 0.52 g tablets, 11.1 mm diameter and 4.28 mm thick; disintegration 307, 156, 263, 209 and 261 s for F1 to F5 in 200 mL distilled water at 25 C.

## Agbamu et al. 2022, Journal of Pharmaceutical Research International (abstract only)
- URL: https://consensus.app/papers/details/3172afef40f0539fb0cc50772483f122/
- Effervescent paracetamol tablets, 15 to 25 % each of citric acid and sodium bicarbonate, wet granulation. Disintegration "between 3 +/- 43.06 to 5 +/- 16.3 min" (as printed, units garbled) and effervescence time 3 to 5 min; more acid and bicarbonate gave shorter disintegration and effervescence times.

## Mazdi et al. 2025, Drug Development and Industrial Pharmacy (abstract only)
- URL: https://consensus.app/papers/details/8c5e3b01842757e191f5d0c693becd90/
- Optimised paracetamol 500 mg and ascorbic acid 200 mg tablets, 2.9 % PVP, sodium bicarbonate to citric acid 1:1.5; hardness 45 +/- 20 N; disintegration under 5 min; dissolution under 10 min.

## Elagina et al. 2022, Pharmacy and Pharmacology (abstract only)
- URL: https://consensus.app/papers/details/bc1135fa763b5a44b71f9ead39adbe1f/
- Furazolidone effervescent tablets form the solution in 100 ml of water at 20 C in less than 5 minutes.

## Taymouri et al. 2019, Journal of Reports in Pharmaceutical Sciences 8(2) (record page opened)
- URL: https://brieflands.com/articles/jrps-147387
- Bismuth sub-citrate effervescent tablet, optimised formulation disintegration time 95.33 +/- 1.15 s. Tablet mass, size and water volume are not on the page.

## Limits
- For all but Atchade, tablet mass, diameter and test volume are not in the abstracts, so the times cannot be scaled to a 3.79 g, 14 mm cube. The published times are used only as a plausibility check on the order of magnitude (about 1.5 to 5 min for 0.5 g tablets).
