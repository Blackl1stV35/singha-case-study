## A. Phoenix Media, "The Costs of Facebook, Google and LINE Ads in Thailand for 2025"

- URL: https://www.phoenixmedia.co.th/the-costs-of-facebook-google-and-line-ads-in-thailand-for-2025/
- Author Robert Wee, Managing Partner, Phoenix Media; published 3 February 2026. Date read 20 October 2026. Tier S (one agency's own client campaigns).
- Method as stated on the page: figures come from the agency's own client campaigns ("our observed average costs"); Facebook figures are "primarily drawn from lead-generation and conversion-focused campaigns across multiple industries". No sample size, date range, industry split or method for the averages. Not independently verified.

- Google ads, 2025: CPM THB 368 (467); cost per view THB 0.13 (0.11); CPC THB 11; CTR 3.33 percent; cost per conversion THB 199 (349); conversion rate 2.80 percent.
