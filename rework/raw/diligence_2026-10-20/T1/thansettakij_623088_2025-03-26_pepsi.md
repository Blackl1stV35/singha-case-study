## Extract (Thai as printed, team translation)

- Lead: "เป๊ปซี่ ... มวยรองตลาดน้ำดำที่กินส่วนแบ่งการตลาด 39.1% เป็นรองแชมป์อย่าง โค้ก ที่มีส่วนแบ่งการตลาดอยู่ที่ 51% สำหรับสมรภูมิตลาดน้ำอัดลมซึ่งมีมูลค่ากว่า 6.6 หมื่นล้านบาท" (Pepsi holds 39.1 percent of the black soda market, behind Coke at 51 percent, in a carbonated soft-drink market worth over THB 66,000 million.) No year, no source and no measure (value or volume) is attached to the 39.1, 51 or 66,000.
- Company speaker (the marketing chief executive of Suntory PepsiCo Beverage (Thailand)): "ตลาดน้ำอัดลม ซึ่งมีการเติบโต 2.7%" (the carbonated market, which grew 2.7 percent). No year or window is printed beside the 2.7 percent.
- Closing narration, not a quotation: "สำหรับเทรนด์ตลาดเครื่องดื่มในปี 2568 คาดการณ์ว่าน้ำดำจะยังคงครองส่วนแบ่งตลาดสูงสุด 72% ของน้ำอัดลม, และวัดส่วนราว 38% เป็นของตลาดน้ำสี" (For beverage trends in 2025 it is forecast that black soda will keep the highest share at 72 percent of carbonated drinks, and about 38 percent is colour soda.) The two shares add to 110 percent, so one of them is misprinted (the second may be 28 percent). The measure (value or volume) is not stated.

## Reading (team)

- The "72 percent" is a forecast in the journalist's narration and is internally inconsistent, so it is not used to derive a non-cola value. An earlier version of the record derived a THB 18,480 million non-cola figure from it; that derivation is withdrawn.
- The 39.1 percent is a share of the black (cola) segment and the 51 percent sits in the same sentence; the segment for the 51 percent is not stated. Do not set the 39.1 percent against the 30 percent of the whole market in the Thansettakij article of 16 March 2024.
- Brand Buffet, April 2025 (cited in the first version of the record) was not opened and is dropped as a citation.
