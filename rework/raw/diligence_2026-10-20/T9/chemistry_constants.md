# Molar masses, densities and acid constants (read 20 October 2026)

1. PubChem REST property endpoints, read 20 October 2026 (NIH/NLM database, tier P for the database record):
   - Citric acid CID 311: C6H8O7, molecular weight 192.12. https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/rest/pug/compound/cid/311/property/MolecularFormula,MolecularWeight/JSON
   - Potassium bicarbonate CID 516893: KHCO3, 100.115 g/mol. https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/rest/pug/compound/cid/516893/property/MolecularFormula,MolecularWeight/JSON
   - Densities (PUG-View, Density heading): KHCO3 2.17 at 25 C (source listed: PAC Chemical Database, US DOE, unit not stated, read as g/cm3); citric acid 1.665 g/cm3 at 20 C (Lide, CRC Handbook 86th ed.); citric acid monohydrate 1.542 g/cm3 (CRC 91st ed.).
   - Dissociation constants for citric acid on PubChem: only "pKa = 2.79" (Serjeant and Dempsey 1979) is listed as a string; pKa2 and pKa3 are not on that page. This single value is used only as a sensitivity case, not as the base case.
2. Citric acid pKa (zero ionic strength, 25 C): 3.128, 4.761, 6.396.
