## Decision

1. **Novelty claim, narrowed.** The concept is a *leaf-wrapped frozen drink, eaten peel-and-sip as it thaws*. No leaf-wrapped frozen drink was found in any search. The team must not claim "first in the world".
2. **Disclosed precedent.** The Ratchaburi leaf-wrapped ice-cream bar is the closest precedent. It is a frozen treat, it rests on one 2023 user post, and a 2024 review of the same market does not mention the leaf. It is disclosed as unconfirmed. The submission text (pended) must mention it if the submission is made.
3. **Differentiation.** The concept differs from the bar in two ways that the screen tests: it is a drink, and it is a liquid as it thaws (half-thawed at about 0.6 h in shade, see the summer screen). The novelty argument is the drinking form, not the leaf.
4. **Evidence level.** Absence search, limited by blocked retail and registry sites. Stated as "not found", not as "does not exist".

## Limits

- Most Thai retail and delivery sites could not be read.
- The OTOP registry has no public keyword search.
- The Ratchaburi bar's frozen status, eating mechanics and continued sale rest on one user post. A visit or a second, leaf-specific source would settle them. That is outside the virtual screen.
- The patent search covered Google Patents pages only. WIPO and the Thai DIP portal could not be read. CN106414847A is the one pending candidate that merits a full claim read.
