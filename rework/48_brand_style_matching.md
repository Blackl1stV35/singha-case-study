# 48 Brand style matching for the lime cube mockup

Date: 18 October 2026.

Scope: the style-matching spec for the lime cube (working name Plunk Lime Cube; sodium-free status pending, see section 7.3). It is built from the brand-position research and the product research. Two files are written: this note, and `rework/mockup/style_tokens.json`. The 3D mockup itself and any render or screenshot check are not part of this step.

Status: spec drafted, and the 3D mockup built from it (section 9). Colours and type are ASSUMPTION (design choices). No brand colour or typeface was verified. ## 1 Purpose and the blind-judging rule

1.1 Purpose. The mockup should sit in the same public mood as the organiser's brand: fresh, modern, lively, everyday and direct. It should do that with our own marks, artwork and palette. Here, style matching means matching public brand positions and mood. It does not mean copying marks, colours, copy, trade dress or campaign artwork.

1.2 Audience. The lead segment is men aged 25 to 34, the conversion lead in rework note 46. The tone is plain and light, aimed at a mixing or chilled-drinking moment at home or on the go. The mockup carries no night-out, bar or glassware cue, on any occasion.

1.3 Blind-judging rule. Judge-facing files carry no team name, university, email, author metadata or local file-path hints. This covers the mockup, `style_tokens.json`, any PNG, any HTML title and any file metadata. The organiser's name, logo, lion emblem, wordmark and trademarks are never used in artwork. This note names the organiser only to record which public positions were matched and which were rejected. The competition is SINGHA ULTIMATE CHALLENGE 2026, organised by Singha. The team is an external, double-blind entry.

6.2 Salt basis. Working note rule R1 names potassium carbonate. Its section 2 and the 3.6 g figure use bicarbonate. This spec uses bicarbonate. Carbonate would deliver about 2,015 mg potassium per 330 ml at 1.75 volumes (about 58 percent of the reference), or about 1,730 mg at 1.5 volumes. It would also need two-thirds of a mole of citric acid per mole of CO2, not one-third. The choice is open (L4).

6.3 Potassium wording. The working note ties potassium to CO2 retention. It does not. Potassium follows the bicarbonate charged. A 2.58 g charge (the 1.75-volume design dose) gives about 1,007 mg, and a 2.21 g charge gives 864 mg at 1.5 volumes, whether or not all the CO2 stays in the drink. The mockup prints no potassium figure (L5).

6.4 Density band. At 15 mm, the design mass of 5.28 g gives 1.56 g/cm3, inside the assumed band of 1.25 to 1.60 but near its top. The low end of the mass range (4.93 g) gives 1.46 g/cm3, inside it. The high end (5.80 g) gives 1.72 g/cm3, above the band, so the stated mass range is wider than the band allows (L6). Keeping the high end inside the band needs a side of about 15.4 mm or more, or a narrower mass range. The owner decides.

## 8 Decisions and open items

8.1 Decisions made in this spec.

- D1. Working name for the mockup: Plunk Lime Cube. It was chosen over Kaew Lime and Sipcube because it names the action, has no alcohol or fizz word, and showed no conflict in one search. Kaew Lime remains a Thai-language option, pending Thai spelling and pronunciation. Trademark status is not checked (L1).
- D2. Palette: lime, deep ink and warm off-white. No amber, gold, red, copper or beer-label tones. No verified brand colour is used.
- D3. Type: Sarabun for both pack and web.
- D4. Pack front 70 x 90 x 20 mm, with 80 mm front width as the fallback if the name line does not fit (L9).
- D5. Salt basis for the mockup: bicarbonate, as in working note section 2 and its 3.6 g figure.
- D6. The mockup prints placeholders for every unsettled item. It prints no settled number for sodium, potassium or sugar.
- D7. The mockup carries no alcohol cue, glassware, bar setting or night-out scene.
- D8. Tone matches the lead segment (men 25 to 34): plain, light and everyday.

8.2 Existing files in the mockup folder. `rework/mockup/` already holds files from an earlier frozen-pop concept: a build script, `MATERIALS.md`, label artwork and textures. They are not the cube mockup. They were not used or changed in this step. Their kraft-brown label and leaf-green values are superseded by section 4. A read-only text scan of the earlier scripts and notes found no absolute paths, names or identity text. Their images were not inspected (L24).

8.3 Limits. Each is a stated limit, not an open to-do.

- L1. Name. One search per name was run, which is not a trademark search. The USPTO, WIPO Global Brand, Thai DIP, EUIPO and Thai company registers were not searched. Two earlier names were rejected in that check: Manao, one letter from a registered US soft-drink mark, and Lumi, a juice mark filed in 2013 with status unread. The organiser's marks are treated as protected.
- L2. Thai text. Notifications 450 and 445 were read in English unofficial translations, which say the Thai text prevails. The 450 correction notice (P450-Edit) was not read. No Thai copy is drafted, so the mockup uses English placeholders. The real label needs Thai-language particulars.
- L3. Sodium-free. Pending the Thai text check and a water basis. The consumer's own water may exceed 5 mg per 330 ml. Nothing is measured (S2).
- L4. Salt. Source conflict: rule R1 says potassium carbonate, while section 2 and the 3.6 g figure use bicarbonate. Carbonate roughly doubles potassium and changes the citric acid ratio. The salt is not settled.
- L5. Potassium wording. The working note ties potassium to CO2 retention. Potassium depends on the bicarbonate charged. The working note's wording is wrong and should be corrected.
- L6. Density and mass. The density band (1.25 to 1.60 g/cm3) is ASSUMPTION. At the 1.75-volume design mass (5.28 g) the density is 1.56 g/cm3, inside the band near its top. The high end of the mass range (5.80 g) gives 1.72 g/cm3, above the band. The 15 mm side is kept; the owner must choose a side of about 15.4 mm or more, or a narrower mass range. Excipient, flavour and sweetener masses are ASSUMPTION. No tablet, dissolution or CO2 test was run (S2). The 15 mm, 5.28 g and 1.75-volume figures are design values, not measurements.
- L7. Pouch. No film, sealant, barrier or seal specification is sourced. Bicarbonate and citric acid are moisture-sensitive, so a barrier is needed, and none is specified. There is no converter quote.
- L8. Regulatory route. Food or supplement (Notifications 293, 447 and 448) is not settled (gate G4). The label set assumes food. The controlled-food pre-approval route (clause 8) is not resolved.
- L9. Name-line fit. Estimated at about 61 mm against a 62 mm live width, using average glyph widths. If "height" means cap height, the type name needs about 3.0 mm and the line needs about 68 mm, which does not fit at 70 mm. Fallback: 80 mm front width (72 cm2). Not measured.
- L10. Mockup and render. The 3D mockup is built in rework/mockup/cube_3d.html (section 9). Its render checks ran in headless Chromium at two widths (1280 by 800 and 375 by 812), with a CDN-blocked fallback run; the saved report is rework/screen/verify_cube_mockup_2026-10-18.json. No real device, no other browser and no assistive-technology test was run.
- L11. Colour overlap. Contrast ratios are calculated from hex values and have not been seen on a render.
