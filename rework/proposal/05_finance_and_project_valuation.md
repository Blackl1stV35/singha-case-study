# Finance and project valuation: Lime Cube (19 October 2026)

This is a model output for one product, the Lime Cube, sold in domestic Thailand. It is not investment advice.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the text was captured and read. UNVERIFIED: not captured, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, inputs and tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## Scope: the project, not the group

- One product in domestic Thailand. No group financials, volumes or cost bases are used.
- A dissolvable lime cube: unpriced, with no measured price response.
- Composition per 330 mL: potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 2.10 g, of which 0.52 g reacts with the bicarbonate and 1.58 g is free acid that sets the pH; sucralose 33 mg (about 100 mg/kg). No sodium carbonate is used.
- Dose: 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL at a target pH of 3.5, a working anchor. The dose gate is not set; R&D is to define it.
- Solubility is 0.655 volume at 30 C and 0.592 at 35 C (S, SOURCED; Source 10), so the dose is within solubility at every serve from 5 to 35 C (DERIVED). This is a pure-water check; it does not measure fizz.
- Sweetener: sucralose is permitted at 300 mg/kg in category 14.1.4 (flavoured drinks), condition 127, applied to the drink as consumed (P, SOURCED; Source 8). Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis there. Condition TH3 covers electrolyte drinks only. Classification is an open regulatory question.
- Method: pre-tax, five-year horizon, no terminal value, discount rate 8% to 12% (ASSUMPTION; base 10%).

- The dose gate is not set. No bench test has been run, and taste is untested.
- One cube per pouch is unconfirmed with the organiser. No supplier quote has been obtained and no outside contact has been made. Break-even figures assume the 0.55 recipe as stated.
