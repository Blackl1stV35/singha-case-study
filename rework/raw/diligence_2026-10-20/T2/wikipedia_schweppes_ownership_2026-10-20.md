## What the extract gave

Thailand. Thailand appears once, in the infobox (no section heading), as a list entry under The Coca-Cola Company in the Distributor field: "South Korea, Thailand, Turkey, Tunisia, Trinidad & Tobago, Ukraine, United Kingdom, and Vietnam". The infobox lists the Distributor field by region: Coca-Cola (many countries), PepsiCo (US and Canada), Suntory (22 mostly European countries), Swire Coca-Cola (China, Hong Kong, Taiwan), Asahi Beverages (Australia) and Jafora-Tabori (Israel). Keurig Dr Pepper is listed as manufacturer.

Ownership (History, 21st century):
- "The Coca-Cola Company owns the Schweppes brand in several territories, including 21 European countries" (the sentence continues and names European Refreshments ULC as the vehicle).
- "In a further 22 European countries, the brand is owned by Schweppes International Limited (a subsidiary of Suntory)."
- "The Japanese Asahi Group bought Schweppes Australia in 2008 from Cadbury, and owns the trademark in Australia."
- "It [Keurig Dr Pepper] is the current owner of the Schweppes trademark in Canada and the United States."
- "In China, Hong Kong and Taiwan, Swire's subsidiary Swire Coca-Cola produces Schweppes branded beverages." (a producer, not an owner)

Schweppes Holdings does not appear as an owner in the article text. It appears only in a citation title.
