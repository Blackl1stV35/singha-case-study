## Extracted facts (abstract as printed on the journal page)
- Nine drinks bought in Peshawar: Pepsi, Mountain Dew, 7-up, Mirinda, Coca cola, Sprite, Fanta, Amrat cola, Amrat lemon.
- Brix, in the abstract's order Pepsi, Mountain Dew, 7-up, Mirinda, Coca-cola, Sprite, Fanta, Amrat cola, Amrat lemon: 11.40, 11.90, 10.70, 12.90, 10.37, 10.0, 13.00, 10.6, 10.20 percent. So 7-up 10.70, Sprite 10.0, Amrat lemon 10.20.
- CO2 gas volume (G.V.) in the same order: 3.60, 3.20, 3.70, 1.80, 3.80, 3.80, 1.80, 3.80, 3.70. So 7-up 3.70, Sprite 3.80, Amrat lemon 3.70 volumes. Sprite at 3.80 volumes is 3.80 x 44.01 / 22.414 = 7.46 g/L of CO2 (DERIVED, molar volume at 0 C and 1 atm).
- Citric acid in Mountain Dew, 7-up, Mirinda, Sprite, Fanta and Amrat lemon, in that order: 1337.6, 1459.2, 1816.32, 1196.8, 1664, 1216 mg/L. So 7-up 1459.2 mg/L, Sprite 1196.8 mg/L, Amrat lemon 1216 mg/L. Pepsi, Coca cola and Amrat cola were free of citric acid.

## Limits
- Abstract only; method (titration or chromatography), sampling dates and pH are not in the abstract. Regional formulation (Pakistan) is a PROXY for Thai Sprite or 7UP.
