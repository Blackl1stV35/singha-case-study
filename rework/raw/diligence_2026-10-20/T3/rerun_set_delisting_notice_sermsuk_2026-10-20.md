## Extract (English as printed on the notice, as returned)

- "The SET has approved the delisting of SSC's common stocks from December 20, 2024 onwards."
- "Hence, December 19, 2024 will be the last trading day of SSC's common stocks."
- Legal basis: "By virtue of Section 171 (4) of the Securities and Exchange Act B.E. 2535 (1992)."
- Reason: "Voluntary delisting of securities."
- No earlier approval date is printed. The notice states SSC requested the voluntary delisting.
