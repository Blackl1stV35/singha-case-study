# Oishi Group: ThaiBev tender offer and delisting, 2023 (T4)

Tier S (trade and market news). Date read: 20 October 2026, through the fetch tool.

## Kaohoon International, 13 March 2023
- URL: https://www.kaohooninternational.com/markets/524063
- ThaiBev announced a tender offer for "all 76,279,602 shares in OISHI, which represents 20.34% of the total number of issued and paid-up shares" at THB 59.00 a share. ThaiBev already held 79.66%. Reason given: "it will provide cost savings which would have been incurred in connection with OISHI being a publicly listed entity".
- Oishi is described as "a Thailand-based company engaged in the operation of Japanese restaurants and bakeries, as well as the distribution of food and beverage." No revenue or profit figure is given.

## FoodBev, 24 August 2023
- URL: https://foodbev.com/news/thaibev-ups-oishi-stake-with-acquisition
- The tender offer "closed on 21 August"; ThaiBev acquired another 70,662,764 shares (18.84%), "bringing ThaiBev's ownership up to 98.5%". "The Oishi delisting is expected to occur in due course." Financial details were not disclosed. Oishi Green Tea is the ready-to-drink tea brand; Oishi also runs Japanese restaurants in Thailand.
