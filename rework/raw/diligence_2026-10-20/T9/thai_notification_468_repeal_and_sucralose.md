# Thai Notification 468 B.E. 2568: repeal of Notification 418 Annexes 1 and 2, and the sucralose limit in flavoured drinks (read 20 October 2026)

Tier P (Thai regulator text, Thai language). English wording is our translation; the Thai text has no English version.

## 1. Repeal of Notification 418 Annex 1 (page 1 re-read on 20 October 2026)

- URL: https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=849894195891150848&name=468_FoodAdditives.pdf (788 KB, 36 pages, saved by the fetch tool in the session tool-results folder, outside the repository). Page 1 was rendered to an image and read visually on 20 October 2026; the text layer is not usable.
- Document as printed: "ประกาศสำนักงานคณะกรรมการอาหารและยา เรื่อง คำชี้แจงประกาศกระทรวงสาธารณสุข (ฉบับที่ ๔๖๘) พ.ศ. ๒๕๖๘" (Announcement of the Office of the Food and Drug Board: explanatory statement of Ministry of Public Health Notification No. 468 B.E. 2568, issued under the Food Act B.E. 2522, on criteria, conditions, methods of use and proportions of food additives (No. 4)).
- It says the Ministry of Public Health issued Notification No. 468 B.E. 2568 and that Notification 468 "has effect from 12 December B.E. 2568" (ตั้งแต่วันที่ ๑๒ ธันวาคม พ.ศ. ๒๕๖๘), that is 12 December 2025.
- Clause 1 (our translation): "Repeal Annex 1 and Annex 2 attached to MoPH Notification No. 418 B.E. 2563 (dated 2 September B.E. 2563), as amended by MoPH Notification No. 444 B.E. 2566 (No. 3) (dated 14 November B.E. 2566), and use the annexes attached to MoPH Notification No. 468 B.E. 2568 instead."
- Consequence: the sucralose row in Notification 418 Annex 1 (saved earlier in `thai_notification_418_sucralose.md`) is a repealed instrument. The authority is Notification 468 only.
- Page 1 gives only the effective date. The announcement's own date (6 January B.E. 2569 = 6 January 2026) is on page 5, read on 20 October 2026; the Royal Gazette date of Notification 468 itself was not read.

> **UPDATE (20 October 2026, second verification pass).** The Annex rows below (sucralose 300 mg/kg with conditions 127 and TH3; citric acid and potassium bicarbonate quantum satis) were reopened on 20 October 2026 from the local copy of the Annex and read as page images; they are confirmed. See `thai_notification_468_rows_reconfirmed_2026-10-20.md`. Page 2 of the explanatory announcement was also read (`thai_notification_468_explanatory_announcement_quantum_satis.md`); it is dated 6 January B.E. 2569 (6 January 2026). The statements in section 3 below that the row was not reopened and that the gazette date was not read are superseded by those two pages.

## 2. The sucralose row in Notification 468 (first read 19 October 2026; reconfirmed 20 October 2026, see the update above)

- Saved page: `rework/raw/moph_468_annex_flavoured_drinks_extract_2026-10-19.md` (primary: https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=840096266338836480&name=P468.pdf, the Annex I table, 839 PDF pages; the file is kept outside the repository because of its size).
- Row for category 14.1.4, flavoured drinks: sucralose (INS 955), maximum 300 mg/kg, conditions 127 and TH3, PDF page 678, read from the page image. Citric acid (330), PDF page 197, and potassium bicarbonate (501(ii)), PDF page 534, are "quantum satis" (quantity as needed) in 14.1.4.
- Condition 127: the level is calculated for the product ready to drink. Condition TH3 (text read on 20 October 2026, Annex printed page 835): for mineral-salt beverage products, the additive must be used together with sugar of the type and amount set in the mineral-salt beverage notification; whether the cube drink is a flavoured drink or a mineral-salt beverage is a regulatory question this record does not close.
- The 300 mg/kg level is the same as in the repealed Notification 418 (read on 20 October 2026), so the design arithmetic does not change: 33 mg in about 333 g is 99 mg/kg, 33% of the limit.
