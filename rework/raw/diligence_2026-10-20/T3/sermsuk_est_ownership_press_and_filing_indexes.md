## 1. Brand Buffet, 4 November 2014: Serm Suk sells the est brand

- URL: https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2014/11/est-cola-thaibev-deal/
- Article date 4 November 2014 (BrandBuffet Team). Serm Suk announced the sale of the "เอส" (est) cola brand, launched about two years earlier, to International Beverage Holdings Limited (Interbev) for THB 1.56 billion. Serm Suk and Interbev are both subsidiaries in the ThaiBev group.
- After the sale the buyer holds the trademark and is bound by contract to build the brand (Somchai Bulsook, Serm Suk chief executive). Serm Suk keeps producing and distributing under contract, earning fees that rise with volume; Serm Suk no longer funds brand building.
- Figures printed: est first full year (2013) loss THB 328 million; first half of 2014 loss THB 268 million; carbonated market THB 45 billion; est share 12% in the first half of 2014 against a 20% first-year target; Serm Suk distribution to more than 200,000 traditional shops.
- Analysts quoted: Serm Suk had been loss-making since its Pepsi contract ended.

- Page date 27 November 2024. So Water acquired 99.19% of Sermsuk through the completed tender offer, "paving the way" for delisting from the SET. No SET effective date is given.

## 6. Asia Food and Beverages, August 2024

- URL: https://asiafoodbeverages.com/sermsuk-to-delist-for-better-flexibility-in-business-operation-management/
- ThaiBev announced on 3 July 2024 the plan to delist Sermsuk; So Water to buy the remaining 35.33% for THB 5.91 billion. Reasons given: more efficient management, fewer required procedures, more flexibility in decisions; the group is studying a possible restructuring of its companies.
