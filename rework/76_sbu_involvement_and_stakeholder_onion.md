## 3. The operating model: who owns each gate

### 3.1 Rules of the model

1. Every gate and every phase that has no gate of its own has exactly one accountable unit (A). That unit holds the deliverable and certifies the result against the pass rule.
2. R means responsible for part of the work, C consulted before the result is final, I informed after. A unit appears once per step.
3. Owners are roles inside a unit, not people. No name is used.
4. The pass rule is fixed before the run by the owning unit and written on the pass sheet. The executive sponsor role (U1) signs the gate record against that rule. The sponsor does not reopen a threshold, and a failed rule stops the step it controls, as section 10 already states.
5. Outside contact opens only at the gate that needs it and is listed at Gate 0 (section 3.6, row 3). No outside contact has taken place.
6. Phase 0 is Gate 0. Phase 1 is Gates 1 to 7. Phase 3 is Gates 8 and 9. Phases 2, 4, 5 and 6 are columns of their own.
7. Brand-owner and legal sign-off at Gate 7 is placed with Singha's department by the user on 19 October 2026 (CLAUDE.md section 5). The model keeps that placement: the brand and legal head is a role in U1. The aggregator reading that Boon Rawd Brewery holds the SINGHA marks would make the mark holder the owner instead. That is an open question for the user (limit 11), and the record does not decide it. The team does not hold the sign-off.

### 3.5 What the model shows

- **One unit carries the early gates and most of the middle.** U10 holds Gates 1 to 6 and Gate 9: seven of fourteen steps. Four of them are the regulatory, R&D and sensory steps, which must run in M1 to M5 with separate roles. Running a regulatory opinion, a potassium review, a bench measurement and a blind taste test through one plant group is a single point of failure. The proposal keeps the owner roles separate (regulatory affairs head, R&D lead, sensory lead) and stages the windows (Gates 3 and 4 start at M2). Capacity is not evidenced, so it is a stated limit, not a claim.
- **The alternative design.** A separate R&D and quality unit would spread the load, but it is not a published unit, and the brief allows only sourced units. The record uses the host design and asks the user to confirm it (limit 3).
- **The two Boon Rawd Trading roles (U8, U9) own the commercial half.** They hold three accountable steps between them (Phase 2, Gate 8, Phase 4). This matches where the sources place marketing and distribution.
- **Brand sign-off is outside the programme's own control.** The brand and legal head (U1) holds Gate 7, and Gate 7 blocks sale. The model records this and does not assume the sign-off. The press reports that Singha Corporation's income includes beverage licence fees (limit 11). That fits Singha Corporation as a possible licensor of the beverage brands, but it does not establish who holds the Thai mark.
- **Finance is never accountable.** It holds no A but is responsible for the budget at Gate 8 and the contribution read at Phase 5. This is deliberate: the pass tests that involve money (THB 3.51 floor, cost cap) are written in advance, so Finance supplies the numbers and the owners apply the rule.
