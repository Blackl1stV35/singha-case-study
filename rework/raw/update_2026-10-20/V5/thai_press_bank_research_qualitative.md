# Thai press, market research and bank research: qualitative statements on heat and soft drinks

- Item: V5. Date read: 20 October 2026. All tier S (trade press, a market-research press release and bank research). None gives a coefficient. Thai text below is as returned by the fetch tool; the English gloss is our translation.

## 1. Marketeer, "ศึกแคมเปญน้ำอัดลม ซัมเมอร์ปีนี้ เกือบ(ไม่)ซ่า", 14 April 2020, by NALISA

- URL: https://marketeeronline.co/archives/158545 (WebFetch).
- Fragment: "ซัมเมอร์" คือเวลาทองของตลาดน้ำอัดลม (summer is the golden time of the soft drink market).
- Fragment: ยอดจำหน่ายน้ำอัดลมในช่วงหน้าร้อนนี้จะเพิ่มสูงขึ้นกว่าในช่วงปกติอยู่มาก (soft drink sales in the hot season rise well above normal).
- The only figure on the page is the market value of 56,000 million baht, which is not a seasonal figure. No percentage for the summer uplift.
- A second Marketeer page (https://marketeeronline.co/archives/174217) appeared as a search snippet saying beverages such as soft drinks and green tea sell best in the hot season every year, and that restaurants are about 18 to 20 percent of the soft drink market. Snippet, not opened.

## 2. Mintel press release, "Thai consumers seek value-added drinks amidst soaring temperatures", 27 December 2023

- URL: https://www.mintel.com/press-centre/thai-consumers-seek-value-added-drinks-amidst-soaring-temperatures (WebFetch).
- Facts: Thailand reached a record 45.5 C in summer 2023; 70 percent of Thais actively sought adequate water intake; in 2023 carbonated drinks (70 percent), bottled water (67 percent) and ready-to-drink coffee (60 percent) were the top non-alcoholic choices.
- The page gives no sales-to-temperature coefficient.

## 3. Krungsri Research, Industry Outlook 2025-2027: Beverage Industry, 7 November 2024

- URL: https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/industry/industry-outlook/food-beverage/beverage/io/beverage-2025-2027 (WebFetch).
- Non-alcoholic domestic sales volume rose 7.0 percent in 2023 and 3.5 percent year on year in the first nine months of 2024, "on hotter than normal weather that was partly caused by the year's El Nino". The report does not separate the weather effect from other drivers.
- Steadily rising temperatures are listed among the drivers of the forecast volume growth. No temperature-specific figure.

## 4. Krungsri Research, Industry Outlook 2026-2028: Beverage Industry, 15 June 2026

- URL: https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/industry/industry-outlook/food-beverage/beverage/io/beverage-2026 (WebFetch).
- Carbonated production growth of 2.1 to 3.1 percent a year is "partly tied to" rising temperatures; bottled water sales in 2025 rose 0.9 percent "supported by hotter weather conditions". Q1 2026 carbonated and soda sales rose 5.3 percent year on year, which the report attributes to new product launches, not weather. No monthly data and no coefficient.
- Note for the model: the 2.1 to 3.1 percent is a production growth forecast, not a temperature elasticity.
