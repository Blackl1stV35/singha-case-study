## What it says

- Title: "ประกาศสำนักงานคณะกรรมการอาหารและยา เรื่อง คำชี้แจงประกาศกระทรวงสาธารณสุข ..." covering six notifications of 3 March B.E. 2554 (2011): chocolate (No. 2), beverages in sealed containers (No. 4), tea (No. 3), coffee (No. 3), mineral-salt beverages (No. 2) and food for weight control (No. 2).
- Opening paragraph: the FDA reviewed the contamination standard for yeast and mould in the six notifications, which required "ไม่มียีสต์และเชื้อรา" (no yeast and no mould), to fit the epidemiological and technical data. Clause 1: repeals the yeast and mould requirements in those six notifications. Clause 2: sets new yeast and mould amounts in a table. For mineral-salt beverages (item 6): liquid sterilised or UHT treated, less than 1 per mL; liquid treated by other methods, less than 100 per mL; dried form, less than 100 per gram. Clause 3: the analysis uses the current US FDA Bacteriological Analytical Manual method or an equivalent method. Clause 4: the six notifications have effect from 8 April B.E. 2554 (the Gazette reference printed is Volume 128, Special Part 41 ง, 7 April B.E. 2554).
- Signed: "ประกาศ ณ วันที่ ๒๑ กันยายน พ.ศ.๒๕๕๔ พิพัฒน์ ยิ่งเสรี เลขาธิการคณะกรรมการอาหารและยา" (given 21 September B.E. 2554).

## What it settles

- It explains the yeast and mould change of 2011 (clause 4(5)(e) and the dry-form paragraph of No. 195). It does not mention clause 3, clause 4(1) or clause 7 of No. 195, and it does not change them.
- It confirms the effective date of the "(No. 2)" notification as 8 April 2011, which is the date the FDA list prints (08/04/54).
