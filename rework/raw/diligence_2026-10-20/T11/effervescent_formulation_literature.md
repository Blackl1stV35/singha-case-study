# Effervescent formulation literature used for the binder and filler choice (read 20 October 2026)

All papers were opened through the PubMed Central full-text tool or read as abstracts through PubMed and Consensus, as stated per entry. Tier P for peer-reviewed papers; S for the encyclopaedia entry. Our wording is a summary; numbers are as printed.

## 1. Rosch M, Lucas K, Al-Gousous J, Poeschl U, Langguth P. Formulation and Characterization of an Effervescent Hydrogen-Generating Tablet. Pharmaceuticals 2021;14(12):1327. DOI 10.3390/ph14121327. PMID 34959728, PMC8707073. Full text read.

- Compared four saccharide fillers (spray-dried maltose, lactose anhydrous, dextrates, mannitol) in magnesium plus citric acid effervescent tablets made by roller compaction (18 mm punches, 1.5 g tablets).
- Disintegration: mannitol 72 +/- 3 s (83 +/- 3 s with adipic acid); maltose 238 +/- 25 s; dextrates 257 +/- 15 s; lactose 300 +/- 32 s (complete disintegration, 200 mL water, Ph. Eur. method).
- Resistance to crushing at 40 kN: dextrates 147.4 N; lactose 129.3 N; maltose 121.1 N; mannitol 112.0 N (mannitol lowest, "satisfactory"). Tensile strength: dextrates 1.204, lactose 1.057, maltose 0.963, mannitol 0.865 N/mm. Only the dextrates tablets passed friability (0.72 percent mass loss).
- The authors state that effervescent components are usually hygroscopic and moisture-labile, that water uptake can trigger the reaction and cause sticking, and that anhydrous citric acid begins to take up water at about 62 percent relative humidity at 25 C (their citation). Mannitol-based granule mix showed no mass change up to 70 percent RH in dynamic vapour sorption; a large uptake began from 80 percent (that mix contained a monosodium citrate-coated citric acid, which we do not use).
- Water-soluble lubricants chosen: sodium stearyl fumarate (a sodium salt) and adipic acid; the sodium salt slowed reaction by coating the metal in the acidic medium. Adipic acid at 10 percent was used in the optimised tablet.
- Relevance: mannitol gave the fastest disintegration among the fillers; mannitol is non-hygroscopic enough to hold up to 70 percent RH; sodium stearyl fumarate and a monosodium citrate coat are sodium sources to avoid.

## 2. Effervescent Tablet Preparation by Twin-Screw Melt Granulation with Sorbitol as a Melt Binder. Pharmaceutics 2025;17(5):676. DOI 10.3390/pharmaceutics17050676. PMID 40430966, PMC12115153. Full text read.

- Citric acid and sodium bicarbonate (1:3 molar ratio) with 30 wt percent sorbitol; sorbitol was dried in a desiccator for a week and the granules stored in a desiccator.
- Polyol melting points by DSC (first heating): xylitol 105.7 C, sorbitol 106.8 C, isomalt 165.6 C, mannitol 174.3 C, sucrose 198.4 C. Citric acid degrades from 160 C; sodium bicarbonate from 100 C.
- States that wet granulation with water is inapplicable to effervescent mixes because water starts the reaction; dry granulation needs a relatively higher weight fraction of excipient to reach tablet strength.
- Disintegration of the tablets (100 mL water): 62.7 to 77.8 s; tensile strength all above 2 MPa.
- Relevance: sorbitol works as a binder but the authors dried it and kept everything in desiccators; the sodium bicarbonate system is not ours; sodium stearyl fumarate (1.5 wt percent) was the lubricant.

## 3. Sundaramoorthy A et al. Effervescent Tablets: Comprehensive Review on Formulation Strategies, Manufacturing Technologies, and Quality Evaluation. Int J Innov Sci Res Technol 2025. Abstract read through Consensus (not opened in full).

- States that effervescent tablets need careful choice of binders, fillers, sweeteners, polymers and water-soluble lubricants to prevent premature reaction, that they are highly sensitive to moisture, and that manufacture is typically below 25 C and low humidity with moisture-protective packaging.

## 4. Ubeda A, Llopico J, Sanchez MT. Blood pressure reduction in hypertensive patients after withdrawal of effervescent medication. Pharmacoepidemiol Drug Saf 2009;18(5):417-9. DOI 10.1002/pds.1701. PMID 19248080. Abstract read.

- Context: effervescent paracetamol at 3 g/day carried 74 mmol/day of sodium, mainly from sodium bicarbonate and carbonate excipients; switching to plain tablets lowered systolic pressure by 13.1 mmHg in 34 patients. Shows why sodium in effervescent formats is a recognised issue.

## 5. Fontenele M et al. Molecular Modelling and Prediction of the Physicochemical Properties of Polyols in Aqueous Solution. MATEC Web of Conferences 2025 (Roquette authors). Abstract read through Consensus.

- Solubility of mannitol in water is 0.25 kg per kg of water at 25 C; sorbitol 2.5 kg per kg of water.

## 6. Wong KY et al. Moisture absorption behavior and thermal properties of sucrose replacer mixture containing inulin or polydextrose. Applied Food Research 2022. Abstract read through Consensus.

- At water activity 0.65, mixtures containing erythritol, mannitol or tagatose showed low moisture content and low hygroscopicity.

## 7. Hadjikinova M et al. Sorption characteristics of dietary hard candy. Czech J Food Sci 2018. Abstract read through Consensus.

- Candy made with isomalt sorbed less moisture than candy made with sorbitol under equal conditions (20 C).

## 8. Mannitol, encyclopaedia entry (S): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannitol , read 20 October 2026. States very low hygroscopicity, no water pick-up from air until humidity reaches 98 percent; a popular excipient for chewable tablets; E421; an osmotic laxative in oral doses larger than 20 g. Secondary; the figures are not traced to a primary here.

## Limits

- Handbook of Pharmaceutical Excipients and manufacturer brochures (Roquette, Merck, Beneo) were not opened; compaction, flow and dissolution data for the exact cube size (14 mm) do not exist in the sources read. Cube hardness and friability with the chosen filler are untested.
- No source was opened for leucine solubility or taste, or for PEG 6000 as a dry binder; PEG is excluded on the Thai limit and the ADI (see polyol_tolerance_and_peg_adi.md).
