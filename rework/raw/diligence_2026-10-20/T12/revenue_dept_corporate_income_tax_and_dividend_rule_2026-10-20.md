### Statements as returned

- Rate: "The corporate income tax rate in Thailand is 20 % on net profit (accounting periods 2015)".
- Dividends received by a Thai company from another Thai company:
  - Base rule: "As for dividend income, one-half of the dividends received by Thai companies from any other Thai companies may be excluded".
  - Full exclusion: "However, the full amount may be excluded from taxable income if the recipient is a company listed" and "in the Stock Exchange of Thailand or the recipient owns at least 25% of the distributing company's capital interest".
  - Proviso (second read): "provided that the distributing company does not own a direct or indirect capital interest in the recipient company." The exclusion therefore does not apply where the distributing company holds an interest in the recipient (cross-holding).
  - Holding period: "The exclusion of dividends is applied only if the shares are acquired not less than 3 months before receiving the dividends" and "and are not disposed of within 3 months after receiving the dividends."
- Withholding table (Section 6 of the page, as returned): "Certain types of income paid to companies are subject to withholding tax at source." Row: Dividends, 10 %. The page does not say whether the 10 percent applies to Thai-to-Thai dividends that qualify for the exclusion, and it states no withholding exemption for them.

### Use and limits

- 20 percent: the rate used in the model.
- Full exclusion for Boon Rawd's dividend from Singha Corporation is conditional on a holding of at least 25 percent, the 3-month holding rule and the cross-holding proviso. None of the three is verified (ownership not found), so the model uses the full exclusion only as a stated condition and also shows the half-exclusion case.
- The 10 percent corporate dividend withholding is not modelled (credit and exemption treatment not read).

## B. Individuals page (personal income tax)

- URL: https://www.rd.go.th/english/6045.html
- Date printed on page: "Last updated: 21.03.2024"
- Date read: 20 October 2026
- Access route and tier: as above, S (primary-origin page, read through a page summary).

### Statements as returned

- Dividends are assessable income category 4 ("income in the nature of dividends, interest on deposits with banks in Thailand").
- Tax credit: a taxpayer domiciled in Thailand receiving dividends from a Thai company "is entitled to a tax credit of 3/7 of the amount of dividends received"; the taxpayer grosses the dividend up by the credit when computing assessable income.
- Separate taxation option (Section 3.2): dividends "which tax has been withheld at source at the rate of 10 per cent" may be excluded from assessable income, in which case the credit or refund cannot be claimed.
- The page's own withholding table (Section 4) does not list dividends; the only dividend withholding rate it states is 10 percent, in Section 3.2.

### Use and limits

- 10 percent is used only as an illustrative holder-level rate for Thai individuals who take the separate-taxation option. Whether the ultimate holders of Boon Rawd Brewery are individuals, and which option each takes, is not known.
