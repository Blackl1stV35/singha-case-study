# Thai Notification No. 394 B.E. 2561: clause 3 and clause 5, including the individual-pack provision (saved text, third pass)

- Document: Notification of the Ministry of Public Health (No. 394) B.E. 2561 (2018), Re: Food products required to bear Nutrition Labelling and Guideline Daily Amounts (GDA) Labelling. FDA "(Unofficial)" English translation.
- URL read: https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=509411275630059520&name=No.394.pdf (247 KB). The fetch tool could not decode the compressed PDF and saved it; the text was extracted with pdftotext on 2026-10-20.
- Date read: 2026-10-20. Tier: P (regulator text, regulator's English translation; the Thai original and the amendments by No. 446 and No. 466 are covered in thai_gda_notifications_394_446_466.md).

## Clause 3 (6) and the liquid-product paragraph

"(6) Beverages ... (6.3) Beverage according to (6.1) or (6.2) in dry form". The paragraph after the list of foods says that for food products under (6), (7), (8) and (12) in returnable glass bottles, and liquid food products under (6), (9), (10), (11) and (12), where the area in front of the pack is less than 65 sq.cm and inadequate to display the panels, the "nutrition detective" mobile applications may be used together with display where the products are sold. That paragraph names liquid products and returnable glass bottles; it does not name dry beverages (our reading).

## Clause 5, last paragraph (verbatim, our line breaks removed)

"Additionally, food products under Clause 3 contained in each individual pack which the area in front of pack are less than 65 sq.cm and inadequate to display the nutrition information panel and GDAs labelling, the nutrition panel and GDAs labelling could be displayed on the wholesale packages. Energy value and nutrient contents are calculated based on the individual unit. Nevertheless, the nutrition panel and GDAs labelling can be displayed on each individual pack by complying with the provisions set in this notification."

## Reading for the cube (DERIVED)

- A single-serve pouch holding one 14 mm cube may have a front area under 65 sq.cm. If it is also too small to carry the panels, the panels may go on the wholesale package (the carton of several pouches), with energy and nutrients calculated per individual unit (per cube as sold). The pouch front area is not in the repo and the panels are allowed on each pack in any case.
- This is a Gate 1 and Gate 9 point: the pouch front area, and which package carries the panels. The sodium and potassium values stay per unit either way.
- This text is the 2018 version. Clause 5 (1) was replaced by Notification No. 446 (B.E. 2566) and the GDA annex by No. 466 (B.E. 2568); whether either changed the last paragraph of clause 5 was not seen in the translations read (No. 446 replaces only clause 5 (1); No. 466 replaces the annex).
