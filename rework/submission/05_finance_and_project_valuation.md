# Finance and project valuation: Lemon Soda (19 October 2026)

This is a model output for one product, the Lemon Soda, sold in domestic Thailand. It is not investment advice.

## Tiers

Tiers are defined once, in Appendix A.

## Scope: the project, not the group

- One product in domestic Thailand. No group data is used.
- A dissolvable Lemon Soda cube, unpriced, with no measured price response.
- Composition per 330 mL (basis B): potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 2.10 g (0.52 g reacts with the bicarbonate; 1.58 g is free acid that sets the pH); sucralose 33 mg (about 100 mg/kg); a binder and filler 0.80 g (ASSUMPTION; range 0.5 to 1.2 g; not yet named; not yet checked for sodium; sodium not claimed) and natural lemon flavour 0.05 g (ASSUMPTION; range 0.02 to 0.10 g). No sodium carbonate is used. Reactants are 2.94 g. The central cube mass is 3.79 g (range 3.46 to 4.24 g; DERIVED on ASSUMPTION inputs; not weighed), a 14 mm cube at 1.38 g/cm3. The packed unit with a 1.5 g pouch (ASSUMPTION) is 5.29 g.
- Dose: 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL at a working pH of 3.5, an anchor only; R&D restates the dose gate before the run (section 10, Gate 3). The pH rests on pKa values (S, UNVERIFIED: read from rendered images only).
- Exchange rate: 33.55 baht per US dollar, Bank of Thailand midpoint, 15 October 2026 (P, SOURCED; Source 7).
- Solubility is 0.655 volume at 30 C and 0.592 at 35 C (S, SOURCED; Source 10), so the dose is within solubility from 5 to 35 C (DERIVED). That range is a working range; the Bangkok climate figures are UNVERIFIED. This is a pure-water check, not a fizz test. A 0.44 g/L sparkling threshold (S, abstract only; Source 12) is a soft-drink proxy and sets no dose.
- Sweetener: sucralose is permitted at 300 mg/kg in category 14.1.4 (flavoured drinks), condition 127, applied to the drink as ready to drink (P, SOURCED; Source 8). Only the flavoured-drink rows of the annex were read. Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis there; the binder, filler and flavour are not yet checked against this rule. Condition TH3 covers electrolyte drinks only and is not claimed for the cube.
- Method: pre-tax, five years, no terminal value, 8% to 12% discount rate (ASSUMPTION; base 10%).

Against the 12-cube carton, the 6-cube carton costs THB 0.25 more per cube (4.09 against 3.84), so its contribution is THB 0.25 lower (4.91 against 5.16). The valuation, the scenarios, the break-even and the sensitivities below use the 6-cube contribution of 4.91, the recommended pack. The 12-cube carton, the larger option, has a contribution of 5.16; its maximum fixed cost at base volume and 10% is 719,000 (sensitivity table).

Its price is not verified, so no price comparison is made against it. The products overlap on flavour and differ in form and pack: the cube dissolves in the glass, one cube per pouch. At the assumed 15 THB shelf price, the recommended 6-cube cost of 4.09 THB, which excludes binder and flavour (not priced), is 27% of a 15 THB serve (DERIVED); the 12-cube cost of 3.84 THB, the larger option, is 26%. The can's own claims are not checked here; this model makes no sugar or calorie claim for the cube.

A 15 THB, 330 mL serve (about 45 THB per litre, DERIVED) sits inside the 25.0 to 85.6 THB per litre band of two retailer listings (S, SOURCED; Source 3).

About 128,586 cubes fit in a 20 ft container in 6-cube cartons, the recommended pack, which is 21,431 cartons (DERIVED; S, SOURCED; Source 11; the 190 x 150 x 50 mm carton and 92% packing are ASSUMPTIONS). The 12-cube carton, the larger option, fits about 128,580 cubes in 10,715 cartons.

Potassium is 317 mg per cube, 9.1% of a Thai 3,500 mg per day reference (DERIVED; S, SOURCED; Source 9). A composition figure only.

## Shareholder value

The proposer is Singha Corporation. The desk value below is for the project, not the group, and no group data is used.

At 200,000 cubes a year and a fixed cost of THB 500,000 a year, the base-case NPV is THB 0.88 million (DERIVED on ASSUMPTION inputs: five years, a 10% discount rate, no terminal value, pre-tax; the year 0 and year 1 conventions are those in this file). That is about 0.03% of Singha Corporation's 2025 net profit of about THB 3,085 million (S; press, 23 May 2026; Source 13) (DERIVED). The project breaks even at 146,230 cubes a year (DERIVED). At 500,000 cubes a year the NPV is about THB 5.80 million, and at 1,000,000 cubes it is THB 13.99 million (DERIVED).

At 200,000 cubes a year the NPV stays positive up to a fixed cost of about THB 0.68 million a year (DERIVED). It is THB 2.08 million at THB 250,000 a year, and minus THB 1.51 million at THB 1,000,000 a year (DERIVED).

The pilot is read after 13 weeks of trading plus two weeks, and Singha Corporation can stop it then (the 13 weeks of trading plus two weeks is an ASSUMPTION; section 10, Phases 4 and 5). The spend before that decision is one budget still to be set (decision 9 in section 10).

Value accrues to Singha Corporation. How it passes to the shareholders of Boon Rawd Brewery is not modelled. The figures are a desk estimate, not a forecast of profit, dividends or share price.

## Can cost by annual volume (desk estimate)

This is a desk estimate of the cost per aluminium can, with sugar and flavour in the liquid. It is a reference for can economics, not the cost of the Lemon Soda cube. No Singha Corporation or Boon Rawd Brewery can cost was found, so every input is a desk input.

The variable cost is THB 4.94 per can at the mid input, within a range of THB 3.73 to 6.14 per can (DERIVED). It covers aluminium sheet, non-metal conversion, filling consumables, seaming, CO2, and the liquid with sugar and flavour. The exchange rates are the Bank of Thailand buying and selling rates of 15 October 2026 (Source 7). The aluminium price input has no saved primary source yet, and excise is excluded.

## Limits

- The 1.30 THB pouch base is an ASSUMPTION near the midpoint of an UNVERIFIED overseas listing (USD 0.03 to 0.05). No domestic quote has been found or requested. The 0.67 to 2.70 THB range is also UNVERIFIED.
- Fixed costs, tooling, registration, trademark, pouch minimums and marketing are unsourced; the grid covers them.
- Excise: the 14% ad valorem rate applies if the value base applies, and the base was not read; the 1.26 THB figure assumes that base. Rate from the B.E. 2560 notice; later notices not searched. Whether a dissolvable cube falls under the item is open.
- VAT, freight, cold chain, income tax, depreciation, working capital and terminal value are excluded.
- Comtrade values have estimated weights and no stated currency or unit.
- The sucralose price is UNVERIFIED. The recommended 6-cube cost includes a carton share of THB 0.67 per cube (THB 4 per 6-cube carton, an ASSUMPTION scaled from the 12-cube price). The 12-cube cost includes a carton share of THB 0.42 per cube (THB 5 per 12-cube carton at ten or more pieces, from a retail postal-box listing used as a PROXY; THB 7 for one piece). Print and processing are placeholders. Binder and filler and lemon flavour are not priced, so every per-cube figure here excludes them.
- The wording of condition 127 is not yet confirmed. No bench test has been run, and taste is untested.
- One cube per pouch is unconfirmed with Singha Corporation (section 10, Decision 6). No supplier quote has been obtained and no outside contact has been made. Break-even assumes the 0.55 recipe.
- The shareholder value is a project NPV on ASSUMPTION inputs (volume, fixed cost, shelf price, ex-factory share and the 10% rate). It is not a group valuation, and it does not model how value passes to the shareholders of Boon Rawd Brewery.
