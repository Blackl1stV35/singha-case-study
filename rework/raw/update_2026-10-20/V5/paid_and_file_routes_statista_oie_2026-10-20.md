# Routes to a Thai monthly beverage series: what the pages show and what was not downloaded

- Item: V5 revision. Date read: 20 October 2026. Only web pages were opened. No spreadsheet, CSV or archive was downloaded or opened, because downloading a file needs the user's permission under the repo rules.

## 1. OIE industrial statistics page (official, free, files not opened)

- URL: https://www.oie.go.th/view/1/statistics/TH-TH (WebFetch). Title: Industrial Statistics (Thai), Office of Industrial Economics, Ministry of Industry.
- It says the statistics come from surveys of sample manufacturers and are used to compile the Industrial Index. For each product it lists production volume, sales volume, sales value, finished-goods inventory and production capacity.
- Coverage as the extract reports it: a series from January 2021 to the present (August 2026 data on the page), with older series for January 2000 to December 2015 and January 2016 to December 2020. Only products collected from five or more factories are shown. The page states no units.
- Files linked (paths as given, on www.oie.go.th): /assets/portals/1/fileups/2/files/industry_statistic/Industry_Statistics_TSIC.xlsx and the same name ending .csv (August 2026 data); /assets/portals/1/fileups/2/files/industry_statistic/Industry_Statistics_TSIC_Jan2016-Dec2020.xlsx; and a zip for 2000 to 2015.
- NOT downloaded, so it is unknown whether the files list carbonated soft drinks as a product and in which unit.

## 2. OIE industrial index page (official, free, files not opened)

- URL: https://www.oie.go.th/view/1/mpi/TH-TH (WebFetch). It lists monthly production, shipment, inventory and capacity indices for 75 industry groups, with Excel files under /assets/portals/1/fileups/2/files/Industrial%20index/indexes/month/ (Prodidx1.xlsx, Prodidx2.xlsx, Shipidx.xlsx, Invidx.xlsx, Invratio.xlsx, capidx.xlsx) and a zip ALL75.zip. The extract names no beverage sub-index. Not downloaded.
- The i-Index portal (i.index.oie.go.th) did not respond in the first pass of this item. The two pages above did load on 20 October 2026.

## 3. Statista Market Insights, Thai carbonated drinks sales value

- URL: https://www.statista.com/statistics/1321731/thailand-monthly-carbonated-drink-sales-value/ (WebFetch).
- Title as the page gives it: sales value of carbonated drinks in Thailand from June 2024 to June 2026, in billion Thai baht. Source credited: Statista Research Department. The page shows "Last update: Sep 17, 2026" and a Premium marker. The figures are masked ("***"). The page states no valuation basis. A search result for the same address described an earlier version covering July 2023 to July 2025; that description was not opened and is not relied on.
- Status: paid. Buying or requesting it needs the user's approval. Not attempted.

## 4. Why none of this changes the verdict

- No monthly carbonated drink level series was read in any form. A coefficient needs that series for several years matched to monthly temperature, and the files in sections 1 and 2 have not been opened.
