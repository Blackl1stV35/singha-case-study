# Lime Cube: executive summary (19 October 2026)

## The proposal in one paragraph

Lime Cube is a dissolvable drink cube for domestic Thailand. One cube dissolved in one 330 mL serve makes a carbonated lime drink: a new form of consumption, not a new flavour. The lead segment is men aged 25 to 34, a desk-model result on unverified inputs, with men aged 45 to 59 a later volume segment.

## What is handed over

- Recommended dose: 0.55 volume of carbon dioxide per 330 mL; the gate is for R&D to define.
- Working pH anchor of 3.5; taste untested.
- Composition stated as fact: potassium bicarbonate, citric acid and sucralose; no sodium carbonate.
- One cube per pouch is inferred, not confirmed.
- No nutrition or performance claim. Classification (beverage, edible ice or electrolyte drink) is open.
- Domestic only; the overseas cube brand cited is a labelled analogue.

## The figures at the recommended dose

- Carbon dioxide 0.55 volume per 330 mL serve, about 8.1 mmol (DERIVED).
- Potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 0.52 g to neutralise it, or 2.10 g at pH 3.5 (DERIVED; acid constants S, SOURCED).
- Potassium 317 mg, 9.0% of a Thai reference of 3,500 mg a day (DERIVED; reference S, SOURCED from a secondary copy).
- Sucralose 33 mg, about 100 mg/kg (DERIVED; assumed sweetness target). Category 14.1.4 permits 300 mg/kg under condition 127, for the drink as consumed (P, SOURCED). Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis (P, SOURCED).
- Solubility 0.592 volume at 35°C, the lowest in the serve range (S, SOURCED); the dose is within it at every serve (DERIVED).
- Desk model: about 0.50 volume left after 10 minutes at 25°C (DERIVED). Time above a fizz threshold: about 96 minutes at most, surface only (DERIVED; threshold PROXY, S, SOURCED).
- Cost THB 3.84 per cube in a 12-cube carton (DERIVED; most inputs are ASSUMPTIONS).

## What R&D are asked to define

1. Retention gate for 0.55 volume: CO2 left at 25°C, and after how many minutes (the desk value is not a gate).
2. The pH and acid level; 3.5 is a working anchor.
3. The sweetness accepted at 33 mg of sucralose per serve; no sensory test has run.
4. Chilled, iced or ambient, with the serve rule and pack wording.
5. The acceptable fizz duration, and how it is measured.
6. Potassium declaration and medical wording; kidney function and potassium-sparing medicines first.
7. The product classification, which sets the limits. Condition TH3 covers electrolyte drinks only.
8. Pouch barrier and shelf life; no water-vapour rate or shelf test exists.
9. Thai prices for pouches, print and cartons; all are assumptions.

## What is not yet known

- Taste, demand and Thai retail response are untested; no bench, sensory or shelf evidence exists.
- Pack pressure, headspace and storage temperature are not modelled.
- Novelty and freedom to operate are not assessed; the Thai patent register is unsearched.
- Brand-owner, legal and alcohol-cue sign-off sit with the organiser's department, unchecked here.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the text was captured and read. UNVERIFIED: not captured, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, inputs and tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.
