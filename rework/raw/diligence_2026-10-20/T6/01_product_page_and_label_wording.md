## 1. Company product page, Thai version

- URL: https://www.singhacorporation.com/singhalemonsoda/th/
- Date read: 20 October 2026. Tier: S (the company's own product-marketing page; not a filing or a release).
- Products shown, all 330 ml cans: Singha Lemon Soda ("first lemon soda in the market with no sugar and no calories, with high vitamin C"), Yuzu Lemon Soda, Ume Lemon Soda, Red Lemon Soda, Pink Lemon Soda. Five cards. No Lemon Cream card, no Melon card, no Watermelon card.
- Where to buy (Fresh Fact section): 7-Eleven, major department stores, the Singha Online channel, Shopee and Lazada.
- Sweeteners: "zero sugar and zero calories, using sucralose and acesulfame potassium, which the page says are approved by the Thai FDA".
- Vitamin C: one can provides 30.7 mg, "equivalent to one lemon" (page claim).
- Halal-certified, no animal-derived ingredients, no caffeine in any flavour, can "100% recyclable".
- Company named in the contact block: Boonrawd Trading Co., Ltd., 1003 Sam Sen Road, Bangkok 10300. Footer: "(c) 2020 Singha Corporation Co., Ltd." The call-centre number differs between footer (1275) and contact section (1245).
- Pricing and launch dates: none on the page. The only date is the 2020 copyright year, so the page has not been updated for the later flavours.
