## C. Ownership after the tender offer (source: T3/sermsuk_est_ownership_press_and_filing_indexes.md, sections 1, 4 and 5; tiers S and P as stated there)
- Brand Buffet, 4 November 2014 (https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2014/11/est-cola-thaibev-deal/), tier S: Sermsuk sold the est brand to International Beverage Holdings Limited (Interbev) for THB 1.56 billion; the buyer holds the trademark; Sermsuk keeps producing and distributing under contract.
- TFEX/SET notice, 4 July 2024 (tier P): tender offer by So Water Company Limited for all remaining Sermsuk shares at THB 63.00, type for delisting.
- TipRanks, 27 November 2024 (tier S): So Water acquired 99.19% of Sermsuk through the completed tender offer.
