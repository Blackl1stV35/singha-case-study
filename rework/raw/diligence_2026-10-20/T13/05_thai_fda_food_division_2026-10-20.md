## 2. Labelling Notification No. 474: https://food.fda.moph.go.th/press-release/law474-no2/ (page dated 16 October 2569 BE = 2026)

- Status: "อยู่ระหว่างนำลงในราชกิจจานุเบกษา" (being published in the Royal Gazette). The Food Division asks entrepreneurs to prepare for enforcement. The page gives no effective date.
- Changes listed on the page (as extracted): nine food categories must carry labels even when sold directly to consumers (including food additives, infant formula, foods for special purposes, food supplements and foods with added extracts, nutrients or synthetic substances); sesame and nine tree nuts added to the allergen list; sulfites and lactose removed from the allergen list with a warning kept above stated levels; carrier solvents and packaging gases need not be declared; digital labels allowed as an alternative to part of the label; mandatory font size applies to Thai text only; drained weight requirement removed.
- The Standards Group of the Food Division is the contact named.

## 3. Soda water hardness consultation: https://food.fda.moph.go.th/press-release/law-soda69/ (page dated 14 October 2569 BE = 2026)

- The Food Division invites comment on the principles of amending the hardness requirements for soda water (water with dissolved carbon dioxide); comment deadline 9 November 2569 BE (2026); amends Ministry of Public Health Notification No. 462 B.E. 2568 on drinking water in sealed containers.
- The cube is not sold as soda water in this proposal; the consultation is a watch item only.
