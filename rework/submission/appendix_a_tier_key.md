# Appendix A: tier key

Every figure in the proposal carries a tier. A figure taken from a source also carries that source and the date it was read. The tiers are:

- **P**: regulator text read in full; a company's own filing, release or product page read in full; official statistics; a primary database.
- **S**: any other source read and opened: press, trade portal, consultancy or research page, or a company claim relayed by press.
- **SOURCED**: the page text was read, and its public URL is given.
- **UNVERIFIED**: not read, blocked, not found, or only a search snippet was read.
- **DERIVED**: our arithmetic, from the inputs shown.
- **PROXY**: a stand-in for a value that has not been measured.
- **ASSUMPTION**: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.
- **COMPANY CLAIM**: a statement the company makes about itself. It is not checked independently.

A figure is tiered where it first appears in a file. A later mention repeats the same tier. Sources are listed in each file's Sources section, with the date read.

How the tiers combine and what they cover:

- A figure taken from a source carries P, S or UNVERIFIED for how that source was read, and SOURCED is added to P or S when the page text was read and its public URL is given. A figure that is our own arithmetic or a value we chose may carry DERIVED or ASSUMPTION alone, and it shows its inputs. A sourced figure may also add one of DERIVED, PROXY, ASSUMPTION or COMPANY CLAIM for what kind of figure it is. PROXY and COMPANY CLAIM may also stand alone where the text names the stand-in or the page on which the company made the statement.
- A figure from a source that was opened, but whose value could not be confirmed from the text read, may carry UNVERIFIED alongside S or P. In that pair, S or P describes the source and UNVERIFIED describes the figure. An item known only from a search snippet never carries this pair, because a snippet is not a page that was read.
- A tier is not raised when a figure is quoted again. A figure read second-hand keeps the tier of the copy that was read. A market value printed by the press from a paid dataset, such as NielsenIQ, is S, because the dataset itself was not opened.
- A search snippet is a lead, not a page that was read. An item known only from a snippet is UNVERIFIED and is never given P or S.
- A bracketed qualifier narrows what a tier covers. "Negative finding" means that a primary list was searched by title up to the date given and nothing was found; it does not exclude a change made elsewhere, for example inside a general notification. "Flavoured-drink rows only" means that only those rows of a regulator's text were read. "Read from page images" means that the text was read from images of the pages. "Company" after S, as in "S, company", marks a company's own web page or statement that is held at S.
- Stated limit on company pages: a company's own filing, release or product page counts as P only when it is read in full. The Singha Lemon Soda product pages, English and Thai, were read through a page extract, not in full, so they are held at S and carry the label "S, company". The Singha About page is a company web page that is not a filing, release or product page, so it is S by the general rule. No figure taken from these pages is raised to P. What the company says about itself on them is also a COMPANY CLAIM.

Entity convention: Singha Corporation is the proposer. Boon Rawd Brewery is the holding company in the company's own description; press and company sources describe the group differently. A trademark aggregator lists Boon Rawd Brewery as owner of 21 marks, 12 with SINGHA (S). The trademark register was not reached, and the Thai owner of the SINGHA mark is not confirmed.
