# Notification No. 468 B.E. 2568: condition TH3 and the FDA summary of its clauses (read 20 October 2026)

Purpose here: to see whether the newest additive notification (in force 12 December 2025) still refers to the mineral-salt beverage notification, and whether the summary of its clauses amends No. 195.

- Tier: P (Thai regulator text). English wording is our translation.

- TH3 as printed: "สำหรับผลิตภัณฑ์เครื่องดื่มเกลือแร่ ต้องใช้ร่วมกับน้ำตาลตามชนิดและปริมาณที่กำหนดไว้ตามประกาศกระทรวงสาธารณสุขว่าด้วยเครื่องดื่มเกลือแร่"
- Translation: for mineral-salt beverage products, [the additive] must be used together with sugar of the type and amount set in the Ministry of Public Health notification on mineral-salt beverages.
- That notification is No. 195 (clause 4 (1.2): glucose or fructose at least 2 percent by weight, or sucrose at least 4 percent). The Annex was issued for the additive notification that took effect on 12 December 2025, so the Ministry's newest additive text still points to the notification on mineral-salt beverages by its subject name. This is indirect evidence that No. 195 was treated as live in B.E. 2568 (DERIVED from P).
- The rows for category 14.1.4 (flavoured drinks) of sucralose (300 mg/kg, conditions 127 and TH3), citric acid and potassium hydrogen carbonate (both quantum satis, no condition) were read and saved in the T9 record named above; they are not repeated here.
