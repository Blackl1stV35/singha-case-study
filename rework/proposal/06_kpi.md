# KPI section: Lime Cube (19 October 2026)

Status: desk-based. No bench, sensory, humidity or consumer test has run, and no outside party has been contacted.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the text was captured and read. UNVERIFIED: not captured, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, inputs and tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## KPI tree

Objective: repeat use of Lime Cube, a dissolvable pouch cube made into a lime drink on the spot. As a new consumption form, it is measured by trial and repeat, not only sales.

- **Trial:** units per pilot store per week; national listings.
- **Repeat:** share of first buyers who buy again.
- **Price:** shelf price realised; ex-factory share of shelf.
- **Contribution:** ex-factory revenue less variable cost, per cube.
- **Sell-through:** share of placed stock sold within 90 days.

## Kill criteria

1. **Sweetener:** no permitted sweetener for the product's classification (sucralose or steviol glycosides). No sale.
2. **Dose:** the format stops if R&D's 0.55-volume gate is not met; no threshold is stated until R&D sets it.
3. **Taste:** blind preference below 55% at pH 3.5 (ASSUMPTION). One acid and pH re-set, then stop.
4. **Pouch:** in-pack reaction above 0.5% at 35 °C (ASSUMPTION). No launch until the barrier changes.
5. **Potassium:** a reviewer rejects 317 mg per serve. No sale.
6. **Economics:** realised contribution below 3.76 THB per cube, or repeat below 15% on two cohort reads (ASSUMPTION). Stop scale-up.
