# Organization audit and evaluation of the group

Public sources only; no outside contact or plant access. Evidence reviewed to 20 October 2026.

## Tiers

Tiers are defined once, in Appendix A.

## Scope: the proposer and its holding company

- The proposer is Singha Corporation, and Boon Rawd Brewery is its holding company in the company's own description (see Structure). The About page puts the companies Singha Corporation manages at more than 150 (S, SOURCED; undated).
- Singha Corporation is the proposer, not a client. The group's data and factories are assumed unavailable, so group capability is a benchmark only.
- Lemon Soda is a lemon drink cube for domestic Thailand only, read as one component in the brief's sense (an unchecked reading, not an ingredient count); its ingredients are potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, sucralose, mannitol as binder and filler, and lemon flavour. Its carbonation is an untested desk model.
- Competitor overlap: the comparison product is the Singha Lemon Soda can, a 330 ml ready-to-drink can (S, SOURCED). A launch release reports it on sale in August 2020 (S); two later articles say late 2020 (S). On 20 October 2026 Lotus's Shop Online listed a single can at THB 17.00, and 24-can packs were listed at THB 330 (THB 13.75 a can) at 7-Eleven ALL Online and Lotus's and at THB 325 (THB 13.54 a can) at Makro PRO (S). Two 6-can packs were listed at THB 85 (THB 14.17 a can) at Lotus's and at THB 82 (THB 13.67 a can) at Makro PRO (S). The pack prices are SOURCED and the per-can prices are DERIVED. Across these six retail rows (one single can, three 24-can packs and two 6-can packs) the band is THB 13.54 to 17.00, with a median of THB 13.75. These are online prices; no store shelf price was read. The company's product page says the can has 0% sugar and no calories (COMPANY CLAIM). The can overlaps on flavour. Lemon Soda differs in form (a dissolvable cube made in the glass, one per pouch) and in pack from that can. Lemon Soda makes no sugar claim. The group's lime sparkling water (S, SOURCED) is a different product and is not the comparison product.

## Plant allocation: can an existing line take this cube?

Not shown; the evidence cannot answer yes or no. The cube needs a dry-solids route; no line type, capacity or utilisation is traceable, so no group volume is used. A yes needs line data, dry-powder and sealing capability, site certificates and the legal entity per site. For scale, 200,000 cubes a year need about 79 hours of pouch-line time at 42 pouches a minute (45 to 152 hours across the assumed ranges), about 2% of a 4,000-hour year (DERIVED on ASSUMPTION inputs). A co-packer's stated minimum run of 500,000 to 1,500,000 units per product and formula is the binding constraint, not line time (COMPANY CLAIM, one co-packer's page). The team's setting is a pilot batch at a dry-solids co-packer named at Gate 6; the operations director owns it, by M2.

## Limits

1. No Singha Corporation or Boon Rawd Brewery cost, supply, capacity, line or innovation-budget data was found; Singha Corporation has not been asked, and no outside party has been contacted.
2. The product class is a Gate 1 question. The proposed class is flavoured drink (category 14.1.4). Whether the cube is a mineral-salt beverage under clause 3 of Notification 195 is not settled, and no regulator guidance on how the "main ingredient" test is applied was found. If the cube were classed as a mineral-salt beverage, its potassium (about 960 mg per litre) would be about 4.9 times the ceiling of 195 mg per litre, and the formula would have to change. A post-March-2023 amendment inside a general notification is not excluded. Sale is blocked until Gates 1, 2, 7 and 9 pass.
3. Sucralose is checked at flavoured-drink level only (300 mg/kg); sub-category rows were not read, so the design (55 mg a serve, about 167 mg/kg) holds only if none sets a level below about 167 mg/kg.
4. The recommended dose, 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL, is the carbon dioxide the reaction releases at 0 °C. It is below solubility at every serve from 5 to 35 °C (0.592 volume at 35 °C), but how much stays dissolved at serve is not measured. The R&D lead restates the pass rule before the bench run (Gate 3), and the 0.55 volume dose is held after one rework.
5. Taste is untested; the pH 3.5 target is a working anchor; carbonation is unmeasured. The desk model estimates, none measured: dissolution at 25 °C of about 6 minutes stirred and about 11 minutes not stirred for central assumed inputs (3 to 22 and 5 to 74 minutes across the assumed ranges); dissolved carbon dioxide above the 0.44 g/L sparkling threshold for about 1.7 to 3.4 hours at 20 °C by surface loss alone if the full dose dissolves, or about 15 minutes if bubble formation is counted (a champagne proxy); a drink with less than 41% of the dose dissolved at serve starts below the threshold. The Bangkok April temperature for the 35 °C serve limit is UNVERIFIED; the sourced Bangkok figures are monthly means of 27.3 to 30.7 °C (P), which do not verify that limit.
6. Singha Corporation's registration form refers to three components; this is unchecked. The design uses one component in the brief's sense (one cube, one of the brief's axes), not one ingredient: its recipe lists potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, sucralose, mannitol as binder and filler, and lemon flavour.
7. One cube per pouch is an ASSUMPTION. The alcohol-cue ruling is a Gate 7 item owned by the brand and legal function; it is not assessed here.
8. Pouch, print and sucralose costs are assumptions or unverified listings. The reactant prices are China ex-works asking prices, not Thai landed prices. The binder price is an import-value proxy, and the lemon flavour is not priced. Pouch-machine speeds are makers' claims for other machines, not measured rates for this cube.
9. No store shelf price was read for the can; the prices quoted are online listings of 20 October 2026. The filling plant, the can's unit cost, the line capacity at the group's plants and the number of outlets that carry the can are not verified. The company that would book the cube's sales is not known.
