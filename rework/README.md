# rework: index of records

This folder holds the numbered records of the competition work. The index is current as of 19 October 2026. Records are kept even when they are retired. A banner at the top of each retired text record says so. Binary records (PNG, PDF, MP4) cannot carry a banner, so their status is recorded in this index.

Sourcing policy: [SOURCING_POLICY.md](SOURCING_POLICY.md). Primary sources come first; secondary sources are allowed and labelled; every figure is tiered and dated. Standing working rules for this repo: [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

## Folders

- `judge/`: the four judge-facing files: the hero render, the front and back label, and the 3D page. Nothing else goes here. It is the earlier set, held until gate G1 is cleared, so it does not yet show the merged design.
- `mockup/`: the team build for the cube. It holds the build scripts, the style tokens, the label and hero renders, the materials records, the GPU render evaluation, and the 3D page.
- `screen/`: analysis scripts with their results (kill tests, cost model, financial projection, persona model), the browser check, and its screenshots.
- `raw/`: raw workflow outputs, the literature register extract, and 12 pages of the NSO 2021 survey text used by the conversion study.
- `charts/`: chart images used in the records.
- `archive/leaf_wrapped_mockup/`: the retired leaf-wrapped mockup scripts, materials record, label artwork, and textures. Nothing in `mockup/` depends on them.
