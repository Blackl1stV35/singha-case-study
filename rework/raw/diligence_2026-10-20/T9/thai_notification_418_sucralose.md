# Thai food additive notification: sucralose in flavoured drinks (read 20 October 2026)

> **SUPERSEDED (added 20 October 2026 after verification).** Notification 418 Annexes 1 and 2 were repealed by Notification 468 B.E. 2568, in force from 12 December 2025 (see `thai_notification_468_repeal_and_sucralose.md`). This page is corroboration only: it shows that the earlier instrument set the same level. The authority for the product is Notification 468 (`rework/raw/moph_468_annex_flavoured_drinks_extract_2026-10-19.md`). Do not cite Notification 418 as current law.

Source: Ministry of Public Health Notification No. 418 B.E. 2563 (2020), "Criteria, conditions, methods of use and proportions of food additives (No. 2)", Annex 1, printed pages 534-536, Thai text.
URL: https://exfood.fda.moph.go.th/law/data/announ_moph/P418.pdf  (Thai FDA copy of the Royal Gazette notice, vol. 137 special part 237 Ngor, 9 October 2563)
Tier P (law). Read 20 October 2026: the PDF text layer drops Thai glyphs, so the pages were rendered to images and read visually (pages 535 and 537 of the PDF).

Entry "SUCRALOSE (ซูคราโลส), INS 955, other name 4,1',6'-trichlorogalactosucrose, function: sweetener (สารให้ความหวาน)".
Row read: category 14.1.4 "เครื่องดื่มแต่งกลิ่นรส" (flavoured drinks); maximum permitted 300 mg/kg (ปริมาณสูงสุดที่อนุญาต มก./กก.); footnotes 127 and TH3; year set 2559 (B.E.).
Same table: 14.1.3.1 to 14.1.3.4 (nectars) 300; 14.1.5 (coffee, tea, herbal infusions) 300; 14.2.7 (alcohol-free flavoured) 700.
Footnote 127 (page 628 of the annex, printed): "คำนวณในสภาพพร้อมบริโภค" = calculated in the ready-to-consume state (as consumed). Our translation.
Footnote TH3: excludes products under MoPH Notification No. 195 B.E. 2543 on mineral-salt beverages (not our product). Our translation. (SUPERSEDED reading, 20 October 2026 second pass: the current Notification 468 states TH3 as "for mineral-salt beverage products, must be used together with sugar of the type and amount set in the mineral-salt beverage notification" (Annex printed page 835). Both tie TH3 to the mineral-salt beverage notification, but the 468 text is a sugar-pairing duty and not an exclusion. See `thai_notification_468_rows_reconfirmed_2026-10-20.md`. The 418 text was read from the Thai FDA copy as a page image and was not re-read.)
So the limit is 300 mg/kg of the drink as consumed. Our drink: 33 mg in about 334 g is about 99 mg/kg (our arithmetic).
