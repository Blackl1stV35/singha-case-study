## Figures as stated on the page (WebFetch, quoted)

- "While 44% of consumers say they are trying to 'strictly reduce' their sugar consumption" (44%).
- "Only 27% of consumers say they are willing to switch to salt or sugar substitutes." (27%).
- "While 40% of Thais prefer drinks at full sweetness, 36% favor less sweet options and 24% are undecided."

## Limits stated by the page

- The page does not state the sample size, the survey base, or the fieldwork dates. The only year-like text is "2025" in an image file name, which is not a fieldwork date.
- Wording: the proposal records (rework/proposal/11) say "open to sugar or salt alternatives"; the page says "willing to switch to salt or sugar substitutes". The number is the same. The page wording is used here.

## Use in this study

The 27 percent is the sugar-reduction pool share. It is used as the pool's size share (a market pool, not a Singha customer count) with an ASSUMPTION range of 20 to 35 percent, because the base is not stated.
