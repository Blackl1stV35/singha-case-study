## What the notice says (our translation)

- Clause 1: the earlier notice dated 27 September B.E. 2564 is cancelled.
- Clause 2: for the tax calculation, the sugar to be measured in drinks is "total sugar" (Total Sugar) in the product, "both the sugar that occurs naturally and the sugar added in the production process" (ทั้งน้ำตาลที่มีอยู่ตามธรรมชาติและน้ำตาลที่เติมในกระบวนการผลิต).
- Clause 3 (1): for a powder, granule or concentrated drink that dissolves in water, where the label gives a mixing ratio of water to the product, sugar is measured on the volume of the drink made by mixing water with the product. Clause 3 (2) and (3) cover other mixing ratios. Where several preparations are given, the largest sugar result applies.
- Clause 5: the measurement method is an AOAC method (Official Methods of Analysis of AOAC International) or an equivalent method accredited to ISO/IEC 17025, for the test "Total Sugar".
- Clause 6 and 7: results from the Excise Department's own laboratory, or from a laboratory meeting the Department's GLP/EXD standard, are the basis for the tax; the Department may reject a result.
- Clause 8: the filing must state "sugar in grams per 100 millilitres of drink" and "net volume of the drink after mixing per serving".

## What it does not say

- The notice does not list which sugars or polyols count; it names the test "Total Sugar" only. Polyols such as mannitol and isomalt are not named. The Thai FDA nutrition FAQ defines total sugars as the sum of monosaccharides and disaccharides and does not name polyols (see thai_fda_faq_445_water_analysis_sugar.md). Whether a polyol is read into "Total Sugar" for excise is not stated (UNVERIFIED).
- Later notices after the 30 September B.E. 2565 entry were not searched beyond the regional office list. The Royal Gazette copy of a related notice (https://www.ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/DATA/PDF/2564/E/289/T_0016.PDF) returned HTTP 403 and was not read; the bot check was not bypassed.
