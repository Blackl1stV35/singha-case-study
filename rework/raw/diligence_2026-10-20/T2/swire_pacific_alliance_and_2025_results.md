## A. Swire Pacific press release, 9 February 2024

- URL: https://www.swirepacific.com/en/investor-relations/updates-to-our-shareholders/press-releases/thainamthip-and-swire-coca-cola-to-form-a-north-asean-bottling-alliance
- Date read: 20 October 2026. Release date: 9 February 2024.
- Parties: The Coca-Cola Company, ThaiNamthip Corporation Limited, Swire Coca-Cola Limited.
- Deal: definitive agreements to form a strategic bottling alliance in north ASEAN (Thailand, Laos, Vietnam, Cambodia). The Coca-Cola Company divests its minority ownership of ThaiNamthip to ThaiNamthip and Swire Coca-Cola. Subject to regulatory approval, Swire Coca-Cola takes a controlling stake in ThaiNamthip's bottling operations in Thailand and Laos. ThaiNamthip takes a minority stake in Swire Coca-Cola's bottling operations in Vietnam and Cambodia. Ownership percentages are not stated in the release.
- Facts in the release: Coca-Cola "celebrating 75 years in Thailand" in 2024; ThaiNamthip a bottling partner of The Coca-Cola Company since 1959; over 200 SKUs to more than 430,000 outlets; five bottling plants with combined capacity of more than 450 million unit cases a year; more than 50 sales offices, more than 50 warehouses, more than 1,500 delivery trucks, more than 8,000 associates. Swire Coca-Cola is described as the fifth largest Coca-Cola bottler in the world by volume.
- Note: the 450 million unit case capacity here (2024 release) differs from the 564 million unit case maximum capacity in the TNCC filing (2025). The filing is later and is used.
