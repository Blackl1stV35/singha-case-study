# Platform policies for alcohol and branded content, read 20 October 2026

Task T16. Route: WebSearch (lead), then WebFetch of the platform pages (model-written extracts with quoted sentences). Tier P for the platform's own page where the page was read; the reading is an extract and not a full read.

## A. TikTok for Business, alcohol advertising policy

- URL: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-alcohol?lang=en
- "Last updated: September 2025" (page footer). Date read 20 October 2026. Tier P (platform's own page, extract).
- Quoted: "To advertise, promote, sell, or feature branding of alcoholic products and beverages" (restricted); "Our advertising policy for alcohol is based on applicable local laws and regulations."
- Quoted: "Targeting or appealing to people below the legal drinking age"; "Featuring people below the age of 25".
- The page does not address alcohol-free or non-alcoholic products that carry an alcohol brand name. Thailand is not named on the page.
- Use in the record: a paid ad that shows the SINGHA name or lion may be reviewed under the alcohol branding rule. The "below 25" rule applies to alcohol ads and is applied here as a conservative cast rule.

## B. TikTok, branded content policy

- URL: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en
- Last-updated date not shown. Date read 20 October 2026. Tier P (platform's own page, extract).
- Quoted: "When posting Branded Content, you must enable the commercial content disclosure toggle." and "your content will be automatically labelled to indicate that it is Branded Content."
- Quoted: "You are responsible for ensuring that any Branded Content that you post complies with all applicable laws and regulations."
- Restricted industries section: the extract reads "Branded Content that promotes or reviews products or services from the following restricted industries is allowed" and then lists "Alcohol: Including alcoholic beverages (wine, beer, spirits, etc.), alcohol clubs and subscription services". The sentence is ambiguous in the extract (it reads as permission with restrictions). The team reads the full clause before the first post. For this proposal the rule applied is: no creator post may show alcohol, a drinking occasion or an alcohol brand.
- Non-alcoholic beverages are not named in the extract.
