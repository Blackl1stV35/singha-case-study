# Effervescent tablet hardness benchmarks in newtons (read 20 October 2026, second verification pass)

Purpose: the brief asked for a typical effervescent tablet hardness. Four sources were opened. Units are newtons (N) in all four. Read through WebFetch (an extraction of the page) unless the entry says the page was read as an image.

1. Jungbunzlauer, "CITROCOAT EP, a new effervescent compound for versatile applications" facts sheet (undated), https://www.jungbunzlauer.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/CITROCOAT-EP-a-new-effervescent-compound-for-versatile-applications.pdf . Tier S (supplier sheet, undated). Read as page images (the 4-page PDF saved by the fetch tool), page 4: "5 g tablets with a diameter of 25 mm were compressed with 70 kN compression force"; 2 percent polyethylene glycol (PEG) 4000 as lubricant in all trials; Figure 1 axis "Tablet hardness [N]" (0 to 180), tablet mass 5 g, n = 20. Bars: tablets with the hand mix (a dry blend of raw materials) 67.9 N at 70 kN; tablets with CITROCOAT EP 117.8 N at 70 kN; tablets with CITROCOAT EP 70.2 N at 44 kN (the compression force was cut by 37 percent, from 70 kN to 44 kN, to match the hand mix). Error bars are drawn; the sheet gives no standard deviation in text. Tablet thickness is not given.
2. Ansar A, Rahardjo B, Noor Z, Rochmadi R. "Optimasi Teknik Pembuatan Tablet Effervescent Sari Buah dengan Response Surface Method" (Optimisation of the processing technique of the fruit juice effervescent tablet), Jurnal Teknologi dan Industri Pangan 20(1):25, 2009, https://journal.ipb.ac.id/jtip/article/view/317 . Tier P (peer-reviewed; abstract only was read). The optimum hardness was 40.53 N at a compression force of 2339.8 N; the fastest solubility, 41.99 s, was at 1417.6 N; 1500 N, 350 mg/g citric acid and 500 mg/g sodium bicarbonate were suggested as a compromise. Tablet mass and diameter are not in the abstract.
3. Taymouri S, Mostafavi A, Javanmardi M. "Formulation and optimization of effervescent tablet containing bismuth sub-citrate", Journal of Reports in Pharmaceutical Sciences 8(2):236-244, 2019, DOI 10.4103/jrptps.JRPTPS_11_19, https://brieflands.com/articles/jrps-147387 . Tier P (peer-reviewed; abstract only was read). Optimised formulation (500 mg citric acid, 5 percent PEG 6000, 3 percent PVP K30, direct compression, bicarbonate to citric acid molar ratio 3): disintegration 95.33 +/- 1.15 s, friability 0.73 percent, pH 6.0, hardness 72.3 +/- 5.5 N. Mass, diameter and force are not in the abstract.
4. Roquette, "Effervescent tablet formulation of Vitamin C for direct compression", https://www.roquette.com/health-pharma/resources/effervescent-tablet . Tier S (company technical page that promotes its own maltitol; the extraction gave the page date as 19 October 2026, not independently confirmed). Tablet mass 2,400 mg, 20 mm flat punch, hardness 100 N, effervescence 2 min or less in 200 mL of water, friability "passed" with no number. Composition: ascorbic acid 250 mg, maltitol 995 mg, citric acid and sodium bicarbonate mix 900 mg, sucralose 8 mg, flavours 59 mg, sodium benzoate 100 mg, PEG 4000 90 mg (the extraction's mg values sum to 2,402 mg against the stated 2,400 mg). Thickness and force are not given.

## Reading (DERIVED)

- Reported hardness runs from 40.53 N to 117.8 N across the four sources, with 67.9, 70.2, 72.3 and 100 N in between. Two values are peer-reviewed abstracts (P) and two are supplier pages (S).
- These are breaking forces of round flat tablets (a diametral test). A 14 mm cube has no diameter, and a cube pressed in a square die has no sourced hardness benchmark, so the cube's hardness cannot be read from these values: stated limit.
- Mass per unit of punch face (our arithmetic, in `spec_revalidation_results.json`, key compaction): the cube carries 19.34 mg/mm2 of face against 3.50 (Pharmaceutics 2025), 7.64 (Roquette) and 10.19 (Jungbunzlauer), so it is 1.90 to 5.52 times the sourced tablets, which means a deeper powder fill and a larger pressure gradient.
- No density of a commercial effervescent tablet could be derived: none of the opened sources gives thickness. The 1.250 g/cm3 lead stays UNVERIFIED.

## Not used

- Amoxicillin effervescent tablets (Aslani and Sharifian, Adv Biomed Res 2014;3:209): hardness 40.66 to 56 and 60.6 to 74.6, no unit printed in the text read (Europe PMC XML on 20 October 2026: "No unit is given"; Table 2, 5 and 6 are images). Unit stays UNVERIFIED and the values are not used as newtons.
- Search snippets of effervescent tablet hardness (a roughly 40 N minimum for uncoated tablets; 15 to 25 N for fusion and direct compression tablets; 5 to 10 kp) were not opened and are leads only.
- RSC Pharmaceutics 2025 review "Recent advances in the technology of effervescent tablets" (pubs.rsc.org, DOI 10.1039/D4PM00229F): HTTP 403, not read.
