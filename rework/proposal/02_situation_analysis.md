## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the text was captured and read. UNVERIFIED: not captured, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, inputs and tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## What the situation means for the cube

1. **Price and cost are not settled.** The Singha can anchors at about 45 THB per litre (DERIVED from S6). A ladder of 15, 25 and 35 THB per 250 ml is an ASSUMPTION, not agreed. At 330 ml it gives about 20, 33 and 46 THB (DERIVED).

2. **Cost per cube is 3.84 THB** in a 12-cube carton at pH 3.5 (DERIVED). Reactants 0.118 are DERIVED from import values (PROXY); pouch 1.30, print 1.50 and processing 0.50 are ASSUMPTIONS, giving 3.42. The carton share of 0.42 is a PROXY, taken from a mailer-box price of 5 THB at ten or more units (S21).

3. **The pouch base, 1.30 THB, is an ASSUMPTION.** No Thai pouch price was found, so a non-Thai listing is used as labelled overseas evidence: USD 0.03 to 0.05 per piece (S14, UNVERIFIED). At 33.55 THB per USD (S13; SOURCED; the mid-rate of the 15 October 2026 buying and selling rates is DERIVED), that range is 1.01 to 1.68 THB. Across the THB 0.67 to 2.70 pouch band (USD 0.02 to 0.08, with the top rounded up to 2.70 as an ASSUMPTION), the cube costs 3.21 to 5.24 THB (DERIVED). One cube per pouch is inferred, not confirmed.

4. **The central idea is a new form**: a dissolvable lime cube that makes a carbonated lime drink from water. It makes no sugar claim. Fizz and taste are untested.

5. **Excise classification is open.** The 02.02 wording names other sweetening substances as well as sugar (S12). If the cube falls there, its nil sugar places it in the 0 THB per litre band; the 14% ad valorem applies if its value base does, and that base was not read.

6. **Acid and potassium at 0.55 volume.** The target pH of 3.5 is a working anchor (ASSUMPTION); taste is untested. Per 330 ml, potassium bicarbonate is 0.81 g, and citric acid is 0.52 g at stoichiometric balance or 2.10 g to reach pH 3.5 (DERIVED from S22). Potassium is 317 mg (DERIVED from the bicarbonate), 9.0% of the Thai reference of 3,500 mg per day (S20; composition only). No sourced acid range was found.

7. **Dose and solubility.** The recommended dose is 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 ml. Solubility at 1 atm is 0.655 volume at 30 °C and 0.592 at 35 °C (S15, S16; SOURCED). So 0.55 volume is within solubility at every modelled serve from 5 to 35 °C (DERIVED; about 0.04 volume below the 35 °C value). The desk model, with no bench test, shows no gas leaving at once and a surface-only fizz upper bound of about 96 minutes (DERIVED; threshold input unchecked); nucleation is not modelled. NIST rows for 0 to 25 °C (S17) differ from Lange by -2% to +11%; NIST values for 30 and 35 °C were not obtained. The gate is for R&D to define.

8. **Sweetener and permissions.** Sucralose is recommended at about 100 mg/kg (33 mg per 330 ml), a design level (ASSUMPTION). The sweetness target is an ASSUMPTION, untested. The Thai level for sucralose in category 14.1.4 is 300 mg/kg under condition 127, which applies to the drink as consumed (S18; SOURCED). Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis in that category (S18). Condition TH3 applies only to electrolyte drinks; the Thai FDA clarification extends a major-category permission to its sub-categories (S19). The classification (beverage, edible ice or electrolyte drink) is open and needs a regulatory view before sale.

9. **Demand is untested.** A purchase-intent model, not a consumer finding, puts men aged 25 to 34 ahead in about 94% of draws (DERIVED; ASSUMPTION inputs).

10. **Brand and sign-off.** The product name omits SINGHA. The SINGHA name and emblem are authorised for this proposal as the organiser's brand. Brand-owner, legal and alcohol-cue questions sit with Singha's department; the team has not checked them.
