Task T16. Route: WebFetch (model-written extract of the page, quoted sentences kept; not the raw page text). Tier S for DataReportal (Kepios analysis of platforms' own ad-planning tools); ETDA pages are the regulator's own site (P) but the figures are dated 2022.

## A. DataReportal, "Digital 2026: Thailand"

- URL: https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-thailand
- Date read: 20 October 2026. Page published 8 November 2025; data are "late 2025" (internet users as of October 2025). Tier S.
- Population 71.6 million (UN, October 2025); median age 40.6; urban 55.2 percent; female 51.4 percent, male 48.6 percent.
- Population by age (end 2025): 25 to 34 is 14.0 percent; 35 to 44 is 14.2 percent.
- Internet users 67.8 million (94.7 percent of population), October 2025; +2.4 million (+3.7 percent) from October 2024.
- Social media user identities 56.6 million, 79.1 percent of the population, October 2025; +7.5 million (+15.2 percent) from late 2024. 83.5 percent of internet users used at least one platform. 55.2 percent of identities female, 44.8 percent male (adults 18+).
- Facebook ad reach 51.5 million (71.9 percent of population; 75.9 percent of internet users); 51.0 percent female, 48.8 percent male.
- Instagram ad reach 20.6 million (28.7 percent of population); 56.5 percent female, 41.5 percent male.
- TikTok ad reach (users 18+): quote "TikTok had 56.6 million users aged 18 and above" in late 2025; "TikTok ads reached 96.3 percent of all adults aged 18 and above"; 83.5 percent of the internet user base; "55.2 percent of TikTok's adult ad audience in Thailand was female, whereas 44.8 percent was male"; potential reach +3.00 million (+5.6 percent) from end 2024 to late 2025 and -2.46 million (-4.2 percent) from July to October 2025. Note: the first extract of this page (an earlier fetch) mis-stated the TikTok line; the second, quote-only fetch is the one used. TikTok's tools only show audience data for users aged 18 and over.
- LINE: 56 million monthly active users (78.2 percent of population; 82.6 percent of internet users); 46 percent female, 54 percent male; unchanged from October 2024.
- YouTube ad reach 47.2 million (65.9 percent of population); 51.5 percent female, 48.5 percent male.
- X 13.3 million; Messenger 36.5 million.
- The page has no time-spent, influencer or e-commerce figures.
- Limit: ad-planning "potential reach" counts accounts, not unique people, and overlaps across platforms.

## B. ETDA, Thailand Internet User Behavior (page for survey year 2565 / 2022)

- URL: https://www.etda.or.th/th/บริการของเรา/สถิติและข้อมูล/... (percent-encoded path; the page title reads "การสำรวจพฤติกรรมผู้ใช้อินเทอร์เน็ตในประเทศไทย (Thailand Internet User Behavior) 2565"). Reached from https://www.etda.or.th/th/Our-Service/statistics-and-information.aspx
- Date read: 20 October 2026. Page covers fieldwork April to July 2022 (dated, not current). Tier P (regulator's own survey), figures are 2022.
