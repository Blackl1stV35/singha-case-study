Notes on the scan:
- The page boundaries are as the fetch tool's extraction of each page reported them. Page 15 ends with unnumbered B.E. 2554 rows (probiotics, residue, radioactive contamination, edible salt) that fall between No. 332 and No. 344. The coverage of numbers 333 to 473 is complete within the extraction.

## 4. What this page settles

- On 20 October 2026 the Thai FDA's list marks Notification No. 195 B.E. 2543 and its "(No. 2)" notification of B.E. 2554 as in force (tier P for the list's status field, read through page extraction).
- Negative finding (tier P, from page extraction): no amending or repealing notification on electrolyte or mineral-salt beverages, and no title naming No. 195 or No. 332, appears among the list rows for Nos. 333 to 473, read on list pages 1 to 15 on 20 October 2026 in two passes.
- It does not settle whether a general notification issued after the March 2023 compilation changed clause 3, 4(1) or 7 by name inside its body under a title that does not name No. 195. That is a stated limit. The route that would settle it is the Royal Gazette index (not reachable here: the Gazette site returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool on 20 October 2026, for the home page and for the PDF paths) or the Gate 1 Thai opinion.
