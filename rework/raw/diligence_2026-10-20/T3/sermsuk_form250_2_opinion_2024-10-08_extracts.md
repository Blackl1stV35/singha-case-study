Revenue from Sales and service

focused on increasing sales channel as well as developing a comprehensive and efficient product

Cost of goods sold and service 
For the years 2021 - 2023 and the first 9-month period of 2023 and 2024, the Business has costs of 
goods sold and service of THB 6,643.94 million, THB 7,823.54 million, THB 8,869.46 million, THB 
6,661.82 million, and THB 7,001.62 million, calculated as gross profit margins of 30.93%, 28.26%, 
28.30%, 27.79% and 29.47%, respectively. The Business’ cost of goods sold consists of the cost of 
leavening agent, other raw materials, direct labor, packaging, bottling, and related tax expenses, etc. 
 
In 2022, the Business’ costs of goods sold and service increased by THB 1,179.60 million, or 17.75%, 
compared to the previous year which increased in greater proportion than the increase in income due 
to the rise in raw material and packaging costs. However, the Business still focuses on cost management 
of raw materials and packaging to be efficient, including increasing production efficiency to alleviate the 
impact. In 2023 the Business’ costs of goods sold and cost of service increased by THB 1,045.92 million 
or 13.37% compared to the previous year, which is consistent with the increase in income. Additionally, 
the Business has focused on increasing production efficiency and managing the procurement of raw 
materials and packaging to obtain appropriate and better prices. Lastly, for the first 9-month period of 
2024, the Business’ cost of goods sold and services increased by THB 339.80 million or 5.10%, comparing 
to the same period of the previous year. This increased in lower proportion to sales due to continuous 
improvement in production efficiency with a positive impact on packaging costs compared to the same 
period of last year. 
 
Distribution costs 
For the years 2021 - 2023 and the first 9-month period of 2023 and 2024, the Business had distribution 
costs of THB 2,351.04 million, THB 2,559.92 million, THB 2,875.19 million, THB 2,152.11 million, and 
THB 2,279.85 million, respectively. The Business’ distribution costs consist of marketing, commission, 
shipping and fuel costs and expenses related to employees, etc. 
 
In 2022, the Business’ distribution costs increased by THB 208.88 million or 8.88% compared to the 
previous year, due to the increased in transportation costs according to income growth and the impact 
of rising fuel prices, with the continuous organization of promotional activities to help drive sales and 
investment in the "Crystal" drinking water brand so that it can continue to be a sustainable leader in 
the drinking water market.  
 
In 2023, the Business’ distribution costs increased by THB 315. 27 million, or 12.32%, compared to the 
previous year. This is due to the increase in transportation costs which is in line with the growth of 
income and the impact of increased fuel prices in contrast to the previous year. The Business also 
invested in the drinking water brand "Crystal" continuously by launching a new campaign “Think Clear, 
Drink Crystal” to enhance the drinking water market and strengthen product positions through the 
creation of unique packaging labels. It comes with music from the sound of the water, which receives 
a good response from consumers.  
 
Additionally, for the first 9-month period of 2024, the Business’ distribution costs increased by THB 
127.75 million, or 5.94%, compared to the same period of the previous year. This is due to an increase 
in transportation costs which in line with higher sales including sales promotion and marketing
