## Big C
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_C
- "Big C is now operated and managed under Berli Jucker Public Company Limited, part of TCC Group." Berli Jucker "owns a majority stake in Big C in Thailand, Laos, and Cambodia". In February 2016 Groupe Casino agreed to sell its stake to Charoen Sirivadhanabhakdi (TCC Group acquiring Casino's 58.6% stake). Store counts as printed: 2019, 153 hypermarkets, 63 Big C markets and 1,018 Mini Big C stores; 138 Big C Supercenters in Thailand in 2022. No private-label statement beyond a 1993 note.

## Tops
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tops_Supermarket
- "operated under the name Tops Supermarket in Thailand by Central Food Retail, a subsidiary of Central Retail Corporation"; "It is the largest supermarket chain in Thailand and operates 235 stores nationwide." Central Group is privately owned by the Chirathivat family (Wikipedia, Central Group page, 'still privately owned by the Chirathivat family as of 2025'). No private-label statement.

## Makro and Lotus's
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charoen_Pokphand (the CP Axtra address redirected here)
- "Siam Makro Public Company Limited officially changed its name to CP Axtra Public Company Limited (CPAXT)" in 2023; CP Axtra operates "under the brands Makro and Lotus's"; "over 305 large-format stores across Thailand" as of 2025. The page makes no mention of the aro brand and has no private-label statement; the company's own investor page lists "expanding private label products" as a strategy (see cp_axtra_major_shareholders_and_strategy_2026.md).
