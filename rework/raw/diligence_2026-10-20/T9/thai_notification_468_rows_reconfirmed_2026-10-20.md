## What this settles and what it does not

- Settled: the 300 mg/kg limit for sucralose in category 14.1.4, with conditions 127 and TH3, is confirmed from the primary text on 20 October 2026. Citric acid and potassium bicarbonate are quantum satis in category 14.1.4 with no condition.
- The earlier saved reading of the repealed Notification 418 (`thai_notification_418_sucralose.md`: TH3 "excludes products under Notification 195") differs in direction from the 468 text above (a sugar-pairing duty on mineral-salt beverages). Both tie TH3 to the mineral-salt beverage notification (No. 195). The 468 text is the current authority.
- Meaning for the cube drink: if it is classed as a flavoured drink (14.1.4) and not as a mineral-salt beverage, TH3 does not apply. If it were classed as a mineral-salt beverage, sucralose would have to be used with sugar as Notification 195 sets (sugar at least 2 or 4 percent), and the drink has no sugar. The cube drink also fails the Notification 195 composition (see `thai_notification_195_mineral_salt_beverage.md`), so it could not be sold as a mineral-salt beverage in any case. The Thai FDA's own classification of the product is not known: stated limit.
- Not settled: whether any later notification amended Notification 468; the Annex was read as published in the file above.
