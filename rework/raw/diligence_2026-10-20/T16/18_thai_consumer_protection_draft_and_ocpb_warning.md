## A. Draft amendment to the Consumer Protection Act B.E. 2522 (paid promoters)

- URL: https://www.tilleke.com/insights/thai-consumer-protection-overhaul-to-address-digital-commerce-and-influencer-marketing
- Publication date: 17 September 2026. Date read 20 October 2026. Tier S (law-firm commentary; the OCPB draft itself not opened).
- Quoted: the OCPB "released for public comment a draft bill to amend the Consumer Protection Act B.E. 2522 (1979)"; "The public consultation period is open until October 10, 2026."
- Quoted: a new definition of "hired advertising person for selling goods or services", covering "influencers, content creators, live streamers, affiliate marketers, and virtual online media operators" who "receive monetary compensation or other benefits for advertising goods or services".
- Quoted: "Disclosure is required when the business owner employs the advertiser," "pays or provides other benefits for the advertisement, or provides free or discounted products or services."
- Quoted: "criminal liability is extended explicitly to advertising media owners (including digital platforms hosting advertisements)".
- Quoted: "The draft is still subject to further revisions before proceeding through the legislative process." Not enacted as of the article.
- Status for the proposal: a draft that would require paid creators to disclose the commercial link. Not law yet on the evidence read. The campaign labels every paid post whether or not the draft is enacted.

## B. OCPB warning to influencers and online sellers, 29 October 2025

- URL: https://aseanconsumer.org/read-news-ocpb-warns-influencers-and-online-sellers-against-misleading-advertising
- Date of warning: 29 October 2025. Date read 20 October 2026. Tier S (consumer-group news summary; the OCPB notice itself not opened).
- Named claims: "overwhelming sales" advertising and "sold out" advertising, which the OCPB treats as possibly "false or exaggerated advertising".
- Legal basis named: Consumer Protection Act B.E. 2522 (no section numbers given). Ads may not contain untrue, inflated or deceptive content.
- Threshold named: online sellers or influencers earning THB 1.8 million or more a year from selling or promoting products need a Direct Marketing Business licence from the OCPB. No penalty amounts given.
- Use in the record: campaign scripts must not use "sold out", "best seller" or sales-volume claims unless a verified figure exists; creator income is not in the campaign scope.
