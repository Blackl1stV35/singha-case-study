## Reading (team translation)

- "ตลาดเครื่องดื่มอัดก๊าซแบบกระป๋อง" = the canned gas-charged (carbonated) drinks market, valued at THB 13,172 million. No year, no source and no retail-or-manufacturer statement is attached. The sentence also does not say which year the THB 1,357 million belongs to (it follows the 2021 volume of 28 million litres, so 2021 is the natural reading, but it is not stated).
- Scope is cans only. This figure is a different definition from the 66,000 and 76,900 figures (all pack types) and must not be set against them as a share.
