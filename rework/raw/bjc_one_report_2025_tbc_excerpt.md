It locates at WHA industrial estate, Saraburi, Nong Khae District, Saraburi province. Currently, there are 4 production lines for cans..."

Quoted, page 6 (2022 events):
- "Construction began on a new aluminum can production line with a production capacity of 400 million cans or 200 million bottles per year, increasing production capacity by approximately 10% from the current level. Commercial operations are planned to commence in September 2023."

Page 13: Thai Beverage Can Limited, BJC share 50.00% (share table).

Derived (DERIVED, our arithmetic on a stated approximation): if 400 million cans a year is about 10% of current capacity, current capacity is about 4,000 million cans a year. The report does not state a total. The 10% is rounded in the source, so the derived figure is approximate.

Conflicts to resolve: the 2022 statement says a new line was planned for September 2023, while the 2025 report says 4 lines plus a fifth (hybrid) line added in 2025. The report does not give a per-line table, so the line count is not reconciled. The 2012 figure of 1.8 to 2.2 billion cans (secondary, a news report) is older and does not match the derived figure.
