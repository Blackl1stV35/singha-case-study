## Reading for record 74

- The 0 baht tier for 0 to 6 g of sugar is the same in the B.E. 2560 extract and in the press report of phase 4 (S). The cube's sugar is nil by the Thai nutrition definition; even if the whole 0.80 g of mannitol were counted as sugar, it is 0.24 g per 100 mL, far inside the tier.
- The position after phase 4 is a stated limit: the primary decree was not opened and a later schedule was not searched.
