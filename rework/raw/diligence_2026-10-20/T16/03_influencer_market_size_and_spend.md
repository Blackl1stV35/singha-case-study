## 1. MI Group (Media Intelligence Group) spend figures

- Brand Buffet, 16 December 2025, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2025/12/media-industry-spending-2026-forcast/ . Presenter: MI Group Chairman and CEO Phophat Ruangdetwoarachai. Tracked ad spend 2025 (January to November actual) THB 78,272 million (-0.35 percent); full-year 2025 forecast THB 85,727 million (-0.06 percent); 2026 forecast THB 88,271 million (+0.64 percent) in this article. Untracked influencer spend: 2025 THB 23,100 million; 2026 THB 24,332 million. Influencer share of the all-in total: 15.5 percent in 2025 and 21.3 percent in 2026. Inconsistencies inside the page: 85,727 + 23,100 = 108,827 but the page prints 108,900; 88,271 + 24,332 = 112,603 but the page prints 110,603.
- Marketing Oops, 5 January 2026, https://www.marketingoops.com/reports/industry-insight/media-industry-outlook-2026/ . Same producer; 2026 tracked forecast printed as THB 86,271 million (+0.64 percent), which differs from the Brand Buffet figure of THB 88,271 million for the same forecast. Influencer marketing budget "over THB 23,000 to 24,000 million" sits on digital platforms. Real spend 2025 THB 108,900 million.
- Positioning, 20 December 2025, https://www.positioningmag.com/insight/110011 . Same speaker; untracked influencer money "about THB 22,000 to 23,000 million"; average influencer share of brand marketing budgets 21 percent; digital 51 percent of tracked spend in 2025.
- Spread to report: untracked influencer spend 2025 THB 22,000 to 23,100 million (three readings of one producer's estimate; secondary).
