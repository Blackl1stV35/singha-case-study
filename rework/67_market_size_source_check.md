The 12-month windows in the NielsenIQ citations are the only dated labels. The two NielsenIQ values and their growth rates are consistent with a rising run: 70,772 / 1.09 = 64,928 for the 12 months to November 2023, and 66,000 for the 12 months to January 2024 is 1.7 percent above that (DERIVED, script key `niq_window_consistency`). That suggests, but does not prove, one series. The 62,000 and 60,000 values name no source and are not counted in that series.

The 68,000 and the 67,000. ThaiBev's 70,772 less 4 percent is about 67,900, close to the 68,000 that Thansettakij labels 2024 and to the 67,000 that Bangkokbiznews gives for 2025. The 68,000 may therefore be a 2025 level, or a 12 month window to August 2025 (the colour-soda figure of 17,300 from Bangkokbiznews is for September 2024 to August 2025; the 17,391 from Thansettakij has no period printed, and is 25.6 percent of 68,000). The pages do not say. This is a possible reading, kept as a stated limit. It is not presented as a second 2024 value.

## 4. Reconciliation: one defensible range

- Best-supported single value: THB 70,772 million, 12 months to November 2024, +9 percent (NielsenIQ, as printed by Thansettakij on 14 January 2025). Tier S. It is the only value with a named data house, a dated window and a growth rate together.
- Two NielsenIQ 12-month values, not one series: about THB 66,000 million (February 2023 to January 2024, mostly 2023) and THB 70,772 million (December 2023 to November 2024), both S via press. They are ten months apart and are shown as two windows, not as the same year. Definition: national Thai carbonated soft drinks, cola and non-cola, regular and zero sugar. Level (retail or manufacturer): not stated by any source, so it is a stated limit.
- Spread of all press and bank statements, not a range: THB 60,000 million (AJ Thai's Big Cola section, 3 April 2025, no year, no source) to THB 76,900 million (KResearch 2025 estimate, a forecast). The endpoints have different windows and one is a forecast, so this is a spread of statements. Other statements inside it are 62,000 (2023, no source named, Thairath and GotoManager) and 66,000 (no year in the Thansettakij intro of 3 April 2025).
- 2025: a range of values, not one figure, and the direction is disputed. The press and compilation values are THB 67,000 million (Bangkokbiznews of 13 October 2025, no source named, labelled 2025, window not stated) to THB 70,000 million (Marketeer compilation of April 2026, which includes brand forecasts), both S. The 68,000 that ThaiBev labels 2024 may be a 2025 level (section 2). KResearch estimates THB 76,900 million for 2025 (S, estimate or forecast), above that range and not confirmed by any bottler data. Direction: a ThaiBev executive reports a fall of 4 percent (S, measure not stated), and Bangkokbiznews reports a fall of 8 percent for January to August 2025 (S, no source named); ThaiNamthip's first-half 2025 carbonated revenue fell 16.5 percent (P as filed, DERIVED); Hadthip's infographic gives a national NARTD fall of 2.2 percent (S, all NARTD). The Marketeer compilation (+6.1 percent on its own labels), KResearch's +2.0 percent forecast (mapping inferred) and Euromonitor volume (+2.1 percent, carbonated and soda) point up. No source settles the direction, so the deck states that the sources disagree.
- Not reconcilable from the sources: the 2025 direction in full, the 13,172 canned figure, the 60,000, 62,000 and 68,000 statements for 2024 and 2025 that name no source, and whether the Coca-Cola bottlers' revenue sits inside the NielsenIQ value (section 3).

## 5. Lemon-lime or clear sub-segment

No size for lemon-lime or clear carbonated drinks alone was found for 2024 or 2025. What exists:

- Best-supported non-cola size: THB 17,300 million for September 2024 to August 2025, NielsenIQ as printed by Bangkokbiznews (13 October 2025, S). Thansettakij (13 October 2025) prints 17,391 million for the colour-soda segment with no data house and no period (S, unattributed). The volume wording in both articles ("growth slowed, down from the previous year by 6%") is ambiguous: it may mean that the growth rate slowed by six points, or that volume fell 6 percent. The record does not read it as a fall. As the 2023 to 2025 articles use the term, "colour soda" appears to mean the whole non-cola segment (GotoManager, 29 October 2023, prints cola 75 percent and colour 25 percent), whereas Positioningmag in 2020 lists clear separately. The 17,300 is 25.8 percent of the 67,000 value, which has no stated window, so that ratio is mixed and is not a share (DERIVED). The ratio to 70,772 is dropped because the windows differ. Thansettakij prints "30 percent of the total market", which fits neither 68,000 nor 67,000, so the source is inconsistent with itself.
- Clear soda in 2019: Positioningmag, 26 March 2020, gives a 2019 market of THB 56,000 million split into black 71 percent, colour 23 percent and uncoloured (clear) 6 percent, with no source printed for the split (S). The article says the market grew almost 12 percent from about 50,900 million; its own figures give 10.0 percent, so neither growth figure is used. Six percent of 56,000 is about THB 3,360 million (DERIVED). It is six years old and is a lead only: lemon-lime is a part of clear soda, but the article does not say so, and no current clear value was found. Page: `positioningmag_73001_2020-03-26_type_split.md`.
- The Marketeer table of 17 April 2026 lists "colour or clear" at 18 percent of 2024 value and zero sugar at 15 percent as a separate line, so 18 percent of 66,000 (about 11,900) is only the regular non-cola line and a floor for the segment, not its size.
- Dark-cola shares disagree. AJ Thai says dark cola is about 85 percent of the carbonated market (Thansettakij, 3 April 2025, no measure). Pepsi's narration says 72 percent is a forecast for 2025 beside a 38 percent colour share, which add to 110 percent (Thansettakij, 26 March 2025, `thansettakij_623088_2025-03-26_pepsi.md`). Neither is used to size a segment.
- ThaiNamthip's filing puts Fanta and Sprite together at 66.0 percent of the "other flavoured carbonated drinks" sub-category in 2024 (a Frost & Sullivan statistic quoted in the filing), with no value for the sub-category. Euromonitor has a lemonade/lime sub-category, paywalled and not opened.
- Closed as a stated limit.
