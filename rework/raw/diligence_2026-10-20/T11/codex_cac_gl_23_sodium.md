## Table of conditions for nutrient content claims (page 3), sodium rows, "not more than"

| Claim | Condition |
|---|---|
| Low | 0.12 g per 100 g |
| Very low | 0.04 g per 100 g |
| Free | 0.005 g per 100 g |

The table gives sodium on a per 100 g basis only. It has no separate per 100 mL line for sodium (other nutrients such as energy and sugars do have liquid lines).
In milligrams: free 5 mg, very low 40 mg, low 120 mg, each per 100 g (our unit conversion).

## Related clauses

- Clause 5.2: a claim that a food is free of salt can be made if the food meets the conditions for free of sodium in the Table.
- Clause 4.1: the only nutrition claims permitted relate to energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat and components thereof, fibre, sodium, and vitamins and minerals with Nutrient Reference Values.
- Clause 7.2, non-addition of sodium salts ("no added salt"): may be made if (a) the food contains no added sodium salts, including but not limited to sodium chloride and sodium tripolyphosphate; (b) the food contains no ingredients that contain added sodium salts; (c) the food contains no ingredients that contain sodium salts used to substitute for added salt, for example seaweed. Footnote 5: competent authorities may permit the addition for technological purposes of sodium salts other than sodium chloride as long as the final food would still comply with the conditions for "low in sodium".
- Clause 6 (comparative claims): based on the food as sold, taking into account further preparation required for consumption according to the instructions on the label.
