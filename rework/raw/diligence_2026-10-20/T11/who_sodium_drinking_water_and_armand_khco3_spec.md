- Verbatim: "Sodium salts (e.g. sodium chloride) are found in virtually all food (the main source of daily exposure) and drinking-water. Although concentrations of sodium in potable water are typically less than 20 mg/l, they can greatly exceed this in some countries."
- Verbatim: "It should be noted that some water softeners can add significantly to the sodium content of drinking-water."
