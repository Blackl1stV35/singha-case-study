## Facts

- Each tablet contains potassium bicarbonate 2.5 g and citric acid 2.1 g, which in solution provides 25 mEq (978 mg) potassium.
- Also contains FD&C Yellow No. 6 (and lake), microcrystalline cellulose, mineral oil, orange flavor, saccharin and talc; the tablets are described as sugar-free.
- The label states no sodium content in any section read.

## Use here

A market analogue only: a potassium bicarbonate and citric acid effervescent tablet that carries insoluble excipients (microcrystalline cellulose, talc, mineral oil). That is the type of binder and filler we reject for a clear lemon drink. The label gives no sodium analysis, so it is not a sodium source. It is a drug product, not a food.
