## 3. What this changes in the record

1. Scope of Singha Corporation: the English About page (`raw/diligence_2026-10-20/T13/01`) says it manages the operations of more than 150 group companies. This Thai profile page says it was founded to oversee the beverage business. The two company pages differ, and both are company claims. The record shows both.
2. Plant count: the Thai profile page gives 3 breweries and 9 soda and drinking-water plants. The plant page gives eight manufacturing companies and the history page gives nine. These are further unreconciled counts.
3. Distribution network: the Thai profile page gives 300 agents. The history page gives 12,000 subcontractors or dealers. Unreconciled.
4. Marketing: the Thai profile page says Singha Corporation manages and markets the products. The press article (efinancethai, 12 March 2026) and the Boon Rawd Trading release (13 March 2026) place marketing and distribution in Boon Rawd Trading. Unreconciled; the record uses Boon Rawd Trading for the cube's marketing because the release is a primary source.
