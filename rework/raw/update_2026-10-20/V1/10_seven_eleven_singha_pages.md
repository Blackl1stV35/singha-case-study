## 10e. Reading

- Three opened 7-Eleven ALL Online Singha pages list the Lemon, Red, Pink, Ume, Watermelon, Lemon Cream and Sparkling Lime lines and no Yuzu. The Yuzu product URL that the search index holds returns 404.
- This leans toward Yuzu not being offered on 7-Eleven ALL Online now, but a 404 can have other causes and the pages carry no date. It is a reading, not a proof.
- Earlier (file 03) the 7-Eleven search page showed the same "technical problem" notice. It sits on the shop pages too, so the 7-Eleven pages were read with that notice present.
