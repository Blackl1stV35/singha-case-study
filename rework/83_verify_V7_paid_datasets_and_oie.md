## 1. Answer

Partly. The paid datasets cannot be read for 2024 or 2025. The OIE statistics file can be reached but was not opened, because downloading it needs the user's permission. The OIE home page returns HTTP 400, but the OIE statistics page (https://www.oie.go.th/view/1/statistics/TH-TH) loads. It links an Excel file and a CSV file of per-product statistics for January 2021 to August 2026, and the page says they cover sales value, sales volume, production volume, finished-goods inventory and capacity. Whether the file has a row for carbonated drinks is not known. The OIE sales value series is read here only through a bank note (LH Bank, 26 March 2026) that names OIE as its source, so that series is tier S and not P.

- NielsenIQ. The NielsenIQ Thailand dataset itself was not reachable. Three NielsenIQ-attributed Thai values were found in trade press and were opened in this pass. They are about THB 66,000 million for the 12 months to January 2024, THB 70,772 million for the 12 months to November 2024, and about THB 17.3 billion of colour soda for September 2024 to August 2025. No NielsenIQ-attributed total for calendar 2025 was found.
- 2025 carbonated totals found. Three press pages print a total for 2025, and a fourth prints one with no year. The totals are about THB 67,000 million (Bangkokbiznews, no source named), about THB 76,900 million (Thansettakij 638312, 8 September 2025, Kasikorn Research Center credited), THB 76,900 million as a 2025 forecast (Marketeer, 9 May 2025, no source credited) and "over THB 66,000 million" (Thansettakij 623088, 26 March 2025, no year and no source). - Euromonitor. Both Thailand pages opened (Carbonates in Thailand; Soft Drinks in Thailand, both November 2025). Each shows table titles for value from 2020 to 2025, but no figure is visible without purchase. The level and currency are not stated on the visible page.
- Statista. The monthly carbonated sales-value series (June 2024 to June 2026, billion baht) and the production-volume series are opened and masked. The Statista Market Insights outlook page for carbonated soft drinks in Thailand is also masked. No 2024 or 2025 figure is visible. That outlook page states that its at-home revenue is valued at retail selling prices including sales and consumption taxes, but the figure itself is hidden.
- OIE. The OIE home page returned HTTP 400 in every attempt, and the built-in browser was denied. The statistics page and the industrial index page (https://www.oie.go.th/view/1/mpi/TH-TH) loaded. Three OIE PDF reports were opened: the October 2025 brief, the 2024 annual report and the 2025 annual report. They give beverage production indices and volume statements, not a baht sales value for carbonated soft drinks.
- Sales value through the LH Bank note (tier S, OIE named). Total beverage sales value was THB 448.1 billion in 2567 (2024) and THB 433.3 billion in 2568 (2025). No absolute baht value for carbonated drinks from OIE was found, and none is derived here.

Verdict: closed as limits. What can be read was read and is recorded. What cannot be read (the paid datasets, and the OIE file without the user's permission to download it) is stated in section 5 with its route in section 6. Nothing further can be done at the desk without the user's decision.

## 4. Conflicts and spreads (recorded, not resolved)

1. 2024 carbonated total. NielsenIQ-attributed 70,772 (F1, 12 months to November 2024) against 68,000 (F4, unsourced, labelled 2024). Spread 2,772 million baht, about 3.9 percent of the NIQ value. The windows and sources differ, so the two cannot be reconciled from the pages read.
2. 2025 carbonated total. The 2025 totals found are 67,000 (F5, Bangkokbiznews, no source named), 76,900 (F23, Thansettakij 638312, 8 September 2025, Kasikorn Research Center credited) and 76,900 as a forecast (F24, Marketeer, 9 May 2025, no source credited). A fourth page, Thansettakij 623088 (26 March 2025), prints "over 66,000" (F25) with no year and no source, so it is listed and not counted as a 2025 total. None of the pages names NielsenIQ for its total, and no NielsenIQ-attributed 2025 total was found. The spread of the dated totals is 9,900 million baht, which is 14.8 percent of 67,000 and 12.9 percent of 76,900 (F29). With the undated 66,000 included it is 10,900 million baht, 16.5 percent of 66,000. The primary and the data house for each value are not available, so this stays a range. All of these values are kept unused. The colour soda figure (F3) has a different window (September 2024 to August 2025) and shows 25.8 percent of 67,000, while the article prints 30 percent.
3. Scope of the OIE value series against the Krungsri value. F6 gives THB 433.3 billion for all beverages in 2025. Krungsri's USD 30,398.3 million converts to about THB 999.5 billion at its own rate (F20). The ratio is about 2.3. The non-alcoholic ratio is about 1.7 (F21 against F7). Neither source states the level, the scope or the method, so this stays a spread.
4. OIE LH Bank split sentence. The LH Bank note's sentence on the split of total sales value (page 2 text, read from the image) assigns about 55.9 percent to non-alcoholic drinks. Its own absolute values give 44.1 percent for non-alcoholic (14,986.28 of 33,994.42) and 55.9 percent for alcoholic. This record uses the absolute values only.
5. Scope of OIE non-alcoholic value (F7) against the NielsenIQ carbonated value (F1). These cover different scopes (all non-alcoholic drinks against carbonated drinks only), and the carbonated share of 39.5 percent has no printed year. No reconciliation is attempted in this record.
6. Krungsri 2023 figures. Inside the Krungsri 2025-2027 extract, 10.6 billion litres of non-alcoholic plus 2.97 billion litres of alcoholic does not add to the stated 13.02 billion litres. This is recorded in the raw file and was not corrected.
7. The two values of 76,900 are not two confirmations. Thansettakij 638312 credits Kasikorn Research Center. Marketeer names no source. Both pages print an 11.6 percent growth figure for 2565 to 2567, but Marketeer does not say where it took the value from. The Kasikorn Research Center page of 30 May 2025 does not print 76,900. The share check is consistent (F28 against F27, 34 percent), which supports reading F23 as a Kasikorn-based figure but does not prove it. Set against the NielsenIQ-attributed 2024 value (70,772, F1), the 67,000 is 5.3 percent lower and the 76,900 is 8.7 percent higher (DERIVED), and Kasikorn Research Center forecasts carbonated growth of only 2.0 percent for 2025. So even the sign of the 2025 change depends on which 2025 total is read. The bases are probably different. This is not reconciled.
8. Non-alcoholic totals and carbonated share across sources. Kasikorn Research Center forecasts THB 226,000 million of non-alcoholic sales for 2025 (F26, business sales to B2B and direct B2C customers). The OIE value read through LH Bank is THB 202.5 billion for 2568 (F7), and the Krungsri value converted at its own rate is about THB 347.1 billion (F21). The KResearch total is about 1.12 times the OIE value (DERIVED). The carbonated share is 34 percent in the KResearch forecast (F27) and 39.5 percent in the LH Bank chart (F11, year unconfirmed), a gap of 5.5 points. The scopes, years and bases differ, and no reconciliation is attempted.
