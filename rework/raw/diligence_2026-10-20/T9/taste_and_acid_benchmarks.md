# Sweet-sour and acid-level benchmarks (read 20 October 2026)

1. Foods 2020 (open access), "Taste Interactions between Sweetness of Sucrose and Sourness of Citric and Tartaric Acid among Chinese and Danish Consumers", https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7600934/ . Tier P (peer-reviewed). Samples: sucrose 2.50% w/v; citric acid 0.140% w/v (Table 1; no pH given).
