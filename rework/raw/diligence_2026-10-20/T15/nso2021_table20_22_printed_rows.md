Title note: the Table 20 title in the table of contents and on the printed table (p. 41) reads "frequency of consumption of non-alcoholic bottled drinks" (Thai, our translation) and does not include the word "sweet". The section narrative on printed p. 41 (PDF page 60, section 3.4.8.7) describes the same table as "consumption of non-alcoholic bottled drinks that are sweet-tasting" (our translation of "การบริโภคเครื่องดื่มไม่มีแอลกอฮอล์บรรจุขวดที่มีรสหวาน"), and Table 2 (printed p. 12) calls the group "high-risk sweet-tasting packaged beverages". T15 reads Table 20 as the sweet bottled drink series on the strength of the narrative and Table 2; the title alone does not say so. The 5-6 days plus every day columns reproduce the Table 2 figures (see `nso2021_table2_sweet_beverage_5plus_days_by_sex.md`).

## Use in this study

- The printed everyday shares (18.2 for 25 to 44 and 16.0 for 45 to 59) give the per-person sweet-drink habit for the age bands (sex is not split in these tables).
