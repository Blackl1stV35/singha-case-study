# Finance and project valuation: Lemon Soda (20 October 2026)

This is a model output for one product, the Lemon Soda (Singha Lemon Soda Cube), sold in domestic Thailand. It is a desk model on assumed volumes and costs. It is not a forecast and not investment advice.

## Tiers

Tiers are defined once, in Appendix A.

## Scope: the project, not the group

- One product in domestic Thailand. No group cash flow is modelled; reported profit serves only as a scale denominator in the shareholder value section.
- A dissolvable Lemon Soda cube, unpriced, with no measured price response.
- Composition per 330 mL: potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g (about 317 mg of potassium); citric acid 2.10 g (0.52 g reacts with the bicarbonate; 1.58 g is free acid that sets the pH); sucralose 55 mg (about 167 mg per kg of drink, equal in sweetness to about 10 g of sugar per 100 mL; the sweetness target is an ASSUMPTION); a binder and filler of 0.80 g mannitol, tableting grade (INS 421), with isomalt as the fallback (ASSUMPTION for the amount; range 0.5 to 1.2 g); and natural lemon flavour 0.05 g (ASSUMPTION; range 0.02 to 0.10 g; carrier not yet specified). No sodium carbonate is used. Reactants are 2.97 g. The central cube mass is 3.79 g (range 3.46 to 4.24 g; DERIVED on ASSUMPTION inputs; not weighed), a 14 mm cube at 1.38 g/cm3. The listed amounts sum to 3.82 g, and the weighed cube settles the difference before print (Gate 9). The packed unit with a 1.5 g pouch (ASSUMPTION) is 5.29 g.
- Sodium: no sodium salt is in the recipe, and sodium from the recipe is 0 to about 2 mg a serve (DERIVED). Water adds its own: 0 to about 7 mg a serve at the WHO typical level of 20 mg per litre (P), or about 33 to 68 mg at the 100 to 200 mg per litre reported for Thai tap water (S, a news report; not measured). No sodium-free or low-sodium claim is made.
- Dose: 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL at a working pH of 3.5, an anchor only; the desk model gives a pH of about 3.4 to 3.5, and R&D restates the dose gate before the run (section 10, Gate 3). The pH rests on citric acid pKa values at zero ionic strength (S, UNVERIFIED: read from rendered images only).
- Exchange rate: 33.55 baht per US dollar, Bank of Thailand midpoint, 15 October 2026 (P, SOURCED; Source 7).
- Solubility is 0.655 volume at 30 C and 0.592 at 35 C (S, SOURCED; Source 10), so the dose is within solubility from 5 to 35 C (DERIVED). That range is a working range; the Bangkok climate figures are UNVERIFIED. This is a pure-water check, not a fizz test. A 0.44 g/L sparkling threshold (S, abstract only; Source 12) is a soft-drink proxy and sets no dose. Desk-model estimates, none measured: dissolution takes about 6 minutes stirred and about 11 minutes not stirred at 25 C for a central set of assumed inputs (3 to 22 and 5 to 74 minutes across the assumed ranges); if the full dose dissolves at serve, surface loss alone keeps dissolved carbon dioxide above the threshold for about 1.7 to 3.4 hours at 20 C, and bubble formation could cut that to about 15 minutes (champagne proxy); a drink with less than 41% of the dose dissolved at serve starts below the threshold. Taste is untested.
- Sweetener and permissions: sucralose at about 167 mg/kg is below the 300 mg/kg permitted in category 14.1.4 (flavoured drinks), condition 127, applied to the drink as ready to drink (P, SOURCED; Source 8). Only the flavoured-drink rows of the annex were read. Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis there, and mannitol is listed for flavoured drinks at quantum satis (P; Source 8); the lemon flavour is not yet checked against this rule. Condition TH3 covers electrolyte drinks only and is not claimed for the cube.
- Method: five years, no terminal value, and a 10% discount rate shown at 8% and 12% (ASSUMPTION: the Bank of Thailand policy rate of 1.00% from the decision of 26 August 2026, S, Source 17, plus a 9-point premium). The valuation of record is after excise and after tax: a contribution of THB 3.94 a cube after excise, 20% corporate income tax (Revenue Department, S, Source 15), and the setup outlay depreciated over five years. The earlier basis, before excise and before tax at a planning price of THB 9.00 (contribution THB 4.91), is shown beside it and labelled.

## Volume scenarios

No domestic volume evidence was found. Scenarios are ASSUMPTION: 50,000 (low), 200,000 (base) and 1,000,000 (high) cubes a year, with year 1 at 50%; 500,000 is also shown in the NPV table. The base of 200,000 cubes equals 3 units per store per week at an assumed 1,282 store-equivalents (ASSUMPTION, back-solved from the base volume), which is the pilot target.

Line time is not the constraint. Pre-made pouch machines list about 15 to 80 pouches a minute as maker maxima on a dry-cycle basis (P; COMPANY CLAIM; Source 24), and 60 a minute is a mid-range maker figure. At the planning point of 42 pouches a minute (60 a minute at 70% efficiency; both ASSUMPTIONS), 200,000 cubes need about 79 hours of pouch-line time (45 to 152 hours), about 2% of a 4,000-hour year (DERIVED). A co-packer's stated minimum run of 500,000 to 1,500,000 units per product and formula (COMPANY CLAIM; Source 23) is the binding constraint: at 200,000 cubes a year it is 2.5 to 7.5 years of base volume (DERIVED).

## Shareholder value

The proposer is Singha Corporation. The desk value below is for the project, not the group. No group cash flow is modelled; reported profit serves only as a scale denominator.

At 200,000 cubes a year and a fixed cost of THB 500,000 a year, the base-case NPV is THB 0.17 million after excise and tax (DERIVED on ASSUMPTION inputs: five years, a 10% discount rate, no terminal value, 20% tax; the year 0 and year 1 conventions are those in this file). On the earlier basis, before excise and before tax, it is THB 0.88 million. The after-tax value is about 0.005% of Singha Corporation's 2025 net profit of about THB 3,085 million (S; press, 23 May 2026; Source 13), and the earlier-basis figure is about 0.03% (DERIVED). The project breaks even at 184,325 cubes a year with no cannibalisation (148,076 before excise; 146,230 before excise and before tax) (DERIVED). At 500,000 cubes a year the NPV is about THB 3.32 million, and at 1,000,000 cubes it is THB 8.59 million (DERIVED).

At 200,000 cubes a year the NPV stays positive up to a fixed cost of about THB 0.54 million a year (THB 0.68 million before excise and before tax) (DERIVED). It is THB 1.14 million at THB 250,000 a year, and minus THB 1.78 million at THB 1,000,000 a year (DERIVED).

The sign of the base value is not robust. With no cannibalisation the NPV is zero at a contribution of about THB 3.64 a cube, only THB 0.31 (8%) below THB 3.94 (DERIVED). The unpriced binder and flavour, the assumed pouch, print and processing costs and the excise base can each move the contribution by that much or more. At the top of the mannitol import range (THB 1.27 a cube) the contribution falls to about THB 2.67 and the NPV to about minus THB 0.51 million (DERIVED).

The value depends on how many cube buyers would otherwise buy a can. Each such buyer costs the can's contribution of about THB 4.13 after excise and before aluminium (DERIVED on PROXY and ASSUMPTION inputs; an upper bound on the loss, because the aluminium cost the can would have carried is not netted). The NPV is zero when about 7.5% of cube buyers would otherwise buy a can, and negative above that (DERIVED). The figure is approximate: the aluminium not netted would raise it, and the binder and flavour not yet priced in the cube would lower it. At 25% the NPV is minus THB 0.39 million, at 50% minus THB 0.94 million and at 100% minus THB 2.04 million (DERIVED). The review level is 7%, just under the break-even: if 7% or more of cube buyers say they would otherwise have bought a can, the model is rerun with the measured share, volume and contribution before any scale. A stated switcher is counted as losing one can for each cube bought (ASSUMPTION); if a switcher loses half a can per cube the break-even stated share is 15.0%, and at one and a half cans it is 5.0%.

Stated uncertainty. Across the stated input ranges (200,000 draws of every input with a fixed seed; conditional on the ranges, not market data) the chance of a positive NPV is 0.26 after excise and tax (0.40 before excise and tax; 0.58 on a simpler structure). The test fixed in advance needs 0.95, and no basis reaches it. Every 90% range includes zero. The expected NPV after excise and tax is minus THB 0.88 million, and its 90% range is minus THB 3.40 to plus THB 1.59 million. The expected NPV is zero at about 335,000 cubes a year across the ranges. Fixed cost and shelf price explain most of the spread. In the model, the pilot's demand read is worth THB 0.03 to 0.25 million, and pilot spend is not sized from sources.

The pilot reads demand after 13 weeks of trading plus two weeks and can stop then (the 13 weeks of trading plus two weeks is an ASSUMPTION; section 10, Phases 4 and 5). The stop or scale call is made against the stop and scale table fixed in advance (owner role: executive sponsor). The pilot budget is the team's setting, with its basis below: committed THB 24,686, conditional THB 60,000 and a ceiling of THB 84,686 (owner role: finance lead).

How value reaches shareholders. The profit is earned by the group company that sells the cube. The model books it in Singha Corporation, and the booking entity is a stated limit. If Boon Rawd Brewery held all of Singha Corporation and Singha Corporation paid out all of the profit (both ASSUMPTIONS; neither the holding nor the payout was found in the sources read), Boon Rawd Brewery would receive about THB 151,000 a year in years 2 to 5, or about THB 136,000 if only half of each dividend is excluded from its taxable income. A dividend from another Thai company is half excluded, or fully excluded where the recipient holds at least 25% of the distributing company's capital and meets a holding-period rule (Revenue Department; S, read through a page summary; Source 15); those conditions were not verified. Year 1 is a loss year and pays no dividend. Each 10% stake in Boon Rawd Brewery, if it passes the dividend through, would receive about THB 15,000 a year before holder tax.

Scale. Incremental profit equal to 0.1% of Singha Corporation's 2025 net profit needs about 1.1 million cubes a year with no cannibalisation (0.9 million before excise). Singha Corporation's 2025 net profit is a single-company press figure; consolidated group profit was not found. Boon Rawd Brewery's 2022 net profit was THB 5,793 million (Thaiger, 18 April 2025, S; Source 18), so against a wider base the shares would be smaller.

At the base volume the cube is a learning option, not a value driver. Its value is small on every basis and can be zero or negative. The figures are a desk estimate, not a forecast of profit, dividends or share price.

## Pilot budget

The pilot budget is the team's setting (owner role: finance lead). Its basis is the arithmetic below, in THB.

- Committed THB 24,686: creator kits 442 (nine creators, 12 cubes each, at THB 4.09 a cube); a commission cap of 4,000 (500 cartons at THB 8.00 a carton sold through a creator code); Route A 18,000; and a 10% contingency of 2,244. - The cap is THB 27.38 per first purchase at a 30% repeat (THB 24.22 at 15%). It is the contribution floor of THB 21.06 a carton (THB 3.51 a cube before excise, times 6) times 1.3 cartons per buyer at a 30% repeat (1.15 at 15%; ASSUMPTION).

Commission-only terms are an ASSUMPTION; no creator has been approached.
