## 13c. Reading

- The Singha Online shop holds a product page for a Yuzu tray, with the title "สิงห์เลมอนโซดา รสส้มยูซุ 1 ถาด". The page body does not load in the fetch tool.
- "ไม่พบสินค้า" appears on the Ume page too. Ume is a flavour that Makro and 7-Eleven show with a price and no out-of-stock label on 20 October 2026 (files 07 and 10). Because the same string sits on both pages, it does not separate Yuzu from a flavour that other shops show with a price. A likely reading is that the fetch tool receives a page shell whose product data loads by script, but that is an inference and the pages do not say it. The string is not read as "Yuzu is off sale".
- Other results from the same search (titles only, not opened): Singha Online pages for Lemon Soda 4x6P, Ume, Lemon Cream, Pink, and merchandise (Hawaii shirt, sunglasses, tank top).
- The search tool's summary also repeated claims from the Singha brand pages (sucralose and acesulfame potassium, 30.7 mg vitamin C, 70 mg sodium, halal). Those were not read from a page by me and are not used.
