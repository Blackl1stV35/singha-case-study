## 4. Regulation, brand safety and what a creator may say

### 4.1 Thai Food Act B.E. 2522 and the FDA food advertising criteria B.E. 2569 (the Thai texts and the FDA criteria are P; the penalty figures are S, from an unofficial English translation)

Raw: [10](raw/diligence_2026-10-20/T16/10_thai_food_act_and_fda_advertising_criteria.md).

- Section 40: false or deceptive advertising of quality, usefulness or indication of a food is prohibited (penalty, section 70 per the English unofficial translation: up to 3 years and up to THB 30,000, or both).
- Section 41: advertising benefit, quality or property of a food for commercial benefit needs prior approval of the sound, picture, film or text (penalty, section 71: up to THB 5,000). Section 42: the licensing authority may order an advertisement stopped (section 72 penalties).
- FDA announcement on food advertising criteria B.E. 2569, Royal Gazette 27 July 2026 (volume 143, special issue 182 ง, pages 60 to 67), effective 28 July 2026, cancelling the 2021 announcement. Read from page images; the wording is paraphrased; a Thai legal review is the settling step (Gate 7).
  - Article 3: "advertising" covers any act that lets the public see or learn of a message about food for commercial benefit. A creator's paid post about a food or drink is advertising.
  - Article 4: a message must not be unfair or likely to harm society; in particular: a message that recommends or praises a food's benefits by medical or public health personnel, or by someone who implies that status (item 6); a message that disparages another maker's product (item 7).
  - Article 5: no false or exaggerated benefit claims; no claims of cure, relief, treatment or prevention of disease; no effect on body structure or function; no skin or beauty claims; no weight-reduction or body-shaping claims; no academic or statistical reference that the FDA has not assessed for a health claim (item 8).
  - Article 7: benefit, quality or property claims that are not on the approved label, health claims (MoPH notification No. 447, B.E. 2566) and any advertisement showing a benefit need prior submission.
  - Annex 1 bars claims such as "detox", "boost immunity", "fast results" and "FDA certified".
  - Annex 2 (no prior approval, with conditions): price and quantity; a price comparison that does not name a competitor; where it is sold; product details (name, formula, how to consume); taste, aroma and texture words; statements of liking; awards with year; production standard certification without the FDA symbol; "no added" statements only if true to the approved formula; "limited edition" for a time-limited run. Item 18 allows "ซ่า" and similar fizz words for carbonated or cooling products; the deck holds fizz words until a measured test exists, which is stricter than the criteria.
- Consequence: a creator's paid post is advertising. The cube's approved text supports composition, use and taste statements only. Any benefit, health, sugar, sodium, hydration, electrolyte, potassium-benefit or fizz claim is outside the no-approval route.

### 4.2 Alcohol Control Act B.E. 2551, section 32 (S, Wikisource transcription; Gazette original not opened)

Raw: [11](raw/diligence_2026-10-20/T16/11_alcohol_control_act_s32_and_platform_policies.md).

- Section 32 prohibits advertising an alcoholic beverage, or showing its name or mark, in a way that boasts of its properties or induces people to drink, directly or indirectly. Producers of alcoholic beverages may advertise only information and socially constructive knowledge, and may not show the product or its package except the beverage symbols or the producer's corporate logo as set by ministerial regulation.
- Section 3 includes marketing communications (including sponsorship of events and sales promotion) in "advertising".
- Penalty per the Wikisource copy (section 43): up to 1 year, up to THB 500,000, or both, and a daily fine while the breach continues.
- Reading, not a legal opinion: the cube uses the SINGHA name and lion, which are marks of the group's alcoholic beverages. Whether a creator post that shows the name or lion on a non-alcoholic product is a display of an alcoholic beverage's mark is a question for the Gate 7 ruling. The record does not settle it, and the team does not hold the alcohol-cue check (Gate G1 placed it with Singha's department).

### 4.5 What a creator may and may not say about this product

Built from deck Appendix A (label rules), section 4.1 to 4.4 above, and the Thai survey wording rules in section 7.

- May say: the composition as stated on the approved label (potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, sucralose, lemon flavour; no sodium carbonate); "Drop one cube into 330 ml of water and stir"; "Serving: one cube made up to 330 ml"; "single-serve pouch"; "contains an intensive sweetener: sucralose" once the Gate 1 opinion confirms the sweetener; taste words (sour, refreshing, lemony); the creator's own experience of taste in plain words.

### 5.6 Cost per first purchase and break-even against the contribution floor

Base inputs: cost per click THB 11.5 (PROXY); click-to-first-purchase 1.0 percent (ASSUMPTION); contribution per carton THB 29.46 at base (THB 4.91 per cube, DERIVED) and THB 21.06 at the floor (THB 3.51 per cube, repo floor).

- Cost build (base, per cube): pouch 1.30, print 1.50, processing 0.50, reactants 0.118 (rework/proposal/06 line 105), and carton share 0.67 for the six-cube carton. The total is THB 4.088, shown as 4.09. These are ASSUMPTIONS or DERIVED on repo inputs. Binder (0.80 g) and lemon flavour (0.05 g) are not priced anywhere in the repo, so the base contribution of THB 4.91 is an upper bound. Headroom to the floor is THB 1.40 per cube before commission and THB 0.07 per cube after the THB 8.00 commission (3.58 less 3.51). Any unpriced cost above THB 0.07 per cube after commission fails the 3.51 test. The earlier script note listed 3.97 because it omitted the reactants line; the total is unchanged.
- Paid social (Route B, own listing, no commission): cost per first purchase = THB 11.5 / 0.01 = **THB 1,150** (DERIVED). One carton at the base contribution (THB 29.46) recovers about 2.6 percent of it. To recover THB 1,150 at the base contribution, a buyer must buy about 39 cartons (234 cubes at THB 4.91; DERIVED). **Flag: paid social does not recover its first-purchase cost in one carton or inside the 13 weeks at the desk inputs.**
- The best PROXY in the record (click-to-first-purchase 2.02 percent, a global Triple Whale food and beverage conversion rate) still gives THB 569 per first purchase (DERIVED), about 21 times the cap of THB 27.38 in section 5.7. Break-even click-to-purchase at Phoenix's cost per click would need to be 42.0 percent against the cap (DERIVED).
- Creator programme, option B (commission THB 8.00 per carton, kits only): 6.4 buyers over the 13 weeks at base, spend THB 1,048 (kits THB 981.60 and commission THB 66.56), cost per first purchase about **THB 164** (DERIVED), contribution after commission THB 178.55, net result about minus THB 803 (DERIVED). Break-even needs about 35 buyers, that is about 352,000 views at the base rates, against 64,000 planned (DERIVED; 5.5 times the plan).
- Creator programme, option A (adds flat fees of THB 87,207): 8.9 buyers, spend THB 88,330, cost per first purchase about THB 9,925 (DERIVED), net result about minus THB 87,989. Break-even needs about 3,163 buyers and about 31.6 million views at the base rates, against 89,000 planned (DERIVED). The flat fees, not the kits, drive the loss.
- Commission and the floor: the carton commission is a selling cost outside the THB 4.91. At base cost, a commission of THB 8.00 per carton leaves THB 3.58 per cube, above the THB 3.51 floor. The highest commission that keeps base contribution at the floor is THB 8.40 per carton (DERIVED). At the floor cost (THB 3.51 with no commission) there is no room for any commission. The option B commission is therefore a ceiling of THB 8.00 per carton, and any higher rate fails the floor test. The planning value of 10 percent of the THB 90 carton (THB 9.00 per carton, used in the earlier model and in the TikTok Shop note in raw 22) leaves THB 3.41 per cube at base, below the floor, so it fails the test. The THB 8.00 setting is a team choice below the ceiling; no source sets it. Headroom to the floor is THB 1.40 per cube before commission and THB 0.07 per cube after the THB 8.00 commission (section 5.6 cost build).
- Monte Carlo (20,000 draws, seed 20261020, ASSUMPTION distributions as stated in the results file): probability that option B's 13-week net result is at or above zero is 0.7 percent; for option A it is 0.0 percent; the probability that a paid first purchase meets the cap is 0.0 percent. That 0.0 is structural, not a finding: the lowest paid cost per first purchase in any draw is THB 569 (THB 11.5 divided by the 2.02 percent best PROXY conversion), and the highest cap in any draw is THB 29.48 (at a 40 percent repeat). The paid cost per click is drawn between THB 11.5 (mode) and THB 27.50 (global PROXY). Median creator cost per first purchase: option A THB 9,060 (10th to 90th percentile THB 3,766 to 24,495); median paid cost per first purchase THB 1,617 (THB 957 to 3,429). These are model readings, not forecasts.

What this means for the proposal: creator flat fees for micro and mid posts do not pay back in 13 weeks at any plausible view or purchase rate. The proposal therefore funds creators by kit and commission (option B) and treats any flat fee as a separate brand-building decision outside the pilot's contribution test. Paid social is not a scale route on these inputs and is gated.

### 5.7 Route B threshold proposed by the team (the cap)

- Cap on cost per first purchase = THB 21.06 (floor contribution per carton) x (1 + measured repeat cartons, with one extra carton per repeat buyer). At a 30 percent repeat the cap is THB 27.38; at a 15 percent repeat it is THB 24.22 (DERIVED from the repo floor, ASSUMPTION on repeat).
- Setting: the learning tranche starts only when the week 4 non-paid read is at least 1.0 percent click-to-first-purchase. Tranche rule (week 8, cohort 1): the scale tranche is released only if the measured cost per first purchase is at or below the cap at the measured repeat, and the measured contribution per cube after commission is at least THB 3.51. Scaling rule (week 12): a route is scaled beyond the released tranche only if repeat is at least 15 percent in both cohorts and the measured contribution per cube after commission is at least THB 3.51. Basis: the repo's 3.51 floor (proposal/10 and 06) and the proposal's repeat targets. Tier: the cap is DERIVED on the repo floor of THB 3.51 per cube and depends on the repeat, which is an ASSUMPTION (30 percent base, 15 percent floor); the 1.0 percent week 4 gate is an ASSUMPTION (the desk base in proposal/10 Step 1). The cap therefore moves with the repeat assumption.
- Expected result at base inputs: the learning tranche (THB 20,000) buys about 1,739 paid clicks and about 17 first purchases (DERIVED). Those clicks give a paid-traffic conversion read within about half a percentage point at 95 percent confidence (DERIVED on a 1 percent rate). That is the only reason to spend it. At base inputs the scale tranche is not released.

### 5.13 Stop rule

Stop the creator programme, or the affected creator, at once if any of these occur:

2. A controversy about a creator that the team cannot clear in writing within one week.
3. A platform or regulator order to stop a post.

Stop Route B (paid social) if any of these occur:
4. The week 4 non-paid click-to-first-purchase rate is below 1.0 percent: no learning tranche.
5. The week 8 measured cost per first purchase is above the cap (THB 27.38 at a 30 percent repeat, THB 24.22 at a 15 percent repeat): no scale tranche.

Stop or hold scaling of the whole route if any of these occur:
6. Measured contribution per cube after commission falls below THB 3.51 on the sold cartons, or the commission rises above THB 8.40 per carton.

8. Sell-through below 60 percent of placed stock within 90 days (repo rule).

## 6. Summary of what the pilot can and cannot show

- It can show whether a non-paid creator wave lifts listing or code purchases from a baseline, whether the creator buyers repeat at 8 and 12 weeks, and whether the commission structure holds the floor.
- It cannot show a brand-lift effect with confidence. The brand-lift set is optional and unfunded.
- It cannot show a paid-media return. At desk inputs paid social does not return its first-purchase cost.
- It cannot show creator-level audience age or gender. Platform-level male shares are the only audience split used.

## 9. Proposal text (judge-facing, for the proposal file)

**Creator campaign.** We propose a 13-week creator pilot that tests whether short videos from food, drink and lifestyle creators bring first purchases of the Singha Lemon Soda Cube, and whether those buyers repeat. The pilot works with 20 creators in the nano tier (1,000 to 10,000 followers) and the micro tier (10,000 to 100,000 followers), each posting two videos. We do not use macro or mega creators in the pilot. The benchmark lower bound for one TikTok macro post is about THB 26,833, which is above the whole committed creator budget of THB 5,480 (proxy). Per-post fee levels in these tiers are taken from a global planning benchmark and a Thai travel estimate, both of which are proxies; no published Thai rate card was found.

**Content.** The videos show one cube dropped into 330 mL of water and stirred. The glass-based formats, namely the make-it-in-the-glass demonstration, a blind taste test of the finished drink against plain water (reported in taste words only) and the non-alcoholic home-bar drink, start only after the Gate 7 review has cleared glassware in writing. Until then the videos show the cube dissolving in a plain, unbranded container that is not a drinking glass. Creators use approved composition and serving wording. The videos make no health, sugar, sodium, hydration, electrolyte, fizz or sales-volume claim, and show no alcohol, drinking occasion or alcohol brand.

**Compliance.** Every paid post carries a paid-partnership label, and every creator's public record is checked before the first post. Scripts are reviewed against the Thai Food Act and the Thai food advertising criteria B.E. 2569 before use. The Alcohol Control Act section 32 and the Gate 7 legal review govern any use of the SINGHA name or the lion; no creator post uses either until that review is complete in writing. Each platform's branded-content rule is read on the day of posting. Creator contracts require takedown within 24 hours on request.

**Budget.** The committed creator and paid-social budget on start is THB 5,480: creator kits (THB 982), a commission cap of THB 4,000 at THB 8.00 per carton sold through a creator code, and a 10 percent contingency. Route A in-store sampling is not costed, because the store count is not yet set. A further THB 60,000 is conditional. A learning tranche of THB 20,000 for paid social is released after the week 4 read if the non-paid click-to-first-purchase rate is at least 1.0 percent (a planning assumption). The scale tranche of THB 40,000 is released under the tranche rule: at week 8, the measured cost per first purchase is at or below the cap of THB 27.38 at the measured repeat, and the measured contribution is at least THB 3.51 per cube after commission. Scaling rule: a route is scaled beyond the released tranche only if, at week 12, repeat is at least 15 percent in both cohorts and the measured contribution is at least THB 3.51 per cube after commission. The cap is the floor contribution of THB 21.06 per carton (THB 3.51 per cube, the proposal's contribution floor) times 1.3 cartons per buyer at a 30 percent repeat, which is a planning assumption; at a 15 percent repeat the cap is THB 24.22. The ceiling is THB 65,480. A version with flat fees for micro and mid-tier posts (THB 87,207 of fees) commits about THB 101,460 with kits, commission and a 10 percent contingency, and has a ceiling of about THB 161,460. At the desk inputs it does not recover those fees within the pilot.

**What the numbers say.** At desk inputs a paid click costs THB 11.5 (proxy). At a 1.0 percent click-to-first-purchase rate (a planning assumption), a first purchase costs about THB 1,150, and one carton contributes THB 21 to 29. Paid social does not recover its first-purchase cost in the pilot: at base contribution a first purchase needs about 39 cartons to pay back, and about 55 cartons at the floor. The proposal therefore treats paid social as a capped learning test, not as a scale route. The creator programme is also a trial: at desk inputs it does not return its kit and commission cost within 13 weeks. The contribution after commission stays at or above the floor of THB 3.51 per cube only while the commission stays at or below THB 8.40 per carton. The planning value of THB 9.00 per carton (10 percent of the carton price) would fail it, so the proposal sets THB 8.00. The base contribution is an upper bound, because binder and lemon flavour are not priced; after the THB 8.00 commission the headroom to the floor is THB 0.07 per cube.

**Measurement and stop rule.** Each creator has a unique code. Listing clicks are tracked with tagged links. The pilot reads click-to-first-purchase in weeks 1 to 4 on non-paid traffic, repeat at weeks 8 and 12, and contribution per cube after commission. Creator activity stops at once after a compliance breach that is not fixed within 24 hours, or after a controversy about a creator that cannot be cleared within a week. Scaling rule: a route is scaled beyond the released tranche only if, at week 12, repeat is at least 15 percent in both cohorts and the measured contribution is at least THB 3.51 per cube after commission. The tranche rule in the budget paragraph governs the release of the scale tranche at week 8.

**Limits.** No creator, agency or panel has been contacted. No creator has been named for the pilot or approached, and creators will be chosen by criteria after approval. No TikTok or Instagram creator has yet been checked against those criteria. Creator views per post are planning assumptions, not measured Thai figures. Creator audience age and gender could not be read; platform-level male shares are used. No Thai beverage benchmark for cost per click, cost per acquisition or engagement was found. The Thai Consumer Protection Act amendment on paid promoters is a draft and was not enacted on the evidence read. The Gate 7 legal review and the brand-owner sign-off on the SINGHA name and lion are not held by the team. Route A in-store sampling is not costed because the store count is not set. Binder and lemon flavour are not priced, so the contribution figures are upper bounds. No Meta or TikTok Thailand platform release was read.
