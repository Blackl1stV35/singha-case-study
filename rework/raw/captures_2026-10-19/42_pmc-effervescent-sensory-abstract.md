## Extracted facts (abstract only)

- Title: Sensory Evaluation of Effervescent Nutritional Supplements: Identification and Characterisation of Off-Tastes.
- Journal: Molecules.
- Year: 2025. Published 13 February 2025; volume 30, issue 4, article 854.
- Sample: four effervescent nutritional supplements with different compositions were profiled. The abstract does not give a sample size of tasters.
- Settings: profiling was done in three settings: smell before tasting, odour during tasting, and aftertaste with lingering aroma.
- Main unpleasant sensations in solution: bitterness, astringency and a metallic quality.
- Flavourings: mixed effects, sometimes improving taste and sometimes making it worse.
- Fruity notes: some raised perceived sweetness and sourness and partly masked the unpleasant sensations. The effect varied by product and by setting, because perception changes as the product is processed in the mouth.
- Authors' suggestion: these perceptual interactions could support masking strategies that reduce reliance on additives.
- Figures: the abstract gives no numerical values, units or sample size.
