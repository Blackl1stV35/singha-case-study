## What the abstract states (our paraphrase around short fragments)

- Setting: soft drink purchases in Japan across convenience stores, pharmacy, supermarkets, vending machines and electronic commerce; five design points to avoid overstating the weather effect, among them TV advertising and the time of day.
- Method: a fixed effects model with purchase amount as the dependent variable.
- Result on temperature: "mean temperature has a positive effect on purchasing behavior regardless of the time zone".
- Other weather terms: rainfall has a negative effect at night; sunlight, humidity, wind speed and atmospheric pressure show no significant effect.
- The abstract gives NO magnitude, NO unit, NO sample size and NO period.
