# BioNumbers: diffusion coefficient of HCO3- in water (two records)

- URLs opened: https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=110406 and https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=102626
- Date read: 2026-10-20
- Tier: S (curated database of values quoted from books and papers; the originals were not opened). Status: SOURCED (secondary).

## Extracted facts
- BNID 110406: HCO3- at 25 C in freshwater, 1.09e-9 m2/s. Reference: P. G. Falkowski and J. A. Raven, Aquatic Photosynthesis, Princeton University Press 2007, p. 160, table 5.2.
- BNID 102626: 1150 um2/s = 1.15e-9 m2/s (cyanobacteria context; temperature and medium not stated). Reference: Fridlyand L, Kaplan A, Reinhold L, Biosystems 37(3), 1996, p. 232, table 1.

## Use
- With Zeebe 2011 (1.10e-9 from the MD fit) and the conductivity-based 1.19e-9 printed in Zeebe 2011, the HCO3- bracket in rework/71 is 1.0 to 1.2e-9 m2/s with the central at 1.10e-9. The earlier bracket of 1.5 to 2.1e-9 was an unsupported assumption and is withdrawn.
