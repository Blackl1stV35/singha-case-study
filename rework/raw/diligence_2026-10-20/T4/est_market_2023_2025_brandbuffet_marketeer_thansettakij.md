# est share, pack mix and market data, three trade-press pages (T4)

Tier S for all three (trade press). Date read: 20 October 2026, through the fetch tool; Thai quotations as returned; English translation is ours.

## 1. Brand Buffet, 17 October 2023
- URL: https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2023/10/est-strategy-to-gen-z-new-branding/
- est: "ครองส่วนแบ่งทางการตลาดอยู่ 9.1%", up from 7.1% before the rebrand. New Asian-style recipe; "91% ของผู้ร่วมโหวตกว่า 100,000 คน ชื่นชอบสูตรใหม่" (91% of over 100,000 voters liked the new recipe). Pack design "est Cola Awesome Monster" with 8 monster characters on PET bottles and cans.
- Distribution: "จากปัจจุบันที่ขยายไปแล้วกว่า 1 แสนร้านค้า" (expanded to over 100,000 outlets so far) and to push further into grocery stores (โชห่วย).
- Market: "กว่า 6.2 หมื่นล้านบาท" (over THB 62,000 million); the article gives value growth of 16% against 1.8 to 2% the year before, and est growth of 23% for March to August 2023 against 16% for the total market.
- Not in the article: sugar-free line, lemon-lime, price.

## 2. Marketeer Online, 15 March 2024
- URL: https://marketeeronline.co/archives/345506
- "มูลค่าการตลาด 66,000 ล้านบาท (นีลเส็นไอคิว ระหว่างกุมภาพันธ์ 2566-มกราคม 2567)" (THB 66,000 million, NielsenIQ, February 2023 to January 2024); market growth 12%.
- est volume share "8.9%"; est growth 15% above the soft-drink market; penetration 26% rising to 28%; northeast sales growth 40%.
- Regional mix: central 28%, northeast 19%, north 10%, south 5% (Bangkok 37% in the Thansettakij article of 16 March 2024).
- Pack mix as printed here: PET 70%, glass 18%, can 12% (the Thansettakij article of 16 March 2024 prints PET 64%, glass 18%, can 2 to 3%; the two pack mixes disagree).
- Overseas: Malaysia own plant investment, Cambodia opened in January, China double-digit growth.
- "สถิติปีที่ผ่านมาคนไทยบริโภคน้ำอัดลม 2,100 ล้านลิตร" (last year Thais consumed 2,100 million litres of carbonated soft drinks).
- Not in the article: other brands' shares, sugar-free share, pack sizes, prices, budget.

## 3. Thansettakij, 3 April 2025
- URL: https://www.thansettakij.com/business/marketing/624003
- Headline: summer cola war, Coke, Pepsi, est and Big Cola promotions ("ลด แลก แจก แถม"). Retailers cut shelf prices by an average of THB 5 to 10 a bottle during the promotions.
- Market value "6.6 หมื่นล้านบาท" (THB 66,000 million); shares printed: Coke 51%, Pepsi 39%, est 8.9%, Big Cola 5% (the four figures add to 103.9%, so they come from different sources or bases).
- Big Cola: no price change "มานานกว่า 17 ปี" (for over 17 years); low-sugar consumers "ประมาณ 10%" of drinkers (Big Cola's estimate). Pepsi forecast: the low-sugar drinks market will grow 3 times faster than regular drinks. Pepsi launched a Japanese plum (บ๊วย) flavour to target Gen Z.
- Summer weighs about 30% of annual sales (Pepsi and Big Cola).
