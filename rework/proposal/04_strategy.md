# Strategy: Lime Cube (19 October 2026)

Domestic Thailand only.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the page text is saved in rework/raw and was read; this is the repository rule. UNVERIFIED: the page was not saved, or only a snippet or summary was read; a figure stays UNVERIFIED until its page is saved. DERIVED: our arithmetic, with the inputs and their tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## Target segment

Lead segment: men aged 25 to 34. Men aged 45 to 59 are a later volume segment.

- Current alcohol drinking: 68.6% of men aged 25 to 44 and 65.4% of men aged 45 to 59 (2024) [P, UNVERIFIED; source 1]. The 25 to 44 cell is a PROXY for 25 to 34, not a sugar measure.
- Potency (current drinking times age-adjusted daily sugar-sweetened share): men 25 to 34 against men 45 to 59 is 1.15 (90% interval 1.03 to 1.28); against men 35 to 44, 1.04 (1.00 to 1.09), so that order is not established [P, DERIVED; source 2; the men-by-age daily share is unpublished, an ASSUMPTION].
- A purchase-intent model, not a consumer finding, puts men 25 to 34 ahead in 94% of draws, by about 0.5 to 0.7 points [DERIVED; source 2 and ASSUMPTION inputs].
- Everyday use of sugar-substitute drinks, a proxy for intensive sweeteners, is 0.6% at 25 to 44 and 1.0% at 45 to 59 [P, SOURCED; source 2]. The older bands lead; the proxy does not carry over.
- Hot-season drinking is the occasion; Bangkok's April mean maximum is 35.7 °C [P, UNVERIFIED; source 3; station 48455 values not confirmed]. No mixing or chilled-use fit is tested.

## Proposition

The central claim is a new consumption form: one dissolvable pouch cube in 330 mL of water releases carbon dioxide and makes the drink [ASSUMPTION].

Novelty is not established: dissolvable cubes and tablets already sell abroad, for example Waterdrop, Ricola and Incrediballs [P and S, UNVERIFIED; sources 10 to 12]. The claim is format, not first or new product.

## Coverage of the three organiser axes

One component is enough. Against the organiser's three form axes: Ingredients and Nutrition, moderate; Alternative Formats, moderate; New Trend-Driven Target, weak.

- Potassium is 14.8% of the Thai reference of 3,500 mg a day (518 ÷ 3,500) [P, DERIVED]. The reference is MOPH Notification 182 (B.E. 2541), page not saved [P, UNVERIFIED; source 4]. A later notification (No. 445) gives the same value, but its Gazette text was not read [S, UNVERIFIED; source 5].

## Format and pack

- One cube per pouch is inferred (ASSUMPTION), not confirmed with the organiser. Cartons of 6, 12 or 24 (carton sizes ASSUMPTION).
- Reactant masses per 330 mL at 0.9 volume: potassium bicarbonate 1.33 g; citric acid 0.85 g stoichiometric, 3.42 g at pH 3.5, 5.97 g at pH 3.0 [DERIVED]. The cube mass is not yet derived. Target pH near 3.5 is an ASSUMPTION; a sensory test decides it.
- Cost per cube at pH 3.5 in a 12-cube carton: 3.90 THB (3.48 THB before carton, plus 0.42 THB carton) [DERIVED]. Inputs: reactants 0.18 THB (PROXY, source 17); pouch 1.30 THB (PROXY, base chosen, ASSUMPTION; source 17); print 1.50 THB (ASSUMPTION placeholder); processing 0.50 THB (ASSUMPTION); carton 5 THB per 12 cubes (retail, UNVERIFIED; source 17).
- The 6- and 24-cube cartons give 4.15 and 3.85 THB, assuming carton prices of about 4 THB and 9 THB (ASSUMPTION).
- The 1.30 THB pouch base is a chosen value near the midpoint (1.34) of a non-Thai listing range of USD 0.03 to 0.05 per piece [S, PROXY, UNVERIFIED; source 17]. At the Bank of Thailand midpoint rate for 15 October 2026 [P, SOURCED; source 18], that range is 1.01 to 1.68 THB. Across 0.67 to 2.70 THB, cost runs 3.27 to 5.30 THB per cube; a 3.00 THB pouch is a stress case only (5.60 THB) [DERIVED].
- About 128,600 cubes fit a 20-foot container for each carton size [DERIVED; carton sizes ASSUMPTION].

## Price and channel

No channel is chosen.

- A 330 mL sparkling can sold for about 15 THB at launch [S, UNVERIFIED; source 13]. Two Tops cans captured on 18 October 2026 were out of stock (16 and 17 THB) [S, SOURCED; source 7].
- Shelf price 10 to 20 THB, base 15; ex-factory share 60% (50 to 70%) [ASSUMPTION]. At 15 THB: 9.00 THB ex-factory; gross margin 56.7% before freight and overhead [DERIVED].
- Break-even shelf price: 6.50 THB at the 3.90 base; 5.45 to 8.83 THB across the pouch range; 9.33 THB at the stress case [DERIVED]. Registration, tooling, trademark and marketing are excluded.
- Channels: convenience stores (about 15,945 7-Eleven stores, end 2025 [P, UNVERIFIED; source 8]), online marketplaces, and in-store sampling at 150 THB per trial [ASSUMPTION].
- Paid social does not pay on base inputs: a paid click at 11.5 THB [S, PROXY, UNVERIFIED; source 9] exceeds the 5.10 THB serve contribution (9.00 − 3.90). At a 1.0% click-to-purchase rate [ASSUMPTION], cost per first purchase is about 1,150 THB (11.5 ÷ 0.010); at the 0.08% PROXY low end of source 9, it is 14,375 THB (11.5 ÷ 0.0008) [DERIVED].

## Decisions still open

1. **Dose, under review.** The design dose is 0.9 volume of CO2 per 330 mL [ASSUMPTION]. At 25 °C only 0.759 volumes stay dissolved, so about 16% leaves at once [DERIVED]; the full dose holds only to about 19 °C. Solubility (Lange-attributed table): 0.759, 0.655 and 0.592 volumes at 25, 30 and 35 °C [S, UNVERIFIED; source 6]. Gates by option:
   - 0.9 volume: at least 0.6 volume dissolved at 10 minutes, at serves of 30 °C or below [ASSUMPTION for the gate value].
   - 0.55 volume: gate not set; to be defined by R&D.

   Options: (a) keep 0.9 with a chilled or iced serve [DERIVED]; or (b) use 0.55 volume, which stays dissolved at every modelled Thai serve, giving about 317 mg potassium (9.1% of the reference) [DERIVED]. Fizz and taste are untested.
2. **Sweetener.** Sucralose is recommended, not chosen; its Thai permission is open and blocks sale. The FDA-hosted annex has no 14.1.4 row for it; the gazetted annex is unread [P, UNVERIFIED; source 16]. Steviol glycosides are the fallback.

## Stated limits

- One cube per pouch and the one-component reading are unconfirmed with the organiser.
- No Thai price was found for the reactants or pouch; the 1.50 THB print cost is a placeholder [ASSUMPTION].
- Fizz duration is a surface-only upper bound; no decay curve is measured. No desk figure answers the medical question (potassium-retaining medicines; kidney disease).

## What the strategy does not claim

- No sugar-free, sodium-free, health, hydration, electrolyte, potassium-benefit, validated-fizz or shelf-life claim. The fizz is a mechanism only.
- No Thai novelty or consumer result (segment figures are model outputs), market size or sales forecast.
- No freedom-to-operate statement. EP 4,516,115 A2 (Biofar, pending) is an unchecked risk [P, UNVERIFIED; source 14]; US 5,254,355 is also unchecked [P, UNVERIFIED; source 15].
- The SINGHA lion is authorised for this proposal only. Print or sale needs brand-owner and legal sign-off from Singha's department, which the team does not hold; the alcohol cue is unchecked.
