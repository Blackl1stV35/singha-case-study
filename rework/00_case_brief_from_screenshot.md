# Case brief: from the competition screenshot only

> **Decision, 18 October 2026:** the three components are **not** expected. One component is enough, with no restriction on the entry's scope beyond the claim set in [rework/51](51_final_conclusion_and_recommendation.md). This replaces the page 2 reading below. See [rework/52](52_pivot_and_retirement.md).

Source: the Singha University post for the SINGHA ULTIMATE CHALLENGE 2026 (Facebook, dated 17 September), attached as a screenshot. This is the only case statement. The team's workspace corpus (Singha.docx and Singha.md) is team working material, not the case, and nothing in this brief is taken from it.

## What the brief says (translated from the Thai text)

- **Challenge:** "The Innovative Bev & Bite". Create and develop a new-form food or beverage product, using an innovation that has never existed before, to answer three core axes.
- **Axis 1, Ingredients and Nutrition:** novelty in ingredients or raw materials, and in nutritional value.
- **Axis 2, Alternative Formats:** appearance and serving or consumption formats that can create new experiences.
- **Axis 3, New Trend-Driven Target:** responding to consumers and to future eating and drinking trends.
- **Who may enter:** undergraduate students or graduates (bachelor level or equivalent) from any discipline (the post names Food Science, Engineering, Business and Design as examples). Team entries are welcome.
- **What winners get:** mentoring from practitioners, a plant tour opportunity, and cash prizes totalling 100,000 THB: champion 60,000 THB, first runner-up 30,000 THB, second runner-up 10,000 THB.
- **Purpose stated in the post:** turn an idea into real innovation, moving from the classroom into Food and Beverage innovation in the real business world. The post also says "if you have an idea that wants to break the existing frame, your chance has come".
- **Deadline:** online registration and idea submission run from the post date to 22 October 2569 (2026).

## What the brief and the registration form do not say

- The judging criteria and their weights.
- The submission format (idea length, deck, prototype, or business plan).
- Whether the product must be sold in Thailand, or in any particular market.
- Whether a business case, a prototype or consumer evidence is required.
- Any rule on originality checks or on use of company data.

### Registration form, first page (read 18 October 2026)

The post links to a Google Form. Its first page was read through the desktop Chrome session. The form says the full details are in the next section. Its Next button sits behind a required account-email field, so I did not click it at first. The user later clicked through to the submit page.

- **Team size:** 2 to 4 members per team.
- **Eligibility:** every member must be an undergraduate or student at bachelor level or equivalent. No institution, faculty or major limit. Mixed teams allowed.
- **Participation:** the form says members can join the programme's key activities. Details are on page 2 (see below).
- **Prizes:** over 100,000 THB in total. Registration is open now and closes 22 October 2026 at 23:59.
- **Axes, as the form words them:** "3 main approaches, whether raw materials, product format, or global megatrend" (ทั้งวัตถุดิบ รูปแบบผลิตภัณฑ์ หรือเมกะเทรนด์โลก). The word "or" is loose here. Page 2 settles the reading (see below).

### Registration form, second page (read 18 October 2026, after Next was clicked)

- **Challenge statement, official wording (translated):** develop a food or beverage product using new ingredients or raw materials and/or production innovation. It must be a product that has never existed before, or one with a new form of consumption. It should create change in the food and beverage industry. This matches the screenshot.
- **Three components:** "in developing and designing the product, it should consist of 3 key components":
  1. **Ingredients & Nutrition:** a new product type, or a new raw material added, to meet the target group's needs precisely.
  2. **Alternative Formats:** change the look and/or the way a traditional food or beverage is consumed, to create a new consumption experience.
  3. **New Trend-Driven Target:** clearly meet the needs of the target consumer group in response to global megatrends.
- **Reading of "or":** "should consist of 3 key components" points to all three being expected, which is stricter than page 1's "or" wording. Treat all three as expected. This is a reading of the form's wording, not a stated rule.
- **Attendance:** attend all project activities where possible. If a member cannot attend, tell the team in advance; this does not affect idea evaluation.
- **Finals:** only the final 12 teams that enter the D-Day round must do onsite pitching at Singha Complex.
- **Dates:** page 2 lists no project dates in text, only the registration deadline.

Not in the registration form: the judging criteria and weights, the submission format (idea length, deck, prototype or business plan), and any originality or data-use rule. Pages 1 and 2 were read directly. The user reports that the submit page has no information on these either. The rubric below is therefore ours, not the organiser's, and should be replaced if the organiser publishes criteria later.

## Assumptions declared for this rework

1. **Home market is Thailand.** The post is in Thai, the prizes are in baht, and the post asks for real-business feasibility. This is the most likely reading. A different market would be a separate decision for the team.
2. **Singha is the organiser, not our client.** The team is an external entry. No group data, factories, importers or trademarks are assumed available to us. Any use of company capability is labelled as an assumption.
3. **"Never existed before" is tested against Thai shelves and online stores.** No novelty claim is accepted without a dated search record.
4. **The earlier export study, the malt-fibre verdict, and the corpus pillars are not constraints.** They may be used as background only, and any reuse must be shown as reuse.

## Provisional rubric (ours, not the organiser's)

The registration form publishes no criteria, so each idea is scored 0 to 2 on:
- **Novelty** on the Thai market (never existed, or a new form of use), with evidence.
- **Fit** to the three components. Page 2 says all three are expected, so an idea that covers only one should score low on fit, though it is not automatically out.
- **Feasibility** for a Thai food or beverage business (ingredient access, production route, Thai food-registration path).
- **Consumer case** (a reason a named target would pick it up, with evidence).

This rubric is provisional. Replace it if the organiser publishes criteria later.
