# Thai CKD prevalence, saved extracts (T10)

Date read: 20 October 2026. Each block is the text or table read on the page named; values are copied as shown on that page. Tier of the source: P (peer-reviewed paper, official survey).

## 1. Thai SEEK (abstract page only; full text paywalled)
- URL: https://academic.oup.com/ndt/article/25/5/1567/1853385 (Ingsathit A et al., Nephrology Dialysis Transplantation 2010;25(5):1567-1575, DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfp669). Only the abstract was readable.
- Sample 3,459 adults, mean age 45.2 years (SE 0.8), 54.5% female; 626 CKD cases.
- Overall CKD prevalence 17.5% (95% CI 14.6 to 20.4). By stage: I 3.3% (2.5 to 4.1); II 5.6% (4.2 to 7.0); III 7.5% (6.2 to 8.8); IV 1.1% (0.7 to 1.5). Stage V not reported in the abstract.
- Prevalence after adjusting for persistent positive albuminuria results: 14.4%. Awareness: 1.9%.
- Stage 3 plus 4 = 8.6% is our arithmetic (7.5 + 1.1), not a published figure. Age-by-sex tables were not readable.
- Note: Thai SEEK staged by eGFR plus albuminuria markers, so stages I and II are albuminuria-defined; the stage 3 to 5 share is the part that matters for potassium handling.

## 2. NHES V (2014), eGFR below 60
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8560950/ (Aekplakorn W et al., Scientific Reports 2021;11:21366, DOI 10.1038/s41598-021-00694-9).
- Planned sample 22,095 adults aged 20 and over; 18,118 took part; 17,329 analysed (creatinine and urinalysis complete). CKD-EPI (non-Black) equation.
- eGFR below 60, probability weighted: all 5.3% (4.8 to 5.8), men 4.1% (3.6 to 4.6), women 6.5% (5.4 to 7.7). Any CKD stage 1 to 5: 8.7% (8.1 to 9.3).
- Table 2, eGFR below 60 by age (%; all / men / women): 20-39: 0.20 / 0.08 / 0.32; 40-49: 0.81 / 0.67 / 0.94; 50-59: 2.6 / 3.4 / 1.9; 60-69: 12.2 / 8.8 / 15.3; 70 and over: 32.7 / 25.1 / 38.4.
- Table 1 (all ages): diabetes 9.8%, hypertension 28.5%.
- Stage 3a/3b/4/5 splits are only in Figure 1, not read.

## 3. NHES III (2004), CKD stage 3 to 5, MDRD with Asian factor
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2781792/ (Ong-ajyooth L et al., BMC Nephrology 2009;10:35, DOI 10.1186/1471-2369-10-35).
- 3,117 adults aged 15 and over (1,557 men, 1,560 women). CKD defined as self-reported dialysis or GFR below 60 (MDRD x 1.233 Asian factor, x 0.742 female). Urine albumin not measured, so only stages 3 to 5.
- Age-standardised CKD stage 3 to 5: 8.9% (8.0 to 9.9); stage 3 8.1%, stage 4 0.2%, stage 5 0.2%. Men 5.5% (4.3 to 6.8); women 12.3% (10.8 to 13.8).
- By age: 15-29 0.4% (0.1 to 1.2); 30-44 2.3% (1.3 to 4.1); 45-59 13.1% (10.1 to 16.8); 60 and over 39.8% (35.6 to 44.1).
- Hypertension 22.5% (men 26.0%, women 19.1%); diabetes 7.0% (men 6.6%, women 7.5%), age-standardised.
- Caveat for use: older equation (MDRD), likely to overstate against CKD-EPI; NHES V (2014) is the better age-specific base.
- Re-read 20 October 2026 (Table 2 of the paper): stage splits by age, both sexes only. 15-29: stage 3 0.2, stage 4 0, stage 5 0, total 0.4 (0.1 to 1.2). 30-44: 1.5 / 0 / 0.2, total 2.3 (1.3 to 4.1). 45-59: 12.4 / 0.3 / 0.2, total 13.1 (10.1 to 16.8). 60 and over: 37.7 / 1.4 / 0.5, total 39.8 (35.6 to 44.1). By sex, all ages only: men 5.5 (4.3 to 6.8), women 12.3 (10.8 to 13.8). The paper gives no age table by sex, so any men by age value from NHES III would be our scaling (men to all ratio 5.5 / 8.9 = 0.62).

## 4. Comparing the three Thai estimates (our reading, used in the T10 range)
- All-age values: Thai SEEK stage III plus IV 8.6 percent (sample mean age 45.2; our sum); NHES III stage 3 to 5, age-standardised 8.9 percent (MDRD, 2004); NHES V eGFR below 60, 5.3 percent (CKD-EPI, 2014). SEEK and NHES III agree with each other and sit well above NHES V. SEEK and NHES III are on the older equation or an older survey; the SEEK equation was not stated in the abstract.
- By band (T10 script, ckd_crosscheck, NHES III row used divided by the NHES V both-sex mid value): 11.5 times for "25-34" (2.3 against 0.20), 4.6 times for "35-44" (2.3 against 0.51, interpolated), 6.5 times for "45-59" (13.1 against 2.0) and 1.8 times for "60+" (39.8 against the 22.45 midpoint of the 60-69 and 70+ rows). NHES V (2014, CKD-EPI) is used for the low and mid values and NHES III for the high value, for every band; SEEK has no age table and is used only as the all-age cross-check.
