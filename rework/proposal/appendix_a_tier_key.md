# Appendix A: tier key

Every figure in the proposal carries a tier, a date and a source. The tiers are:

- **P**: regulator text read in full; a company's own filing, release or product page read in full; official statistics; a primary database.
- **S**: any other source read and opened: press, trade portal, consultancy or research page, a company claim relayed by press, or a search snippet where the snippet is the only text available.
- **SOURCED**: the page text was read, and its public URL is given.
- **UNVERIFIED**: not read, blocked, not found, or only a search snippet was read.
- **DERIVED**: our arithmetic, from the inputs shown.
- **PROXY**: a stand-in for a value that has not been measured.
- **ASSUMPTION**: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.
- **COMPANY CLAIM**: a statement the company makes about itself. It is not checked independently.

A figure is tiered where it first appears in a file. A later mention repeats the same tier. Sources are listed in each file's Sources section, with the date read.

How the tiers combine and what they cover:

- The tiers answer two questions. P, S, SOURCED and UNVERIFIED say how a source was read. DERIVED, PROXY, ASSUMPTION and COMPANY CLAIM say what kind of figure it is. A figure can carry one label of each kind, for example S, PROXY, or P, PROXY for an official statistic used as a stand-in for a different quantity. A figure labelled DERIVED shows its inputs and their labels, for example DERIVED on PROXY and ASSUMPTION inputs.
- A tier is not raised when a figure is quoted again. A figure read second-hand keeps the tier of the copy that was read. A market value printed by the press from a paid dataset, such as NielsenIQ, is S, because the dataset itself was not opened.
- A bracketed qualifier narrows what a tier covers, and it never raises a tier. "Negative finding" means that a primary list was searched by title up to the date given and nothing was found; it does not exclude a change made elsewhere, for example inside a general notification. "Flavoured-drink rows only" means that only those rows of a regulator's text were read. "Read from page images" means that the text was read from images of the pages.
