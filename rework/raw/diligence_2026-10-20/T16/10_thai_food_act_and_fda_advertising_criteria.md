# Thai food advertising law: Food Act B.E. 2522 sections 40 to 42 and the FDA advertising criteria B.E. 2569, read 20 October 2026

Task T16. Tier P for both texts (the Thai FDA's own PDFs; the Royal Gazette special issue). Translation of the Thai text into English is ours unless stated.

## A. Food Act B.E. 2522, Chapter 5 and the penalty sections

- Thai text, Chapter 5 (หมวด 5 การขึ้นทะเบียนและการโฆษณาเกี่ยวกับอาหาร): https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=536788384698474496&name=law_act_5.pdf (2 pages; PDF created 28 August 2023). Text extracted with a PDF reader from the file saved by the fetch tool; the Thai sara-am (ำ) renders as a separate vowel in the extraction and is restored here.
- English "unofficial translation", full Act: https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=536782813802930176&name=law_act_EN.pdf (18 pages; PDF created 21 July 2016). Listed on https://food.fda.moph.go.th/food-law/category/food-act-be2522/ .
- Section 40 (Thai): "ห้ามมิให้ผู้ใดโฆษณาคุณประโยชน์ คุณภาพ หรือสรรพคุณของอาหาร อันเป็นเท็จ หรือเป็นการหลอกลวงให้เกิดความหลงเชื่อโดยไม่สมควร". English (official unofficial translation): "False or deceptive advertising of the quality, usefulness or indication of a food is prohibited."
- Section 41 (Thai): a person who wishes to advertise the benefits, quality or properties of food on radio, television, film, newspapers or other print, or by any other means for commercial benefit must submit the sound, picture, film or text of the advertisement to the licensing authority for consideration first, and may advertise only after permission is given (ผู้ใดประสงค์จะโฆษณาคุณประโยชน์ คุณภาพ หรือสรรพคุณของอาหาร ... หรือด้วยวิธีอื่นใดเพื่อประโยชน์ในทางการค้า ต้องนำเสียง ภาพ ภาพยนตร์ หรือข้อความที่จะโฆษณาดังกล่าวนั้นให้ผู้อนุญาตตรวจพิจารณาก่อน เมื่อได้รับอนุญาตแล้วจึงจะโฆษณาได้).
- Section 42: the licensing authority may order the producer, importer, distributor or advertiser to stop an advertisement that breaches section 41, or to stop producing, importing, distributing or advertising food that the Committee finds lacks the advertised benefit, quality or property.
- Penalties (English translation): section 70, advertising food in breach of section 40, imprisonment of not more than 3 years and a fine of not more than THB 30,000, or both (the translation reads "and a fine ... or both"); section 71, breach of section 41, a fine of not more than THB 5,000; section 72, not following an order under section 42, imprisonment of not more than 2 years or a fine of not more than THB 20,000 or both, plus a daily fine of THB 500 to 1,000 for the period of non-compliance.

## B. FDA announcement "หลักเกณฑ์การโฆษณาอาหาร พ.ศ. 2569" (food advertising criteria B.E. 2569)

- PDF: https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=923831933513900032&name=69_Advertising.pdf (30 pages) listed on https://food.fda.moph.go.th/food-law/category/fda-announcement/ as "หลักเกณฑ์การโฆษณาอาหาร พ.ศ. 2569", effective 28/07/69.
- Royal Gazette marking on the first page: เล่ม 143 ตอนพิเศษ 182 ง, 27 กรกฎาคม 2569 (27 July 2026), pages 60 to 67. Issued by the Office of the Food and Drug Administration Committee (สำนักงานคณะกรรมการอาหารและยา), dated 13 กรกฎาคม พ.ศ. 2569 (13 July 2026), signed by the Secretary-General of the Food and Drug Administration Committee. Effective from the day after publication (28 July 2026). It cancels the announcement of the same name dated 25 March 2564 (2021) (ข้อ 2).
- Reading method: the Gazette pages use an embedded font that does not extract as text, so the eight Gazette pages were rendered to images and read by eye; the annex pages 12 to 19 were read the same way. The wording below is a close paraphrase and short quotation in Thai; the translation is ours.
- ข้อ 3: "การโฆษณาอาหาร" means doing, by any means, anything that lets the public see or learn of a message about food or its ingredients for commercial benefit. "ข้อความ" includes words, sound, pictures, signs and any act that can be understood as a message. A creator's paid post about a food or drink is therefore "advertising" under this definition.
- ข้อ 4: a food advertisement must not carry a message that is unfair to consumers or likely to harm society, including: (1) a message that makes people think a substance is in the food or an ingredient when the truth is otherwise; (2) a message that misleads about how the food is consumed; (3) a message likely to divide the public; (4) a message that supports illegal acts or damages good morals; (5) a message that may cause dangerous imitation; (6) a message that recommends, certifies or praises benefits, quality or properties of the food by medical and public health personnel, or by someone who claims or implies that status; (7) a message that compares with or disparages another maker's product.
- ข้อ 5: advertising of benefits, quality or properties must not be false or deceptive (section 40), including: (1) false or exaggerated; (2) suggests cure, relief, treatment or prevention of disease; (3) suggests an effect on body structure or function; (4) sexual enhancement; (5) skin or beauty; (6) weight or fat reduction (except foods for weight control approved by the FDA); (7) body shaping; (8) use of academic references or statistics that have not been assessed for a health claim by the FDA.
- ข้อ 6: without prior submission: (1) non-commercial scientific information; (2) corporate-image advertising; (3) advertising that only gives facts, using the wording in Annex 2. If any benefit, quality or property claim is mixed in, an application with documents is needed.
- ข้อ 7: advertising that must be submitted for approval includes claims about benefits, quality or properties that appear on the approved label (other claims need assessment and label approval first); promotional nutrient claims must follow the nutrition-label notification; health claims must follow the MoPH health-claim notification (No. 447, B.E. 2566); and any Article 6 advertisement that shows a benefit, quality or property.
- ข้อ 8: approval by media type (silent visual, audio, audio-visual), application forms ฆอ.1 and ฆอ.3, scripts and storyboards in the application; approved text must be reproduced as approved, the approval number must be shown in the approved media, and an approval lasts no more than 5 years.
- ข้อ 9: the advertised food must be correct in standard, formula, additives and label; the food name may be shortened only if the type is clear; any warning must match the label and be shown (print warnings at a letter height of at least 1/25 of the advertising area; audio-visual media may use a clear voice-over or a super of at least 5 seconds).
- ข้อ 10(8): electrolyte drinks (เครื่องดื่มเกลือแร่) may show only the benefit tied to loss of minerals from exercise. ข้อ 10(11): for beverages containing alcohol, information must follow the Alcohol Control Act and need not be submitted to the FDA. ข้อ 10(10) restricts energy-drink advertising to corporate image.
- ข้อ 11: label content is not itself advertising, but a label image or label text shown in media can be advertising. ข้อ 12: the licensing authority may cancel an approval; an advertisement in breach can be ordered stopped.
- Annex 1 lists messages not allowed, with examples: disease claims (for example lowering cholesterol, blood sugar), effects on body functions (for example "detox", "boost immunity", "restore"), sexual claims, beauty claims, weight-loss and body-shaping claims (for example "weight loss", "slim", "before and after"), and "fast results", "FDA certified" (อย. รับรอง).
- Annex 2 lists advertising that does not need prior approval, with conditions. Items read that bear on this campaign: promotions and giveaways with true details under the Consumer Protection Act (item 1); price and quantity (item 2); price comparison with no comparison to a competitor's product (item 3); where it is sold (item 4); a halal, kosher or similar logo with a letter of certification (item 5); awards, with year (item 6); production-standard certification such as GMP or HACCP, without using the FDA symbol (item 7); product details: name, formula and the way to consume or prepare the product (item 8); "no added ... / not using ..." statements only if true to the approved formula (items 9 and 10); "new" for a product on sale for no more than 1 year with evidence (item 11); "number 1 in sales" with a survey and year (item 14); taste, aroma and texture words (item 16); statements of liking (item 17); the words "ซ่า", "เย็นซ่า", "ซาบซ่า", "เย็น" or "คูล" for carbonated drinks or products with a cooling ingredient (item 18); comparing taste only with the company's own products (item 20); "limited edition" for a time-limited run (item 28). Item 8 adds: where a flavouring is used, a picture of the flavour source must carry the text that the picture only suggests the flavour (the item cites FDA order No. 343/2548 on ingredient pictures on labels).
- Annex 3 and Annex 4 (pages 18 to 30 of the PDF): Annex 3 items 29 to 31 read (health-choice mark, BCG-model recycling statements, "FDA registered number" statements, which must follow FDA rules); the remaining annex pages (the warning texts of Annex 4 and later rows) were not read.
- Limit: this announcement was read from page images, not from a text layer; the wording of clauses is paraphrased and the numbering follows the Gazette. A Thai legal review (Gate 7) is the settling step.
