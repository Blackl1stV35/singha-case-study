# Wiet and Beyts 1992: sensory characteristics of sucralose and other high intensity sweeteners (abstract record)

- Citation: S. G. Wiet, P. K. Beyts, Journal of Food Science (1992), as listed on the AGRIS record
- Date read: 20 October 2026. Route: WebFetch of the record page (model-summarised abstract).
- Tier: S (abstract-level record). Status: SOURCED at abstract level.

## Extracted facts
- Trained panel, magnitude estimation at six concentrations in a simple aqueous system, sucralose against sucrose, aspartame, saccharin and acesulfame-K.
- The abstract states that the sweetness potency of sucralose is 400 to 700 times sucrose on a weight basis (the record prints "70O", read as 700) and that the potencies of all the sweeteners were concentration dependent.
- Used as the potency RANGE in the taste model: 400 (low), 600 (central), 700 (high). The central 600 comes from the Wikipedia sucralose page ("about 600 times sweeter than sucrose", wikipedia_physical_constants.md) and the EFSA 2026 news summary ("around 600", efsa_sucralose_2026_news_summary.md), both S. An earlier version of this page attributed the 600 to JECFA (capture 63); that monograph was not opened in this task and is not cited.

## Limits
- Abstract only; the sucrose-equivalent concentrations behind 400 and 700 were not read.
