## Revised verdict plan for the Korea track

Status: not validated, as before. The blockers have changed:

1. **Volume evidence (decisive).** Obtain the group's own Korea sell-in and sell-out for Singha Lemon Soda Zero and other soda SKUs. That is the only source that can test the share ramp, the company claims and the realised price. Request it from the company; do not infer it.
2. **Realised price.** Obtain the group's transfer price to its Korean importer. Replace the Thai basket proxy with it.
3. **Fixed cost.** Confirm which group functions (sales, marketing, compliance) are shared and at what cost. Replace the 500m KRW assumption with that figure.
4. **G1 sparkling market size.** Still no independent 2023 to 2025 series for sparkling or soda. A paid Nielsen or Euromonitor table, or a KOSIS or aT series, is needed.
5. **G5 demand.** No Korea-specific sparkling or zero-sugar demand data. Pilot velocity (K5) is the test.
6. **G6 regulatory.** MFDS registration route, sweetener class limits (notice 2025-76 not read), botanical ingredient listing, and the zero-sugar labelling rule status.
7. **Name clearance.** Class 32 coverage for the new product name in KIPRIS.

Killer checks, in order: sweetener and botanical permission (K6), group sell-out evidence (K3), realised price above the break-even contribution (K1).
