## Sentences used

- Title as translated: "Notification of the Ministry of Public Health (No. 195) B.E. 2543 (2000) Re: Electrolyte Drinks." The translation calls the class "electrolyte drinks"; the Thai term is เครื่องดื่มเกลือแร่ (literally mineral-salt drink). Our records use "mineral-salt beverage" and "electrolyte drink" for the same class.
- Clause 3: "Electrolyte drinks mean drinks which is mainly consist of minerals and also to include dry electrolyte drink." The Thai text says the mineral salts are the "main ingredient" (ส่วนประกอบหลัก); the English "mainly consist of minerals" is looser, and the Thai prevails.
- Clause 4(1): "One litre of electrolyte drink shall consist of:" then "(1.3) Potassium not more than 195 milligrams. (if any)", with (1.1) sodium 460 to 920 mg, (1.2) glucose or fructose at least 2 percent by weight or sucrose at least 4 percent, (1.4) bicarbonate not more than 793 mg, (1.5) citrate not more than 819 mg.
- Clause 4(7) as first issued: "Artificial sweeteners are not allowed to be used." (Thai: no sweetener used in place of sugar. Repealed in 2020, see n195_amending_notifications_2554_2563.md.)
- Clause 7(2): "(2.1) Not suitable for children and infant consumption. (2.2) For those who lost perspiration from exercise only. (2.3) Should not consume more than .......unit per day" with a total not over 1 litre a day, in a red rectangular border on a white background, letters not smaller than 2 mm.
- Closing: "(Published in the Government Gazette Vol. 118, Special Part 6 Ngor, dated 24th of January 2001)" and "Notified on 19th September 2000."
- Footer note: "In case of any discrepancy between the Thai original and the English translation, the former will take priority."

## Further clauses as translated (added in the revision of 20 October 2026)

The file was fetched again on 20 October 2026 (SHA-256 4a258369e0093fdd2d4e4a1d45a8ca72b69f5326d7d9b9288b263752fcecd2e7, 50,742 bytes, the same as above), its text layer extracted with pdftotext, and the sentences below were read from the extraction. They are the translation's wording, tier S. The Thai wording of the same clauses is in n195_gazette_original_B_E_2544.md.

- Clause 1: "The notification of the Ministry of Public Health No. 108 B.E. 2530 (1987), Re: Electrolyte drinks, dated 20th November B.E. 2530 (1987), shall be repealed."
- Clause 2: "Electrolyte drinks is prescribed food to have qualities or standards."
- Clause 4(2): "Odour and taste inherent to the specific characteristics of such electrolyte drinks."
- Clause 4(3): "Free of sediment, except those derived from the usage of certain flavouring ingredients."
- Clause 4(4): "Water to be used in electrolyte drinks production shall have qualities or standards conforming to the notification of the Ministry of Public Health, Re: Drinking water in sealed containers."
- Clause 4(5)(a): "Coloform bacteria shall be detected less than 2.2 per 100 millilitre of electrolyte drinks by Most Probable Number Method. (MPN)" (the translation misspells coliform).
- Clause 4(5)(b): "Escherichia coli shall not be detected."
- Clause 4(5)(c): "Free of pathogenic microorganisms." (replaced in 2020)
- Clause 4(5)(d): "Free of toxic substances released by microorganisms or other toxic substances in quantity which may be hazardous to health."
- Clause 4(5)(e): "Free of mold." (replaced in 2011)
- Clause 4(6): "Free of contaminants, except the following:" with arsenic 0.2, lead 0.3, copper 5, zinc 5, iron 15, tin 250 and sulfur dioxide 10 ppm at most (replaced in 2020).
- Clause 4(8): "Free of alcoholic ingredients, except alcohol used as solvent of some flavouring which is the ingredients of electrolyte drink."
- Clause 4(9): "Free of caffeine."
- Clause 8: "Food Registration or Food Labelling , which are issued to follow the notification of the Ministry of Public Health No. 108 B.E. 2530 (1987), Re: Electrolyte drinks, dated 20th November B.E. 2530 (1987), prior to this notification, shall be valid for 2 years as from the enforcement date of this notification."
- Clause 9: "Electrolyte drink producers or importers, whose permits issued prior to this notification, shall apply for food serial number within one year as from the come into force date of this notification. After applying for food serial number, the electrolyte drink producers or importer shall be abated from the stipulation in Clause 5 for a period of 2 years after this notification come into force. As a result, the remaining labels are allowed to be used until last but not exceeding to 2 years after this notification come into force."
- Clause 10: "This notification shall come into force after 180 days as from the day following date of its publication in the Government Gazette."

## What this page settles and what it does not

- It gives an English wording for clause 3, 4(1) and 7 that the Thai FDA itself published, so our English quotations of those clauses do not rest only on our own translation. It predates the amendments (it still shows 4(5)(e), 4(6), 4(7) and the dry-form paragraph in their first form), so it is not evidence of what is in force today.
- No English edition of the 2023 compilation or of the 2011 "(No. 2)" notification was found (the FDA page links the same 2003 file for both).
