Method. No web search or fetch was run in this task or in either fix pass. Every figure comes from a verified record, from a saved page, or from a results file that a script in rework/screen produced. Tiers follow rework/submission/appendix_a_tier_key.md, which reads P as a page or document read in full. This record applies one rule to every figure:

- P: the figure was read as verbatim text from the issuer's own document (a PDF text layer extracted locally, or a table parsed from the issuer's page as printed). Section 8 names the pages.
- S: the figure comes from a model-written WebFetch extract, even where the source is a company filing, an investor page or a product page. The extract is not the page read in full. The Singha product page is S for this reason (C21).

Shelf prices are online listings read on 20 October 2026 (S). They are not store shelf prices. Per 330 mL figures are DERIVED from the pack price and the pack size. THB values converted from USD use THB 33.5495 per USD, a team midpoint (C8). The published Bank of Thailand reference rate for 15 October 2026 was 33.5410, and the effect is under 0.03 percent. EUR values use the ECB reference rate of 19 October 2026, cross-rated through USD (EUR 1 is THB 37.66). Printed figures are rounded half up (1.125 prints as 1.13, 14.625 as 14.63). Medians are computed on unrounded values.

## 0. Summary

1. Price. Named-seller listings read on 20 October 2026 give 31 lemon, lime and clear can listings at THB 12.40 to 28.67 per 330 mL, with a median of THB 14.05 (68d set A; DERIVED from S). The sugar-free 24-can listings from named sellers read THB 12.40 to 14.63. Two six-can listings at 7-Eleven, Singha Lemon Soda at THB 28.67 and Schweppes lime zero at THB 28.33, are outliers whose cause is not known. The planning price of Lemon Soda, THB 15 per 330 mL, is an ASSUMPTION. It sits THB 0.95 above the main median, THB 0.87 above the Sprite No Sugar 24-can listing at THB 14.13, THB 1.25 below the sugar-free 7UP No Sugar single can at THB 16.25 and THB 2.00 below the sugar-free Singha single can at THB 17.00. The single-can median of THB 16.62 mixes regular and sugar-free cans and is given for completeness.
2. Margins. Brand owners, bottlers, franchisors and group segments measure different things. Their margins are not comparable and are not ranked (section 2). The Thai bottler margins read are ThaiNamthip 38.5% gross and 10.6% net (FY2024, P, DERIVED), Haad Thip 42.2% gross and 6.90% net as printed (FY2025, S) and Sermsuk 29.25% gross and 3.07% net (FY2024, P, DERIVED). No margin was found in these records for Suntory PepsiCo Beverage (Thailand), Coca-Cola (Thailand) Limited, Thai Drinks or the proposer.
3. Strategy. Lemon Soda competes on its form (a dry cube, one per 330 mL serve) and on the Singha name. At THB 15 it sits below the single cans read and above the sugar-free pack prices read from named sellers. It does not compete on packs of cans or bottles. The record claims no price advantage over sugar-free cans or packs.
4. Consumers. None of the five records holds a consumer test, survey or buyer profile for a lemon cube. Section 5 lists the signals that the records do support.
5. Checks and conflicts. The 28 checks made before this pass (section 8, part A) found 27 figures in the saved pages; check 20 was not found and is counted as unverified. This pass added 32 saved-page string checks and 31 arithmetic and results-file checks. All 32 strings are found and all 31 checks pass (section 8, part B; rework/screen/t68_fix_checks.py). Section 9 lists the conflicts (C1 to C34). The 68e can-ladder medians came from a first-revision results file and are withdrawn (C20). The lead decides C7, C8, C18 and C24 before any judge text uses them.

## 2. Margin comparability note

Margins in this record are not comparable across rows and must not be ranked against each other. Five kinds of company appear.

3. Group segment: ThaiBev's non-alcoholic beverages business (36.58% gross, FY2025). It mixes bottling, brands, F&N drinks and dairies. It is not a Thai or soda-only figure.
4. Whole-company figures with other lines: Osotspa (40.1% gross, 2025). Its four segments include personal care, healthcare and confectionery, so it is not an energy-drink margin.
5. Partial or aggregated accounts: waterdrop (trend.at, 6 May 2026, partial scope, S). Its net margin is DERIVED from accounts that the source says cover only some subsidiaries.
