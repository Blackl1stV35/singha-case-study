# FAO JECFA specification monographs: mannitol, isomalt and sucralose (saved reading)

All three were opened on 20 October 2026. The PDFs were fetched through the WebFetch tool (binary saved by the tool) and their text layer was extracted with PyMuPDF. Tier: P (FAO/WHO JECFA specification text). Status: SOURCED for the limits below. The sodium figures at the end of each section are our arithmetic (DERIVED); none of the three monographs sets a sodium limit.

Sodium bound used: if the whole sulfated-ash limit were sodium sulphate, sodium is at most 0.3237 of the ash (2 x 22.98977 / 142.04214). This is an upper bound, because ash also holds potassium, calcium and magnesium sulphates.

## 1. Mannitol, INS 421

- URL: https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/jecfa_additives/docs/Monograph1/Additive-275.pdf (3 pages).
- Text: "Prepared at the 46th JECFA (1996), published in FNP 52 Add 4 (1996) superseding specifications prepared at the 33rd JECFA (1988) ... Metals and arsenic specifications revised at the 57th JECFA (2001). An ADI 'not specified' was established at the 30th JECFA (1986)."
- Assay: not less than 96.0 percent and not more than 102.0 percent on the dried basis. Description: white, odourless, crystalline powder. Functional uses: sweetener, humectant, texturizer, stabilizer, bulking agent. Solubility: soluble in water, very slightly soluble in ethanol.
- Purity limits: loss on drying not more than 0.3 percent (105 C, 4 h); sulfated ash not more than 0.1 percent (2 g sample); chlorides not more than 70 mg/kg; sulfates not more than 100 mg/kg; nickel not more than 2 mg/kg; reducing sugars not more than 0.3 percent; total sugars not more than 1.0 percent (as glucose); lead not more than 1 mg/kg; pH of a 10 percent solution 5 to 8.
- Derived: sodium at most 0.1 percent x 0.3237 = 324 mg/kg, so at most 0.26 mg in 0.80 g. Total sugars at most 8 mg in 0.80 g (0.0024 g per 100 mL made up at 330 mL); reducing sugars at most 2.4 mg.

## 2. Isomalt (hydrogenated isomaltulose), INS 953

- URL: https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/jecfa_additives/docs/monograph5/additive-241-m5.pdf (5 pages).
- Text: "Prepared at the 69th JECFA (2008), published in FAO JECFA Monographs 5 (2008), superseding specifications prepared at the 46th JECFA (1996) ... An ADI 'not specified' was established at the 29th JECFA (1985)."
- Definition: a mixture of hydrogenated mono- and disaccharides whose principal components are 6-O-alpha-D-glucopyranosyl-D-sorbitol (1,6-GPS) and 1-O-alpha-D-glucopyranosyl-D-mannitol dihydrate (1,1-GPM). Assay: not less than 98 percent of hydrogenated mono- and disaccharides and not less than 86 percent of the GPS and GPM mixture, anhydrous basis.
- Description: "Odourless, white, crystalline slightly hygroscopic substance". Functional uses: sweetener, bulking agent, anticaking agent, glazing agent. Solubility: soluble in water, very slightly soluble in ethanol.
- Purity limits: water not more than 7.0 percent (Karl Fischer); sulfated ash not more than 0.05 percent (5 g sample); D-mannitol not more than 3 percent; D-sorbitol not more than 6 percent; reducing sugars not more than 0.3 percent; nickel not more than 2 mg/kg; lead not more than 1 mg/kg.
- Derived: sodium at most 0.05 percent x 0.3237 = 162 mg/kg, so at most 0.13 mg in 0.80 g. Water allowed in 0.80 g: up to 56 mg, against 2.4 mg of drying loss for mannitol (about 23 times).

## 3. Sucralose, INS 955

- URL: https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/jecfa_additives/docs/Monograph1/Additive-444.pdf (6 pages). Found through a search; the WHO JECFA database entry (https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/2340) lists the specification as code R (1993) with metals limits revised in 2004 and ADI 0 to 15 mg/kg bw (37th JECFA, 1990).
- Text: "Prepared at the 41st JECFA (1993), published in FNP 52 Add 2 (1993). Metals and arsenic specifications revised at the 63rd JECFA (2004)."
- Assay: not less than 98 percent and not more than 102 percent, anhydrous basis. Functional use: sweetener. Solubility: freely soluble in water, methanol and ethanol.
- Purity limits: water not more than 2.0 percent (Karl Fischer); sulfated ash not more than 0.7 percent; methanol not more than 0.1 percent; triphenylphosphine oxide not more than 150 mg/kg; lead not more than 1 mg/kg; specific rotation +84.0 to +87.5 degrees (10 percent solution); other chlorinated disaccharides and chlorinated monosaccharides pass the thin-layer test.
- Derived: sodium at most 0.7 percent x 0.3237 = 2,266 mg/kg, so at most 0.075 mg in 33 mg of sucralose.
