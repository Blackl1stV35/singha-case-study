# Kasikorn Research Center (KResearch): non-alcoholic beverage outlook notes of 2 December 2024 and 30 May 2025 (opened 20 October 2026)

Item: V7 (revision). These are the notes of the source that Thansettakij 638312 credits for the 2025 carbonated figure of about THB 76,900 million. Tier: S for both. The notes are the originator's own publications, but they are a bank research unit's forecasts and estimates, not an official statistic or a filing, so they are not tiered P. Status: SOURCED (secondary, forecast). Neither note prints a baht value for the carbonated segment. Neither value here is used in the deck or the record.

Reading method. The December 2024 note is a six-page PDF that WebFetch saved as a binary copy. The text layer does not map Thai glyphs, so pages 1 to 3 were rendered to images and read by eye, and the figures were checked at higher resolution. Pages 4 to 6 (exports and other sections) were not read. The Thai text below is the team's transcription from the page images. Translations are the team's own.

## 1. KResearch, Industry Analysis and Outlook No.09, 2 December 2567 (2 December 2024)

- URL: https://media.settrade.com/settrade/Documents/2024/Nov/021224-K-Research-Non-Alcohol-Industry.pdf
- Date read: 20 October 2026.
- Title on page 1: แนวโน้มอุตสาหกรรมเครื่องดื่มไม่มีแอลกอฮอล์ไทย (Thai non-alcoholic beverage industry outlook). Publisher: KResearch (KASIKORNTHAI). Date printed: 2 ธันวาคม 2567.
- Page 1, first bullet: "ยอดขายของธุรกิจเครื่องดื่มไม่มีแอลกอฮอล์ปี 2568 คาดจะอยู่ที่ 2.29 แสนล้านบาท โต 3.3% จากปีก่อน". Team translation: sales of the non-alcoholic beverage business in 2025 are expected at THB 229,000 million, up 3.3 percent.
- Page 2, figure 2 (bars, unit printed as แสนล้านบาท, hundreds of thousands of millions): 2563 1.97; 2564 1.95; 2565 2.02; 2566 2.12; 2567f 2.22; 2568f 2.29. That is THB 197,000, 195,000, 202,000, 212,000, 222,000 and 229,000 million. Source line printed on the chart: "Euromonitor, NielsenIQ, NFI, ประมาณการโดยศูนย์วิจัยกสิกรไทย" (Euromonitor, NielsenIQ, NFI, estimates by KResearch).
- Page 2, figure 3 (share of the non-alcoholic market by type, 2568): carbonated drinks (น้ำอัดลม) 34 percent; bottled water (น้ำดื่มบรรจุขวด) 21 percent; energy drinks 10 percent; ready-to-drink tea 8 percent; functional drinks 7 percent; other 20 percent. Source line: "Euromonitor, NielsenIQ, NFI, ประมาณการโดยศูนย์วิจัยกสิกรไทย". The figure title says 2568, a forecast year.
- Page 2, text: "ยอดขายน้ำอัดลมและน้ำดื่มบรรจุขวด คาดว่าจะขยายตัว 2.6% และ 4.8% ในปี 2568" (carbonated 2.6 percent, bottled water 4.8 percent). The two together hold more than 55 percent of the market.
- Page 2, footnote 1 (attached to the THB 229,000 million total), transcribed: "ครอบคลุมไปยังกลุ่มลูกค้า B2B ทั้งร้านค้าส่ง-ร้านค้าปลีก ร้านอาหาร โรงแรม เป็นต้น รวมถึงกลุ่มลูกค้า B2C ที่เป็นผู้บริโภคทั่วไปโดยตรง". Team translation: covers B2B customers, including wholesale and retail shops, restaurants and hotels, and also B2C customers who are general consumers directly. This states the customer coverage of the total. It does not say whether the value is at retail selling price or at the maker's price.
- No carbonated baht value is printed on the pages read.

Arithmetic (DERIVED, not printed in the note): 34 percent of 229,000 is 77,860 million. 34 percent of 226,000 is 76,840 million.

## 2. KResearch, Econ Digest page of 30 May 2568 (30 May 2025), IAO133

- URL: https://www.kasikornresearch.com/th/analysis/k-social-media/Pages/Non-alcohol-Industry-IAO133-FB-2025-05-30.aspx
- Date read: 20 October 2026 (WebFetch extract, Thai text as returned).
- Title as returned: เครื่องดื่มไม่มีแอลกอฮอล์ไทย คาดปี 2568 โตชะลอลง ทั้งในประเทศและตลาดส่งออก. Publication date: 30 May 2025.
- Total, exact sentence as returned: "ยอดขายของธุรกิจเครื่องดื่มไม่มีแอลกอฮอล์ปี 2568 คาดว่าจะอยู่ที่ 2.26 แสนล้านบาท". Growth 2.0 percent, slowing from 4.5 percent the year before. This revises the December 2024 forecast (THB 229,000 million, +3.3 percent) down.
- Carbonated and bottled water, exact sentence as returned: "ยอดขายน้ำอัดลมและน้ำดื่มบรรจุขวด คาดว่าจะขยายตัว 2.0% และ 2.6% ในปี 2568 ชะลอตัวลงจากปี 2567". Team translation: sales of carbonated drinks and bottled water are expected to grow 2.0 percent and 2.6 percent in 2025, slower than 2024.
- Share, exact sentence as returned: "ตลาดน้ำอัดลมและน้ำดื่มบรรจุขวด มีส่วนแบ่งตลาดรวมกันกว่า 55% ของยอดขายธุรกิจเครื่องดื่มไม่มีแอลกอฮอล์ทั้งหมด". No separate carbonated share or baht value is printed.
- The number 76,900 does not appear on this page.
- Data sources named on the page: the Thai Meteorological Department, for a temperature forecast only. The market figures name no source on this page. The page offers a "Download Full Paper" file (966.06 KB). No URL for it is printed in the extract. The file was not located or downloaded.

## 3. What these two notes allow

- THB 226,000 million for 2025 non-alcoholic sales is printed by KResearch on 30 May 2025, as the Thansettakij 638312 article says. This matches the article's numeral for the total.
- The KResearch note of 2 December 2024 prints 34 percent for carbonated drinks. The Thansettakij pair (76,900 and 226,000) gives 34.0 percent. The numbers are consistent.
- The KResearch page of 30 May 2025 does not print 76,900. The baht value of the carbonated segment is therefore reported only by the Thansettakij article (as KResearch data) and, with no source, by Marketeer. The KResearch full paper, if it prints 76,900, was not read.
- The December 2024 chart source lines list NielsenIQ together with Euromonitor, NFI and KResearch estimates. This does not make 76,900 a NielsenIQ figure. It is a composite estimate that shows no split by source.
