# Malt-fibre sparkling: status (2026-10-17)

**Status: not promising.** Not presentable to the competition as the lead concept. The status is pending the user's confirmation to drop it, because the user earlier asked that malt-fibre not be dropped yet.

Concept (from the corpus, Singha.md): a non-alcoholic carbonated barley-malt drink with soluble barley beta-glucan added, lightly sweetened (not zero), in a slim 325 ml can, with Original Malt, Malt Lime and Malt Ginger lines.

## Why it is not promising

1. **No viscosity-stated supply.** Beta-glucan is viscous and unstable in drinks. No barley beta-glucan or barley-fibre beverage ingredient has a technical data sheet giving viscosity in mPa·s at a stated concentration and temperature.
   - Cargill Barliv: the only beverage claim is vendor marketing (0.75 g per serve, no numbers).
   - Megazyme and Neogen: research reagents, cSt grades with no concentration or temperature.
   - GrainFrac Barley Balance: no viscosity on the listing or the TDS.
   - Kerry, Ingredion and Ceres: no usable specification found.
   - A US patent (11,116,242) sets a limit of at most 55 mPa·s near 0.2 percent w/v for wort-based drinks. It is a patent limit, not a supplier specification.
2. **The fibre claim is blocked at the claimed dose.** The claim dose of 0.75 g beta-glucan is about 6.5 times short of the 1.5 g per 100 ml proxy threshold for a fibre source claim (about 4.9 g per can needed). It is about 13 times short of the 3 g per 100 ml figure. Singapore's primary thresholds and Korea's functional-raw-material annex were not read.
3. **Dose basis conflict.** Scan 1 treats 0.75 g as beta-glucan. Scan 2 treats it as 0.75 g of 70 percent Barliv product (0.525 g beta-glucan). The gap is 6.5 to 13 times on the first basis and 9 to 19 times on the second. The conclusion holds on both.
4. **No route to a health or functional claim.** Barley beta-glucan is not confirmed on Singapore's approved health-claims list or on Korea's general-food functional list. Singapore bars health claims (Reg 9(2)).

## What would be needed to reopen it

- A supplier technical data sheet with viscosity in mPa·s at 0.23 percent w/v (and 0.33 percent w/v of 70 percent powder) in carbonated water at 4 and 20 °C. Direct requests to Cargill, Kerry and Ingredion are the credible route.
- Confirmation of the dose basis.
- Singapore fibre thresholds and the SFA novel-food status of barley beta-glucan. If review is needed, it takes 9 to 12 months and cannot finish before 22 October 2026.
- The Cargill FDA GRN 344 releasable PDF (ucm269545.pdf, over 10 MB). It may hold the full specification. **It needs your explicit approval to download.** It is not downloaded.

Drop malt-fibre from the 22 October submission, subject to your confirmation. If the fibre hook is still wanted, the trend-track screen points to a fibre soda using acacia or resistant maltodextrin (`09_trend_tracks_and_20pct_test.md`).
