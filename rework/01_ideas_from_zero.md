## Finalists (6 of 8 each, the judge's tie)

### A. Leaf-wrapped frozen coconut drink pop (Alternative Formats)

- **Format:** a frozen coconut or pandan drink sealed in a banana or pandan leaf, peeled open and sipped as it thaws.
- **Target:** Thai buyers who value plastic-free, traditional packaging.
- **Novelty (2):** none found after 6 Thai and English searches. Closest is the Rimping supermarket's banana-leaf produce wraps in Chiang Mai (2019). No leaf-wrapped drink was found.
- **Fit (2):** peel-and-sip while it thaws is a new consumption ritual.
- **Feasibility (1):** a leaf is not waterproof, so a thawing drink needs an inner film, which may remove the plastic-free reason to buy. Leaf supply and cold-chain cost are unverified.
- **Consumer case (1):** plastic-free buyers are named, but the evidence is about produce, not drinks.
- **Kill test:** a leaf-only wrap leaks during a 2-hour thaw at 30 °C, and an inner film needed to stop leaks removes the plastic-free reason to buy.
- **First test (3 days):** freeze 30 coconut-milk pops in two wraps (leaf only, and leaf with a food-grade inner film). Thaw 15 of each at 30 °C for 2 hours and log leaks. Then a 20-person blind intercept: peel-and-sip versus a cup, plus a willingness-to-pay question.
- **Unverified:** leaf supply; food safety of leaf contact with drinks; Thai FDA rules for leaf packaging; consumer acceptance.

### B. Midlife women standardised-isoflavone unsweetened soy drink (New Trend-Driven Target)

- **Format:** a 250 ml unsweetened UHT soy drink with a labelled isoflavone dose per serving, sold as an evening drink.
- **Target:** Thai women aged 40 to 59 with hot flushes or disturbed sleep, who prefer a daily food to capsules.
- **Novelty (2):** none found after 4 Thai and English product searches. Closest is Holistic Way Menopause Relief, a Singapore capsule (press release, 6 August 2026). It is a capsule, not a drink.
- **Fit (2):** a named group with a named reason. The reason is tied to a Mintel Thailand women's wellness report (June 2026). Only the public summary was read; the full report is paywalled.
- **Feasibility (1):** no Thai isoflavone extract supplier was verified. The route (supplement or food), the menopause claim wording and dose safety are unresolved. The agent reported that plain Thai soy milk carries little isoflavone naturally (not checked by me), so a labelled dose needs added extract.
- **Consumer case (1):** a named target, but no Thai demand test.
- **Kill test:** a desk check of Thai FDA food and supplement rules, or a registration adviser, shows the pack can carry only nutrient content and no menopause-linked benefit. That removes the midlife reason to buy.
- **First test (3 days):** spike plain soy milk with a standardised isoflavone powder at two doses. Then a blind taste and purchase-intent test with 30 Thai women aged 40 to 59. Check the powder's certificate of analysis. **Gate:** the dose range needs a safety review before any consumer tasting.
- **Unverified:** Thai population size; the only dose reference found is a small Iranian pre-post study, which is not Thai evidence; no store audit yet; claim wording and regulatory category.

### C. Thai-herb non-alcoholic evening RTD for sober-curious adults (New Trend-Driven Target)

- **Format:** a 250 ml chilled can of non-alcoholic herbal drink (lemongrass, butterfly pea, kaffir lime), positioned as a dinner-time alternative to alcohol.
- **Target:** Thai adults aged about 22 to 35 who drink little or none and want a dinner-time ritual drink.
- **Novelty (1, verdict "unclear"):** only 2 product searches. The closest are STOCKHOLME Sober Bar in Bangkok and The Sarojin's alcohol-free menu, both venues, not packaged products. No canned Thai-botanical RTD was found, but the search was thin.
- **Fit (2):** sober-curious adults are a trend target, supported by Innova Market Insights (December 2025), GlobalData, and a Thai Post column citing Mintel (2023). The desk check found the Thai Post column does not support sober-curious demand, and the national data do not show drinking falling.
- **Feasibility (2):** Thai botanicals and a canning or retort co-packer are public routes.
- **Consumer case (1, proposed 0):** no Thai survey. The desk check found that the Thai Post column does not support sober-curious demand, and the 9.6 percent figure is unsourced. The follow-up search found no Thai source for drinking less, and the NSO data show drinking rising from 2021 to 2024. See [02_desk_kill_tests.md](02_desk_kill_tests.md).
- **Regulatory:** whether alcohol-association marketing limits reach a non-alcoholic brand is unverified and needs legal review.
- **Kill test:** a store and online audit (Makro, Tops, 7-Eleven, Lazada, Shopee, Boots, Villa Market) finds a Thai-botanical non-alcoholic RTD already sold in an evening or alcohol-alternative position.
- **First test (3 days):** a blind taste of three Thai-botanical bases (for example lemongrass with kaffir lime, butterfly pea with lime, pandan with ginger) with 40 adults aged 22 to 35 who drink little or none. Then purchase intent at 50 to 70 THB.

## Reserves

- **Green-banana RS2 RTD (score 5), Ingredients & Nutrition, first reserve.** Unripe banana powder is already sold in Thailand with health claims, so only the ready-to-drink form is new. RS2 survival under pasteurisation and cold-fill is unproven. The agent cited Thai FDA notifications 445 to 448 for labels and claims; their thresholds are unchecked. This is the only idea that covers the Ingredients component, so it moves up if the team wants all three components covered.
- **Scent-release lid (score 6), Alternative Formats, second alternate.** air up sells a near-identical scent-led drinking product abroad. No evidence it sells in Thailand, which is absence only. No Thai food-grade aroma-insert supplier found.
- **GLP-1 savoury protein broth (score 6), New Trend-Driven Target, third alternate.** Its reason depends on association with a prescription medicine, which Thai food advertising rules probably bar (not verified). Use only if that association is dropped.
- **Seaweed soluble-fibre drink (score 6), Ingredients & Nutrition, excluded.** It is in the same concept space as the fibre soda the team already ruled out, so it is not counted as fresh. It can return only if the team shows it is distinct.
