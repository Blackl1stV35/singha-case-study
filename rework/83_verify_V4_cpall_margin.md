## 1. Answer

Question: what are the gross margin and the net margin of CP ALL Public Company Limited for 2024 and 2025, and of its convenience store business if reported separately?

- Group (CP ALL consolidated, all categories), gross margin: 22.72 per cent for 2025 and 22.58 per cent for 2024. Basis: gross profit from sales and services over sales and services revenue, which is the company's own basis. Tier P. Status SOURCED. The company prints these figures to two decimals in its year-end 2025 listed company snapshot on the SET site.
- Group, net margin: 2.76 per cent for 2025 and 2.57 per cent for 2024 when net profit attributable to equity holders is divided by total revenue. Tier DERIVED from P figures. On the sales revenue base the same profit gives 2.847 per cent (2025) and 2.643 per cent (2024), which the broker Trinity prints as 2.8 and 2.6 per cent (S). The company's own printed "NP Margin" is 3.12 per cent (2025) and 2.99 per cent (2024). Those values reproduce as profit for the year, before minority interests, over total revenue. The company does not state that basis.
- Convenience store business: gross and net margins are not sourced for either year (UNVERIFIED). The sourced convenience store figures are a 46 per cent share of 2025 revenue (P, the period is not shown on the extract) and the 3Q25 segment net profit of 5,202 million baht, up 16.4 per cent (S, news report, one quarter only).
- CP ALL alone (company-only, separate statements): not sourced. The only figures come from AI-generated summaries, which the policy does not allow as sources. They are kept as leads only.
- Effect on the 21.6 per cent proxy: a sourced figure exists for the group, so it can replace the proxy for the CP ALL operator line. It cannot replace the proxy as a convenience store margin. See section 5.

## 3. Basis and definitions

- Company gross margin. The company's gross margin is gross profit from sales and services over sales and services revenue. The Trinity exhibit gives sales of 990,663 and gross profit of 225,117 for 2025, which gives 22.72 per cent and confirms the printed figure. Other income (32,060 for 2025 per the Trinity exhibit) is excluded.
- Total revenue. Total revenue for 2025 is 1,022,142.85 million baht, and it includes other income. The SET snapshot states it as 1,022,143 million baht.
- Profit for the year and attributable profit. Trinity's 2025 lines give EBT 38,919, tax 7,045 and minority interest 3,669. EBT less tax is 31,874, which rounds to the 31,875 figure in the Quartr lead. Less minority interest, 31,875 less 3,669 gives 28,206, the net profit that the company prints as its net profit. For 2024, EBT 35,893 less tax 6,381 gives 29,512, and less minority interest 4,166 gives 25,346. The printed NP margins match profit for the year over total revenue, so the company's printed NP margin is the pre-minority basis. The company itself does not state the basis.

Can a sourced figure replace it? Partly.

- A sourced group figure exists: 22.72 per cent for 2025 and 22.58 per cent for 2024, both primary (F1 and F2). This replaces the proxy for the CP ALL operator line only.

- The figure is not a median. The proxy is a median of three peers, and the CP ALL figure is one company.
