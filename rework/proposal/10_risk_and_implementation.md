## Implementation plan (after the competition)

No step starts before the competition ends. Each gate is a question, and R&D or the panel sets the value.

1. **Formulation bench test (R&D).** Measure dissolved CO2 and persistence at 5, 20 and 35 C serves, at 0.55 volume in 330 mL. Gate question: what retention will R&D accept, compared with the published threshold [J]? Feeds the dose, the fill, and whether any fizz wording is possible.
2. **Sensory test (panel).** Compare pH values, with 3.5 included, and sucralose levels. Gate question: which pH band and sweetness level does the panel accept? Feeds the acid load and sweetener level, and replaces the sweetness assumption.
3. **Pack and film specification.** Moisture barrier and seal for the pouch, then storage testing. Gate question: how much gas build-up in a sealed pouch is acceptable, and which film meets it? Feeds the pouch choice, the largest cost input.
4. **Regulatory opinion.** Written view from a Thai regulatory adviser on product class, condition TH3, label wording and the excise base, plus a medical adviser's view on potassium. Gate: written opinion and a proposed class put to the organiser. Feeds the sweetener condition, label and excise base.
5. **Cost quotes through the organiser's process.** Thai prices for pouch, print, potassium bicarbonate and citric acid, requested only once the organiser approves outreach. Gate: quotes replace the placeholders. Feeds cost per cube and carton size.
6. **Pilot batch.** Small batch only after steps 1 to 4 pass. Gate: all four met, class settled, emblem signed off. Feeds the pilot channel and volume, which the organiser decides.

## Decisions owned by the organiser

None of these has been decided. The project does not decide them and has not contacted anyone about them.

- Product classification: beverage, edible ice or electrolyte drink.
- Sold unit: one cube per pouch (inferred, not confirmed with the organiser) and carton size of 6, 12 or 24 cubes. An overseas analogue [L] makes 400 to 600 ml per cube (P, SOURCED), against the 330 ml serve here (ASSUMPTION).
- The pilot channel.

## Limits

- Desk model only: no bench test, no sensory test, no measured fizz decay.
- Taste is untested. pH 3.5 is a working anchor. The 0.55-volume gate is not set; R&D must define it.
- The sucralose limit comes from an annex extract [E]. The potassium reference rests on secondary copies [C].
- Pouch and print costs are assumptions. The figure of about 128,580 cubes per 20 ft container (DERIVED, [H]) assumes 12-cube cartons and volume limits. It is not a load plan.
- Overseas analogues [I, L] are cited only because no domestic evidence for dissolvable cubes is among the sources used here. International expansion is out of scope.
- Several sources were unreadable or blocked (a climate table, a container PDF and three retailer listings). No figure relies on them.
- Claims are barred: no sugar-free, zero-sugar, sodium-free, health, electrolyte, hydration, potassium-benefit or validated-fizz claim. The composition may be stated as fact: potassium bicarbonate, citric acid and sucralose, with no sodium carbonate.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database.

S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets.

SOURCED: the page text was read; the public URL is listed under Sources.

UNVERIFIED: page text not read, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet read.

DERIVED: our arithmetic, inputs and tiers shown.

PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured.

ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.
