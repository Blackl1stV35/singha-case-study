## What the abstract states (short fragments, our paraphrase around them)

- Method: a two-step econometric model that splits the temperature effect on firms into a long-term trend part and a short-term heat-wave and cold-wave part.
- Scope: six liquid refreshment beverage (LRB) categories. The abstract itself does not name the country, the period or the data source.
- Long-term trend: demand rising "about 0.21% (63 million gallons)" a year on account of the rising temperature trend.
- Short-term, heat wave: weekly demand "increases by about 2.1% per degree".
- Short-term, cold wave: weekly demand "decreases by only 0.4% per degree".
- Asymmetry: the heat-wave response is about five times the cold-wave response.
