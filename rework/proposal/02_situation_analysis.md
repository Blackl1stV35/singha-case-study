## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the page text is saved in rework/raw and was read; this is the repository rule. UNVERIFIED: the page was not saved, or only a snippet or summary was read; a figure stays UNVERIFIED until its page is saved. DERIVED: our arithmetic, with the inputs and their tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## What the situation means for the cube

1. **Price and cost are not settled.** The Singha can (about 45 THB per litre; S3) is the anchor. A price ladder of 15, 25 and 35 THB per 250 ml is an ASSUMPTION and is not agreed. At 330 ml it gives about 20, 33 and 46 THB (DERIVED from 60, 100 and 140 THB per litre), the lowest about 1.3 times the Singha can (DERIVED: 19.8 ÷ 15).
2. **Cost per cube is 3.90 THB** at pH 3.5 in a 12-cube carton. DERIVED: reactants 0.18, pouch 1.30, print 1.50 and processing 0.50 give 3.48, plus a carton share of 0.42 (retail carton price of 5 THB per 12 cubes; S, UNVERIFIED). Reactant cost and processing are ASSUMPTION, and print is a placeholder.
3. **The pouch base is 1.30 THB**, a chosen value (ASSUMPTION) near the midpoint of a non-Thai pouch listing of USD 0.03 to 0.05 per piece (S10, UNVERIFIED). At the Bank of Thailand mid-rate of about 33.55 THB per USD (S9, tier P, SOURCED), that range is 1.01 to 1.68 THB, midpoint 1.34 (DERIVED). The cost range is 0.67 to 2.70 THB, with its ends from unverified snippets (S10). Across it, other lines fixed, the cube costs about 3.27 to 5.30 THB (DERIVED: 3.90 − 1.30 + pouch). A 3.00 THB pouch is a labelled stress case only and gives 5.60 THB (DERIVED). One cube per pouch is inferred, not confirmed with the organiser.
4. **The central claim is a new consumption form**: a dissolvable lime cube that turns water into a fizzy lime drink. The cube makes no sugar claim.
5. **Excise classification may matter.** Item 02.02 may apply to a dissolvable cube. An intensive sweetener counts even with no sugar, which would place the cube in the 0 THB per-litre band, with 14% ad valorem if the base applies (S4). Classification is unresolved.
6. **Acid load is untested.** At the 0.9-volume dose under review (discussed below), the calculation gives potassium bicarbonate 1.33 g (DERIVED: 13.3 mmol of CO2 × 100.1 g per mole) and citric acid 0.85 g at stoichiometric balance. Citric acid is 3.42 g at pH 3.5 and 5.97 g at pH 3.0. The pH 3.5 total is about 10.4 g per litre (DERIVED: 3.42 ÷ 0.33). No sourced lemon-lime acid range is available. Taste is untested.
7. **The dose is under review.** The design is 0.9 volume of CO2 per 330 ml. Its gate is at least 0.6 volume dissolved at 10 minutes at serves of 30 °C or below (ASSUMPTION for the gate value). Solubility at 1 atm is 0.655 volume at 30 °C and 0.592 at 35 °C (S5, Lange-attributed table, tier S, UNVERIFIED). A NIST-based cross-check gives 0.676 and 0.600 (S5, tier P, UNVERIFIED). Both sets sit below 0.9 volume, so the dose is above the ceiling at Thai temperatures. A 0.9-volume dose would lose about 16% at once at 25 °C and 27 to 34% at 30 to 35 °C (DERIVED). The 25 °C value of 0.759 volume is for pure CO2 at 1 atm; in a sealed pack the dose holds fully only at or below about 19 °C (DERIVED). Open-glass retention is unmeasured.
   - At 0.55 volume, the dose stays below solubility at every modelled Thai serve, including 35 °C. Gate not set; to be defined by R&D.
8. **Sale-blocking permission.** Sucralose is recommended, not chosen. The Thai permissions for sucralose and potassium bicarbonate are open, and each blocks sale until confirmed. Steviol glycosides are the fallback.
9. **Demand is untested.** No consumer test of the cube has been run. A purchase-intent model, not a consumer finding, puts men aged 25 to 34 ahead in about 94% of draws, by about 0.5 to 0.7 points (DERIVED; ASSUMPTION inputs).
10. **Brand.** The product name omits SINGHA. The SINGHA name and emblem are authorised for this proposal; brand-owner and legal sign-off sit with Singha's department.
