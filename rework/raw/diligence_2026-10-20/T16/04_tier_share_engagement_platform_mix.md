# Influencer tiers, engagement and platform mix in Thailand, trade press, read 20 October 2026

Task T16. Route: WebFetch (model-written extracts with quoted Thai sentences; translation ours). Tier S for all.

## IdeasLabs (Thai MarTech company) database, first half of 2025

- Marketing Oops, 24 September 2025, https://www.marketingoops.com/reports/research/influencer-marketing/ (headline: nano-micro influencers hold 71 percent of the market, ROI up 70 percent). Body text: nano 6 percent, micro 9 percent, real users 22 percent, mid-tier and macro together under 7 percent; nano and micro average engagement rate 6 to 9 percent; mass entertainment pages 3 to 4 percent; storytelling and review content nearly 2 times the engagement of ordinary ad content; budget growth over 23 percent year on year; publisher-services plus KOL campaigns grew 46 percent on average, some months 70 percent; TikTok about 38 percent of the platform mix, Instagram 25 percent, Facebook 19.5 percent, Lemon8 12.9 percent, YouTube and X together 5 percent. No sample size or method. Conflict kept: the headline's 71 percent does not match the body's tier shares (6 plus 9 percent), and the "ROI 70 percent" is a growth figure.
- Brand Buffet, 2 November 2025, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2025/11/ideaslabs-reveals-nano-micro-influencer-win-the-influencer-marketing-game/ : same producer and period; here nano 47.6 percent, micro 23.9 percent, real user 22 percent, mid-tier and macro together under 7 percent (the introduction attributes 47.6 percent to nano and micro together; the body attributes it to nano alone). Same ER 6 to 9 percent for nano and micro and 3 to 4 percent for mass pages. A brand can fund several nano or micro creators with the budget of one macro creator. No fee figures. Spread for the nano and micro share: 15 percent (Marketing Oops body), 71 percent (Marketing Oops headline), 71.5 percent (Brand Buffet 47.6 plus 23.9). Not reconciled.
- Marketing Oops, 15 June 2026, https://www.marketingoops.com/reports/industry-insight/influencer-marketing-2026/ : IdeasLabs data for the first quarter of 2026; brands move away from mega influencers; four creator groups still receive brand budgets: foodie, mom and kid, lifestyle, beauty; TikTok and Facebook Reels named as key short-video channels; no numbers.

## AnyMind Group, State of Influencer Marketing Report 2026 (AnyTag first-party data, full-year 2025, 10 APAC markets)

- Brand Buffet, 6 February 2026, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/02/state-of-influencer-marketing-report-2026/ and Marketing Oops, 6 February 2026, https://www.marketingoops.com/reports/industry-insight/anymind-influencer-marketing-thailand-trend/ : nearly 7,000 campaigns and over 1.1 million influencers; results-driven campaigns 42.47 percent of all campaigns in 2025 (30.67 percent in 2024; 28.24 percent in 2023); in Thailand 66.0 percent of campaigns ran on TikTok, Instagram 18.9 percent, Facebook 7 percent; food and drink took the largest budget share, 39.4 percent (fashion and beauty 15.0 percent, business 9.6 percent, lifestyle and home 7.4 percent); nano influencers on TikTok averaged an engagement rate of 6.77 percent and on X 4.10 percent; nano is 1,000 to 10,000 followers and micro 10,000 to 100,000; the article suggests hiring 50 to 100 nano influencers for the budget of one celebrity. No fees, no tier shares, no ROI.
- Marketing Oops, 5 September 2026, https://www.marketingoops.com/exclusive/trending-exclusive/influencer-marketing-2026-2/ : AnyMind report "Influencer to Commerce in Thailand 2026" (2023 to 2025 data): TikTok above 50 percent of influencer campaigns in 2025; Instagram and YouTube next; on TikTok Shop for some brands affiliates can bring up to 60 percent of revenue against 40 percent for the brand storefront; communities lift brand revenue by up to 25 percent in some cases; influencer content did better than brand content on cost per engagement and cost per thousand impressions (no figures).
- Spread for TikTok's share of Thai influencer campaigns: 66.0 percent (AnyMind APAC report, 2025), above 50 percent (AnyMind Thailand report, 2025), about 38 percent (IdeasLabs, first half 2025, platform mix). Different bases; not reconciled.

## Tier definitions used by Thai trade press

- Marketing Oops, 18 May 2026, https://www.marketingoops.com/how-to-4/influencer-tier-and-brand-objective/ : mega 1 million followers and above (reach, awareness, launches); macro 100,000 to 1 million (awareness, consideration); micro about 10,000 to 100,000 (consideration, conversion; KPIs engagement, CPC, click, conversion); nano 1,000 to 10,000 (conversion campaigns, affiliate, social commerce on TikTok Shop and Shopee Affiliate; KPIs CPC, conversion, engagement); "end user" real customers (loyalty, retention, advocacy). No mid band is defined. The figure also quotes AnyMind qualitatively: higher-reach influencers may have lower follower trust and smaller ones stronger engagement and conversion.
- Brand Buffet, 2 October 2024, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2024/10/cmmu-do-and-dont-guideline-for-influencer-marketing/ : CMMU (Mahidol University) guidance: match the influencer to the brand and check past conduct and controversies; do not judge by follower count alone; micro and nano creators often build more trust. No disclosure or legal rules in the article.
