## 2. Retail reach

- CP ALL, listed-company snapshot, https://lssmedia.setlink.set.or.th/2025/YE/CPALL-YE68-ListedCompanySnapshot-EN.html (read 20 October 2026, P, company filing snapshot). "bringing the total number of stores to 15,945 by the end of 2025". The same document reports "opened a total of 700 new stores in Thailand" and "a total of 138 international stores, comprising 112 in Cambodia and 26 in Lao PDR". The figure is therefore most likely Thailand only, but the document does not say so explicitly. Tier P; scope read as Thailand (inference, stated).
