# Strategy: Lime Cube (19 October 2026)

Scope: the Thai domestic market only.

## Tier key

## 1. Target segment

The lead segment is men aged 25 to 34; men aged 45 to 59 are a later volume segment.

- Alcohol drinking is 68.6% of men aged 25 to 44 and 65.4% of men aged 45 to 59 (2024) [P, UNVERIFIED; source 1]. These drinkers are a PROXY for the non-alcoholic audience, and 25 to 44 stands in for 25 to 34.
- Potency is 1.15 for men 25 to 34 against men 45 to 59 (90% interval 1.03 to 1.28) [DERIVED; UNVERIFIED inputs; ASSUMPTION].
- Everyday sugar-substitute drink use, a PROXY for sweetener use, is higher at 45 to 59 (1.0%) than at 25 to 44 (0.6%) [P, UNVERIFIED; source 2]. It does not support the lead segment.
- The hot-season occasion is a hypothesis; no climate figure is used [ASSUMPTION]. No mixing or chilled-use test has been run.

## 2. Proposition, format and cost

**Strategic position: compete on form, not flavour.** Singha already sells a 330 mL lime can at THB 15, launched March 2026 [P, SOURCED; source 11; S for a news reprint, source 12]. Lime Cube overlaps it on flavour; the difference is form: one dissolvable cube per pouch that makes the drink in the glass, not a ready-to-drink can. The company claims sugar-free with 0% calories for its can; Lime Cube makes no sugar-free claim. Singha's lemon soda is a separate product [S, SOURCED; source 18]. The group's own lime can is the closest substitute, so cannibalisation is tracked as a KPI.

The format is one dissolvable cube per 330 mL serve, which releases carbon dioxide and makes the drink [ASSUMPTION]. Waterdrop [S, SOURCED; source 16] and Ricola [P, SOURCED; source 15] have launched overseas cubes, cited only because no domestic example was found.

At 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL, the dose is about 8.1 mmol [DERIVED]: potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 0.52 g stoichiometric and 2.10 g at the working pH of 3.5; sucralose 33 mg (about 100 mg/kg). The carbonating agent is potassium bicarbonate; no sodium carbonate is used. Potassium is 317 mg, 9.1% of the Thai reference of 3,500 mg a day [DERIVED; S, SOURCED from third-party copies of Notification 182, primary not opened; source 3], a composition figure, not a benefit claim. Citric acid, carbonic acid and water pKa values are S, UNVERIFIED [source 20]. Taste is untested.

One cube per pouch, in cartons of 12 [ASSUMPTION]. A 20-foot container holds about 128,580 cubes [DERIVED; container dimensions S, source 10].

Cost: THB 3.84 per cube in a 12-cube carton. The 12-cube cost includes a carton share of THB 0.42 per cube (THB 5 per 12-cube carton, from a retail box listing used as a PROXY). Reactants THB 0.12 [DERIVED; source 7; PROXY]. Pouch THB 1.30, print THB 1.50 and processing THB 0.50 are ASSUMPTIONS. An overseas pouch listing (USD 0.03 to 0.05; S, PROXY, UNVERIFIED; source 8) is THB 1.01 to 1.68 at the 15 October 2026 rate of 33.55 THB per USD [P, SOURCED; source 6]. The pouch band of THB 0.67 to 2.70 is an ASSUMPTION, giving THB 3.21 to 5.24 per cube.

## 3. Price and channel

No channel is chosen: convenience stores, online marketplaces or in-store sampling.

- Singha's can sells at THB 15 [P, SOURCED; source 11]. Singha Soda, a different product, is about THB 8 a can [DERIVED; source 19].
- Shelf price THB 10 to 20 (base THB 15), ex-factory share 60% [ASSUMPTION]. The base price equals the can; gross margin at THB 15 is 57.3% before freight and overhead [DERIVED].
- Break-even shelf price is THB 6.40 at base cost and THB 5.35 to 8.73 across the pouch band [DERIVED]. Registration, tooling, trademark and marketing are excluded.
- About 15,945 7-Eleven stores at end-2025, read as Thailand-only by inference [P, SOURCED; source 13].
- On these inputs, a first purchase costs about THB 1,150 in paid clicks (THB 11.5 per click [S, SOURCED, PROXY; source 14]; 1% conversion [ASSUMPTION]), about 223 cubes to recover at THB 5.16 contribution per serve [DERIVED].

## 4. Decisions, limits and claims

Open decisions:

- Dose: 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL is recommended. Solubility at 35 °C is 0.592 volume [S, SOURCED, Lange-attributed table; source 4]; the desk model keeps the full dose dissolved from 5 to 35 °C [DERIVED; no bench test]. R&D is to set the release gate; no value is given.
- Sweetener: sucralose at about 100 mg/kg is within the 300 mg/kg limit for category 14.1.4 under condition 127 [P, SOURCED; source 5]; only the relevant flavoured-drink rows were read, not the full annex. The amount rests on an untested sweetness target. Condition TH3 applies only to electrolyte drinks.
- Classification and sub-category placement within 14.1.4 are open; placement decides the sucralose limit, so a regulatory adviser must decide.
- Brand: the SINGHA lion appears only as the organiser's brand; the product is named Lime Cube. The lion emblem was confirmed for this proposal. Brand-owner and legal sign-off sit with Singha's department and are not held here; the alcohol-cue question is unchecked.

Limits:

- Desk model only: no bench, sensory or shelf test. Fizz duration is a surface-only upper bound; no decay curve is measured. The only threshold found, 0.44 g/L CO2 for sparkling water, is from an abstract [S, abstract only; source 21] and is a PROXY for a soft drink.
- One cube per pouch and the one-component reading are unconfirmed; no Thai reactant or pouch price was found.
- The NSO inputs are UNVERIFIED, so the segment ranking is a model output.
- Two Singha pages conflict on the parent company; the strategy does not depend on it [source 18].
- The name Lime Cube overlaps the competitor's lime flavour name. A register and shopper-confusion check is open, and no legal view is given.
- No desk figure answers the question for people on potassium-retaining medicines or with kidney disease.
- The EP 4,516,115 A2 pending status is the register mirror's assumption [P, SOURCED; source 17]; US 5,254,355 A is unchecked, with no freedom-to-operate view.

Claims: no benefit, nutrition or function statement is made, and Lime Cube carries no sugar-free status. The composition is stated as fact: potassium bicarbonate, citric acid and sucralose, with no sodium carbonate. The fizz is a mechanism only. No Thai novelty, consumer result, market size or sales forecast is claimed.
