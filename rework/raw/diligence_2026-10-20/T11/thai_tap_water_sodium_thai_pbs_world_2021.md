## Use in record 74

- Water enters the made-up drink at 330 mL per serve. At 100 and 200 mg per litre it adds 33 and 66 mg of sodium per serve (DERIVED). The WHO typical value of 20 mg per litre (6.6 mg per serve) is therefore not a ceiling for Thai tap water; this reported figure is five to ten times higher.
- No Thai measurement of tap or bottled water sodium was opened. Closed as a stated limit; the label and any claim test must name the water, and the water's own sodium must be reported separately.
