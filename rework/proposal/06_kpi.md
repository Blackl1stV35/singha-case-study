# KPI section: Lemon Soda (20 October 2026)

Status: desk-based. No bench, sensory, humidity or consumer test has run; no outside party has been contacted. Every target below is an assumption until the pilot reads.

## Tiers

Tiers are defined once, in Appendix A.

## KPI tree

Objective: repeat use of Lemon Soda, a dissolvable pouch cube made into a lemon drink on the spot. As a new form, it is measured by trial and repeat, not only sales.

Overlap: the Singha Lemon Soda can (330 mL; listed on the company product page, tier S; on sale online on 20 October 2026, tier S; Sources 3 and 8) is the comparison product, and it matches Lemon Soda on flavour. Its online prices read on 20 October 2026 are THB 17.00 for a single can at Lotus's Shop Online and THB 13.54 to 13.75 a can in 24-can packs (pack prices S, SOURCED; per-can prices DERIVED). Across six retail rows the band is THB 13.54 to 17.00, with a median of THB 13.75. These are online prices; no store shelf price was read. Lemon Soda differs on form (a cube made into the drink in the glass, one cube per pouch) and on pack (no ready-to-drink can). The company's product page says the can has 0% sugar and no calories (COMPANY CLAIM); that claim is not checked here, and Lemon Soda makes no sugar or calorie claim.

- **Trial:** units per pilot store per week; national listings.
- **Repeat:** share of first buyers who buy again.
- **Price:** shelf price realised; ex-factory share of shelf.
- **Contribution:** ex-factory revenue less variable cost, per cube.
- **Sell-through:** share of placed stock sold within 90 days.
- **Cannibalisation:** share of Lemon Soda buyers who would otherwise have bought the Singha Lemon Soda can.

## Kill criteria

1. **Sweetener:** no permitted sweetener for the classification (sucralose or steviol glycosides). No sale.
2. **Dose:** R&D restates the 0.55-volume gate before the run. A dose or acid failure gets one rework, then the format stops.
3. **Taste:** blind preference below 55% at pH 3.5 (ASSUMPTION). One acid and pH re-set, then stop.
4. **Pouch:** in-pack reaction above 0.5% at 35 °C (ASSUMPTION). No launch until the barrier changes.
5. **Potassium and class:** a medical reviewer rejects about 317 mg per serve, or potassium sits above the ceiling for the chosen class (about 960 mg per litre against 195 mg per litre if the Gate 1 opinion classes the cube as a mineral-salt beverage). No sale until the class or the formula changes.
6. **Economics:** contribution below 3.51 baht per cube before excise on the recommended six-cube carton (DERIVED: 4.91 baht base less 1.40 baht for a 2.70 baht pouch, the ASSUMPTION high case; the cost excludes binder and flavour, not priced), or repeat below 15% on two cohort reads (ASSUMPTION). Stop the scale-up.
7. **Pilot reads:** below 3 units per store per week on two reads. Stop expanding that route.
8. **Paid social:** cost per first purchase above THB 27.38. Paid social stops.
9. **Food safety:** no verified food-safety certificate for a named plant (Gate 6). No pilot batch.
10. **Creator conduct:** a compliance breach not fixed within 24 hours, or a controversy about a creator that cannot be cleared within a week. Creator activity stops at once.
11. **Cannibalisation:** 7% or more of cube buyers say they would otherwise have bought a can. This is a review trigger, not a stop: the value model is rerun before any scale.
