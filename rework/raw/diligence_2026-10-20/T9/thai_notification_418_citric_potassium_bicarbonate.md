# Thai food additive notification: citric acid and potassium hydrogen carbonate in flavoured drinks (read 20 October 2026)

> **SUPERSEDED (added 20 October 2026 after verification).** Notification 418 Annexes 1 and 2 were repealed by Notification 468 B.E. 2568, in force from 12 December 2025 (see `thai_notification_468_repeal_and_sucralose.md`). This page is corroboration only: it shows that the earlier instrument set the same level. The authority for the product is Notification 468 (`rework/raw/moph_468_annex_flavoured_drinks_extract_2026-10-19.md`). Do not cite Notification 418 as current law.

Source: Ministry of Public Health Notification No. 418 B.E. 2563 (2020), Annex 1, Thai text. URL: https://exfood.fda.moph.go.th/law/data/announ_moph/P418.pdf . Tier P (law). Read 20 October 2026 from rendered page images (PDF pages 154 and 422; the text layer drops the Thai glyphs).
- CITRIC ACID (กรดซิตริก), INS 330, function: acidity regulator, antioxidant, colour-retention agent, sequestrant. Category 14.1.4 "เครื่องดื่มแต่งกลิ่นรส" (flavoured drinks): "ปริมาณที่เหมาะสม" (quantity as needed, i.e. good manufacturing practice); year set 2559.
- POTASSIUM HYDROGEN CARBONATE (โพแทสเซียมไฮโดรเจนคาร์บอเนต), INS 501(ii), also named potassium bicarbonate; function: acidity regulator, foaming agent (สารช่วยให้ฟอง), stabiliser. Category 14.1.4: "ปริมาณที่เหมาะสม"; year 2559.
So both reactants are permitted in flavoured drinks without a numeric ceiling. Translation of the Thai quantity phrase is ours.
