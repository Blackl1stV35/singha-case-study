## Extract (Thai as printed, team translation)

- "ซึ่งมีมูลค่าตลาดรวมอยู่ที่ 56,000 ล้านบาท ในปี 2019 เพิ่มขึ้นเกือบ 12% จากที่ปีก่อนหน้าอยู่ที่ราว 50,900 ล้านบาท โดยส่วนแบ่งของตลาดน้ำอัดลมไทยแยกตามประเภท ได้แก่ น้ำอัดลมสีดำ 71% น้ำอัดลมสี 23% และน้ำอัดลมไม่มีสี 6%" (A total market of THB 56,000 million in 2019, up almost 12 percent from about THB 50,900 million the year before; split by type: black soda 71 percent, colour soda 23 percent, uncoloured (clear) soda 6 percent.)
- No source is printed for the market value or the type split. The next sentence cites Nielsen only for brand shares (Coca-Cola 35.7 percent, Pepsi 25.4 percent, Fanta 15.6 percent in 2019).
- The same article says Pepsi's 7UP was being developed as a clear, healthier product ("เซเว่น-อัพ ฟรี", no sugar, no colour, no caffeine).

## Growth check

- The article says the 2019 market rose "almost 12 percent" from about 50,900 million. Its own printed figures (56,000 against 50,900) give 10.0 percent. The article is internally inconsistent on growth. Neither growth figure is used in the record.

## Derived (script key `colour_clear_segment`)

- Clear soda at 6 percent of THB 56,000 million is about THB 3,360 million for 2019 (DERIVED, source of the split not named). Colour plus clear (non-cola) was 29 percent in 2019. For 2024 to 2025, Bangkokbiznews gives colour soda at THB 17,300 million (NielsenIQ, September 2024 to August 2025), and Thansettakij gives 17,391 million with no source or period printed beside it. No share is drawn from these figures here (see `nielseniq_press_series_2023_2026.md`).
- Lemon-lime is a part of clear soda (Sprite and 7UP are the clear brands) but the article does not say so. No current lemon-lime or clear value was found.
