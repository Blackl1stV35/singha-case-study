# In-house capability assessment: Track A and Track D (draft for team consensus)

Reading of "in-house": the Singha group's own plants, staff and certified sites. The candidate has no plants and cannot use group assets (`singha-group-perspective`). Every answer below is a capability benchmark, not an access route.

## Verdicts

| Track | Route | End-to-end status |
|---|---|---|
| A: zero-sugar apple-ginger sparkling soda, 330 ml can | in-house with partner | conditional |
| D: sugar-free sparkling botanical tea, can | partner required | not presentable |

## What the evidence shows

**Plants.** The company's own group page lists eight factories by name only (Pathum Thani, Khon Kaen Brewery, Singha Beverage, Chiangmai Beverage, Suratthani Beverage, Wang Noi Beverage, Boonrawd Asia Beverage, Mahasarakham Beverage). A third brewery at Bang Len appears in trade sources from 2012 to 2015 but not on the list. Plant counts conflict across sources (seven, eight and nine). No location, product or certificate is given for any plant.

**Line data.** The only line data are from a 2015 vendor article:
- Wang Noi: PET still-water line, no documented carbonation or can filling.
- Surat Thani: a carbonation unit feeding a glass line for soda water (325 ml and 400 ml glass). No can carbonation is evidenced.
- Chiang Mai, Mahasarakham, Boonrawd Asia: names only.

**Food safety.** No FSSC 22000, ISO 22000, HACCP or GMP certificate is verified for any plant, including the Singha Beverage FSSC claim in the corpus. The FSSC public register did not return results in the scan.

**Can product.** A sugar-free 330 ml Singha Lemon Soda is sold in Thailand (product-level evidence of canned sugar-free carbonation). Its filling plant is not named in any source. The Thai FDA registration number on the can would identify the manufacturer.

**Tea.** Group RTD tea runs through DRINK ENTERPRISE, a 2023 joint venture of Boonrawd Trading, Modulus Venture and PTT Oil and Retail. Its production site is not named. The corpus states the group has less depth in sugar-free RTD tea.

**Flavour and R&D.** TFF/TCFF is the named flavour and R&D co-development partner. Its GHP, HACCP and FSSC claims (corpus line 167) are unchecked. The corpus lists no disclosed carbonation or water-processing capability for TFF (line 171).

**Fibre.** No in-house soluble-fibre product, supplier or dosing line was found.

## Gaps for each track

- **Track A:** can-scale carbonation (undocumented); fibre dosing and stability in a carbonated acidic can (unverified); apple-ginger flavour compounding (TFF/TCFF, unchecked); certified can-filler (unnamed).
- **Track D:** tea and botanical extraction (no in-house line, no named partner, Thai tea extract not searched); roselle and jasmine extracts (no supplier, price or certificate of analysis); clarity and cold-creaming control; hot-fill or aseptic can route for tea (not identified); butterfly-pea colour (see `13_track_D_end_to_end.md`).

## What would settle it before 22 October

1. Thai FDA food-registration check on a Singha Lemon Soda 330 ml can. This names the filler and settles in-house or co-packer.
2. DBD registry check for each Singha entity (objective and address).
3. FSSC 22000 public register search, by entity name and for candidate co-packers.
4. TFF/TCFF written answer on flavour capability and certificate numbers.
5. Co-packer enquiries (carbonated 330 ml can, certificates, MOQ and fee): Osotspa, Simple Foods, Nawon, NPV.

## Limits

- The company-stated plant list is names only. Plant-level capability for 2026 is not verified.
- The corpus is China-focused and has no Singapore or Korea content. The Singapore and Korea regulatory facts rest on the scans.
- Whether Singha would choose to make either product is outside this assessment.
