# Lemon Soda: executive summary (20 October 2026)

Proposer: Singha Corporation. Holding company: Boon Rawd Brewery, in the company's own description; press and company sources describe the group differently.

## The proposal in one paragraph

Lemon Soda is a dissolvable drink cube for domestic Thailand. One cube in one 330 mL serve makes a lemon drink in the glass; carbonation is designed and not yet measured. It is a new form of consumption, not a new flavour. Men aged 25 to 34 are the proposed lead, a proposal decision and a test, not a finding: their desk margin over men aged 35 to 44 is small and its range includes zero, so a difference of zero cannot be ruled out. The lead rests on sugar-sweetened drinking, so transfer to a sweetener-only product is untested. Men aged 45 to 59 are a later volume segment, pended. The desk case is small. At 200,000 cubes a year and THB 500,000 fixed cost, the net present value is THB 0.17 million after excise and tax, and across the stated ranges the chance of a positive value is 0.26, about one in four. The proposal is a gated proof programme, not a launch: a pilot capped at THB 84,686 tests demand and switching from the can, the two inputs that decide the value.

## What is handed over

- Dose: 0.55 volume of carbon dioxide per 330 mL, a working design value. It is the carbon dioxide the reaction releases at 0°C; how much stays dissolved at serve is not measured. R&D restates the fizz pass rule before any test (section 10, Gate 3).
- Working pH of 3.5 (the desk model gives about 3.4 to 3.5); taste untested.
- Composition, stated as fact: potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, sucralose, mannitol as binder and filler (tableting grade, INS 421; isomalt is the fallback) and natural lemon flavour. The recipe specifies no sodium salt, no sodium carbonate and no lubricant, and a supplier declaration for each ingredient is required before pack print (Gate 9). Sodium from the recipe is 0 to about 2 mg a serve (DERIVED, resting on supplier declarations still to be obtained). Water adds its own sodium: 0 to about 7 mg a serve at the WHO typical level (P), or about 33 to 68 mg at the level reported for Thai tap water (S, a news report; not measured). No sodium-free or low-sodium claim is made. Binder and flavour amounts are assumptions until a cube is weighed.
- One cube per single-serve pouch is the team's setting; a six-cube carton (190 x 150 x 50 mm) is an assumption. No nutrition, health or performance claim is made, and no sugar-free, zero-sugar, hydration, sodium-free or fizz wording is printed.
- Domestic only. No dissolvable drink cube was found on sale in Thailand in desk searches of Thai retail listings read on 20 October 2026, although cubes sell overseas (for example Ricola Drink Cubes; company release, P). The claim is "not found in desk searches", not "none exists".

## The nearest competing product

The nearest competing product is the Singha Lemon Soda can, a 330 mL can. It is listed on the company product page (S, company). A company release reprinted by the press gives the launch as August 2020 (S), and two later articles say late 2020 (S); the launch price of the lemon can was THB 16 in 2020 (S). On 20 October 2026 a single can was listed online at THB 17.00 at Lotus's Shop Online, and 24-can packs at THB 330 at 7-Eleven and Lotus's and THB 325 at Makro PRO, which is THB 13.54 to 13.75 a can (pack prices SOURCED, per-can prices DERIVED; S). Two of these 24-can prices are marked down. The 7-Eleven price of THB 330 is a promotion valid to 20 October 2026, the day it was read, and the page prints a list price of THB 408, which is THB 17.00 a can. The Makro PRO price of THB 325 is printed below an earlier price of THB 330. The Lotus's page showed no promotion or earlier price. The low end of the band is therefore partly a promotional online reading. Across six online retail rows, which include six-can packs, the band is THB 13.54 to 17.00 and the median is THB 13.75. A seventh Singha Lemon Soda row, a 7-Eleven six-can pack listed at THB 172 (THB 28.67 a can, about twice that retailer's 24-can price per can), was read and left out of the band as unreconciled; with it the band would run to THB 28.67 and the median would stay at THB 13.75. These are online prices; no store shelf price was read.

The can overlaps Lemon Soda on flavour, since both are lemon. The forms differ: Lemon Soda dissolves in the glass, one cube per pouch; the competitor is a ready-to-drink can. The company's page carries its own nutrition claim, 0 percent sugar and no calories (COMPANY CLAIM), and names sucralose and acesulfame potassium as its sweeteners. Lemon Soda makes no nutrition claim. At the assumed shelf price of THB 15 a serve (ASSUMPTION), Lemon Soda sits above the 24-can sugar-free packs of 7UP, Sprite and Schweppes sold through the brand owners' own online store accounts (THB 12.40 to 14.63 per 330 mL) and below the sugar-free single cans (THB 16.25 and THB 17.00). Some smaller packs and some other 24-can listings read higher, up to THB 19.29 per 330 mL, so THB 15 sits inside the full band of sugar-free lemon and lime can listings (THB 12.40 to 19.29 per 330 mL). The proposal therefore presents Lemon Soda as competing on form and on the Singha name, not on price (online prices read on 20 October 2026, S).

## The figures at the recommended dose

- Potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 0.52 g to neutralise it, or 2.10 g anhydrous for a working pH of 3.5 (DERIVED; acid constants S, UNVERIFIED in this handover).
- Binder and filler: mannitol 0.80 g and lemon flavour 0.05 g (ASSUMPTIONS; ranges 0.5 to 1.2 g and 0.02 to 0.10 g). Mannitol is listed for flavoured drinks at quantum satis in Notification 468 (P, flavoured-drink rows only). Central cube mass 3.79 g (range 3.46 to 4.24 g) at a 14 mm side, density 1.38 g/cm3 (DERIVED on these ASSUMPTIONS; not weighed). Packed unit 5.29 g with a 1.5 g pouch (ASSUMPTION). These figures were computed on the earlier 33 mg of sucralose; at the 55 mg now specified the listed components sum to about 3.82 g, and the central mass, range, density and packed unit are restated at Gate 5. Net weight on the label: 3.8 g specification, which holds on rounding, verified by weighing before print (Gate 9).
- Potassium 317 mg a serve, about 9 percent of the Thai reference of 3,500 mg a day (DERIVED; reference P, Notification 445 Annex 3, English translation published by the Thai FDA). Per litre it is about 960 mg. Medical review is required at Gate 2.
- Notification 195 (Thai mineral-salt beverages), clause 4(1.3), sets potassium at no more than 195 mg per litre where it is present. The text is unchanged from the Gazette original through the Thai FDA compilation of about 29 March 2023 (P, read from page images). No later amending notification was found by title on the Thai FDA list to 20 October 2026 (P, negative finding); a post-March-2023 amendment inside a general notification is not excluded. Whether the cube is a mineral-salt beverage under clause 3 is the Gate 1 question, and the proposed class is flavoured drink. If the Gate 1 opinion classes the cube as a mineral-salt beverage, its potassium would be about 4.9 times the ceiling (DERIVED) and the formula would have to change.
- Sucralose 55 mg a serve, about 167 mg per kg of drink (DERIVED), equal in sweetness to about 10 g of sugar per 100 mL (ASSUMPTION for the sweetness target; sucralose is 600 times sucrose, S). Category 14.1.4 permits 300 mg/kg for flavoured drinks under condition 127. The annex wording reads 'ready to drink'; the regulatory opinion settles it (P for the flavoured-drink rows only; the sub-category rows and the edition check are open).
- Solubility 0.592 volume at 35°C, the lowest in the 5 to 35°C serve range (S, SOURCED).
- Desk model (DERIVED; nothing is measured). Dissolution in 330 mL at 25°C takes about 6 minutes stirred and about 11 minutes not stirred for a central set of assumed inputs, and 3 to 22 minutes stirred and 5 to 74 minutes not stirred across the assumed ranges; this holds only if the cube breaks into fine pieces. At 20°C, surface loss alone keeps the dissolved carbon dioxide above the 0.44 g/L sparkling threshold for about 1.7 to 3.4 hours if the full dose dissolves at serve; bubble formation could cut this to about 15 minutes (champagne proxy, S, PROXY). If less than 41 percent of the dose is dissolved at serve, the drink starts below the threshold. The threshold comes from an abstract-only study of sparkling-water sensitivity (S, PROXY for a soft drink). None of these values is a gate or validated.
- Cost THB 4.09 per cube in the recommended 6-cube carton, before binder and flavour (DERIVED; pouch and print costs ASSUMPTIONS; no Thai quote): reactants 0.12, pouch and print 2.80, processing 0.50 and a carton share of 0.67 (THB 4.00 per 6-cube carton, an ASSUMPTION). The binder price is a PROXY from import unit values, THB 0.08 to 1.27 per cube, which puts the cost at THB 4.17 to 5.36 (DERIVED); the lemon flavour is not priced. The larger 12-cube carton costs THB 3.84 per cube (DERIVED), with a carton share of THB 0.42 per cube (THB 5 per 12-cube carton, from a retail box listing used as a PROXY).
- Price chain and contribution (DERIVED on PROXY and ASSUMPTION inputs): shelf price THB 15; invoice THB 9.34 after VAT and margins; excise THB 1.31; net THB 8.03; contribution THB 3.94 a cube after the cost of THB 4.09. Before excise, at the planning price of THB 9.00 (60 percent of THB 15, an assumption), the contribution is THB 4.91. The valuation uses THB 3.94. At the top of the binder price range the contribution falls to about THB 2.67 after excise, close to the floor of THB 2.54 after excise (THB 3.51 before excise).
- Value (DERIVED; a desk model on stated ranges, not market data): at 200,000 cubes a year, THB 500,000 fixed cost, five years and a 10 percent discount rate (assumption), the net present value after excise and tax is THB 0.17 million at base inputs. Across the stated ranges the chance of a positive value is 0.26 (0.40 before excise and tax; 0.58 on a simpler structure). The expected value is minus THB 0.88 million, and the 90 percent range is minus THB 3.40 million to plus THB 1.59 million. The test fixed in advance needs a chance of 0.95, and no basis reaches it. Break-even with no cannibalisation is 184,325 cubes a year after excise and tax (148,076 before excise). The operating break-even, which is fixed cost divided by the contribution after excise, is about 127,000 cubes a year. The value is zero when about 7.5 percent of cube buyers would otherwise buy a can; the review level is 7 percent. At the base volume the cube is a learning option, not a value driver.
- Line time: 200,000 cubes a year need about 79 hours of pouch-line time (45 to 152 hours), at 42 pouches a minute, which is 60 a minute at 70 percent efficiency (both ASSUMPTIONS; 60 is a mid-range maker figure, and pre-made pouch makers list about 15 to 80 a minute as maker ratings, dry-cycle at the top of the range, P, COMPANY CLAIM). That is about 2 percent of a 4,000-hour year. A co-packer minimum run of 500,000 to 1,500,000 units per product and formula (COMPANY CLAIM) is the binding constraint, not line time.
- About 128,316 cubes per 20 ft container in 6-cube cartons (21,386 cartons, a volume estimate at 92 percent of the container's internal volume; DERIVED; S specifications; packing an ASSUMPTION); payload 28.33 t. The larger 12-cube option holds about 128,316 cubes in 10,693 cartons (DERIVED, same inputs).

## Gates and asks (section 10 of the proposal)

1. Gate 3, dissolved CO2: bench cubes at 0.55 volume are made at a laboratory by M2. R&D restates the pass rule for a 0.55-volume dose, which the earlier rule cannot meet, and fixes the measurement method before the run; dissolved CO2 is measured at 5, 20 and 35°C.
2. Gate 4, taste: a blind sensory test at pH 3.5 and either side, with two or three sweetness levels and a check for a potassium off-note, against the Singha Lemon Soda can. The pass mark is at least 55 percent blind preference (ASSUMPTION). The 55 mg sucralose per serve is an assumption until then.
3. Gate 1, class and sweetener: a written Thai regulatory opinion on the class (flavoured drink, category 14.1.4, is the team's setting), condition 127 and the sub-category rows, and on whether the cube is a mineral-salt beverage under Notification 195 clause 3, with a search of the Royal Gazette index for any amendment after March 2023. Excise item 02.02 covers sweetened or flavoured drinks (P, SOURCED); the 14 percent ad valorem rate applies if the value base applies, the base was not read, and the value case uses the excise as an assumption. Condition TH3 applies only if the cube is classed as an electrolyte drink.
4. Gate 2, potassium: confirm the permission for potassium bicarbonate, and obtain a medical adviser's view on about 317 mg per serve (9 percent of the Thai reference), including people on potassium-retaining medicines or with kidney disease. Sale waits for that review.
5. Gate 5, pack and quotes: film and seal specification, a warm and humid storage test (35°C for 8 weeks, an assumption) started by M2, and Thai quotes, binder and flavour included, requested only after the Gate 0 record names the provider. No outside party has been contacted. The pass tests are an in-pack reaction of at most 0.5 percent of the bicarbonate at 35°C and a contribution of at least THB 3.51 per cube before excise (ASSUMPTION and DERIVED floor).
6. Gate 6, route: a named plant or co-packer for dry solids and pouch sealing, with a verified certificate for that site, set against the co-packer minimum run.
7. Gate 7, brand and copy: written sign-off on the lion and SINGHA from the mark holder, with confirmation of which entity owns the SINGHA mark in Thailand; rulings on alcohol cues, glassware, bubbles and lemon imagery; and a register search for the name.
8. Gates 8 and 9, pilot and pack release. Pilot routes: Route A is an in-store trial in 20 stores (three sampling days a store, 20 samples a day, THB 15 a sample; committed THB 18,000, range THB 12,000 to 30,000), read at weeks 8 and 13: scale if both reads are at or above 3 units per store per week, stop if both are below, otherwise hold. Route B, paid social, starts only at a 1.0 percent non-paid click-to-first-purchase rate (ASSUMPTION), with a cap of THB 27.38 per first purchase at a 30 percent repeat (THB 24.22 at 15 percent). A creator pilot uses nine confirmed creators, four mid-tier and five mega-tier, on commission-led offers; no flat fee is committed, and flat fees (THB 3,489,200 for one post each) are not recommended. Budget: committed THB 24,686, conditional THB 60,000 for paid social in a learning tranche of THB 20,000 and a scale tranche of THB 40,000, ceiling THB 84,686. Scale needs repeat of 15 percent or more in both cohorts, a contribution of at least THB 3.51 per cube before excise (THB 2.54 after excise), and a cannibalisation share under 7 percent. All targets are assumptions until the pilot reads. Pack release: net weight and shelf life are printed only from weighed and stored samples.

Section 10 sets the thirteen-week marketing pilot (M7 to M10), the concept pre-test, the stop and scale rules, a measured decision at M10 to M11 (scale one route, repeat once in the same stores, or stop), and twelve settings that the team has made, each with its basis, owner role and timing. Owners are roles, not people. The only ask is that the executive sponsor sign the Gate 0 record, which names one owner for each gate and the first budget ceiling. Sale is blocked until Gates 1, 2, 7 and 9 pass; a pilot batch also needs Gates 3 to 6 and 8.

## What is not yet known

- Taste, demand and Thai retail response are untested. Dissolution time, fizz time and pH are desk-model estimates; nothing has been measured or weighed.
- The binder is named (mannitol, with isomalt as fallback), but its price is an import-value proxy, the lemon flavour is not priced, and the supplier declarations that no sodium salt is present are not yet held.
- Pack pressure is an estimate only, from an assumed reaction limit; storage is not tested; novelty and freedom to operate are unassessed.
- The Thai patent register is unsearched, and the trademark register was not reached.
- The lion emblem is authorised for this proposal only. Written sign-off for the lion and SINGHA sits with the mark holder's brand and legal function and is not held here; which entity owns the SINGHA mark in Thailand is unconfirmed; the alcohol-cue question is unchecked. No campaign creative or sampling material is released before that sign-off.
- The size of the Thai carbonated soft-drink market is contested. NielsenIQ values printed by the press are about THB 70,772 million for the 12 months to November 2024 and about THB 66,000 million for the 12 months to January 2024 (S, press relays; the dataset was not opened). Values found for 2025 run from about THB 67,000 million (no source named) to about THB 76,900 million (Kasikorn Research Center, relayed on 8 September 2025, and a Marketeer forecast of 9 May 2025); none names NielsenIQ, and no source states whether its values are retail or manufacturer values. No market is sized from them in this proposal.
- No store shelf price was read for any product; the can prices above are online prices. No sourced Thai distributor margin was found. The closest sourced value is the National Statistical Office's 2023 wholesale value-added share for non-alcoholic beverages, 16.2 percent (P, PROXY; a value-added measure, not a margin), and the 10 to 20 percent band used in the model is an ASSUMPTION. The 21.6 percent retailer margin is a group all-category proxy; CP ALL's 2025 group gross margin is 22.7 percent (P, group figure), and its net margin is about 2.8 percent (DERIVED from P figures: attributable net profit over total revenue; the snapshot's own printed net profit margin is 3.12 percent).
- No creator has been approached, and whether the creators accept commission-only terms (an ASSUMPTION) is unknown. No clinician has reviewed the serve or the potassium wording.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, release or product page read in full, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source read and opened, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, handbooks reproduced online, and any company web page held at S (marked "S, company"). SOURCED: the page text was read, and the public URL is given. UNVERIFIED: not read, blocked, unreadable, or only a search snippet was read; a snippet that has not been opened is a lead, not evidence, and is never given P or S. DERIVED: our arithmetic, with the inputs and their tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced. COMPANY CLAIM: a statement the company makes about itself, not checked independently. A bracketed qualifier narrows what a tier covers: "negative finding" means a primary list was searched by title up to the date given and nothing was found, which does not exclude a change made elsewhere; "flavoured-drink rows only" means only those rows of a regulator's text were read; "read from page images" means the text was read from images of the pages; "company" after S marks a company's own web page that is held at S. The Singha Lemon Soda product pages, English and Thai, are held at S as undated marketing copy. This is a conservative hold, not a finding that the pages fail the P test, and it raises no figure to P.
