## Page 6 (part 1, page 6), team translation of the Thai

"From data of Frost & Sullivan (Thailand) Co., Ltd., considering sales in Thailand for the year ended 31 December 2567 (2024), the company ranks first in the NARTD industry with a market share of 15.6 percent in the company's main product groups, ranks first in carbonated soft drinks with a market share of 51.4 percent. Among sub-categories: the Coca-Cola brand has the highest share in cola with 47.9 percent; the Fanta and Sprite brands have the highest combined share in other flavoured carbonated drinks with 66.0 percent ... Frost & Sullivan expects the Thai NARTD market to grow at a CAGR of 7.4 percent over 2567 to 2572 (2024 to 2029) to THB 337.6 billion by 2572 (2029)."
- Page 6 also states 404 million unit cases sold in 2024, 5 plants in the licensed area, 22 production lines, about 495,000 outlets served.
- The text does not say whether the 51.4 percent is by value or by volume, nor whether it is TNCC alone or the Coca-Cola brand family across Thailand.
