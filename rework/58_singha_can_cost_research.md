## 2. What this means

- **The can's production cost per unit at Singha cannot be validated from public sources.** Metal is the largest share, but the share is 58 to 78% depending on the source and the definition.
- **Our metal figure is uncertain.** The 9.6 g used in `rework/53` is about half the 18 to 21 g implied by the IMARC model. Until the can size is confirmed, the THB 1.00 metal figure has a range of about THB 1.00 to 2.00 at the same aluminium price. The can size and the aluminium price are both to be confirmed.
- **Nothing here changes the verdict in `rework/57` or `rework/56`.** The pouch-versus-can comparison still has no production cost on the can side.

## Audit corrections (19 October 2026)

- **The 9.6 g metal figure** from earlier records is withdrawn (see [61](61_singha_can_cost_model.md)). The 14.4 to 18.5 g sheet range replaces it.
- **The IMARC plant model** gives about 18.0 g of can per can (1,000,000 g / 48,000 cans x 86.5%). It is not an 18 to 21 g range. Use 18.0 g.
- **Metal shares** (58%, 78%, 60 to 65%, 70%), the Thai can market size and the Thai Beverage Can volume growth come from search snippets. The pages are not saved in the repo. Unverified.
