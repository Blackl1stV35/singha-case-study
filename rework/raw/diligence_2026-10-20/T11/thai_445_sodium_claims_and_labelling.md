## Annex 4 general conditions

- 1.1(1) (Thai page ๑): the claims "free from" or "low" are not allowed if the food or food type naturally already meets the condition without any special production process, recipe modification or adjustment, to avoid misleading consumers into thinking only one maker's product has the property; a plain statement of fact about the food type as a whole is allowed (the notification's own example: vegetable cooking oil naturally contains no cholesterol).
- 1.1(2): where a food or food type can be developed by a different recipe, process or substituted raw material so that nutrient content differs between formulas of the same food type (example given: soy milk, plain and flavoured), the food may carry "free from" or "low" if the nutrient content meets the criteria.
- 2.1 Case 1: foods with a reference serving size in Annex 2 (and similar foods) use Table 1; a reference serving of more than 30 g or 2 tablespoons: calculate on the amount consumed per serving shown on the label and on the reference serving size. Case 2: other foods use Table 2, per 100 g or 100 mL.
- 2.2: if a nutrition claim is made and the food has more than 13 g total fat, 4 g saturated fat, 60 mg cholesterol, 300 mg sodium or 13 g total sugars per serving, that amount must be shown next to the claim.
- 2.4: if a nutrient and its amount are shown outside the nutrition panel without intent to claim, a statement that no claim is intended must go with it (examples given: "contains sodium 200 mg per 30 ml, not a low sodium food").
- Sugars in Annex 4 mean mono- and disaccharides ("น้ำตาล หมายถึง mono- และ di-saccharides"); free = less than 0.5 g.

## Clause 3 (who must carry a nutrition label under 445) and Annex 1

- Clause 3: nutrition labelling is required for (1) foods with a nutrition claim, (2) foods with a health claim, (3) foods using nutritional value in sales promotion, (4) other foods the Minister notifies. Beverages in dry form for sealed containers are notified separately (see thai_gda_394_446_466_beverages.md).
- Annex 1 section 2.3 (English page 10): in the standard format the mandatory nutrients are marked with an asterisk; the list includes sodium and potassium.
- Annex 1 rounding table (English page 13): sodium and potassium, milligram: under 5 mg is declared as 0; 5 to 140 mg rounded to steps of 5; over 140 mg to steps of 10. Cut-off for "may be declared as 0" is under 5 mg.
- Annex 1 section 1.7 (dual display): for foods that must be mixed with other ingredients before eating, the panel is shown as sold and as prepared.
- Annex 1 section 2.4 energy factors (English page 11): organic acid 3 kcal/g; sugar alcohols: isomalt and lactitol 2 kcal/g, xylitol 2.4, maltitol 2.1, sorbitol 2.6, mannitol 1.6, polyglucitol 3.0, erythritol 0 kcal/g.
- Annex 3 (Thai RDI, English page 36): sodium 2,000 mg; potassium 3,500 mg; chloride 2,300 mg.
- Annex 2 (reference serving sizes; English pages 16 to 17 and 26): category 2 beverages, item 2 carbonated and non-carbonated beverages: 200 milliliters; item 3 beverages, powder or concentrated, prepared before consumption: the defined amount for preparing 200 milliliters of beverage. Remark (page 26): a dry food that is reconstituted with water or another liquid of insignificant nutrient content takes the reference serving of the reconstituted category. Section 4.1(2) (page 27): a container finished in one occasion is declared as one serving, but the amount per serving must not exceed 200 percent of the reference serving size (330 ml is 165 percent of 200 ml).
