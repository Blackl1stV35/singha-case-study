# KPI section: Lime Cube (19 October 2026)

Status: desk-based. No bench, sensory, humidity or consumer test has run, and no outside party has been contacted.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the page text is saved in rework/raw and was read; this is the repository rule. UNVERIFIED: the page was not saved, or only a snippet or summary was read; a figure stays UNVERIFIED until its page is saved. DERIVED: our arithmetic, with the inputs and their tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## KPI tree

Objective: repeat use of Lime Cube, a dissolvable pouch cube made into a lime drink on the spot. As a new consumption form, it is measured by trial and repeat, not only sales.

- **Trial:** units per pilot store per week; national listings.
- **Repeat:** share of first buyers who buy again.
- **Price:** shelf price realised; ex-factory share of shelf.
- **Contribution:** ex-factory revenue less variable cost, per cube.
- **Sell-through:** share of placed stock sold within 90 days.

## Kill criteria

1. **Sweetener:** neither sucralose nor steviol glycosides is permitted for the beverage route. No sale.
2. **Dose:** the 0.9-volume design fails if dissolved CO2 is below 0.6 volume at 10 minutes at a serve of 30 °C or below (ASSUMPTION). The 0.55-volume option then takes its own test (gate not set; to be defined by R&D). If that also fails, the format stops.
3. **Taste:** blind preference below 55% at pH 3.5 (ASSUMPTION). One acid and pH re-set, then stop.
4. **Pouch:** in-pack reaction above 0.5% at 35 °C. No launch until the barrier changes.
5. **Potassium:** a reviewer rejects 518 mg per serve. No sale.
6. **Economics:** realised contribution below 3.70 THB per cube, or repeat below 15% on two cohort reads (ASSUMPTION). Stop scale-up.
