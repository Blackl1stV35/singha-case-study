## Notes

- The brochure gives one output figure for the whole block. It is an upper figure, qualified by can format and by the third-party capper or filler wording (the two slides differ on which one: slide 2 says "filler", slide 4 says "capper").
- The slide 4 footnote adds that the values on that slide are examples taken from measurements at customer facilities and are not a general rule. The 135,000 cans per hour sits on that slide, so it is an example figure, not a guaranteed rating. This was missing from the first version of the record and was added on 20 October 2026.
- The only can size named is 500 mL, for the standard deviation figure. No 330 mL rate is given.
- Converted to cans per minute: about 2,250 (135,000 divided by 60).
