## 1. The Nation, 2 November 2017

- URL: https://www.nationthailand.com/business/30330691
- Article date 2 November 2017; announcement made 1 November 2017.
- Suntory Beverage & Food Asia Pte Ltd and PepsiCo form a drinks joint venture in Thailand; the venture will serve as the bottler for both companies in Thailand.
- Ownership: Suntory 51 per cent, PepsiCo 49 per cent.
- Predecessor: International Refreshment (Thailand) Co Ltd (IRTC), which ran PepsiCo's Thailand beverage business, to be renamed Suntory PepsiCo Beverage (Thailand) Co., Ltd.
- Brands: PepsiCo retains marketing and innovation for Pepsi-Cola, 7-UP, Mirinda, Gatorade, Lipton and Aquafina. Foods (Lay's, Sunbites, Tawan) are not part of the venture.
- Plants: Amata City Industrial Estate, Rayong (opened 2012); Nong Khae Industrial Estate, Saraburi (opened 2016, doubled the capacity of the first plant).
- No sales or share figures are given. PepsiCo present in Thailand since 1952; "hundreds of millions of US dollars" invested since 2012.
