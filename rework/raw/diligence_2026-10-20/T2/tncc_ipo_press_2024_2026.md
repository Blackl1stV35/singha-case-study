Filing of 612,451,687 new shares (10.00% after the offering; 5,512,065,185 shares before, 6,124,516,872 after). Rights from The Coca-Cola Company and Schweppes Holdings in 63 of 77 provinces plus Laos (100% via Lao Coca-Cola Bottling Co., Ltd.); 30% of a Cambodian bottler. Shareholders as of 21 April 2025: SWB (Singapore; owned through Swire Pacific, Hong Kong listed) 55.59% before and 50.03% after the IPO; TNTL (Thai holding company of the Sarasin, Boonsoong and Kiangsiri families) 29.95% before and 26.95% after. Over 250 products; five plants (Pathum Thani, Rangsit, Nakhon Ratchasima, Khon Kaen, Lampang); 21 lines, 450 million unit cases a year (2024 wording; the 2025 filing says 22 lines and 564 million); Pathum Thani can line 2,000 cans a minute; brands Coke, Fanta, Sprite, Schweppes, A&W root beer, Minute Maid Splash and Pulpy, Namthip water, Ou-ha (Thai: อู-ฮ่า).
