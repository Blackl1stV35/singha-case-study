# Background and trends: Thailand, domestic market

Scope: Thailand, domestic market. Thai-language sources are summarised in English. Overseas analogues are labelled, because no domestic evidence for dissolvable cubes was found.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing or release, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the page text was read, and the public URL is given. UNVERIFIED: not read, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, with the inputs and their tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## Background: the group and its beverage business

Boon Rawd Brewery is the holding company, following a 2000 reorganisation. Singha Corporation was set up in 2001 and oversees Boon Rawd's subsidiaries, which its About Us page puts at more than 150 companies (undated). It reports directly to the Boon Rawd board (Source 1; S; SOURCED).

The two English home pages disagree on whether Singha Corporation is an affiliate or a subsidiary of Boon Rawd (Source 2; S; SOURCED). The beverages category lists six products: Singha Water, Singha Soda, Singha Lemon Soda, Purra, Purra Vitamin Water and FIJI (Source 2). Treating that category as the nearest unit for a non-alcoholic product is an ASSUMPTION.

The group already sells a lime sparkling water. Its launch release, dated 13 March 2026, describes Singha Sparkling Water Lime Flavour, a 330 ml can priced at 15 baht, launched in March 2026 (Source 3; P; SOURCED). A news reprint posted 17 March 2026 gives the same flavour, can size and price (Source 4; S; SOURCED). The release calls the can sugar-free with 0% calories; this is the company's claim, not checked here. It gives no ingredient list, and carbonation and canning capacity are not evidenced here.

The company's lime can overlaps Lime Cube on flavour. The difference lies in form and pack. Lime Cube is a dissolvable cube that makes the drink in the glass, one cube per pouch. The company sells a ready-to-drink 330 ml can. Singha Lemon Soda is a separate product, not the lime product (Source 5; S; SOURCED). Lime Cube carries no Singha name.

Limit: no consolidated accounts, innovation budget or group R&D spend were available, and no group financial ratio is used.
