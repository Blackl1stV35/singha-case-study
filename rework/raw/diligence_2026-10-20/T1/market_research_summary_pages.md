# Market-research summary pages for Thai carbonated drinks (paywalled; only the free text was read)

Date read: 20 October 2026. Tier: S. None of these pages shows a baht or volume figure, except StrategyHelix.

## Euromonitor, "Carbonates in Thailand" report page (summary dated November 2025)
- URL: https://www.euromonitor.com/carbonates-in-thailand/report
- Free text gives no value or volume. It gives the definition: "Sweetened, non-alcoholic drinks containing carbon dioxide are included here." Cola and non-cola, regular or low calorie; non-cola includes Lemonade/Lime, Tonic Water/Mixers/Other Bitters, Orange and Other Non-Cola; sparkling juices included; carbonated bottled water, carbonated RTD tea and carbonated energy drinks excluded. Historical data 2020 to 2025, forecast 2025 to 2030.
- Free text also says Boon Rawd Brewery Co Ltd "recorded the most dynamic off-trade volume growth in carbonates in Thailand" (no figure).
- So a Euromonitor lemonade/lime sub-category exists but its size is behind the paywall (report price on the page: USD 1,195).

## Statista Market Insights, carbonated soft drinks, Thailand
- URL: https://www.statista.com/outlook/cmo/non-alcoholic-drinks/soft-drinks/carbonated-soft-drinks/thailand/
- Revenue, volume and growth are masked ("Limited Access"). Definition: sweetened and carbonated non-alcoholic drinks including cola, lemonades and tonic water; excludes non-carbonated soft drinks, RTD tea, energy and sports drinks. Values in USD, top-down model, updated twice a year.

## StrategyHelix, "Carbonated Soft Drinks Market in Thailand"
- URL: https://strategyh.com/report/carbonated-soft-drinks-market-in-thailand/
- Printed: the market is "projected to grow from USD 3.4 billion in 2026 to USD 4.1 billion by 2031, representing a CAGR of 3.61%". Scope 2021 to 2031; no data sources, level or THB figure shown. Team conversion at the Bank of Thailand reference rate for 15 October 2026 (33.5410, P; see `bot_fx_report123_2026-10-20.md`) gives about THB 114,039 million (DERIVED). The team midpoint of the buying and selling rates (33.5495, DERIVED) gives 114,068 million; that midpoint is not a published rate. Either figure is far above every other value and has no stated level (script: rework/screen/t1_market_size_reconcile.py). Treated as an outlier of undefined definition.
