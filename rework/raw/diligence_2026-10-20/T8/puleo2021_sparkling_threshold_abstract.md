# Puleo, Castillo, Di Monaco and Stieger 2021, "Is it still water?" (sparkling sensation threshold)

- Source: Puleo S, Castillo MT, Di Monaco R, Stieger M. Is it still water? Relationships between sparkling sensitivity and consumption frequency of carbonated waters. Food Research International 147 (2021) article 110584. doi 10.1016/j.foodres.2021.110584
- URL read: https://research.wur.nl/en/publications/is-it-still-water-relationships-between-sparkling-sensitivity-and/ (institutional research portal page with the abstract; the full paper was not opened)
- Date read: 2026-10-20
- Quoted: "Average detection threshold of sparkling sensation (BET) was 0.44 g/L CO2 concentration." Concentration range tested: "with CO2 concentrations ranging from 0.03 to 1.05 g/L". 100 participants, mineral (sparkling) water, detection threshold by ascending series.
- Tier: S (peer-reviewed journal article, but only the abstract on a repository page was read; this matches the tier in submission 09, S, abstract only). An earlier version of this page said P; changed to S on 2026-10-20. Status: PROXY for a soft drink (the value is for carbonated mineral water; applied here to a lemon-flavoured soft drink with sugar and acid, which is a limit). The threshold is a detection threshold in mineral water, not a bound on how long fizz lasts.
