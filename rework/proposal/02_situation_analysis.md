## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing or release, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the page text was read, and the public URL is given. UNVERIFIED: not read, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, with the inputs and their tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## What the situation means for the cube

1. **The problem is form, not flavour.** The lime flavour already sells at 15 THB per 330 ml can, so the cube must win on form and pack: a dissolvable lime cube that makes a carbonated drink from water. Fizz and taste are untested.
2. **Price is not settled.** A ladder of 15, 25 and 35 THB per 250 ml is an ASSUMPTION. At 330 ml it gives about 20, 33 and 46 THB (DERIVED). The lowest rung is about 20 THB per 330 ml (about 60 THB per litre), above the 15 THB can (about 45 THB per litre; DERIVED from S6).
3. **Cost per cube is 3.84 THB** in a 12-cube carton at pH 3.5 (DERIVED). Reactants (0.118) are DERIVED from UN Comtrade unit values (S26; PROXY), with an UNVERIFIED sucralose price. Pouch 1.30, print 1.50 and processing 0.50 are ASSUMPTIONS; no Thai quote was requested. The 12-cube cost includes a carton share of THB 0.42 per cube (THB 5 per 12-cube carton, from a retail box listing used as a PROXY). A 20 ft container holds about 128,580 cubes in these cartons (DERIVED; S24, S25; packing is an ASSUMPTION).
4. **The pouch base is an ASSUMPTION.** No Thai pouch price was found. The only evidence is an overseas listing of USD 0.03 to 0.05 per piece (S14; UNVERIFIED), which at 33.55 THB per USD (S13; P, SOURCED; DERIVED mid-rate of 15 October 2026) is 1.01 to 1.68 THB. Across a 0.67 to 2.70 THB band (the top is an ASSUMPTION), the cube costs 3.21 to 5.24 THB (DERIVED). One cube per pouch is inferred.
5. **Excise classification is open.** Item 02.02 covers sweetened or flavoured drinks, with or without added sugar (S12). The desk reading puts the cube in the 0 THB per litre band, but the ad valorem base was not read. A regulatory adviser must decide.
6. **Acid and potassium at 0.55 volume.** The pH target of 3.5 is a working anchor (ASSUMPTION); taste is untested. Per 330 ml: potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 0.52 g at stoichiometric balance or 2.10 g to reach pH 3.5 (DERIVED; acid constants S, UNVERIFIED, S22). Potassium is 317 mg, 9.1% of the Thai reference of 3,500 mg per day, for scale only (S20: a third-party copy of Notification 182; No. 445 from a consultancy page; primary text not opened). Composition: potassium bicarbonate, citric acid and sucralose; no sodium carbonate.
7. **Dose and solubility.** The dose is 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 ml. Solubility at 1 atm is 0.655 volume at 30 °C and 0.592 at 35 °C (S15, S16; S, SOURCED), so the dose sits within solubility at every modelled serve from 5 to 35 °C (DERIVED). That range is a modelling range; Bangkok climate figures are UNVERIFIED. NIST rows for 0 to 25 °C differ from Lange by -2% to +11% (S17). A desk model with no bench test gives a surface-only fizz upper bound of about 96 minutes (DERIVED), using a 0.44 g/L threshold (S27; abstract only; PROXY for a soft drink). The gate for 0.55 volume is for R&D to define.
8. **Sweetener and permissions.** Sucralose at about 100 mg/kg (33 mg per 330 ml) is a design level (ASSUMPTION). The Thai level for sucralose in category 14.1.4 is 300 mg/kg under condition 127, which the annex applies to the drink ready to drink (S18; P, SOURCED; flavoured-drink rows read, not the full annex). Bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis (S18). The FDA clarification extends major-category permissions to sub-categories; its sucralose row was not read (S19). TH3 applies only to electrolyte drinks, and the product makes no electrolyte claim. The classification needs a regulatory view before sale.
9. **Demand is untested.** A purchase-intent model, not a consumer finding, puts men aged 25 to 34 ahead in about 94% of draws, supporting direction only; the size of the gap is not supported (DERIVED; ASSUMPTION inputs).
10. **Brand and sign-off.** The product is named Lime Cube and does not use the SINGHA name. The lion emblem was confirmed for this proposal. Brand-owner and legal sign-off sit with Singha's department and are not held here; the alcohol-cue question is unchecked.
