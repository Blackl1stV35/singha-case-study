## 1. Items

- **02.01**: mineral water, soda water and soft drinks with no added sugar or other sweetening substance and no flavouring. Ad valorem 14%; 0 baht per litre.
- **02.02**: mineral water and soft drinks that have added sugar or other sweetening substances, or flavouring, or other drinks, excluding fruit juice (02.03). Ad valorem 14% in every tier. Per-litre baht depends on sugar grams per 100 ml (tiers below).

The "other sweetening substances" wording means an intensive sweetener counts. A drink with no sugar still falls under 02.02 if it is sweetened or flavoured.

## 3. Derived (DERIVED, our arithmetic)

- Per 330 ml can at the 1 October 2566 schedule: 0 baht (up to 6 g), 0.33 baht (over 6 to 8 g), 0.99 baht (over 8 to 10 g), 1.65 baht (over 10 g). These are per-litre rates times 0.33 litre.
- A sugar-free, sweetened lime drink falls in tier (ก): 0 baht per litre, but still under 02.02 at 14% ad valorem, if the value base applies.
