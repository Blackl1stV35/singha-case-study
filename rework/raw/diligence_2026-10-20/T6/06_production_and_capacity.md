## Can maker (primary, BJC 56-1 One Report 2025; see the excerpt file)

- Thai Beverage Can Limited (TBC), Saraburi (WHA industrial estate, Nong Khae): 4 can lines plus Can Line 5 (hybrid, added 2025 at TBC 2); can sizes include 330 ml regular and 330 ml sleek cans.
- TBC maximum capacity 3,880 million cans a year in 2025 (3,780 in 2024; 3,440 in 2023); actual production 2,307 (2025), 2,214 (2024), 2,027 (2023) million cans; usage 59.46%, 58.57%, 58.92%.
- Main domestic customers named: T.C. Pharmaceutical Industries, ThaiNamthip Corporation PCL, Nestle (Thai), Singha Beverage Co., Ltd. and Beer Thip Brewery 1991. The filing does not say which product Singha Beverage fills.
- TBC is the largest of five aluminium can makers in Thailand (2025).

## Canning line speeds

- No canning line speed (cans a minute) was found for Boon Rawd, Singha Beverage or any plant that might fill the can. The only line speed published is a PET water line (72,000 bottles an hour at the new Wang Noi site).
