## Reading

- 76,900 is printed in the Kasikorn-attributed paragraph, as the carbonated segment, for 2025.
- The 2 percent growth in the same paragraph is printed for the whole non-alcoholic total (226,000 million). The article prints no growth rate for the carbonated segment. This is relevant to the mapping of the note's unlabelled 2.0 and 2.6 percent pair (see the KResearch excerpt file).
