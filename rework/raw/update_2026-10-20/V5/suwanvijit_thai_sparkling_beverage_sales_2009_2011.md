# Suwanvijit, Choonpradub and McNeil (2009) and Suwanvijit (2011): the only Thai monthly beverage regression found

- Item: V5 revision. Date read: 20 October 2026. Both pages were opened. Neither has a temperature variable.

## 1. Journal paper

- Work: Wassana Suwanvijit, Chamnein Choonpradub and Nittaya McNeil, "Sales Analysis with Application to Sparkling Beverage Products Sales in Southern Thailand", International Journal of Business and Management, vol. 4, no. 7, 2009. DOI 10.5539/ijbm.v4n7p43.
- Page opened: https://ccsenet.org/journal/index.php/ijbm/article/view/3002 (WebFetch). Abstract only; the full text is a PDF linked from the page and was not read.
- Content: monthly sparkling beverage sales revenue from 14 provinces of Southern Thailand, 2000 to 2006; linear regression on log sales revenue per 1,000 population; the abstract names quarterly effects, flavour-by-branch and branch-by-year interactions; annual per capita value 297 baht across about 8.087 million residents; different Muslim population shares go with different flavour preferences.
- The page does not mention temperature or weather anywhere.

## 2. Doctoral thesis of the same first author

- Work: Wassana Suwanvijit, "Graphing, Modeling and Forecasting Business Data with Application to Sparkling Beverage Products Sales in Southern Thailand", Ph.D. thesis (Research Methodology), Prince of Songkla University, 2011.
- Pages opened: https://kb.psu.ac.th/psukb/handle/2010/8249?mode=full and https://kb.psu.ac.th/items/dd5c8941-72e2-4f9c-8b03-6584731047aa/full (WebFetch). The repository lists 11 files; the page shows five and does not state an access status.
- File opened: Abstracts.pdf (4 pages, 252 KB, public download link on the repository page), text read locally with PyMuPDF (python -I, in its own scratch folder). The English abstract on pages 3 and 4 says: study 1 models quarterly revenue (additive model R-squared 0.91; best model R-squared 0.97); study 2 is an observation-driven multiple linear regression that forecasts 12 months by flavour and package type (R-squared 0.95, mean absolute percent error 6 percent) and reports that "the seasonal effects found in this study" are present; study 3 uses Lee-Carter models with Holt-Winters for 24 months.
- No temperature or weather variable is named in the abstract. The chapters on the second study were not opened (the repository page shows only the first five files).

## 3. What this is and is not

- It is the only Thai monthly beverage regression found in this item. It models one company's sparkling beverage revenue in the south (flavour and package type), 2000 to 2006.
- It gives a seasonal profile (month or quarter effects), not a temperature coefficient. If the month effects can be read from the second-study chapter, they could be compared with TMD monthly means for southern stations, but they would still mix temperature with rain, the tourist season and Ramadan, and they are 20 years old.
- Tier S for what the pages state. Status: not a usable coefficient; recorded as a lead and a possible route (full text of the thesis chapters or of the journal PDF).
