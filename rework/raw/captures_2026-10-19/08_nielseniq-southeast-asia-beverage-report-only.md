## Extracted facts
- Article date: 08 May 2025, as shown on the page.
- Survey sample: "8,000+" (stated on the page as methodology).
- Survey period: not stated, except for the 2023 comparisons and one "last month" question.
- Regional figures for Southeast Asia (survey shares; period not stated):
  - 77% prefer quality drinks over cheap options when going out.
  - 21% ("just over a fifth") say bar staff recommendations matter when choosing spirits.
- Country figures (period not stated unless noted):
  - Philippines: 48% rate value as an important factor in drink choices. 44% say relaxation motivates drink choices.
  - Malaysia: importance of value up 10 percentage points compared with 2023. 31% say menus are an important factor.
  - Thailand and Vietnam: importance of value up 4 percentage points each, compared with 2023 (the comparison year is implied in the extraction).
  - Indonesia: 83% prefer quality drinks over cheap options. 31% say menus are an important factor. 26% say a venue's social media post about a drink helps them decide. 44% report seeing drinks brands mentioned or advertised on social media last month. 26% say brand reputation matters when choosing spirits, 3 points above the SEA average for on-premise consumers.
  - Vietnam: 80% prefer quality drinks over cheap options.
  - Thailand: 78% would rather spend on one or two high-quality drinks than three or more lower-standard ones (22% chose the latter). 39% rate ambience and atmosphere as important. 34% say menus are an important factor (ambiguous; see limits).
  - Indonesia and Singapore: spirits brands ranked fifth for trustworthiness and reliability (a rank, not a percentage).
- Excluded from this capture: a 66% figure on purchases after seeing food or drink on social media. The page presents it as global, not Southeast Asian.

## Limits
- All figures are consumer survey percentages. None is a market size, sales or volume figure. The survey is on-premise and largely spirits-related in context. The period is not stated for most figures.
- The 34% menus figure is ambiguous on the page: it may be a regional figure or a Thailand-only figure. It is recorded under Thailand as the best reading and is unconfirmed.
- The two 44% figures measure different things (Indonesia social media exposure; Philippines relaxation motive) and are not comparable.
- The page's related-article teasers contain no figures.
