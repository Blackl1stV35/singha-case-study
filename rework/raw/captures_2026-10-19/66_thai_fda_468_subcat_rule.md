## Document identity (as printed, page 1)

- Announcement of the Thai FDA (Office of the Food and Drug Board), clarifying MOPH Notification No. 468 (B.E. 2568), issued under the Food Act B.E. 2522. Subject: criteria, conditions, methods of use and proportions of food additives (No. 4).
- Clause 1 repeals Annexes 1 and 2 of MOPH Notification No. 418 (B.E. 2563) and replaces them with the Annexes of No. 468.

## Effective date

- In force from 12 December B.E. 2568 (CE 2025), as printed on page 1. B.E. 2568 is CE 2025.

## Sub-category inheritance rule (page 2, explanation of Annex 1)

- If the conditions for an additive permit use in a major category, for example 14.1.4, the additive is also permitted in its sub-categories 14.1.4.1, 14.1.4.2 and 14.1.4.3.
- If the conditions permit use only in a sub-category, the additive is permitted only in that sub-category.
- English wording is our translation. The Thai text has no English version.

## Related clause (page 2, clause 4)

- Where two or more additives with numeric maxima in the same functional group are combined, the sum of (use level divided by permitted maximum) across them must not exceed one. Our translation.
