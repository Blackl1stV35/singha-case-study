# Upstream packaging survey, Thailand domestic (18 October 2026)

Status: survey complete and track closed with limits. Sixteen agents ran: five tracks, two verification checks per track, and one completeness critic. Raw output: [raw/wue7aai6h_packaging_survey.json](raw/wue7aai6h_packaging_survey.json).

Scope: Thailand domestic only. Singapore and Korea items are removed, including the BCRS fee, Korea tariffs and sugar levy, container freight and export listings. Products: A, the leaf-wrapped frozen drink with a PET inner pouch (working design). B, the 250 ml UHT soy drink, which is not advanced (rework/40, decision D1).

No outside party is contacted in this survey. Every item that needs a quote, a certificate, a lab or an adviser is closed as a limit (section 5).

## 1. Bottom line

- **No option has a supported Thai price for the pouch, the carton or the outer wrap.** The only Thai price on a packaging item is one retail bagasse box, and it was out of stock at capture.
- **All ten verification checks are marked refuted.** The critic found no verified candidate. Most refutations are "not supported as written" (no primary text found, or a source that does not say it). Read the verdicts as "unverified", not as "wrong". A few checks found real errors, such as the leaf price attribution and the 0 °C gel cost identity (section 4).
- **The survey supports cost levers, not material prices.** The levers that survive are:
  1. **Leaf, farm-gate, off-peak.** Tani farm-gate is 9 to 10 THB/kg (one 2024 article, Khlong Krachong group, Sukhothai). The 40 to 70 THB/kg figure in the same article applies to banana leaf in general, so it is a peak-season upper bound, not a Tani price. Derived: about 2 to 3 THB per leaf at 3 to 4 leaves per kg.
  2. **Omit plastic straws.** A peel-and-sip drink needs none.
  3. **Minimise mass per unit.** The draft packaging fee is per kg, and no plastic rate is published.
  4. **Insulation at listing prices.** An EPS-type foam box at THB 25 to 40, plus two 0 °C gel packs at THB 18 to 25 each. Derived shipment cost: about THB 75 to 90 (listing prices, sizes unconfirmed).
  5. **PET pouch.** Keep antimony-free PET. Its cost stays unpriced until a converter quote exists, which is out of plan.

## 4. Verification and contradictions

**The ten checks.** Each of the five tracks had two verifications. All ten are marked refuted. The reasons:

- **Barrier, PET:** supplier and price not on the cited pages. The food-contact claim held on the Thai text.
- **Barrier, PE:** the per-bag cost is unclear, and the freezer source was unreadable.
- **Leaf, banana:** the price attribution was wrong (corrected in D-P2). Food-contact status is open, not disproved.
- **Leaf, pandan:** the food-contact basis was a secondary summary.
- **Bio, bagasse:** food-contact scope not found.
- **Bio, SIG carton:** the Gazette and the precedent source were unreadable.
- **Cold, EPS box:** food-contact scope unresolved. The verdict reads "refuted" but the note says "unresolved".
- **Cold, 0 °C gel:** the cost is for a −30 °C freezer gel or a 25-piece set, not a 0 °C pack.
- **Policy, straws:** the roadmap is a 2019 plan, and the price is not on the cited page.
- **Policy, national-park notice:** the primary source could not be opened. The date and penalty held.

## 5. Limits (closed; no outside contact in the plan)

- **No Thai THB/kg price** for any food-contact film, laminate, carton or bottle. Quotes are needed from Toyobo, Polyplex, SRF, a converter, SIG and Petform. None is requested.
- **No minimum order quantities** published by any Thai converter checked.
- **Gazette texts not read:** the TIS 2948 Gazette and amendment No. 2; the national-park notice of 5 April 2022; the PCD phase 2 list; the EPR draft text. The Thai text of Notification 435 was read for PET and PE only; PP and the Annex tables were not checked directly.
- **Leaf wrap area not defined.** Per-wrap leaf cost cannot be computed until a wrap is measured.
- **No freezer or taste test** for PLA, PBAT, EVOH, paper or the 0 °C gel set.
- **Not evaluated:** aluminium foil or metallised laminate (a standard freezer and light barrier), nylon laminate, PBS film, and coated barrier films (SiOx, AlOx, PVDC). These are missing options, and they are closed as limits.
- **Secondary packaging not costed:** print, labels, closures, shrink sleeves, trays and pallets.
- **Product B:** no aseptic-compatibility specification for the PET bottle, and no carton price. B is not advanced.
- **Absence claims are weak.** Several Thai retailers and catalogue searches could not be read, and the verifiers note that several refutations rest on absence of evidence.
