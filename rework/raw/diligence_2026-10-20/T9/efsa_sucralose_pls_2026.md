# EFSA plain-language summary: re-evaluation of sucralose (E 955)
- Tier: P (primary, EFSA)
- URL: https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/plain-language-summary/re-evaluation-sucralose-e-955-food-additive
- Date read: 2026-10-20 (publication date shown: 16 February 2026)
- Extract as returned by the page: "There is no need to change the previously established acceptable daily intake (ADI)" "of 15 mg/kg body weight per day of sucralose (E 955)." The panel "could not confirm the safety of the proposed extension of use of E 955" for fine bakery wares unless baking temperature and time are restricted.
- Used for: EU ADI 15 mg/kg bw/day.
