# Xiao et al. 2014: gas transfer velocities of CH4 and CO2 in a subtropical shallow pond (Tellus B 66, 23795)

- Source: S. Xiao, H. Yang, D. Liu, C. Zhang, D. Lei, Y. Wang, F. Peng, Y. Li, C. Wang, X. Li, G. Wu, L. Liu (2014), Tellus B: Chemical and Physical Meteorology 66(1), 23795, doi 10.3402/tellusb.v66.23795

- Date read: 2026-10-20 (WebFetch, model-summarised; values returned as printed in the abstract and results; the full text was not read in detail)
- Tier: S (read through a summary). Status: SOURCED at summary level.

## Extracted facts
- Static closed floating chambers with a cavity ringdown analyser logging at 1 Hz; k derived from the diffusive flux, bubble flux excluded; each measurement about 30 minutes.
- CO2 k600 at site N in summer (July 2013): 2.83 cm/h.
- CO2 k600 at site M in autumn (October 2013): 0.65 cm/h in the abstract; section 3.2 gives 0.72 cm/h (the paper is internally inconsistent by 0.07 cm/h). Overcast weather with light rain and lower temperatures.
- Wind speeds were too low to be measured most of the time. Site and season are confounded. The authors attribute the autumn values to surface-water dissolved oxygen (CO2) and wind (CH4), and discuss phytoplankton metabolism and disturbance of the water body.

## Use in rework/71
- 0.65 cm/h is the lowest coefficient in the sources read for this task (Cole and Caraco 1998: 2.65; Cole et al. 2010: 1.5 to 3.1 cm/h; Xiao et al. 2014: 0.65 to 2.83 cm/h). It is a floating-chamber value at one pond site, not a drinking glass, and it is not a bound. A search for lower reservoir values (a lead of 0.07 to 0.39 m/d was not found again) was stopped by the search budget.
