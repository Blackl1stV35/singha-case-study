# Track A: zero-sugar apple-ginger sparkling soda with fibre, end to end (draft for team consensus)

Status: conditional. Not final. For the team meeting.

## 1. Concept and target

- A zero-sugar sparkling soda in a 330 ml can. Singapore first, Korea later.
- Target (team proposal, to confirm): urban working adults 25 to 40, with Gen Z as a secondary target. No health claim.
- Positioning: a clean sparkling soda for everyday drinking. The fibre is an ingredient feature, not a claim.

## 2. Product specification (working)

- **Base:** carbonated water.
- **Flavour:** apple-ginger. Apple must be a flavour, not juice, because juice is free sugar and blocks the Singapore sugar-free tagline. The ginger source must add no sugar. The ginger rhizome needs an SFA listing check (record S/N 133).
- **Sweetener:** a blend of steviol glycosides and/or sucralose, designed to sit within the caps. The steviol cap is unresolved (160 ppm or 125 ppm, depending on classification), so design to 125 ppm until read. Sucralose cap 300 ppm (third-party table; to confirm).
- **Fibre:** acacia (Fibregum-type) or resistant maltodextrin. Dose is a scan assumption of 5 to 10 g per can. No fibre claim unless the per-100 ml threshold is confirmed.
- **Nutri-Grade:** a sweetened zero-sugar soda is at most Grade B. The mark is optional for Grade B.

## 3. Regulation

**Singapore (lead)**
- BCRS: the 330 ml can is in scope. The S$0.10 deposit applies, and the Deposit Mark is required from 1 October 2026.
- Sugar-free: at or below 0.5 g per 100 ml with an energy statement. The general-beverage condition in the HPB guide is unread.
- Fibre claim: threshold unread. Default is no fibre wording on pack.
- Additives: acacia and resistant maltodextrin status to be confirmed by SFA feedback (not yet answered).
- Health claims are barred (Reg 9(2)).

**Korea (later)**
- 무당 rule: sweetener statement and calorie statement above 20 kcal per 100 ml (in force from 1 January 2026).
- MFDS overseas plant registration, at least 7 days before each import declaration.
- 소비기한 date on food shipped for import.
- No functional wording: the fibre and ginger are not on the approved functional list.

## 4. Production route

- **Route:** in-house with partner. The group's carbonated soda capability is shown only in glass (Surat Thani, 2015). Can-scale carbonation and filling are not evidenced in-house.
- **Partner needed for:** can filling and carbonation at can scale (co-packer, to be confirmed), and fibre supply.
- **Flavour:** TFF/TCFF co-development, subject to its certificate and flavour-capability answer.

## 5. Supply and cost

- Resistant maltodextrin: asking prices of USD 2.43 to 10 per kg (25 kg MOQ), about USD 0.012 to 0.10 per can at 5 to 10 g. Vendor figures.
- Acacia fibre: no public bulk price. Retail prices (UK) overstate bulk cost.
- Sweeteners: monk fruit at about USD 0.0034 per can at 10 mg. Immaterial per can.
- Co-packer fee: none published. Required from enquiries.

## 6. Economics (`strategy/data/track_economics.csv`)

Exporter cost per can, standalone route: Singapore S$0.37 in-house, S$0.41 partner. Korea KRW 414 in-house, KRW 454 partner (freight proxy KRW 1,900 per 20 ft).

Required CIF for the unit-margin test (20 percent of CIF):
- Singapore: **S$0.53 in-house**, S$0.58 partner.
- Korea: **KRW 591 in-house**, KRW 649 partner.

Customs-average CIF (S$0.48, KRW 528) fails both routes. Importer-ceiling CIF passes:
- Singapore at S$2.50 shelf: CIF S$1.09, net contribution 56 percent. Volume for a 20 percent operating margin (in-house, S$300k fixed): about 763,000 cans a year, about 0.19 percent of the category.
- Korea at KRW 2,000 shelf: CIF KRW 873, net 43 percent. Volume: about 2.0 million cans a year.

The result depends on the price basis, which the team must decide (see `09_trend_tracks_and_20pct_test.md`).

## 7. KPIs and kill criteria

- K1: net contribution at least 20 percent of CIF. Kill if the realised CIF is below S$0.53 (in-house) and no premium price is evidenced.
- K2: break-even share at most 1.0 percent of category.
- K6 (binary): sweetener within caps, fibre wording resolved, additive status confirmed, Thai FDA and co-packer identity known.
- Venue and shelf velocity: at least 3 cans per store per week in a pilot (benchmark is an assumption).
- Kill: fibre dose not stable in the carbonated can at 40 °C for 4 to 8 weeks; sweetener cannot be placed within caps; no co-packer quote.

## 8. Timeline

- After 22 October: bench prototype at pH about 3 with acacia and resistant maltodextrin across a dose ladder, 330 ml cans at target CO2; haze, sediment, foam and aluminium pick-up; 40 °C storage for 4 to 8 weeks.
