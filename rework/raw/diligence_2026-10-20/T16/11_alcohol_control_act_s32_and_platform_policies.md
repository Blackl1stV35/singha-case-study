## A. Alcohol Control Act B.E. 2551

- URL: https://th.wikisource.org/wiki/พระราชบัญญัติควบคุมเครื่องดื่มแอลกอฮอล์_พ.ศ._2551
- Section 32, paragraph 1 (Thai): "ห้ามมิให้ผู้ใดโฆษณาเครื่องดื่มแอลกอฮอล์หรือแสดงชื่อหรือเครื่องหมายของเครื่องดื่มแอลกอฮอล์ ... อันเป็นการอวดอ้างสรรพคุณหรือชักจูงใจให้ผู้อื่นดื่มโดยตรงหรือโดยอ้อม" (no one may advertise an alcoholic beverage or show its name or mark in a way that boasts of its properties or induces others to drink, directly or indirectly).
- Paragraph 2: advertising or public relations by producers of alcoholic beverages of every kind is limited to information and socially constructive knowledge, and may not show the product or its package except the symbols of the beverage or the producer's corporate logo as set by ministerial regulation. Paragraph 3: paragraphs 1 and 2 do not apply to advertising originating outside the Kingdom.
- Section 3 definitions: "การโฆษณา" means any act, by any means, that lets the public see, hear or learn of a message for commercial benefit, and expressly includes marketing communications (การสื่อสารการตลาด); "การสื่อสารการตลาด" covers activities aimed at selling goods or services or building image, public relations, sales promotion, product display, organising or sponsoring events and direct marketing.
- Section 43 (penalty for breach of section 32): imprisonment of not more than 1 year or a fine of not more than THB 500,000 or both, and a further fine of up to THB 50,000 a day while the breach continues (as given on the Wikisource copy).
- What this means for the cube (reading only, not a legal opinion): the cube uses the SINGHA name and lion, which are marks of the group's alcoholic beverages. Whether a creator post that shows the name or lion on a non-alcoholic product counts as showing the mark of an alcoholic beverage is a question for the Gate 7 ruling; this record does not settle it. Creator content should show no alcohol, no alcohol brands and no drinking-occasion cues.
- Not found: any Alcohol Control Committee notification on shared brand names for non-alcoholic products; not opened.

## B. Meta advertising standard on alcohol

- URL: https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/alcohol (most recent change-log entry read: 26 June 2024).
- The page lists Thailand among the countries where "our policies prohibit ads promoting or referencing alcohol ... based on local law" (list includes Thailand, Norway, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Turkey and others). Covered content includes brand depictions, alcohol consumption, "promotion of alcohol brands". Non-alcoholic products are allowed "as long as they do not depict alcohol or consumption"; the page does not address a non-alcoholic product that carries an alcohol brand name. Minimum targeting age 18 and "applicable local laws" apply elsewhere.
- Reading: a paid Meta ad or a paid-partnership post that carries the SINGHA name could be reviewed under the alcohol standard in Thailand. This is a risk to test at Gate 7, not a finding.

## C. YouTube paid promotion

- URL: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en (no date on the page; footer 2026).
- Creators who feature branded content, sponsorships, endorsements or other commercial relationships must select the paid-promotion option, which adds a disclosure label at the start of the video; creators and partners are responsible for local legal requirements; the page is not legal advice; branded content must follow Community Guidelines and Google Ads policies; age and country limits can be set for branded content.
