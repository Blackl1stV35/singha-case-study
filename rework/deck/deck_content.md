# Slide deck content (presentation version, 20 October 2026)

Proposer: Singha Corporation. Product: Singha Lemon Soda Cube, shown as "Lemon Soda".
Timing: 13 minutes 45 seconds for the presentation, within the 15-minute limit (questions excluded). Each slide shows its timing in brackets.
Scope rule: anything the desk model does not cover is labelled OUT OF SCOPE. Nothing outside the assumption boundary is presented as a finding.
Tiers (P, S, SOURCED, DERIVED, PROXY, ASSUMPTION, COMPANY CLAIM, OUT OF SCOPE) are defined in Appendix C.
Layout: startup style. One claim per slide, one hero number or one visual, at most three supporting lines, a section navigation bar at the top, and no footers or footnotes on the slides.

- **Desk chemistry:** pH about 3.4 to 3.5 (working pH 3.5). Sweetness about 10 g of sugar equivalent per 100 ml at 55 mg of sucralose (assumption).
- **Label box:** "Drop one cube into 330 ml of water and stir." "Serving: one cube made up to 330 ml." "Contains an intensive sweetener: sucralose (working choice)." "Single-serve pouch." "Net weight 3.8 g (specification, verified by weighing before print)."

## Section 5: The plan

### Slide 11. Pilot [1:00]
- **Layout:** three columns (concept pre-test M3 to M5; pilot M7 to M10; measured decision M10 to M11). A survey box across the bottom. A budget strip at the very bottom.
- **Headline:** Pilot: demand and repeat are tested before any paid media is scaled, and a 20-store trial can tell a halving of demand from the 3-unit target.
- **Concept pre-test:** recruited panel of men aged 25 to 59 who drank alcohol in the past year; one brand-blind concept, signed off by M3; top-2-box intent by age cell at THB 15 (assumption); rule fixed in advance; no media, no sale.
- **Pilot, 13 weeks:** click-to-first-purchase on non-paid traffic in weeks 1 to 4. Route A: 20 in-store stores, read at weeks 8 and 13; scale if both reads are at least 3 units per store per week; stop if both are below; otherwise hold. Route B: paid social starts only if the non-paid read reaches a 1.0% click-to-first-purchase rate (assumption). Creator pilot: Appendix D.
- **Measured decision:** scale one route, repeat once in the same stores, or stop. Scale needs repeat of 15% or more in both cohorts, contribution of at least THB 3.51 per cube before excise (THB 2.54 after), and a cannibalisation share under 7%.
- **Survey box:** a screening test at 1,377 completes per cell for a 5-point lead. The modelled lead is about 1.2 points, which needs about 22,000 per cell. At 1,377 per cell, the test confirms the direction of the lead in 12.7% of simulated runs and rules out a 5-point lead in 56.4%. Fieldwork waits for the regulatory opinion and the panel approval.
- **Budget strip:** committed THB 24,686; conditional THB 60,000 for paid social; ceiling THB 84,686.
- **Out of scope:** pilot results. Every figure in this slide is from simulated pilots.

### Appendix A. Label rules
- **Ingredient list, as fact:** potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, sucralose, mannitol and lemon flavour. No sodium salt, no sodium carbonate, no lubricant.
- **May say:** "Drop one cube into 330 ml of water and stir." "Serving: one cube made up to 330 ml." "Single-serve pouch." "Contains an intensive sweetener: sucralose." Net weight as a specification, verified by weighing before print. Potassium only where the Thai panel requires it.
- **Required declarations:** the Thai nutrition and GDA panels, with sodium and potassium shown. These are declarations, not claims.
- **Barred:** sugar-free, zero-sugar, no-added-sugar; sodium-free, low-sodium, very-low-sodium; health, function, electrolyte, hydration and potassium-benefit; calorie; certification and award wording.
- **Held until the brand ruling or a legal review:** "no salt added"; novelty words; fizz, sparkling and bubbles until a measured test exists; lemon fruit imagery, glassware and bottle shape; alcohol cues; the lion or SINGHA beyond this proposal.
- **Sodium, basis:** the recipe has no sodium salt. Sodium from the recipe is 0 to about 2 mg a serve. Water adds 0 to about 7 mg at the WHO typical level, or about 33 to 68 mg at the level reported for Thai tap water. These are calculations; nothing has been measured.
- **Pack precaution (draft):** "Contains about 320 mg potassium per cube. Ask a doctor or pharmacist first if you have kidney disease or take medicines that can raise blood potassium. Do not drink more than one cube a day."

### Appendix D. Creator campaign
- **Layout:** left: the slide text. Right: the budget table.
- **Slide text:** a creator pilot tests first purchases and repeat before any paid media is scaled. Nine creators (four mid-tier and five mega-tier channels) over 13 weeks. Each creator receives a product kit and a commission per carton sold through a unique code. The offers are commission-led; no flat fee is committed. Every post carries a paid-partnership label and uses approved wording. No post shows alcohol, a drinking occasion or a health claim.
- **Budget (THB):** creator kits 442 (nine creators, 12 cubes each, at THB 4.09 a cube); commission cap 4,000 (500 cartons at THB 8.00 a carton sold through a creator code); Route A 18,000; contingency 10%; committed total 24,686. Conditional: a learning tranche of 20,000 for paid social, released after the week 4 read if the non-paid click-to-first-purchase rate is at least 1.0%; a scale tranche of 40,000, released at week 8 if the cost per first purchase is at or below THB 27.38. Ceiling 84,686.
- **Why flat fees are not used:** at the lower-bound benchmark fees (mid-tier 33,550 a post; mega-tier 671,000 a post), the nine creators cost 3,489,200 for one post each. Recovering that from the THB 27.38 contribution per buyer takes about 127,000 buyers, about one million cubes in the pilot, five times the planned annual volume.
- **Stop rule:** creator activity stops at once after a compliance breach not fixed within 24 hours, or after a controversy that cannot be cleared within a week.
- **Out of scope:** no creator has been contacted or named in this document. If creators refuse commission-only terms, the pilot runs Route A and the conditional paid social only.
