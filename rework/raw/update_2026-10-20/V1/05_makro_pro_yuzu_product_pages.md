## 5d. Reading

- Three URLs, two products: a Yuzu 6-pack at THB 84 and a Yuzu 24-pack at THB 324. Both pages exist and show a price on 20 October 2026.
- Both carry the delivery-area notice. The notice names "the area you selected". The fetch tool selects no area, so what area the notice refers to is not shown. The notice also sits on a non-Yuzu product (see file 07), so it is not a Yuzu-only signal.
- No page gives a date, so each price is "as read on 20 October 2026" and not a dated sale price.
- Conflict, recorded per SOURCING_POLICY rule 5: the search tool's own summary for the 6-pack said the price "shows at ฿80 on sale, down from ฿82" and "฿ 13.67 per unit". The opened page shows 84 and 14.00. The summary was not opened as a page, so the opened page governs. Makro prices also differ between pages for one product (file 07: Lemon x6 reads 82 on the collection page and 78 on its product page), so Makro prices are not stable across pages in one session.
