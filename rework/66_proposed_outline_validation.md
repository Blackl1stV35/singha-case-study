# Validation of the proposed 22-slide outline (19 October 2026)

Status: review only. Nothing has been built from this outline. Written for the team. Judge-facing material is not in this record.

## 1. Product mismatch

The outline introduces a product called "Capsule Soda" with a capsule, a machine or device, after-sales service and capsule waste. The product in this repo is the Lime Cube: a dissolvable pressed cube, one cube per pouch, dissolved in 330 mL of water ([rework/52](52_pivot_and_retirement.md), decisions D1 to D5 and D11). The string "Capsule Soda" does not occur in any record. The outline's UVP, its use steps and its operations slide (16) therefore do not describe this product. They must be rewritten, not reused.

## 3. Check against the deck

The Canva draft has eight pages, not yet saved or exported. Its topics are: cover, consumer use (outline 3), recipe (no outline slide), cube and pouch (no outline slide), pack and container (partly outline 16), unit economics (outline 18), desk valuation (outline 19), and unknowns (outlines 12 and 21). The deck therefore covers about 4 of the 22 outline topics. It does not cover market, competition, channels, entry mode or pilot. The outline does not cover recipe, cube, pack or container at all. Any combined outline must include both sets.

## 4. Language and judging

The outline is in Thai. The deck draft is in English. The judge handover and executive summary are in English. Confirm the deck language before building. The outline's slide 3 asks whether there is a business opportunity in Thailand. The proposal does not claim that, and the outline should not either, until a market source exists.

## 5. Recommendation

Do not build the 22-slide outline as written. Two options for the user to choose from:

1. Keep the eight-slide deck and add a recipe slide and a pack slide only where they fit. State the limits on market size, channels, entry mode and concept validation on the unknowns slide.
2. Build a combined outline of about 12 slides that keeps only topics with a source (outlines 1, 3, 5 to 6, 9, 11, 13, 14, 16 to 22) and marks the rest as limits. Outline 8 needs a number or should be removed.

Either way, the product must be the Lime Cube, and no capsule, machine or import content should appear.
