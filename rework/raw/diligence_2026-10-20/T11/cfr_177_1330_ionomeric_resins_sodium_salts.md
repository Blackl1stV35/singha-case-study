# 21 CFR 177.1330, ionomeric resins: sodium partial salts are a permitted food-contact film resin (saved reading, third pass)

- Source read: eCFR, Title 21, Part 177, section 177.1330 (versioner full text, date 2026-10-01). URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-10-01/title-21.xml?part=177&section=177.1330
- Date read: 2026-10-20. Tier: P (US regulation text). The fetch tool returned an extraction in short segments (a 125-character quote limit), not the full page.
- Extracted: the introductory text of the section lists "ammonium, calcium, magnesium, potassium, sodium, and/or zinc partial salts" of ethylene-methacrylic acid copolymers (ionomers). Paragraph (a): copolymers contain no more than 20 weight percent methacrylic acid units (15 percent for the vinyl acetate terpolymers). Paragraph (c): net acidified chloroform-soluble extractives of the finished article not to exceed 0.5 mg per square inch of food-contact surface.
- Reading for record 74 (DERIVED): a sodium-neutralised ionomer is an allowed heat-seal resin for food pouches in the US text. This is not a Thai rule and no Thai pouch rule was read. It is why the pouch film specification must name the seal layer and either exclude a sodium ionomer or show by a migration test that no sodium reaches the cube. The pouch film specification is not in the repo; no migration test exists (stated limit).
