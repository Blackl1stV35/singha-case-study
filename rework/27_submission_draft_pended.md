## Slide 4: the product (A)

- A frozen coconut or pandan drink in a banana or pandan leaf wrap, with a food-grade inner barrier. Peel the leaf and sip as it thaws.
- The newness is the drinking act, not an ingredient.
- Target: Thai buyers who value traditional packaging (a hypothesis; the evidence so far is about produce, not drinks).
- Precedent: a leaf-wrapped frozen ice-cream bar in Ratchaburi, one 2023 user post, unconfirmed.

## Slide 6: virtual evidence and its limits

Each screen states its assumptions. The thaw screens use sourced Bangkok climate and a radial heat model. The leak screens use a Washburn model with an unmeasured damaged-path size. The quantum runs check the method only.

## Slide 7: risks

- **Claim accuracy:** "leaf-wrapped" only. Outside water could defeat the seal, and that is not measured.
- **Holding rules:** whether the product is legally a beverage or an ice cream is for a Thai registration adviser. The ice-cream rule would be not above −2.2 °C.
- **Cold chain:** no Thai rule for processed frozen products or transport. The design target is −18 °C.
- **Leaf supply and food contact:** not verified.
- **Consumer evidence:** none for drinks.

## Slide 8: honest limits

- The novelty search is an absence search, partly limited by blocked sites. It is not proof.
- No physical, taste or consumer test has run, and none is in scope.
- The leaf in the render is a generic photo.
- The judging criteria are not published, so our rubric is ours.

## Speaking version (about 45 seconds)

We propose a leaf-wrapped frozen coconut-pandan drink. You peel the leaf and sip as it thaws. We found no leaf-wrapped frozen drink in extended searches, and we disclose one leaf-wrapped ice-cream bar sold at a Ratchaburi market, whose sale is not confirmed. Our virtual screens say that a street stall in shade gives a half-thawed drink in about 50 minutes, and in full sun in about 38 minutes. So it is kept in an insulated box and eaten within about an hour. We claim leaf-wrapped, not plastic-free. Outside water, such as splash, condensate or ice water, could defeat the leaf seal. That is not yet measured, and we say so.
