## Reading (team; revised 20 October 2026 after the independent check)

- Only the two NielsenIQ-attributed values carry both a data house and a dated window: THB 66,000 million for the 12 months to January 2024 (+12 percent; row 5 names NielsenIQ for the value, row 4 for the growth) and THB 70,772 million for the 12 months to November 2024 (+9 percent; row 6). Their windows overlap and the stated growth rates are consistent with a rising run (script key `niq_window_consistency`), which suggests but does not prove that they come from one series.
- Row 2 (62,000) names no source. It is trade press, window inferred only from the printed growth period (September 2022 to August 2023). It is not counted as NielsenIQ and no series test is drawn from it.
- Year labels conflict by one year across outlets: row 8 labels 66,000 as 2023 and 70,772 as 2024; row 12 labels 62,000 as 2023, 66,000 as 2024 and 70,000 as 2025; row 9 (Thansettakij 26 Mar 2025, saved in `thansettakij_623088_2025-03-26_pepsi.md`) repeats "over 66,000" with growth of 2.7 percent and no year. The 12 month windows in rows 4, 5 and 6 are the only dated labels.
- Row 7 (62,000, +3 percent, "last year") comes from a company statement without a window or source.
- Row 10 labels 68,000 as 2024 and says the market fell 4 percent "this year". 70,772 less 4 percent is about 67,900, close to 68,000, and row 11 gives about 67,000 for 2025. So 68,000 may be a 2025 level, or a 12 month window to August 2025 (colour soda 17,391 is 25.6 percent of 68,000 and 17,300 is 25.8 percent of 67,000, the same window as the colour figure). The pages do not say. This is a possible reading and a stated limit; 68,000 is not presented as a second 2024 value.
- Row 10: colour soda 17,391 is printed as "30% of the total market", which does not fit 68,000 (25.6 percent) or 67,000. Row 11 also prints black 30 percent and colour 30 percent, which cannot both be right. The inconsistency is inside the source.
- No row states retail or manufacturer level.
