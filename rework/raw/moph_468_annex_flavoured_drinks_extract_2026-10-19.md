## Condition codes read in this annex

- **127**: the level is calculated for the product ready to drink ("คำนวณในสภาพพร้อมบริโภค"). The cube is dissolved to make the drink, so the level applies to the drink as consumed.
- **TH3**: applies to mineral (electrolyte) drinks. Such a drink must be used together with sugar, in the amount the annex specifies. This applies only if the product were classed as an electrolyte drink; a flavoured sparkling drink is not one. Classification is an open regulatory question.
- **TH68**: when the additive is used as a flavouring, the amount must not exceed the maximum set by the annex's guideline.
- **188**: where acesulfame is used with aspartame salts, the combined amount must not exceed the stated limit.
- **26, 160, TH1**: not decoded in this extract. They are not needed for the design.

## What this extract settles

- Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid have no numerical limit in category 14.1.4 (quantum satis).
- Sucralose at 300 mg/kg is above the design level: 33 mg in 330 mL of drink is about 100 mg/kg.
- The sucralose condition 127 applies to the drink as consumed, which is how the cube is used.
- Condition TH3 applies only if the product is classed as an electrolyte drink.

## Limits

- The classification of the product (beverage, edible ice, or electrolyte drink) is not settled by this extract. It is a regulatory question for an adviser.
- The condition codes 26, 160 and TH1 were not decoded.
- The Thai FDA's own clarification notice gives a rule that a level permitted for category 14.1.4 also applies to its sub-categories (read earlier, SOURCED from the saved copy of the clarification in the sourcing run).
- The English translation was not used.
