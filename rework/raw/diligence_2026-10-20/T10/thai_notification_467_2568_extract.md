The fetch tool could not render the compressed PDF stream; it saved the 73.5 KB file, which was copied to a scratch folder and read with pdftotext (3,591 characters of text). The document is short and its operative text is reproduced here in substance, with the key clause verbatim.
- Heading: "Notification of Ministry of Public Health No. 467 B.E. 2568 (2025) Issued by virtue of the Food Act B.E. 2522 Re: Nutrition Labelling (No.2)". Given on 27 November B.E. 2568 (2025) by the Minister of Public Health.
- Clause 1: "This Notification shall come into force as from the day following date of its publication in the Government Gazette onwards." (The Thai FDA listing and a secondary database give 12 December 2025 as the date in force; not re-checked in the Gazette.)
- Clause 2 (verbatim opening): "The content of paragraph one of 2.1 under the heading '2. Conditions for displaying the nutrition labelling' of the annex 1 'Nutrition labelling: Format and required conditions' of the notification of the Ministry of Public Health (No. 445) B.E. 2566 (2023) ... shall be repealed and replaced with the following texts:" The new text: the background of the nutrition labelling shall be white (except large containers where it cannot be, which take any single colour); the font colour shall be the same as the frame colour; and a table of font sizes: Section 1 (nutrition information, servings) and Section 2 (amount per serving, energy and kilocalorie) bold, not less than 1.5 mm; Section 3 Part 1 "Total fat, Cholesterol, Protein, Total carbohydrate, Sodium and Potassium" and their numerical values and percentage of Thai RDI, bold, same height of not less than 1 mm; saturated fat and total sugars not less than 1 mm; Part 2 vitamins and minerals not less than 1 mm; Sections 4 and 5 not less than 1 mm.
- What it changes: only paragraph one of clause 2.1 of Annex 1 of Notification 445 (background colour and font sizes of the panel). It does not mention Annex 3 (Thai RDI), Annex 4 (claims) or any change to the nutrient list. Potassium is still listed as a Section 3 Part 1 nutrient in the font-size table.
- Consequence for the T10 record: the potassium line of the standard format, the 3,500 mg Thai RDI and the claim thresholds read from Notification 445 are not changed by Notification 467. The panel as printed would follow 467's white background and font sizes.
