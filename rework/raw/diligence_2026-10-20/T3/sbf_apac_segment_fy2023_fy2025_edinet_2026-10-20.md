# Suntory Beverage & Food, Asia Pacific business segment, FY2023 to FY2025 (annual securities reports on EDINET)

Tier: P (company annual securities reports filed with the Kanto Local Finance Bureau). Used as a PROXY for the Thai Pepsi bottler (Suntory PepsiCo Beverage (Thailand) Co., Ltd. is consolidated in this segment at 51.0%). The segment covers Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Oceania and health foods, so it is not a Thailand-only figure, and its segment profit is not a gross or net margin.

Date read: 20 October 2026 for all three documents. Access route: the WebFetch tool stored each PDF as a binary file, and the text layer was extracted locally with PyMuPDF in an isolated Python run (no OCR). The Japanese below is verbatim from the text layer; the English glosses are ours.

## FY2023 report (S100T62P)

Segment note (scope change): 「組織変更に伴い、従来「アジアパシフィック事業」に含めていたアフリカ事業を、当連結会計年度より「アジアパシフィック事業」から「欧州事業」に組み替えています。これに伴い前連結会計年度についても組み替え後の報告セグメントの区分に基づき作成しています。」
English (ours): Following an organisational change, the Africa business, which had been included in the Asia Pacific business, has been moved from Asia Pacific to Europe from this fiscal year. The prior year is also prepared on the new segment basis.

MD&A: 「［アジアパシフィック事業］ アジアパシフィック事業の売上収益は3,714億円（前年同期比5.3％増、為替中立0.0％増）、セグメント利益は431億円（前年同期比25.4％減、為替中立27.7％減）となりました。」
English (ours): Asia Pacific revenue JPY 371.4 billion (up 5.3%; 0.0% in constant currency); segment profit JPY 43.1 billion (down 25.4%; down 27.7% in constant currency).

Segment table, columns 日本 / アジアパシフィック / 欧州 / 米州 (millions of yen):
- Year 2023: 外部顧客への売上収益 708,141 | 371,435 | 339,274 | 172,871 (total 1,591,722); 計 (including internal) 371,440 for Asia Pacific; セグメント利益 40,455 | 43,075 | 51,725 | 20,982 (total 156,239).
- Year 2022, restated to the new basis: 外部顧客への売上収益 653,199 | 352,615 | 299,105 | 145,477 (total 1,450,397); 計 353,692 for Asia Pacific; セグメント利益 33,430 | 57,731 | 42,131 | 18,212 (total 151,506).

Thailand (FY2023 MD&A): 「タイでは、インフレの低下や観光客需要の回復が進む中、低糖製品を含めた「PEPSI」及び「TEA+」が好調に推移し、販売数量が伸長しました。」
English (ours): In Thailand, with inflation falling and tourism demand recovering, PEPSI, including low-sugar products, and TEA+ performed well and sales volume grew.

## FY2024 report (S100VHVQ)

MD&A: 「アジアパシフィック事業の売上収益は4,020億円（前年同期比8.2％増、為替中立3.1％増）、セグメント利益は454億円（前年同期比5.4％増、為替中立1.0％増）となりました。」
English (ours): Asia Pacific revenue JPY 402.0 billion (up 8.2%; up 3.1% in constant currency); segment profit JPY 45.4 billion (up 5.4%; up 1.0% in constant currency).

Segment table, year 2024: 外部顧客への売上収益 731,814 | 402,049 | 368,081 | 194,819 (total 1,696,765); 計 402,050 for Asia Pacific; セグメント利益 49,083 | 45,404 | 60,356 | 23,684 (total 178,529).

Comparative year 2023 in this report (same segment basis as the FY2023 report): 外部顧客への売上収益 708,141 | 371,435 | 339,274 | 172,871; 計 371,440 for Asia Pacific; セグメント利益 40,455 | 43,075 | 51,725 | 20,982. The 2023 figures agree with the FY2023 report.

Thailand (FY2024 MD&A): 「タイは、「PEPSI」や「TEA+」が好調に推移し、増収となりました。」
English (ours): Thailand: PEPSI and TEA+ performed well and revenue rose.

Note on the correction filings: the FY2024 report lists a 訂正報告書 for the FY2023 report filed on 14 March 2025 (content not read; stated limit). The FY2023 comparatives in the FY2024 report match the original FY2023 report, so the correction does not change the Asia Pacific figures used here.

## FY2025 report (S100XSOA)

MD&A: 「［アジアパシフィック事業］ 売上収益は3,941億円（前年同期比2.0％減、為替中立1.6％減）、セグメント利益は425億円（前年同期比6.4％減、為替中立6.2％減）となりました。」
English (ours): Asia Pacific revenue JPY 394.1 billion (down 2.0%; down 1.6% in constant currency); segment profit JPY 42.5 billion (down 6.4%; down 6.2% in constant currency).

Beverage business (FY2025 MD&A, page 37 of 148): 「飲料事業については、急速な事業環境変化への対応が遅れたベトナム及びタイでは、販売数量が前年同期を下回り、減収となりました。ベトナムでは、競争激化に加えて消費低迷により水カテゴリー以外の飲料市場が縮小し、タイでは、天候不順により主力の炭酸カテゴリー市場が落ち込んだ影響を受けました。」
English (ours): In beverages, Vietnam and Thailand, where the company was slow to respond to rapid changes in the business environment, saw sales volume fall below the prior year and revenue decline. In Thailand, the main carbonated category market fell because of unseasonable weather.

Segment table, year 2025: 外部顧客への売上収益 735,188 | 394,057 | 390,202 | 195,990 (total 1,715,438); セグメント利益 46,950 | 42,516 | 61,559 | 23,529 (segment profit total before the corporate adjustment 174,554).
