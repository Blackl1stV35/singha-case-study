## Extracted facts

- Row: C6H8O7, citric acid. Step 1: t = 25 C, pKa = 3.13. Step 2: t = 25 C, pKa = 4.76. Step 3: t = 25 C, pKa = 6.40. Unit: pKa (dimensionless, negative log of Ka). Table location: left column of printed page 8-46.
- Definition as printed: pKa is the negative logarithm of Ka for HA <-> H+ + A-. The table states that its data apply to dilute solutions.
- Ordering as printed: compounds are listed by molecular formula in Hill order.
- Conditions: all three citric acid rows are at 25 C. No footnote marker is visible on those rows. Ionic strength is not stated for these rows in the visible table.
- Edition: the handbook edition is not stated on the visible pages. The references list on printed page 8-42 cites the Merck Index, 14th edition (2006). That is a cited reference, not the handbook's own edition. The page footer carries a production stamp only.
- Isocitric acid (also C6H8O7, same page) is listed separately and is not the requested compound: step 1 3.29, step 2 4.71, step 3 6.40, all at 25 C.
