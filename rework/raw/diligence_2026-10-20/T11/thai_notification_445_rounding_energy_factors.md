# Thai MoPH Notification No. 445: rounding rule 2.6, energy factors, nutrient list (saved excerpt)

- Source: Thai FDA English translation of Notification No. 445 (B.E. 2566): https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=689363893683888128&name=P445_EN.pdf
- Date read: 2026-10-20. Tier: P. Status: SOURCED.
- The rounding table (clause 2.6, English printed p. 11, PDF p. 14) was read from a rendered page image (pdftoppm, 130 dpi) because the text layer scrambles the columns.

## 1. Clause 2.6 rounding rules (sodium and energy rows, read from image)

- Sodium and potassium, unit milligram. Result from analysis below 5 mg: "Declared as 0". Result 5 to 140 mg: "Rounded up/down as a step of 5". Result above 140 mg: "Rounded up/down as a step of 10". Amount considered insignificant: below 5 mg.
- Energy, unit kilocalorie. Result below 5 kcal: "Declared as 0". 5 to under 50 kcal: "Rounded up/down as a step of 5". 50 kcal or more: "Rounded up/down as a step of 10". Amount considered insignificant: below 5 kcal.
- Vitamins and minerals (except sodium and potassium): do not need to be declared in the calculation of Thai RDI rows.
- Footnote *: "It shall be declared as per serving size. If a food product's reference serving size is undefined because it cannot be classified into any food category or lacks a specified reference serving size, the manufacturer may choose to define the serving size ... 100 grams or 100 ml could be used instead."

## 2. Clause 2.2 and the standard format (English text layer)

- "2.2 All information of nutrient contents and energy values defined in each format of the nutrition labelling shall be declared even though the numerical values of that nutrient or energy may be considered as insignificant due to the conditions outlined No. 2.6 of this Annex."
- Standard format list (Annex 1, section 3, Part 1 and the sequence list): "Sodium*" and "Potassium*" where * means mandatory nutrients in the standard format.

## 3. Energy conversion factors (English text layer, Annex 1, clause 2.4 item 1)

- "Organic acid 3 kcal/g"
- Sugar alcohol: "Isomalt, Lactitol 2 kcal/g"; "Xylitol 2.4 kcal/g"; "Maltitol 2.1 kcal/g"; "Sorbitol 2.6 kcal/g"; "Mannitol 1.6 kcal/g"; "Polyglucitol 3.0 kcal/g"; "Erythritol 0 kcal/g".

## 4. Our arithmetic from these factors (DERIVED)

- Citric acid 2.10 g x 3 kcal/g = 6.30 kcal per serve. Mannitol 0.80 g x 1.6 kcal/g = 1.28 kcal. Total 7.58 kcal per serve. Rounded to a step of 5: 10 kcal declared.
- With erythritol at 0.80 g: total 6.30 kcal, rounded to 5 kcal declared.
- Whether the citric acid factor applies depends on how the regulator reads the organic-acid row; a lab analysis could differ (limit).
