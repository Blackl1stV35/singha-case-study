## Distribution structure

- Boon Rawd Trading Co., Ltd. is the marketing and distribution company for the group: efinancethai (12 Mar 2026) https://www.efinancethai.com/efinReview/efinReviewMain.aspx?name=er-c-20852 states it "takes care of marketing and distribution of all products in the group" (S). The same article lists the three SET-listed companies of the group as Singha Estate (S), S Hotels and Resorts (SHR) and BG Container Glass (BGC). It does not state whether Singha Corporation or Boon Rawd Brewery is listed.
- Singha Corporation was set up in 2001 to manage the operations of more than 150 Boon Rawd group companies and reports to the Boon Rawd Brewery board (Bright TV, 14 Sep 2022, https://www.brighttv.co.th/lifestyle/boonrawd-singha-leo, S; consistent with capture 47, Singha Corporation About Us).
- A distributor in which Boon Rawd Trading holds a stake: InfoQuest (23 Jun 2021) https://www.infoquest.co.th/2021/99496 (S) reports that Boon Rawd Trading took 30% of Siri Pro Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of Srinanaporn Marketing (SNNP, 50%; management 20%), which has 11 distribution centres, reaches about 70,000 retail shops and about 3,600 traditional wholesale shops, and runs cash vans. The article lists Siri Pro as a general consumer-goods distributor; it does not say Siri Pro carries Singha Lemon Soda.
- No source read gives the number of outlets that stock the can, or the split between modern and traditional trade by volume. Positioning (2020) says outlet counts are not disclosed.
