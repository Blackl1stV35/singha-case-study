Executive Summary
Lemon Soda
One cube, dissolved in one 330 ml serve, made in the glass
1 Problem statement
  • Lemon drinks are sold ready to drink in cans.
  • No domestic dissolvable drink cube has been found.
  • The gap is a new form of drinking, not a new flavour.
2 Identity problems
  • Singha already lists a lemon soda can, which its product page says has no sugar.
  • So Lemon Soda overlaps its flavour.
  • The name needs a register search (Gate 7). The lion and SINGHA are for this proposal only.
3 Proposal
  • One cube, dissolved in one 330 ml serve; one cube per pouch, not yet confirmed by Singha.
  • Desk case: THB 4.91 contribution per cube.
  • NPV THB 0.88 million at 200,000 cubes a year, THB 500,000 fixed cost (page 13).
4 KPI and expected goal
  • 3 units per store per week; repeat 30%, floor 15% (all assumptions).
  • 60% sell-through in 90 days; two listings in six months.
  • Goal: a measured go or stop decision at M10 to M11.
The ask: a gated proof programme, not a launch: a go decision, named gate owners and twelve decisions (page 17).
Desk model on assumed volumes and fixed costs, not a forecast. Sources: appendix C.

Marketing campaign

The campaign tests direction and repeat before any paid media is scaled

Concept pre-test

  • Recruited online panel: men aged 25 to 59 who drank alcohol in the past year
  • One brand-blind concept, signed off by M3

  • Rule fixed in advance; it reads direction, not size
  • No media, no sale
M7 to M10, 13 weeks

Pilot, two routes

  • Step 1: click-to-purchase read on non-paid traffic, first four weeks
  • Route A: in-store trial in pilot stores; at least 3 units per store per week
  • Route B: paid social to one listing, only after Singha sets the cost threshold; at base inputs it does not recover its first-purchase cost in one serve (DERIVED)
  • Repeat read at 8 and 12 weeks
M10 to M11

Measured decision

  • Scale one route, repeat once in the same stores, or stop
  • Scale needs repeat of 15% or more on both cohorts
  • And contribution of THB 3.51 or more per cube
  • National listing is read at about M13

Appendix A

What the label may say, and what it may not

May say

  • Composition as fact: potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, sucralose and lemon flavour; no sodium carbonate. The binder and filler are not yet named or checked for sodium.
  • "Drop one cube into 330 ml of water and stir"
  • "Single-serve pouch"
  • "Contains an intensive sweetener: sucralose"
  • Net weight only after weighing

Barred, with no clearance route

  • Sugar-free, zero-sugar and no-added-sugar wording
  • Sodium-free and low-sodium wording
  • Health, function, electrolyte and hydration wording
  • Vitamin, potassium-benefit and calorie wording
  • Certification and award wording; none is verified

Held until Gate 7 or a Thai legal review

  • Novelty words
  • Fizz, sparkling, bubbles and CO2 release, until a measured test exists
  • Lemon fruit imagery; glassware and bottle shape
  • Alcohol cues
  • The lion or SINGHA without written sign-off
Every consumer-facing word needs legal and brand approval before any stimulus, sampling or pilot print.
