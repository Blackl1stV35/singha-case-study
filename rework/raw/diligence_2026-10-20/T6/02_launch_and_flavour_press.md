## Flavour count check (wording as printed; not a slide figure)

- Marketeer, 25 Jun 2024: five flavours in total (Lemon, Yuzu, Red, Ume, Pink).
- Brand Inside, 11 Nov 2025: "6 flavours to date" before Lemon Cream. Marketing Oops and MitiHoon, 10 Nov 2025: "over 6 flavours".
- Reading: five core flavours plus the Melon limited edition (April 2025) make six. "Over six" also fits a seventh. No release gives a total after Watermelon (March 2026). So the count was six or seven before Lemon Cream, depending on whether the press counts the limited editions. No total is derived.
