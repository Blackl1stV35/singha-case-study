# Sucralose sweetness ratio and ADI (read 20 October 2026)

URL: https://www.fda.gov/food/food-additives-petitions/aspartame-and-other-sweeteners-food  (page "Content current as of: 02/27/2025")
Tier P (regulator page). Read 20 October 2026 through WebFetch (extraction).
Quoted: sucralose "600 times sweeter than table sugar (sucrose)"; "Sucralose is 5 mg/kg bw/d" (FDA ADI).
The EFSA ADI for sucralose, 15 mg/kg bw/day, was opened later on 20 October 2026 and is saved in `efsa_sucralose_adi_2026.md` (P, EFSA news story of 17 February 2026). The JECFA ADI was not reached (UNVERIFIED). The FDA 5 mg/kg figure is the stricter reading and is the one used for the intake shares.
