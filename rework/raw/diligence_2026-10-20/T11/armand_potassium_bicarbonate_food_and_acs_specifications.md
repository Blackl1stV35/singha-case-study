# Armand Products potassium bicarbonate: food-grade and ACS-grade sales specifications (saved reading)

Both PDFs were opened on 20 October 2026 (fetched through the WebFetch tool; the text layer was extracted with PyMuPDF). Tier: S for the ACS sheet and for the food sheet, because each is a maker's own sales specification (a company statement, not a compendium). Status: SOURCED for what each sheet prints.

## 1. Food grade (the grade the cube would use)

- URL: https://armandproducts.com/products/media/potassium-bicarbonate-food-grade
- Title on the sheet: "Potassium Bicarbonate, Food Grade, SALES SPECIFICATION"; the last line reads "REV. 05/22".
- Table as printed: total alkalinity (as KHCO3), dry basis, 99.5 to 101.5 wt percent; loss on drying 0.25 wt percent max (footnote: more stringent than the USP 0.30 percent); normal carbonate (as K2CO3) 2.5 wt percent max; heavy metals (as Pb) 0.001 wt percent max; residual solvents "meets requirements"; identification positive for potassium and bicarbonate.
- Notes: meets USP/NF chemical test requirements; meets cGMP and Food Chemicals Codex purity standards; Kosher and Halal certified.
- The sheet has no sodium limit. The assay is total alkalinity as KHCO3, so sodium bicarbonate would count as alkalinity and the assay does not bound sodium (DERIVED).

## 2. ACS (reagent) grade, indicator only

- URL: https://www.armandproducts.com/products/media/acs
- Title: "Potassium Bicarbonate ACS Grade, SALES SPECIFICATION". No revision date is printed.
- Table as printed: total alkalinity (as KHCO3), dry basis, 99.7 to 100.5 wt percent; sodium (as Na) 0.03 wt percent max; calcium (as Ca) 0.002 max; chloride (as Cl) 0.001 max; magnesium (as Mg) 0.001 max; sulfur compounds (as SO4) 0.003 max; insoluble matter 0.01 max; ammonia (as NH4) 5 ppm max; heavy metals (as Pb) 5 ppm max; iron (as Fe) 5 ppm max; phosphate (as PO4) 5 ppm max. Note: meets Food Chemicals Codex chemical test requirements.
- Use in the record: this is a higher grade than food grade, so its 0.03 wt percent sodium limit is an indicator of what the maker can supply (0.24 mg of sodium in 0.81 g), not a limit for the food grade. The planning high case for food grade stays at 0.1 wt percent (ASSUMPTION, 0.81 mg) until a food-grade certificate of analysis states sodium.
