## Reading for record 70

- The 330 baht price is a promotional selling price while the promotion is valid (to 20 October 2026, the date of this read).
- The undiscounted list reading for the case is 408 baht, which is 17.00 a can.
- Record 68d section 2 says promotion prices are not used, but its own 7-Eleven rule reads the lower price as the selling price where the saving equals the difference and the badge is a discount. That rule was applied to D01 and D02. The conflict is recorded in record 70 section 6 for the lead.
