# Effervescent tablet density, porosity, hardness and compaction benchmarks (read 20 October 2026)

All read through WebFetch (extractions); tier P for peer-reviewed papers, S for the supplier white paper.
1. Pharmaceutics 2025, 17(5):676, "Effervescent Tablet Preparation by Twin-Screw Melt Granulation with Sorbitol as a Melt Binder", https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12115153/ . 11.28 mm flat punches, target mass about 350 mg; "a compaction force of 20 kN (equivalent to 200 MPa ...)"; calculated true density 1.8246 g/cm3 (additive rule over citric acid 1.665, sodium bicarbonate 2.2, sorbitol 1.489; the text elsewhere says 1.814); tensile strength all above a 2 MPa acceptance level; effervescence/disintegration 62-78 s. Tablet porosity and breaking force values: not reported in the text.
2. RSC Advances 2019 (Zheng et al.), "Improvements in sticking, hygroscopicity, and compactibility of effervescent systems by fluid-bed coating", https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9072709 . 8.5 mm flat punches at 53, 106 and 159 MPa. At 159 MPa: porosity of uncoated effervescent granule tablets 13.67% unloaded (7.01% loaded); fluid-bed coated groups 9.03-11.51% unloaded.
3. Advanced Biomedical Research 2014, "Formulation, characterization and physicochemical evaluation of amoxicillin effervescent tablets", https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4219208 . Hardness 40.66-56 (direct compression) and 60.6-74.6 (fusion); the extraction did not confirm the unit (read as N, UNVERIFIED unit); effervescence time 90.6-118.5 s.
4. Jungbunzlauer, "CITROCOAT EP: a new effervescent compound for versatile applications" (undated), https://www.jungbunzlauer.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/CITROCOAT-EP-a-new-effervescent-compound-for-versatile-applications.pdf . 5 g tablets, 25 mm diameter, 70 kN compression force; Figure 1 hardness values 117.8, 70.2 and 67.9 (assignment to samples not legible in the extraction); cleaner tablets 85.2 to 137.6. Our arithmetic: 70 kN / (pi x 12.5^2 mm2) = 142.6 MPa.
Limit: no source found that gives a measured density or hardness for a 14 mm cube. A 14 mm cube is not a tablet shape in these papers (all are round flat tablets).

## Second pass (20 October 2026, after verification)
- Item 1 (Pharmaceutics 2025;17(5):676, PMC12115153) opened again: confirmed. 11.28 mm flat punches; "a compaction force of 20 kN (equivalent to 200 MPa at the used punch diameter of 11.28)"; target mass about 350 mg; calculated true density 1.8246 g/cm3 (free-volume calculation uses 1.814); tensile strength above the 2 MPa acceptance level (2.7 MPa quoted for one 760 s, 5 rpm tablet); disintegration 62.67 to 77.83 s. Porosity and breaking-force values are not in the text.
- Item 2 (RSC Advances 2019, PMC9072709): the page returned a browser verification page on the second pass and one WebFetch call returned a tool error; the 8.5 mm punch, 53, 106 and 159 MPa and the 13.67 percent porosity at 159 MPa are quoted from the earlier extraction above and are NOT reconfirmed.
- Our arithmetic (spec_revalidation.py, results key compaction): 20 kN over a 11.28 mm circle = 200.1 MPa (matches the paper); Jungbunzlauer 70 kN over 25 mm = 142.6 MPa (S); a 14 x 14 mm face needs 28.0 kN at 142.6 MPa, 31.2 kN at 159 MPa and 39.2 kN at 200 MPa; the Pharmaceutics tablet is about 1.9 to 2.4 mm thick (350 mg at the paper's true density with 0 to 20 percent porosity), height over diameter 0.17 to 0.21, against 1.0 for the 14 mm cube.
