# Thai MoPH Notification No. 445 (B.E. 2566): sodium nutrient-content claims (saved excerpt)

- Document: Ministry of Public Health Notification No. 445 (B.E. 2566), Re: Nutrition Labelling. Published in the Royal Gazette 4 January 2024 (per the Thai FDA list and the ChemLinked record); in force 2 July 2024 (per the same sources).
- Thai original (official): https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=583907023645712384&name=P445.pdf
- English translation published by the Thai FDA (labelled as the FDA's translation): https://food.fda.moph.go.th/media.php?id=689363893683888128&name=P445_EN.pdf
- Date read: 2026-10-20. Tier: P (regulator text). Status: SOURCED.
- Method: the English text was read from the PDF text layer (pdftotext). The Thai PDF uses a legacy Thai font, so its text layer does not decode. The Thai sodium tables were read from rendered page images (pdftoppm, 110 dpi), Thai PDF pages 40 (printed p. 11) and 45 (printed p. 16). The Thai transcriptions below were typed by the reader from those images. Our translation is marked.
- Saved copies of the PDFs are kept outside the repo (scratch folder) because of size.

## 1. Case 1 and Case 2 (English, clause 2.1 and 2.2, printed pp. 2 to 3)

"Case 1 For food with prescribed reference serving size in Annex 2 to this Notification and food with similar consumption characteristics ... they shall make a nutrient claim according to the defined conditions the Table 1 of this Notification."

"Case 2 For foods that do not follow the conditions in Case 1, to make a nutrition claim, it shall be calculated from the amount of food in 100 grams or 100 milliliters using prescribed conditions in Table 2 of this Annex."

"2.2 ... it shall have the nutrient contents as prescribed below per amount of food consumed per serving as declared on the label and per the amount of reference serving size ..."

Remark on reconstituted foods (English, clause 4.2 area, printed about p. 14): "In case of determining the serving size of a dry food or other food products that need to be reconstituted before consumption (either by adding water or other liquid with insignificant nutrient content), the reference serving size shall be defined based on the food category of such food product as defined in the Table above."

## 2. Table 1, sodium rows (Thai text from image, Thai PDF p. 40, printed p. 11; English from text layer, English PDF pp. 54 to 55)

Thai transcription (sodium, row by row):
- ปราศจาก, ไม่มี (free, without, free of, no, zero): "มีโซเดียมน้อยกว่า ๕ มิลลิกรัม" = contains sodium less than 5 mg (per reference serving and per amount consumed per serving on the label).
  Additional conditions: 1. "ห้ามใช้ข้อความกล่าวอ้างนี้ หากอาหารนั้นโดยธรรมชาติเป็นไปตามเงื่อนไขอยู่แล้ว" = must not use this claim if the food naturally meets the conditions. 2. calculate sodium as sodium in the food, not as salt (sodium chloride). 3. if the food contains salt or an ingredient generally known to contain sodium, mark the ingredient and state that it has very little effect on sodium content ("มีผลต่อปริมาณโซเดียมน้อยมาก"). 4. follow conditions 1.1 and 2.2.
- ปราศจากเกลือ (salt free, without salt): follow the conditions and additional conditions of "free of sodium".
- ต่ำมาก (very low, very low in): "มีโซเดียมน้อยกว่า ๓๕ มิลลิกรัม" = less than 35 mg. Additional: not to be used if the food naturally meets the condition; sodium calculated as sodium, not salt; follow 1.1 and 2.2.
- ต่ำ (low, low in, low source of, little): "มีโซเดียมน้อยกว่า ๑๔๐ มิลลิกรัม" = less than 140 mg. Additional conditions as for very low.
- ลด, น้อยกว่า (reduced, reduced in, lower, lower in, less): sodium reduced by 25 percent or more compared with the reference food.
- โซเดียมน้อย (light in, lite in): sodium reduced by 50 percent or more.
- ไม่เติมเกลือ (unsalted, no salt, no salt added, without salt added): (1) no salt added during production, and (2) the comparison food is similar and normally has salt added in production. Additional: if the product does not meet "free of sodium", it must carry the statement "ไม่ใช่อาหารที่ปราศจากโซเดียม" (not a sodium-free food).
- ใส่เกลือน้อย (lightly salted): salt added at least 50 percent less than the normal food.

English (text layer, same rows): "Contain sodium less than 5 milligrams"; "Contain sodium less than 35 milligrams"; "Contain sodium less than 140 milligrams"; "Reduced sodium content of more than or equal to 25 percent compared to the reference food"; "Reduced sodium content of more than or equal to 50 percent compared to the reference food"; "No salt added during the production process"; "It shall be calculated as the amount of sodium in food not as salt (Sodium chloride)"; "When salt is contained in the food or when some of the ingredients are generally known to contain sodium, that ingredient shall be marked and stated as 'having almost no impact on the sodium content.'"

## 3. Table 2 (per 100 g or per 100 mL, Case 2), sodium rows (Thai image, Thai PDF p. 45, printed p. 16)

- ปราศจาก, ไม่มี: per 100 g "มีโซเดียมไม่เกิน ๕ มิลลิกรัม" (not more than 5 mg); per 100 mL "มีโซเดียมไม่เกิน ๕ มิลลิกรัม" (not more than 5 mg).
- ต่ำมาก (very low): per 100 g not more than 40 mg; per 100 mL not more than 20 mg.
- ต่ำ (low): per 100 g not more than 120 mg; per 100 mL not more than 60 mg.
- ลดปริมาณลง, น้อยกว่า (reduced): reduction of 25 percent or more.

## 5. General conditions and dual display (English text layer, verified in this run)

- Annex 4, 1.1 (1): "However, the claim of 'free from' or 'low' on the label shall complied to these additional conditions as follows: (1) The claim as 'free from' or 'low' are not allowed if any foods or food types naturally complied to the prescribed conditions and do not undergo any particular production methods or any recipe modifications that allow a reduction of the nutrients to the prescribed conditions. ... However, manufacturers may declare a fact on the nutrition information of that particular food or that food types across all brands" (the notification's example: vegetable cooking oil does not contain cholesterol).
- Annex 4, 1.1 (2): certain foods or food types produced from improved production methods or recipes may carry "free from" or "low" if the nutrient content meets the criteria (the notification's example: soy milk, plain and flavoured).
- Annex 1, 1.7 (dual display): "In the case that foods could be consumed with other food contained in the same container or such food need to be mixed with other ingredients and/or have to be processed/cooked as instructed on the label, the declaration of the nutrition labelling of that product shall be declared as condition as sold and prepared/cooked condition as prescribed below." Clause 3 of the remark: the text "product" and "prepared product" are declared as the name of food types.
- Annex 4, 2.4 (other conditions): "If there is a declaration of any specific nutrients and its content outside of nutrition labelling frame on the label ... which do not comply with prescribed criteria for making a claim, it shall be stated that it is not intended to make a claim attached to a declaration of its nutrient content. The examples of the statements are 'contains sodium 200 mg. per 30 ml. - not a low sodium food' ..."

## 6. Notes on the numbers (our reading, marked)

- The sodium-free, very low and low thresholds in Table 1 are "less than" limits, per reference serving and per consumed serving (clause 2.2). Table 2 uses "not more than" limits per 100 mL.
- For the cube (dry beverage prepared before consumption), Annex 2 category 2 item 3 gives the reference serving as the "defined amount for preparing the beverage of 200 milliliters", a product amount that makes 200 mL (corrected wording; the first version wrote it as 200 mL). The consumed serving is 330 mL. Clause 2.2 requires the content per consumed serving and per reference serving. Correction after the second reading: Annex 4 clause 2.1 Case 1 (2) says that where the reference serving is not more than 30 g the test is on the consumed serving and on two-fold the reference serving (the amount that makes 400 mL), so the 330 mL test does not necessarily bind alone; see thai_notification_445_panel_potassium_water_second_pass.md section 3.
- Whether Case 1 or Case 2 applies is a regulatory classification question (limit).
