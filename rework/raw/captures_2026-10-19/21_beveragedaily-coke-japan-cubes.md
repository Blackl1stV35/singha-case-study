## Extracted facts

- Article publication date: 17 June 2021 (last updated 18 June 2021), article header.
- Product format: freeze-dried cubes made from barley tea, green tea and coffee concentrate, sold under the "1,2,CUBE" brand by Coca-Cola Japan. Each cube is made by freezing and vacuum-drying the concentrate. To use one, drop it into hot or room-temperature water. The article says the technology is pending patent (unheaded opening and body text).
- Pack: resealable paper-based pouch holding about 15 cubes; each cube makes about one cup (unheaded body text). Pouch dimensions and cube weight: not on page.
- Launch timing: the two tea cubes from May on Amazon Japan; the coffee cube from June (Eco-friendly section). Month only. The year is not stated beside the months; it is implied by the June 2021 article date. Exact launch day: not on page.
- Retail price: JPY 600 per pouch (the article gives US$5.50 as a conversion; no rate or date given for the conversion) (Eco-friendly section).
- Stated purpose: an easy, tasty and eco-friendly instant option for people drinking at home. The company cites a survey of over 500 adults aged 20 to 40, in which 67.2% wanted better tea or coffee at home during COVID-19. This is the company's survey as reported in the article; it was not verified. The company also says each cube has no individual wrapper, to reduce packaging waste (unheaded body text; Eco-friendly section).
- Environmental claims (company claims as reported, Eco-friendly section): the paper-based pouch uses about 13% less plastic resin and emits about 18% less CO2 than comparable plastic pouches. The article does not define the comparison set beyond "comparable plastic pouches".
- Outcome: the article gives no sales figure. The company will consider other sales channels based on early interest on Amazon; no export plans at present (Eco-friendly section).
