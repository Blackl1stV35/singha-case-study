- Date: 27 November 2024. To: Singapore Exchange Securities Trading Limited. Subject: "Progress on the Delisting Tender Offer for All Remaining Shares of Sermsuk Public Company Limited".
- Conditions precedent (as stated in the 3 July 2024 announcement): (i) the Sermsuk shareholders' meeting resolved to approve the SSC Delisting with not less than three-fourths of the votes and no objections from holders of more than 10 percent; (ii) the delisting approved or waived by the relevant authorities including the SET, financial institutions and other contractual parties.
- "So Water has been informed by SSC that all Conditions Precedent were fully satisfied and accordingly, So Water made the Tender Offer with the Tender Offer period of 45 business days from 19 September 2024 to 22 November 2024."
- "...the Tender Offer period has ended on 22 November 2024, and that So Water acquired 91,786,404 shares in SSC, representing 34.52% of the total number of issued and paid-up shares in SSC, through the Tender Offer, resulting in So Water holding 263,741,208 shares in SSC, representing 99.19% of the total number of issued and paid-up shares in SSC as of 27 November 2024."
- "In view of the foregoing, the SSC Delisting will occur subsequently in due course."
- Signed by the Company Secretary, Thai Beverage Public Company Limited (ThaiBev), Sangsom Building, Chomphon, Chatuchak, Bangkok.

## Reading

- The 99.19% in the SGX announcement (263,741,208 shares) matches the 2025 AGM quorum figure (263,742,657 shares, 99.1885%) within the rounding of the attendance (the AGM count includes shares held after 27 November 2024 and other holders). This is a consistency check, not a new source.
- The minutes of 30 January 2025 state that Sermsuk "has been delisted from the SET". No later Sermsuk statement was read; the SEC filing index read earlier shows no filing after 15 November 2024.
