## Extracted facts

Definition (section: Quick Take, paraphrased):
- A functional beverage is a drink designed to deliver a benefit beyond plain water, such as energy, hydration, gut health, beauty or satiety. It gets that benefit from added functional ingredients such as caffeine, electrolytes or fibre, and most keep sugar low or remove it.
- The page's one-line version reads: "any drink engineered to deliver a health or performance benefit."

Market size (section: "How big is the functional beverage market in 2026?" unless stated):
