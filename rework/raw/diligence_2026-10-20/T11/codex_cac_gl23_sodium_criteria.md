## Clause 7.2 (non-addition of sodium salts), read from the Codex PDF in this run

- Source: https://www.fao.org/input/download/standards/351/CXG_023e.pdf (8 pages; text read with pdftotext after WebFetch saved the file). Date read: 2026-10-20. Tier: P.
- Verbatim: "7.2 Non-Addition of Sodium Salts. Claims regarding the non-addition of sodium salts to a food, including 'no added salt', may be made provided the following conditions are met: (a) The food contains no added sodium salts, including but not limited to sodium chloride, sodium tripolyphosphate; (b) The food contains no ingredients that contain added sodium salts, including but not limited to Worcestershire sauce, pickles, pepperoni, soya sauce, salted fish, fish sauce; and (c) The food contains no ingredients that contain sodium salts that are used to substitute for added salt, including but not limited to seaweed."
- Footnote 5: competent authorities may permit technological sodium salts other than sodium chloride, "as long as the final food would still comply with the conditions for 'low in sodium' claims".
