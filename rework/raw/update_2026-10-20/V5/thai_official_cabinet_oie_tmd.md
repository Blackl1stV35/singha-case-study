# Thai official texts: Cabinet briefing of the Ministry of Industry report, OIE reports, TMD monthly summaries

- Item: V5. Date read: 20 October 2026. Tiers differ by section. Section 1 (the Cabinet briefing) is tier S after the review of 20 October 2026, because its issuer is inferred from the text and the host is not the Cabinet Secretariat or the Ministry. Sections 2 to 5 (OIE reports) and section 6 (TMD pages) are tier P for what each text states (official government releases). The causal statements in them are the issuers' own attributions and are not estimates of an effect.

## 1. Cabinet briefing, 9 July 2567 (2024), Ministry of Industry industrial economy report for Q1/2567 and April 2567 (tier S, direction only; use tier P only after a copy from the Cabinet Secretariat or the Ministry is opened)

- URL: https://infocenter.oic.go.th/upload/cms/1745051594_8866.pdf (WebFetch returned the binary; the PDF was copied to a scratch folder and its text layer extracted locally with PyMuPDF). Five pages, Thai. The text names the Cabinet as acknowledging the report proposed by the Ministry of Industry. The host is an information centre site (infocenter.oic.go.th) and the document carries no letterhead, so the issuer is inferred from the text. The date 9 July 2567 and the Thai passage below were confirmed in the PDF text by the reviewer.
- Q1/2567 passage (text layer, spacing and the sara am character exactly as extracted): เครื่องดื่มที่ไม่มีแอลกอฮอล์น้ าแร่และน้ าดื่มบรรจุขวดประเภทอื่นๆเพิ่มขึ้นจากสินค้าเกือบทุกรายการยกเว้นเครื่องดื่มกาแฟส าเร็จรูปจากสภาพอากาศที่ร้อนจัดท าให้ผู้บริโภคมีความต้องการสินค้าเพิ่มมากขึ้น
- Gloss (our translation): non-alcoholic drinks, mineral water and other bottled drinking water rose in almost every item except instant coffee drinks, because very hot weather raised consumer demand.
- The passage gives no percentage for beverages. The April 2567 paragraph of the same document credits the rise in the manufacturing production index mainly to a low base in April 2566 and to tourism, and credits air conditioners (+24.19 percent) to very hot weather.

## 2. OIE, Report on the Industrial Economics Status Q2/2024 and Outlook for Q3/2024

- URL: https://www.oie.go.th/assets/portals/1/files/quarterly_industrial/Q2_2024andOutlookQ3_2024_en.pdf (binary copied to scratch and text-extracted locally).
- Food industry section: "The MPI of beverage increased by 7.5 percent from rice whisky, beer, soft drinks, and fruit flavored drinks." No weather reason is given for beverages.

## 3. OIE, Report on the Industrial Economics Status Q1/2025 and Outlook for Q2/2025

- URL: https://www.oie.go.th/assets/portals/1/files/quarterly_industrial/Q1_2025_EN.pdf (same handling).
- "The MPI of beverage sector also declined by 3.8 percent"; carbonated soft drinks and soda water down 10.0 percent (carbonated soft drinks 10.3, soda water 8.2); "primarily due to the growing variety of beverage products in the market". No weather reason.

## 4. OIE, Report on the Industrial Economics Status Q2/2025 and Outlook for Q3/2025

- URL: https://www.oie.go.th/assets/portals/1/files/quarterly_industrial/Q2_2025_EN.pdf (same handling).
- "MPI of beverage sector: The index contracted by 8.9 percent"; carbonated soft drinks and soda water down 17.8 percent (soft drinks 19.2, soda water 5.4), attributed to the entry of new beverage products. The non-alcoholic beverage industry is also said to have contracted because some manufacturers paused production for maintenance and others extended halts beyond six months. No weather reason.

## 5. OIE, Report on the Industrial Economics Status May 2025 (monthly brief)

- URL: https://www.oie.go.th/assets/portals/1/files/monthly_report/en_briefMay2025.pdf (same handling).
- "Non-Alcoholic Beverages: The industry contracted by 13.6 percent, led by reduced production of carbonated soft drinks, instant coffee beverages, and purified drinking water." Beverage MPI down 9.6 percent, with carbonated soft drinks and soda down 25.6 percent.
- The same page attributes the air conditioner decline to a weaker domestic market "with the rainy season arriving earlier than the previous year". No weather reason is given for beverages in that month.
