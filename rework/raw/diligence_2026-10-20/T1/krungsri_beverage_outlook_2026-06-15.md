# Krungsri Research, Industry Outlook 2026-2028: Beverage Industry (announced 15 June 2026)

- URL: https://www.krungsri.com/en/research/industry/industry-outlook/food-beverage/beverage/io/beverage-2026
- Date read: 20 October 2026 (page opened by HTTP GET, text read). Tier: S (bank research report). Footnote 3 of the report names Euromonitor as the source of the annual domestic sales volume, and the Office of Industrial Economics as the source of the Q1 2026 monthly value. Euromonitor is paid and was not opened.

## Extract (English, as printed)

- "In 2025, Thailand's domestic beverage sales volume totaled 14,019.1 million liters, valued at USD 30,398.3 million" (footnote 2: converted at the Bank of Thailand average rate for 2025, THB 32.88 per USD).
- "Non-alcoholic drinks recorded domestic sales of 10,764.5 million liters, valued at USD 10,555.5 million. The leading product category was purified and mineral bottled water ... accounting for 59.6% of total non-alcoholic beverage volume. This was followed by carbonated and soda (25.0%) ..."
- "Carbonated and soda: Sales volume rose 2.1% in 2025 to 2.7 billion liters (Figure 11)". "In Q1 2026, the sales volume of carbonated and soda expanded 5.3% YoY".
- "Carbonated and soda: Production volume in 2025 declined -9.8% to 3.7 billion liters (Figure 7). Carbonated drink output fell by more than -10.4% ..." (production includes exports; the report links the fall to maintenance shutdowns and the closure of the Thailand-Cambodia border crossings.)
- "Export volume contracted -5.9% in 2025, while export value grew 4.7%"; Cambodia was over one quarter of carbonated export volume in 2025.
- Forecast: "carbonated and soda growth of 1.8-2.8% per year" (domestic sales volume, 2026 to 2028).
- Footnote 3: "For the domestic market analysis, annual domestic sales volume data is sourced from Euromonitor. For Q1 2026, monthly domestic sales value data is sourced from the Office of Industrial Economics (OIE)."

## Reading (team)

- The report gives volume for carbonated and soda (the page does not define the category, which may be wider than carbonated soft drinks), and no baht value for the category. Team check (not printed on the page): 25.0 percent of 10,764.5 million litres is 2,691 million litres, which matches the printed "2.7 billion liters".
- The USD 10,555.5 million non-alcoholic total at THB 32.88 is about THB 347,000 million, which is far above KResearch's THB 226,000 million for 2025. The two bank notes use different market definitions or sources; the difference is not explained in either.
