# Lime Cube: executive summary (19 October 2026)

## The proposal in one paragraph

Lime Cube is a dissolvable drink cube for domestic Thailand. One cube in one 330 mL serve makes a carbonated lime drink: a new form of consumption, not a new flavour. The lead segment is men aged 25 to 34 (desk model, unverified inputs); men aged 45 to 59 follow later.

## What is handed over

- Dose: 0.55 volume of carbon dioxide per 330 mL; gate for R&D to define.
- Working pH anchor of 3.5; taste untested.
- Composition, stated as fact: potassium bicarbonate, citric acid, sucralose; no sodium carbonate.
- One cube per pouch is inferred. No nutrition, health or performance claim is made.
- Domestic only; no domestic cube listing was found.

## The nearest competing product

Boon Rawd Trading launched Singha Sparkling Water Lime Flavour in March 2026: a 330 mL can at 15 baht. It overlaps Lime Cube on flavour. The forms differ: Lime Cube dissolves in the glass, one cube per pouch; the competitor is a ready-to-drink can. The company describes its can as sugar-free with 0% calories; Lime Cube makes no sugar-free claim.

## The figures at the recommended dose

- Potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 0.52 g to neutralise it, or 2.10 g for pH 3.5 (DERIVED; acid constants S, UNVERIFIED for this repository).
- Potassium 317 mg, 9.1% of a Thai reference of 3,500 mg a day (DERIVED; reference S, secondary copies, primary not opened).
- Sucralose 33 mg, about 100 mg/kg (DERIVED; sweetness target an ASSUMPTION; 600 times sucrose, S). Category 14.1.4 permits 300 mg/kg under condition 127, for the drink ready to drink (P, SOURCED for flavoured-drink rows; full annex not read).
- Solubility 0.592 volume at 35°C, the lowest in the 5 to 35°C serve range (S, SOURCED).
- Desk model: about 0.50 volume after 10 minutes at 25°C; above a 0.44 g/L fizz threshold (about 0.22 volume) for about 96 minutes at most. Threshold: S, abstract only, a PROXY for a soft drink. Neither is a gate or validated.
- Cost THB 3.84 per cube in a 12-cube carton (DERIVED; pouch and print costs ASSUMPTIONS; no Thai quote). The 12-cube cost includes a carton share of THB 0.42 per cube (THB 5 per 12-cube carton, from a retail box listing used as a PROXY).
- About 128,580 cubes per 20 ft container, 12-cube cartons (DERIVED; S specifications; packing an ASSUMPTION).

## What R&D are asked to define

1. Retention gate for 0.55 volume: CO2 left at 25°C, after how many minutes. The desk value is not a gate.
2. pH and acid level; 3.5 is a working anchor.
3. Sweetness accepted at 33 mg sucralose per serve; no sensory test has run.
4. Chilled, iced or ambient; serve rule and pack wording.
5. Acceptable fizz duration and its measure.
6. Potassium declaration and medical wording; kidney function and potassium-sparing medicines first.
7. Classification, which sets the limits. Excise item 02.02 covers sweetened (intensive sweeteners count) or flavoured drinks (P, SOURCED). The 14% ad valorem rate applies if the value base applies; the base was not read. TH3 covers electrolyte drinks only.
8. Pouch barrier, shelf life and Thai prices: no water-vapour rate, shelf test or quote exists; prices are assumptions.

## What is not yet known

- Taste, demand and Thai retail response are untested.
- Pack pressure, headspace and storage temperature are not modelled; novelty and freedom to operate are unassessed.
- The Thai patent register is unsearched.
- The lion emblem was confirmed for this proposal. Brand-owner and legal sign-off sit with Singha's department and are not held here; the alcohol-cue question is unchecked.
