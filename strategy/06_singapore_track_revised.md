## Financial read-across (`04_financial_model_results.md`)

- Base case: NPV about −S$1.0m. The year-3 share (0.6 percent) falls short of the break-even share (1.25 percent).
- Bull case (price at the ceiling, faster ramp): NPV about +S$7.7m; K1, K2, K3 and K7 all pass.
- The decisive variable is the realised CIF price, followed by the exworks cost and the fixed cost.

## Verdict and what would change it

Status: **not validated**. The base case misses K1 and K7; the bull case passes. Entry would need a realised price above the category unit value, a named botanical cleared for SFA, a name cleared on IPOS, and a confirmed importer and co-packer.
