## 1. https://www.singhacorporation.com/en/about-us (read 20 Oct 2026)

- Singha Corporation is described as "an important subsidiary of Boon Rawd Brewery".
- Its role is to oversee management of Boon Rawd's subsidiaries, which the page puts at "more than 150 companies".
- It reports directly to Boon Rawd's Board of Directors.
- Boon Rawd reorganised its group of companies in 2000. Singha Corporation was established in 2001, when Boon Rawd became the holding company and day-to-day operations moved to the individual companies.
- Since then the group has expanded beyond beer, starting with snacks and food, then moving into packaging, logistics, property development and energy (as the extract stated).
- Navigation categories: Beers and Alcohols (Singha, Singha Reserve, Leo, MY BEER, Snowy Weizen, EST.33 Copper, Asahi, Carlsberg, Corona, Kronenbourg); Beverages (Singha Water, Singha Soda, Singha Lemon Soda, Purra, Purra Vitamin Water, FIJI); Snacks (Masita); Food (Pun Dee Rice).
- The page does not mention restaurants, Singha Park, international operations, a distribution company, vision or mission, R&D, quality, supply chain or sustainability.
- Footer address and contact entity: Boonrawd Trading Co., Ltd., 1003 Samsen Road, Dusit, Bangkok 10300. The page does not describe it as a distribution company.
