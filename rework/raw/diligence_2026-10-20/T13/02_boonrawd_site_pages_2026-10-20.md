Access route: WebFetch (model-written extracts, not verbatim; the pages were not read in full by the author). Tier S, COMPANY CLAIM (the company's own self-description pages). Footer on every page: "Copyright 2020 Boonrawd Brewery All Rights Reserved"; the pages carry no other date and a "under renovation" pop-up image name, so the content is dated 2020 or earlier and is not current as of 2026. Names of individual directors are not recorded here on purpose; the proposal assigns roles, not persons.

## 2. Company profile: https://www.boonrawd.co.th/index.php/en/about/profile

- Founded 1933 by Phraya Bhirom Bhakdi; "Thailand's first brewery"; privately owned and managed by the founder's 3rd and 4th generation descendants, with professionals from other fields.
- Brands named: Singha, Leo, B-ing, Purra, Sanvo, Syder Bay, Boonrawd Farm, Pundee, Masita.
- Activities: manufacturing, packaging, agriculture, real estate, food and restaurants, retail fashion and lifestyle.
- "over 50 affiliated companies" (this conflicts with "more than 150" on the Singha Corporation page; the two are unreconciled; no date).
- CSR: environmental programmes, scholarships at "25 colleges and universities", medical assistance and disaster relief through the Phraya Bhirombhakdi Foundation.

## 4. History: https://www.boonrawd.co.th/index.php/en/about/history

Selected entries as extracted:
- 1933: Boon Rawd Brewery registered (4 August). 1934: Singha launched.
- 1970: begins exporting Singha Beer.
- 1989: Singha starts to be produced in cans.
- 1998: Leo launched.
- 2002: distribution network revitalised; within three years Boon Rawd builds a network of 12,000 subcontractors or dealers.
- 2014: Singha Estate Co., Ltd. set up (real estate); food and beverage businesses begin to take shape; expansion into energy.
- 2018: Est.33 (a subsidiary restaurant) launches Kopper Beer in cans; Food Factors Co., Ltd. established to expand into food; the company joins Linfox of Australia in logistics and supply chain.
- Not on the page: the 2000 to 2001 holding company reorganisation; Singha Park.

## Limits

- The site is old (2020 footer) and thin. It names no R&D unit, no regulatory or legal unit and no sustainability report.
- "Our Group" gives 19 companies; the Singha Corporation page gives more than 150; the profile gives over 50. The three counts are not reconciled and no count is used as a fact about structure beyond what each page says.
