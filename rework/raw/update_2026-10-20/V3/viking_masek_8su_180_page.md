## Notes

- The 80 is a dry-cycle maximum (spec row label), on a two-lane machine. The overview sentence says "speeds up to 80 bags per minute" without the dry-cycle wording. The page gives no with-product speed.
- Two lanes means the 80 is a duplex figure. The single-lane Viking 8S-285 and 8S-320 pages read for this item give 60 and 50 on the same dry-cycle basis.
- The page does not say whether motion is continuous or intermittent.
- Effect on the record: this page puts a maker's premade-pouch figure above 60, so 60 is a mid-range maker figure and not a ceiling.
