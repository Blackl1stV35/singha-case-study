# Organization audit and evaluation of the group

Public sources only; no outside contact or plant access. Evidence reviewed to 19 October 2026.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the text was captured and read. UNVERIFIED: not captured, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, inputs and tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## Scope: the organiser's group

- The organiser is Singha. In scope: Boon Rawd Brewery (holding company) and Singha Corporation, which oversees Boon Rawd subsidiaries that its About page puts at more than 150 companies (S, SOURCED; undated).
- The group is the organiser, not a client. Group data, factories and trademarks are assumed unavailable; group capability is a benchmark only.
- The product is a lime drink cube for domestic Thailand only, with one component (see Limits). Its carbonation is a desk model and is untested.

## Limits

1. No Singha or Boon Rawd cost, supply, capacity or line data was found. The organiser has not been asked.
2. Ratio figures stay UNVERIFIED until captured and read. Company pages were read as model-summarised extracts.
3. The product class (beverage, edible ice or electrolyte drink) is an open regulatory question. Sale is blocked until the route is settled.
4. Sucralose is checked at the flavoured-drink level (300 mg/kg, category 14.1.4). Its sub-category rows were not read; the design stays within the limit only if none sets a level below about 100 mg/kg.
5. The recommended dose is 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL, within solubility at every serve from 5 to 35 °C (0.592 volume at 35 °C, the lowest value in that range). The dose gate is for R&D to define; this audit sets none.
6. Taste is untested, and the pH 3.5 target is a working anchor. Carbonation is a desk-model surface upper bound. A Bangkok April temperature figure for the 35 °C serve limit is UNVERIFIED (PDF unreadable).
7. The organiser's registration form wording refers to three components. This is unchecked, and the design uses one.
8. One cube per pouch is inferred (ASSUMPTION). The alcohol-cue risk is not assessed here; sign-off sits with the organiser's department.
