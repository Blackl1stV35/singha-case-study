## Exact text used, by model

- SHTGF30-6 (canning line): "The maximum production capacity can reach 20,000 cans / hour." "It adopts 30 filling valves and 6 capping heads." Main parameter: "300 cans/minute" with "330ml"; "270 cans/minute" with "500ml". Can types: "330ml、 500ml".
- SHTGF20-4/10-2 (can filling sealing machine): "Output, 330ml: 200cans/minute(20-4)/100cans/minute(10-2)". Output at 500 mL: "180cans/minute(20-4)/80cans/minute(10-2)". Filling valves: 20 and 10. Seaming heads: 4 and 2.
- SHTGF9-2 (filler characteristic): "Output: 100cans/minute with 330ml". Filling valves: 9. Seaming heads: 2. The page describes it as "suitable for low-speed filling".
- SHTGF60-8 (aluminum can filling machine): "The maximum production capacity can reach 40,000 cans/hour under high-speed operation" and "The machine adopts 60 filling valves and 8 models." Main parameter: "600 cans/minute" with "300ml"; "500 cans/minute" with "600ml". Can types: "330ml、 500ml".

## Notes

- The SHTGF60-8 row is internally inconsistent: the main table says 600 cans a minute with "300ml" and 500 cans a minute with "600ml", the characteristics say 330 mL and 500 mL, and the description text says "8 models" where the seaming table says "8 heads" (the table value is used in the record). It is recorded, not used as a firm 330 mL rate.
- Re-read on 20 October 2026 (second WebFetch) to confirm the label conflict and the seaming-head counts: SHTGF9-2 has 2 heads, SHTGF20-4/10-2 has 4 or 2 heads, SHTGF30-6 has 6 heads, SHTGF60-8 has 8 heads (seaming table). The page gave the same values on both reads.
- The 20,000 and 40,000 cans per hour figures are stated for SHTGF30-6 and SHTGF60-8 only; SHTGF9-2 and SHTGF20-4/10-2 state no hourly maximum.
- Every figure is the maker's own rating, so it is also a COMPANY CLAIM.
- The other three rows are the only maker-stated 330 mL rates found in the sources read for a filler and seamer line, with a dated copyright.
- Converted to cans per minute: 100 (SHTGF9-2), 200 (SHTGF20-4), 300 (SHTGF30-6). The 20,000 cans per hour for SHTGF30-6 is about 333 per minute, close to the 300 per minute rating; the spread is recorded.
- The page's own tables do not state a product or a fill temperature.
