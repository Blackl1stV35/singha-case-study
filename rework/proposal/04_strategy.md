# Strategy: Lime Cube (19 October 2026)

Scope: the Thai domestic market only. No export, overseas or acquisition option is assessed.

## Tier key

P: a government or regulator text read in full, a company's own filing, official statistics, a peer-reviewed article read in full, or a primary database. S: any other source, including press, trade portals, consultancy pages, company web pages that are not filings, handbooks reproduced online, and search snippets. SOURCED: the text was captured and read. UNVERIFIED: not captured, blocked, unreadable, or only a snippet was read. DERIVED: our arithmetic, inputs and tiers shown. PROXY: a stand-in for what is not measured. ASSUMPTION: a value chosen for the model, not sourced.

## 1. Target segment

The lead segment is men aged 25 to 34; men aged 45 to 59 are a later volume segment.

- Current alcohol drinking: 68.6% of men aged 25 to 44 and 65.4% of men aged 45 to 59 (2024) [P, UNVERIFIED; source 1]. The table covers alcohol drinkers, a PROXY for the non-alcoholic audience; the 25 to 44 band stands in for 25 to 34.
- Potency (current alcohol drinking times the age-adjusted daily sugar-sweetened share): men 25 to 34 against men 45 to 59 is 1.15 (90% interval 1.03 to 1.28); the order against men 35 to 44 is not established. [DERIVED from UNVERIFIED inputs and an ASSUMPTION for the age share.]
- Everyday use of sugar-substitute drinks, a proxy for intensive sweetener use, is higher at 45 to 59 (1.0%) than at 25 to 44 (0.6%) [P, UNVERIFIED; source 2], so it does not support the lead segment.
- Bangkok's April mean maximum, 35.7 °C, marks the hot-season occasion [P, UNVERIFIED; source 3]. No mixing or chilled-use test has been run.

## 2. Proposition, format and cost

The proposition is a format: one dissolvable cube in a 330 mL serve releases carbon dioxide and makes the drink [ASSUMPTION]. Waterdrop and Ricola have launched dissolvable drink cubes [S, SOURCED; sources 16 and 17]. These overseas analogues are cited only because no domestic example was found.

One component is enough. Self-assessed [ASSUMPTION]: moderate on ingredients and nutrition, moderate on alternative formats, weak on a new trend-driven target.

At 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL, the recommended dose (about 8.1 mmol) [DERIVED]: potassium bicarbonate 0.81 g; citric acid 0.52 g stoichiometric and 2.10 g at the working pH of 3.5; sucralose 33 mg (100 mg/kg). The carbonating agent is potassium bicarbonate, not sodium carbonate. Potassium is 317 mg, 9.0% of the Thai reference of 3,500 mg a day [DERIVED; reference S, SOURCED; source 4]. This is a composition figure stated as fact, not a benefit claim. The target pH of 3.5 is a working anchor; taste is untested.

Format: one cube per pouch, in cartons of 12 [ASSUMPTION; not confirmed with the organiser]. A 20-foot container holds about 128,580 cubes [DERIVED; source 11; carton size ASSUMPTION].

Cost: THB 3.84 per cube in a 12-cube carton (THB 3.42 before a THB 0.42 carton) [DERIVED]. Reactants THB 0.12 [DERIVED from import values, source 8; PROXY]; pouch THB 1.30 [ASSUMPTION, a chosen base]; print THB 1.50 and processing THB 0.50 [ASSUMPTIONS]; carton THB 5 [ASSUMPTION; compare source 10]. Cartons of 6 and 24 give THB 4.09 and 3.79 [DERIVED; carton price ASSUMPTION].

An overseas listing range of USD 0.03 to 0.05 per pouch (source 9; S, PROXY, UNVERIFIED) is used only because no Thai pouch price was found. At the 15 October 2026 midpoint of 33.55 THB per USD [P, SOURCED; source 7; DERIVED], it is THB 1.01 to 1.68. Across THB 0.67 to 2.70 per pouch, cost runs THB 3.21 to 5.24 per cube [DERIVED; band ASSUMPTION].

## 3. Price and channel

No channel is chosen: convenience stores, online marketplaces or in-store sampling.

- The captured article gives Singha's sparkling water as lemon, 330 mL, THB 15 suggested retail [S, SOURCED; source 12]. A 325 mL Singha soda, 24 for THB 195, is about THB 8 a can [DERIVED; S, SOURCED; source 13].
- Shelf price THB 10 to 20 (base 15); ex-factory share 60% (50 to 70%) [ASSUMPTION]. At THB 15: ex-factory THB 9.00; gross margin 57.3% before freight and overhead [DERIVED].
- Break-even shelf price is THB 6.40 at base cost and THB 5.35 to 8.73 across the pouch band [DERIVED]. Registration, tooling, trademark and marketing are excluded.
- About 15,945 7-Eleven stores at end-2025, read as Thailand-only by inference [P, SOURCED; source 14].
- Paid social does not pay on base inputs: a paid click at THB 11.5 [S, SOURCED; source 15; one agency's figures, a PROXY] exceeds the THB 5.16 contribution per serve [DERIVED]. At an assumed 1.0% conversion [ASSUMPTION], each first purchase costs about THB 1,150 [DERIVED].

## 4. Decisions, limits and claims

Open decisions:

- Dose: 0.55 volume of CO2 per 330 mL is recommended. Solubility at 35 °C is 0.592 volume [S, SOURCED; source 5], and the desk model keeps the full dose dissolved at serves from 5 to 35 °C [DERIVED; no bench test]. The release gate is not set; R&D is to define it.
- Sweetener: sucralose at 100 mg/kg, within the 300 mg/kg limit for category 14.1.4 (flavoured drinks) under condition 127, which applies the limit to the drink as consumed [P, SOURCED; source 6]. The amount rests on an untested, assumed sweetness target. Condition TH3 applies only to electrolyte drinks. Potassium bicarbonate and citric acid are quantum satis in the category [P, SOURCED; source 6].
- Classification (beverage, edible ice or electrolyte drink) and sub-category placement within 14.1.4 are open; a regulatory adviser must decide.
- Brand: the SINGHA name and lion appear only as the organiser's brand. Print or sale needs sign-off from the organiser's brand-owner and legal functions, which this proposal does not hold. The alcohol-association cue is unchecked.

Limits:

- Desk model only, with no bench, sensory or shelf test. Fizz duration is a surface-only upper bound; no decay curve is measured.
- One cube per pouch and the one-component reading are unconfirmed. No Thai reactant or pouch price was found; no supplier quote has been obtained.
- The NSO inputs are UNVERIFIED, so the segment ranking is a model output, not consumer research.
- Three Thai retailer listings could not be read on 19 October 2026; their stock status is UNVERIFIED.
- Solubility values are for pure water with CO2 only, not this product.
- Two Singha company pages (source 19) conflict on the parent company; the strategy does not depend on it.
- No desk figure answers the question for people on potassium-retaining medicines or with kidney disease.
- The pending status of EP 4,516,115 A2 is the mirror's assumption (source 18). US 5,254,355 A is unchecked. No freedom-to-operate view is given.

Not claimed: no nutrition or functional statement is made. The fizz is a mechanism only. No Thai novelty, consumer result, market size or sales forecast is claimed.
