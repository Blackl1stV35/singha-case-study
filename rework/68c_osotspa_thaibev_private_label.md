## 0. What this review found, in eight lines

1. **est cola belongs to the ThaiBev group, not to Osotspa.** Sermsuk's own filing says Great Brands Limited sold the est brand and trademark in 2014 to Super Brands Company Pte. Ltd. and that Sermsuk makes est for ThaiBev, "who owns the trademark" (G1, P). The 2014 press names International Beverage Holdings Limited (Interbev) as the buyer (D1, S). No page read links the two entity names, so the owner is stated as the ThaiBev group with both names (section 1). Sermsuk PCL makes and distributes est, and Thai Drinks Co., Ltd. markets it (C2, C9, G1). The Osotspa product pages list no cola, no soda and no lemon or lime soft drink (A4).
2. **"S Cola" is est.** The Thai name is เอส โคล่า and the press prints it as est or "เอส". The "S Cola 8.6%" in the deck is est's volume share of March 2024 (C4). The owner of S Cola and the ThaiBev non-alcoholic arm are the same group.
3. **est is the closest maker to the lemon can.** It holds 10.6% of carbonated soft drinks and 24.6% of the coloured (non-cola) segment (October 2025, S, basis not stated; C6). Sermsuk's filing of 8 October 2024 lists "Est Lemon Lime" and "Est Kamikaze" among eight est flavours (G1, P), and the online listings show a Kamikaze Lime flavour (C10, S). The lemon-lime flavour once carried the name est Clear (C7, S, a 2016 article that speaks of it in the past tense). Whether a lemon-lime est is on shelves in October 2026 was not confirmed: the 24 est items the listing page returned include none.
4. **est sells in two price worlds.** Its 325 mL can packs read THB 13.4 to 23.4 per 330 mL (5 listings, median 16.75). Four of them are deliverable, and those read THB 16.8 to 23.4 (median 17.01); the Makro listing at THB 13.4 could not be delivered to the area selected when read. est's bottle multipacks of 360 mL and above read THB 5.1 to 12.1 per 330 mL when 50% promotion rows are excluded (12 listings; S, 20 October 2026; script), and its 250 mL 24-packs read THB 16.2 to 17.9 (3 listings; two of the three are 50% promotion prices, and the one non-promotion listing reads THB 17.88). PET is the main format by volume (64% to 70%, basis not stated; C3, C4). The THB 15 base-case cube price (ASSUMPTION, deck slide 8) sits below all four deliverable can listings and above the one Makro can listing at THB 13.4, which could not be delivered to the area selected; it sits above est's larger bottle multipacks and below its 250 mL 24-packs.
5. **Osotspa sells a sugar-free lemon-flavoured sparkling can, but it is an energy drink.** M-150 Sparkling Pink Lemonade, a 245 mL can at THB 20 (the pack size and price come from the Lotus's listing, F1; the company brand page, A4, gives the product and its vitamins). M-150 Sparkling itself launched at the end of 2023 (company release; the release does not state a flavour). Osotspa has no soda.
6. **No private-label lemon or lime carbonated drink was found** for aro (Makro), Lotus's, Big C or Tops in the online listings read. Aro is Makro's own brand, and Own Brand lines (Aro, Savepak, Petz Friend) make up about 18.5% of Makro's total sales (P, CP Axtra newsroom article dated 6 June 2025; the sentence gives no year; Z1). The aro items that matched the search were jellies and gummies in soda flavours (F1). Private-label soda is a stated limit, not a finding of absence.
7. **No maker reviewed lists a dissolvable cube or tablet drink.** The ThaiBev product groups (B3), Sermsuk's product list (G1) and the Osotspa portfolio (A4) contain ready-to-drink, carbonated, energy, water and dairy lines only.
8. **Sugar-free lemon and lime cans were listed in 2026 at THB 12.4 to 19.3 per 330 mL.** The two large systems sell 7UP No Sugar and Sprite No Sugar in 2026 listings. 7UP No Sugar lemon soda (325 mL cans) reads THB 12.40 to 16.25 per 330 mL across four 2026 listings: six cans at THB 75 at Lotus's (THB 12.69 per 330 mL), 24 cans at THB 293 and 12 cans at THB 161 at the Suntory PepsiCo official store on LazMall, and six cans at THB 96 at the same store (S, 20 October 2026; record 68b). Sprite No Sugar (325 mL cans in 24-packs) reads THB 14.13 to 19.29 per 330 mL across four 2026 listings (median 16.48; S, 20 October 2026; record 68a). Sprite Lemon Plus, a sugar-free lemon soda first reported in Thai news on 29 August 2022, was priced at THB 15 for a 325 mL can in that report (dated; no 2026 price or current shelf presence found). Among the est lines read, the sugar-free item is named as a cola (est Sugar Free); no sugar-free lemon-lime est was found. A lemon cube would therefore enter a market where sugar-free lemon and lime cans, including the proposer's own Singha Lemon Soda, were listed at about THB 12.4 to 19.3 per 330 mL (S; section 4.3). The proposer's can is sugar-free by its maker's own product page (P).
