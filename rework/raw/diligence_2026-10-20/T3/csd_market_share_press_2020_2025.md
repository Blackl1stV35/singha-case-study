All pages read 20 October 2026 through WebFetch. WebFetch converts a page to text and a small model answers the prompt; each block is that answer (an extract), not a verbatim page copy. Tier S (trade or business press) throughout. English translations of Thai are ours. No block gives a data owner (Nielsen, Kantar or other) for the share figures unless stated.

## 1. Thansettakij, 16 March 2024 (the source of the deck's "S Cola 8.6%")

- URL: https://www.thansettakij.com/business/marketing/590894
- Headline topic: summer sales, S (เอส) pushing into carbonated drinks, widening to Gen Z. Article date 16 March 2024 (2567), 06:40.
- Market value: "ตลาดน้ำอัดลมซึ่งมีมูลค่ากว่า 6.6 หมื่นล้านบาท" (the carbonated drink market worth over THB 66,000 million). No year or source given.
- Shares, all in the opening paragraph with no year or source: Coca-Cola "มีสัดส่วน 51%" (Coke, Fanta and Sprite under Coca-Cola (Thailand)); Pepsi "เบอร์สองอย่างเป๊ปซี่ ครองส่วนแบ่ง 30%"; S Cola "ยังคงรักษาส่วนแบ่งการตลาดเชิงปริมาณอยู่ในสัดส่วน 8.6%" (keeps a volume market share of 8.6%), number three.
- Kantar penetration: 26% in 2023 before the rebranding campaign, 28% in 2024. Nielsen IQ: S grew 15% against 12% for the category, February 2023 to January 2024. Share target: regain 11% within 3 to 5 years. Marketing budget THB 200 million for "est Awaken Awesome Senses".
- S sales by pack: PET bottles 64%, glass bottles 18%, cans 2 to 3%. By region: Bangkok 37%, Central 28%, Northeast 19%, North 10%, South 5%.
- Marketer named: Thai Drink Co., Ltd. (บริษัท ไทยดริ้งค์ จำกัด); the marketing director is Supaporn Den-phaisan. The article names no owner or bottler (ThaiBev, Sermsuk and Interbev are not mentioned).
- Not in the article: price, pack sizes in mL, channels, 7UP, Mirinda.

## 3. Gotomanager, 29 October 2023

- URL: https://gotomanager.com/cover-story/5234
- Market over THB 62,000 million, cola 75% and coloured soft drink 25%. Shares as printed (2023): Coke 54%, Pepsi 30%; S third, volume share 9.1% in August 2023, up from 7% before the rebrand.
- Owner chain (Thai): Sermsuk PCL "เป็นผู้ผลิตและจัดจำหน่าย" (producer and distributor) at the 2012 launch; Thai Beverage Logistics later became the major shareholder, within the ThaiBev group; Thai Drink Co., Ltd. is the marketer. Pepsi ended its contract with Sermsuk in the same year as the launch.
- Launch: 2 November 2012. Rebrand March 2023, over THB 150 million, Gen Z 15 to 24. Sales growth 22.6% (September 2022 to August 2023). Marketing 2024: THB 200 million.
- Channels: traditional shops and restaurants nationwide.
- Not in the article: price, numeric pack sizes, any sugar-free line.

## 4. Bangkok Biznews, about October 2023

## 5. Thansettakij, 3 April 2025

- URL: https://www.thansettakij.com/business/marketing/624003
- Article date 3 April 2025. Shares as printed: Coke 51%, Pepsi 39%, S 8.9%, Big Cola 5%. These add to 103.9%, so they cannot be shares of one total; the bases differ and are not stated. Market THB 66,000 million, growth 2.7% (Pepsi section); Big Cola's section gives about THB 60,000 million and 2 to 3% growth.
- Dark cola is 72% of soft drinks per Pepsi and about 85% per Big Cola. Low-sugar is about 10% of the market (Big Cola). Summer is about 30% of annual sales.
- Store checks: average price cuts of THB 5 to 10 a bottle. Re-read verbatim on 20 October 2026: "สังเกตได้ชัดเจนจากการเดินสำรวจตามร้านค้าต่างๆ ที่พบว่ามีการ "ลดราคาต่อขวด" โดยเฉลี่ย 5-10 บาท เลยทีเดียว" is the reporter's own store walk-through and names no brand, so it is a market-wide observation and is not tied to Pepsi, Coke, est or Big Cola. Big Cola says it has not raised prices for more than 17 years.
- Share list as printed (re-read 20 October 2026), introduced as who leads and follows in the Thai carbonated soft drink market: Coke, Coca-Cola (Thailand) Co., Ltd., 51%; Pepsi, Suntory PepsiCo Beverage (Thailand) Co., Ltd., 39%; "เอส" (S), Thai Drinks Co., Ltd., 8.9%; Big Cola, AJ Thai Co., Ltd., 5%. No year and no data owner.
- No pack sizes or shelf prices in the article.

## 7. Brand Buffet, 17 April 2025

- URL: https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2025/04/suntory-pepsico-beverage-strategy-2025/
- Pepsi is 75% of the company's total sales. Pepsi 39.1% of the dark cola segment, second to Coke at 51%. Market THB 66,000 million. Dark cola 72% of value in 2025 (forecast), coloured soft drinks 38%.
- Phase 4 of the Thai sugar tax from 1 April 2025, rates from THB 0.3 to THB 5 a litre; the company says it has been cutting sugar and that 7UP was reformulated to 100% no sugar. Pepsi no-sugar grew 16.1% on average. Sting reached second place in modern energy drinks with a 12 to 13% share in its first year.
- 2025 plan: "Must Win" continues across RTD tea, RTD coffee, energy drinks and carbonated soft drinks; new Pepsi Ba-muey (plum) flavour; THB 1,000 million, the largest investment in seven years; Saraburi carbonated capacity up 50%.
- Not in the article: prices, pack sizes, channels.
