## Arithmetic (DERIVED, our arithmetic)

- 1,450 divided by 62,725 = 2.31 per cent. The printed share is 2.4 per cent. The two do not agree exactly. The record uses the midpoint 2.35 per cent.
- 1,450 divided by 13,172 = 11.0 per cent. The printed 12 per cent of the canned segment does not reconcile with the 1,450 figure. The 12 per cent is not used.
- 12 per cent of 13,172 = 1,581 million baht. Not the printed 1,450. The basis of the 12 per cent is unresolved.
- Year of the 2.4 per cent and the 1,450 million: not printed. The article's sentence says the share was held "two years after launch" (หลังจากเปิดตัวได้ 2 ปี). Record 69 dates the launch to August 2020, so two years after launch is about 2022. The article's market value is the 2021 value. The measurement year is therefore UNRESOLVED between 2021 and 2022. The 2021 market value is the article's; the share's year is not settled by any printed text.
