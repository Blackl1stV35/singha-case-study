# 68e. Competitor due diligence: dissolvable drink analogues and the home-soda route (task T5b, version 5)

Date: 20 October 2026. Version 5, after a third independent check (version 4 followed a second check). Section 11 lists what changed in each version. The changes of version 5 are the Nuun dose basis (500 mL directions with the 473 mL nutrition basis shown), the waterdrop US dose and serving wording, the Ricola launch status (planned dates in the June 2025 release, not confirmed launches), the Interpharma form flag with its 7.25 sensitivity, the Nuun 2021 acquisition tier, the price-change lines, and the multi-pack scope. Scope: overseas drink cubes and tablets that a Thai shopper or retailer could meet (waterdrop Microdrink, Ricola Drink Cubes, Coca-Cola Japan 1,2,CUBE, Nuun, Hydralyte, Berocca and Redoxon with the Thai lemon vitamin tablets, SodaStream). Product in view: the Singha Lemon Soda Cube (one dissolvable lemon cube in 330 mL of water; base shelf price THB 15, an ASSUMPTION; cost THB 4.09 per cube).

Tiers follow rework/submission/appendix_a_tier_key.md and rework/SOURCING_POLICY.md. P is a company filing, a company release or a maker's own page read on the day. S is press, a retailer page or an aggregator. DERIVED is our arithmetic, produced by `rework/screen/analogue_price_per_serve_68e.py` (results in `rework/screen/analogue_price_per_serve_68e_results.json`). Every SOURCED figure has its page saved in `rework/raw/diligence_2026-10-20/T5b/` (each file says that it holds a fetch-tool extract and not a verbatim copy). Prices are converted at the Bank of Thailand USD rate of 15 October 2026 (THB 33.5495, mid of buying sight and selling transfer; read from the saved Bank of Thailand file, read on 19 October 2026, and not re-read live, because the Bank of Thailand page returned navigation only on the re-read) and European Central Bank cross rates of 19 October 2026 (PROXY for a same-day rate): THB 37.6593 per EUR, THB 44.7234 per GBP, THB 0.2120 per JPY, THB 23.8380 per CAD. Tax bases differ and are stated per price: the UK retailer price includes 5% VAT as its page states, the EU store price includes VAT, the US store price excludes sales tax, and Thai marketplace prices are as listed.

## 1. Result in brief

- Thai presence, dated 20 October 2026 (Priceza and retailer pages, S unless stated). No dissolvable soda cube was found on the Thai pages read. Listed in Thailand: Nuun (75 Priceza matches, mostly third-party Lazada sellers, plus Shopee and iHerb); Hydralyte (8 matches: Lazada effervescent tablets, Shopee ice blocks, iHerb sachets); Berocca and Redoxon (ALL ONLINE, the 7-Eleven online shop; Lazada including the Bayer Consumer Health Official Store on LazMall; Shopee; online pharmacies); and lemon vitamin tablets of Demosana, HOF Effer and Interpharma (Lazada, Shopee, TikTok Shop; Demosana rows at Tops Online and Boots). Not found on the rows visible: a waterdrop cube (36 of 1,073 Priceza rows visible, none a cube) and a Ricola Drink Cube (24 visible rows, all lozenges or candy, sold at Lotus's Shop Online at THB 20 to THB 179, including a 250 g gift set at THB 179). Coca-Cola Japan's cube was sold on Amazon Japan only (P, 2021). SodaStream does not list Thailand among its countries (P).
- Price per serve against the can ladder. The cans that the cube meets on the Thai shelf read THB 16.25 to THB 18.00 per 330 mL as single cans, with a single-can median of THB 17.13, and THB 13.75 per 330 mL as the multi-pack median of 18 rows (68d second pass, S, 20 October 2026). The cube at THB 15 is THB 1.25 to THB 3.00 below every single can, THB 1.25 above the multi-pack median (9.1 percent) and THB 0.97 above the median of all 22 direct rows, THB 14.03 (DERIVED).
- Overseas cubes, per cube as sold and per 330 mL. As sold, a waterdrop Focus Lemon-Lime cube is THB 22.81 on sale at a UK retailer (THB 25.34 regular, with 5% VAT), a waterdrop Lemon Lime pack of 12 servings is THB 39.11 a serving in the US store (regular, before sales tax; the page says servings, so one cube per serving is an ASSUMPTION), a Ricola Drink Cube is THB 22.59 on sale and THB 28.21 regular in the waterdrop EU store, and a Nuun Sport tablet is THB 25.13 in the US store (a Strawberry Lemonade tube; the 10-tablet count is an ASSUMPTION): 1.5 to 2.6 times the cube's THB 15 (DERIVED). These units are dosed for 400 to 600 mL (waterdrop UK and Ricola; the US waterdrop dose is an ASSUMPTION carried from the UK product) or for 500 mL (Nuun, the directions on a Canadian listing; 473 mL is its nutrition basis), not 330 mL. Scaled to a 330 mL serve at the label's central dilution (500 mL; an ASSUMPTION that taste scales with the dose), the two sale prices are level with the cube (waterdrop UK sale THB 15.05, Ricola sale THB 14.91), the other prices read 1.1 to 1.7 times the cube (UK regular THB 16.73, Nuun THB 16.58 at 500 mL, Ricola regular THB 18.62, waterdrop US THB 25.81), and across the 400 to 600 mL doses the sale prices run THB 12.42 to THB 18.82 (from about 16 to 17 percent below the cube at a 600 mL dose to about 24 to 25 percent above it at 400 mL). The claim that overseas cubes are dearer per serve therefore holds per cube as sold and at regular prices, and does not hold at the two sale prices once the dose is scaled.
- Thai effervescent tablets, rebuilt from every Priceza row read (34 counted rows, inclusion rule in section 3.3). Lemon or lime vitamin and mineral tablets read a median of THB 7.35 per tablet (10 rows; THB 5.95 to THB 49.00; middle half THB 6.71 to THB 9.00); without the three Interpharma rows, whose effervescent form no listing title shows (UNVERIFIED), the median is THB 7.25 (7 rows). Berocca and Redoxon vitamin tablets read a median of THB 14.90 (14 rows; THB 9.93 at ALL ONLINE to THB 43.27; middle half THB 13.62 to THB 16.39). The cube at THB 15 is level with the Berocca and Redoxon median (THB 14.90; THB 0.10 above). Without the two rows whose title states an expiry year of 2027 the median is THB 14.59 (THB 0.41 above), and on the six Berocca and Redoxon rows whose own title states the effervescent form it is THB 15.13 (THB 0.13 below). The cube is about twice the median lemon tablet (2.04 times; 2.5 times the cheapest, THB 5.95). The earlier statement that Thai tablets sit in the same band as the cube was true for Berocca and Redoxon only and is withdrawn for lemon tablets. These tablets are vitamin supplements with no soda claim.
- Coca-Cola Japan's tea and coffee cube was THB 8.48 a cube before tax in 2021 (tea and coffee, Japan only, no carbonation); scaled to 330 mL a tea cube reads THB 7.00 (400 mL) to THB 13.99 (200 mL).
- SodaStream is a different route. A home soda from a SodaStream flavour mix and a CO2 cylinder costs about THB 13.76 to THB 16.22 per 330 mL before the machine, on the UK cylinder price and its stated yield of up to 60 L (DERIVED from P prices; a floor, because the yield is a maximum). Applying the same yield to the Canadian cylinder price, which states no yield, gives a low end of THB 9.99 (PROXY).
- Ownership changes the picture for two names. Nuun is leaving Nestle: Nestle's own release of 1 September 2026 (Vevey) says it agreed to sell Nuun and six other brands to Yellow Wood Partners for USD 1.0 billion (CHF 0.8 billion), closing expected in the first half of 2027 (P). Ricola Drink Cubes are not an independent precedent: the company release says they were developed with waterdrop (P).
- The nearest analogue to a lemon soda cube is waterdrop's Soft Drink Alternatives range in its US store ("Fizzy, familiar, but different"): Cola, Lemon Lime, Lemon, Grapefruit and Orange cubes at USD 13.99 for 12 servings (P, 20 October 2026), which trend.at calls "zuckerfreie Brausewürfel" (sugar-free fizzy cubes, S). No page read states whether a waterdrop cube makes the water fizz or at what level.
- None of the analogues is a bottler. All owners are brand owners or brand marketers, so their margins are not comparable with the Thai bottlers' margins and are not comparable with each other (section 6).
- waterdrop is a different company from Waterdrop Inc. (NYSE: WDH), whose figures are not used here.

### 3.4 Reading

At THB 15 the cube is a single-serve price between the multi-pack can and the single can.
