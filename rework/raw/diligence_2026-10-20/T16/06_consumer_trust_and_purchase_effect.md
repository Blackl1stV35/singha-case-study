# Thai consumer trust in creators and purchase effect, read 20 October 2026

Task T16. Route: WebFetch (model-written extracts with quoted Thai sentences; translation ours). Tier S for all. None of these is specific to beverages. Samples and fieldwork dates are given where the page gave them.

- Kantar Thai shopper trends 2025, via Marketing Oops, 29 March 2025, https://www.marketingoops.com/reports/kantar-thai-shopper-2025/ : "70 percent of Thais follow influencers and content creators"; "59 percent of Thais like watching product reviews and usage advice" (global average 38 percent); "53 percent of Thai shoppers trust influencers and content creators more than friends or family" (global average 29 percent); 68 percent of Thais shop via mobile. No sample size or fieldwork dates on the page. Tier S (trade press reporting Kantar).
- YouGov with Spark Communications, via Brand Buffet, 26 March 2026, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/03/journalism-one-of-the-most-trusted-professions-in-thai-society/ : survey of 2,000 respondents aged 18 and over across Thailand, "most trustworthy profession": journalists 40 percent, doctors 24 percent, influencers 14 percent, teachers 12 percent, police 7 percent, politicians 3 percent. Fieldwork dates not stated. Tier S. This is the one trust reading that ranks influencers below doctors and journalists.
- Kantar and TikTok via Brand Buffet, 27 February 2025, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2025/02/tiktok-insight-songkran-thai-users-behavior-2025/ : TikTok users, Songkran and summer 2025: 64 percent search for reviews and product recommendations after viewing content; 70 percent look for promotions or discounts; 58 percent plan to buy on TikTok Shop; 55 percent feel closer to a brand after creator videos and 56 percent are inspired to try a new product (these two are from TikTok Marketing Science studies of 2022, not 2025); 95 percent are interested in livestreams (a TikTok-commissioned survey, firm not named); TikTok users budget 25 percent more for food and drink than non-users, and ages 25 to 34 plan to spend an average of THB 4,200 on food and drink (Kantar survey, no sample or dates). The sponsor of the data is the platform.
- AnyMind via Brand Buffet, 10 May 2026, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/05/key-movement-of-thailand-influencer/ : "over 61 percent of consumers trust creator recommendations more than conventional advertising" and "70 percent of brands set aside a separate influencer budget, up from 37 percent in 2019". Labelled in the article as 2026 global trend figures, not Thai.
- Unattributed survey via Brand Buffet, 15 July 2025, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2025/07/micro-and-nano-influencer-marketing/ : "61 percent of consumers trust influencers; 38 percent trust brand content"; "70 percent of teenagers trust influencers more than celebrities". The survey is not named; no sample. Tier UNVERIFIED for the figures (page read; origin not traceable).
- TikTok with Accenture via Brand Buffet, 26 March 2026, https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2026/03/tiktok-shop-discovery-commerce/ : "76 percent of Asia-Pacific consumers are likely to act after watching trusted content". Regional; platform-sponsored.
- Tellscore via Marketing Oops, 19 March 2025: "80 percent of Asian consumers who follow influencers are likely to buy recommended products"; source of the 80 percent not named.
- Spread on trust: 53 percent (Kantar, Thai shoppers trust creators more than friends and family), 61 percent (unnamed, Thai, versus 38 percent for brand content), 61 percent (AnyMind, global, versus ads), and 14 percent (YouGov, share naming influencers the most trustworthy profession). The questions differ; they are not comparable and are not averaged.
- Nothing found: a Thai study that separates beverage or food purchases by creator type; Nielsen Thailand trust-in-advertising data (the Nielsen tag page on Brand Buffet showed nothing after October 2024); ETDA 2024 or 2025 IUB results. Stated limits.
