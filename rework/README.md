## Folders

- `judge/`: the four judge-facing files: the hero render, the front and back label, and the 3D page. Nothing else goes here.
- `mockup/`: the team build for the cube. It holds the build scripts, the style tokens, the label and hero renders, the materials record, and the 3D page.
- `screen/`: analysis scripts with their results, the browser check, and its screenshots.
- `raw/`: raw workflow outputs and the literature register extract.
- `charts/`: chart images used in the records.
- `archive/leaf_wrapped_mockup/`: the retired leaf-wrapped mockup scripts, materials record, label artwork, and textures. Nothing in `mockup/` depends on them.
