## Functional classes in the 468 clarification, Annex 1 (Thai; our translation)

- Bulking agent (สารเพิ่มปริมาณ): a substance other than air or water that adds to the volume of a food without a significant effect on its energy value (terms: bulking agent, filler).
- Carbonating agent (สารให้ก๊าซคาร์บอนไดออกไซด์).
- Carrier (สารช่วยทำละลายหรือช่วยพา): includes diluent, encapsulating agent, dispersing agent, tablet disintegrant, dissolution enhancer, tablet and capsule lubricant, granulation aid, glidant.
- Stabilizer (สารทำให้คงตัว): includes binder and tablet binder. Thickener (สารให้ความข้นเหนียว): includes binder.
- Sweetener (สารให้ความหวาน): substances that give a sweet taste other than monosaccharide or disaccharide sugars; includes intense sweetener and bulk sweetener.

## Annex 4 of the clarification: dry product made up to a drink (Thai, formula only)

Level in the ready-to-drink product F = A x E / C, where A = grams of dry product, E = additive level in the product formula (mg/kg), C = A / D + B, B = mL of water or liquid, D = density of the dry product (use 2 if unknown). The notes say the calculation is preliminary and the actual level should be checked against laboratory analysis, and that if an additive is below 100 percent purity the actual amount is used.

## Annex 5 of the clarification: carry-over (Thai, summary)

An additive permitted in a raw material or component but not in the final product may be present from carry-over if its use in that component follows the annex conditions and the residual in the final product does not exceed the level permitted in the component, scaled by the component's share in the final recipe. An additive not permitted in the component but permitted in the final product may be present only if the component is made solely for use in a final product where the additive is permitted. Relevance: a flavour carrier.

## Limits

- The 468 row for each binder candidate was not opened. The 418 rows are used as a stated proxy for the 468 rows, with the earlier match for potassium bicarbonate and citric acid as the only supporting evidence. This stays UNVERIFIED for 468.
- No Thai ruling was sought on whether an effervescent cube is category 14.1.4.3 (concentrate, solid) or another category; the 468 clarification states that a permission for 14.1.4 also covers 14.1.4.1 to 14.1.4.3.
