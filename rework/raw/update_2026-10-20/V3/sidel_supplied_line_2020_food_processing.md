## Notes

- The article gives two different figures for the line: 33,000 cans per hour for the "33 mL format" and 29,000 cans per hour for the complete line. The first is probably a typo for 330 mL, but the page does not say so. The spread is recorded, not resolved.
- Converted to cans per minute: 29,000 divided by 60 is about 483. 33,000 divided by 60 is about 550.
- The upgraded line in the same article is measured in trays per hour, so it is not used.
