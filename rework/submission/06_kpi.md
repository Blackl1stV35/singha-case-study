# KPI section: Lemon Soda (19 October 2026)

Status: desk-based. No bench, sensory, humidity or consumer test has run; no outside party has been contacted.

## Tiers

Tiers are defined once, in Appendix A.

## KPI tree

Objective: repeat use of Lemon Soda, a dissolvable pouch cube made into a lemon drink on the spot. As a new form, it is measured by trial and repeat, not only sales.

Overlap: the Singha Lemon Soda can (330 mL; listed on the company product page, tier S; retail sale not dated; Source 8) is the comparison product, and it matches Lemon Soda on flavour. Its price is not verified. Lemon Soda differs on form (a cube made into the drink in the glass, one cube per pouch) and on pack (no ready-to-drink can). The can's own claims are not checked here, and Lemon Soda makes no sugar or calorie claim.

- **Trial:** units per pilot store per week; national listings.
- **Repeat:** share of first buyers who buy again.
- **Price:** shelf price realised; ex-factory share of shelf.
- **Contribution:** ex-factory revenue less variable cost, per cube.
- **Sell-through:** share of placed stock sold within 90 days.
- **Cannibalisation:** share of Lemon Soda buyers who switch from the Singha Lemon Soda can.

## Kill criteria

1. **Sweetener:** no permitted sweetener for the classification (sucralose or steviol glycosides). No sale.
2. **Dose:** the format stops if R&D's 0.55-volume gate is not met.
3. **Taste:** blind preference below 55% at pH 3.5 (ASSUMPTION). One acid and pH re-set, then stop.
4. **Pouch:** in-pack reaction above 0.5% at 35 °C (ASSUMPTION). No launch until the barrier changes.
5. **Potassium:** a reviewer rejects 317 mg per serve. No sale.
6. **Economics:** contribution below 3.51 baht per cube on the recommended 6-cube carton (DERIVED: 4.91 baht base less 1.40 baht for a 2.70 baht pouch, the ASSUMPTION high case; the cost excludes binder and flavour, not priced), or repeat below 15% on two cohort reads (ASSUMPTION). Stop scale-up.

## Limits

- The 0.55 dose is recommended; its gate is for R&D to restate before the run (section 10, Gate 3). Ice cases keep 0.50 and 0.47 volume; the every-serve statement covers 330 mL only.
- The annex edition is not yet checked against the B.E. 2568 clarification; the steviol row is unread.
- Desk only; no sample weighed. No domestic evidence was found for potassium bicarbonate, citric acid or the pouch price. Shelf price, margin and fixed costs are ASSUMPTIONS.
- No Thai supplier quote requested. One cube per pouch is assumed; the brief's three-component wording is unchecked.
- Not done: medical reviewer, regulator query, Thai classification opinion, supplier quotes, wider trademark search.
