## 1. No. 394, clause 3 (food products required to bear nutrition labelling and GDA)

"(6) Beverages
(6.1) Beverages which are produced from or contain fruits, edible plants or vegetables whether or not carbon dioxide or oxygen gas dissolves, except dried herbal tea drinks;
(6.2) Beverages which are produced from or contain other constituents which are not fruits, edible plants or vegetables whether or not carbon dioxide or oxygen gas dissolve;
(6.3) Beverage according to (6.1) or (6.2) in dry form"

## 2. No. 394, clause 5 (what must be displayed)

"(1) Nutrition Labelling shall be displayed either one of the following: (1.1) Full format of nutrition facts ... or (1.2) Simplified format of nutrition facts ... in which total energy, total fat, protein, total carbohydrate, sugar and sodium shall be displayed."
"(2) Together with energy value, the content of sugar, fat and sodium (Guideline Daily Amounts, GDAs) shall be displayed, complying to the attachment No.1 of this Notification."

## 3. No. 466 (amends the GDA annex; English translation), attachment

- "1.1 Four figures of vertical cylindrical shape attached together for consecutive displaying of energy value, sugar, fat, and sodium content"
- "2.2 The second section displays energy value and the content of sugar, fat and sodium per one package which is calculated from the amount of one serving size multiplied by the number of times of consumption per pack"
- "Unit of sodium is milligram or mg"
- "2.3 ... Percentage of sodium based on 2,000 mg"
- Clause 2 of No. 466: the earlier annex of No. 394 "shall be repealed and replaced". Clause 1: the notification "shall come into force as from the day following date of its publication in the Government Gazette". Given on 27 November B.E. 2568 (2025). The Thai FDA list gives the effective date as 12 December 2568 (2025).

## 4. No. 446 (English translation)

- Clause 1 repeals clause 5(1) of No. 394 and replaces it with: "(1) Nutrition Labelling shall be complied with format and provisions of displaying of nutrition information of attachment No.1 of the Notification of Ministry of Public Health, Re: Nutrition Labelling" (text re-read 20 October 2026 from the FDA unofficial translation). The simplified format in the original clause 5 (1.2) is therefore replaced; the panel follows Annex 1 of Notification 445, whose formats carry potassium.
- Clause 3: "This notification shall come into force after 180 days from its publication in the Government Gazette." Given on 17 December B.E. 2566 (2023).

## 5. Notes (our reading)

- The cube, a beverage in dry form, falls under clause 3 (6.3). So the GDA panel and a nutrition panel (full or simplified, with sodium) are required for the cube as drafted. The current draft label in the deck (Slide 6) and the KPI sheet ("No nutrition or benefit statement") do not provide for this panel.
- Whether the regulator applies clause 3 (6.2) or (6.3) to a product with a lemon flavour and no fruit is not settled by this text; the dry-form line (6.3) reads on the cube either way (our reading).
- This text is the FDA's unofficial English translation. The Thai original and the FDA's current consolidated version were not read (limit).

## 6. Correction (20 October 2026)

- Section 2 above quotes clause 5 (1.2) of the original No. 394 (simplified format with energy, fat, protein, carbohydrate, sugar and sodium). That wording was repealed by No. 446 (section 4). Do not rely on the simplified format. Notification 467 (B.E. 2568) amends the Annex of Notification 445 (panel background colour per a secondary database); its text was not read.
