## What the abstract states

- Data and method: weekly off-trade sales volumes from 37,346 stores in 651 US counties, 2013 to 2015, matched to daily maximum temperature and US federal holidays; seasonal ARIMA models with exogenous regressors.
- Result: sales of beer, liquor, red wine and white wine are temperature sensitive throughout the year; rose wine, sparkling wine and other wines are not.
- Regional result: "In the warmest regions, liquor and white wine sales do not respond to temperature changes", while they do in the coolest regions (fragment as the extract returned it).
- The abstract gives no per-degree coefficient in the extract.
